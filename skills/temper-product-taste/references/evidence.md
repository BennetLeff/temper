**Engineering extensions for Temper, not claims made by these courses:** Observe cooking tasks with representative users; judge control feedback under noise/glare, spill cleanup, pan placement, fault recovery, and service access. Cross-check product choices against PCB, firmware, enclosure, thermal design, and assembly. Use a test that distinguishes live alternatives, then keep the observation and inference separate. These are proposed applications of the source methods; their outcome is unknown until measured.
