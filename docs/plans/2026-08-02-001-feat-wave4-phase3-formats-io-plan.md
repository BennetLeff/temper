# Wave 4 Phase 3: Formats/IO Migration — Plan

## Goal Capsule

**Objective:** carry the Wave-4 Phase 3 formats/IO surface to decided end states — the kiutils KiCad parse/export engine, the config/netclass/loop/reference loaders, the DSN surface, and the write path — plus the D5 board/netlist re-decide that the Phase-2 residual pass deferred to this pull. The measured surface is `packages/temper-placer/src/temper_placer/io/` (9,229 LOC at origin/main `3040db99e`, 35 modules, 17 of them kiutils-importing at ~4.45k LOC of io/ surface) plus `core/board.py` (803) and `core/netlist.py` (440) as the parse targets. The phase is tractable only because the contract layer landed: net_types, loops, design_rules, gates, and priority are Rust pyclasses in `packages/temper-design-bundle/`, and `packages/temper-io-types/` already ships the DSN primitives, golden serializers, footprint parser, config-board binding, provenance, and zone filler. The gate that closes the phase is the program's "kiutils dependency removed or isolated".

**Product authority:** temper-placer plus temper-design-bundle/temper-io-types maintainers, with residual verdicts (R3) reviewed at the program's product authority.

**Open blockers:** none blocking the pull; the two recorded risk centers are (1) parse-parity finickiness across the kiutils oracle (float-parse semantics plus the B-catalog classes) and (2) the write engine's input entanglement with unmigrated deterministic state and router_v6 types — both resolved in-plan (D2/D5/Q1/Q2), with the honest-R1-reach fallback being an R3 verdict, not silent deferral. "Done" for the phase is: board/netlist are Rust pyclasses, kiutils no longer imports in product code, the corpus round trip is bit-identical, and every io/ module carries a recorded verdict — MIGRATE, RETIRE, or JUSTIFIED-KEEP under the program's R3 procedure.

## Product Contract

### Summary

Phase 3 is the formats/IO migration: the KiCad file surface (parse and export), the config loaders, the DSN surface, and the write path move onto the Rust seeds, and the D5 re-decide lands board/netlist as the parse targets. The phase's dependency spine is unchanged from the program: parse output flows into the contract layer, so the contract migration (board/netlist) precedes the parser, and the parser precedes the writer. Six of the 35 io/ modules are already Rust delegation shims (export_types, footprint_parser, golden_serializers, config_board_binding, zone_filler, design_bundle_preflight), so the phase completes what those shims started rather than starting fresh.

### Problem Frame

The io/ surface is measured at 9,229 LOC across 35 modules (verified 2026-08-03 at origin/main `3040db99e`), of which 17 modules import kiutils (10 io/ runtime: `kicad_parser.py` 228, `kicad_exporter.py` 779, `kicad_metadata.py` 391, `_write_board.py` 532, `_write_modules.py` 271, `_write_tracks.py` 340, `_write_types.py` 90, `_write_zones.py` 106, `zone_manager.py` 352, `provenance.py` 80; 5 io/ TYPE_CHECKING-only: `_parse_board.py` 389, `_parse_modules.py` 418, `_parse_nets.py` 314, `_parse_tracks.py` 98, `_parse_zones.py` 66; plus 2 runtime outside io/: `analysis/_violation_report.py` and `router_v6/constraints_design_rules.py`, both added to the R4 scope below — `placement_exporter.py`'s kiutils mention is a comment, not an import) and 4 import numpy (`dsn_exporter.py` 559, `placement_exporter.py`, `reference_loader.py` 405, `snapshot.py` 197). Six modules are already Rust delegation shims (`export_types.py`, `footprint_parser.py`, `golden_serializers.py`, `config_board_binding.py`, `zone_filler.py`, `design_bundle_preflight.py`, 420 LOC together), so the phase completes a half-landed surface rather than starting fresh.

The parse surface is a re-export hub: `parse_kicad_pcb` constructs `ParseResult` holding `Netlist`/`Component`/`Net` (from `core/netlist.py`), `Board` (from `core/board.py`), traces, vias, pads, and warnings; `parse_kicad_pcb_v6` wraps the legacy parse plus design-rules and stackup extraction into `router_v6/stage0_data.py::ParsedPCB`. The parse consumers are the widest in the phase: `kicad_parser` is imported by 24 src modules and 76 including tests, `config_loader` by 15 src / 55 total, `kicad_exporter` by 5 src / 8 total, and `netclass_loader` by 2 src / 14 total. The write surface is a re-export hub over the `_write_*` family (`kicad_writer.py`), and `_kicad_types.py` exists specifically to break the `router_v6 → io` import cycle by holding `ParseResult`/`TraceData`/`ViaData`/`PadData` with no temper_placer runtime deps. Two dead or stub surfaces sit inside the phase: `net_class_manager.py` (524 LOC, zero consumers measured anywhere in `packages/`) and `parse_kicad_schematic` (`_parse_schematic_sheet` is a `pass` stub that returns an empty netlist, exercised only by `tests/io/test_integration.py`).

The kiutils boundary is the phase's structural risk: kiutils (`kiutils>=1.4.0` in `packages/temper-placer/pyproject.toml`) is the third-party KiCad parse/export library, and the program's Phase-3 row says "kiutils leaves the boundary" with the Wave-3 Q6 "likely stays Python" lean explicitly a verdict to be earned, not inherited. The board/netlist re-decide context: the Phase-2 residual pass JUSTIFIED-KEEP'd both (scorecard in `packages/temper-design-bundle/VERIFICATION.md` lines 219-231 and 555) on D5 dependency rationale plus "entangled beyond the R1 gate's honest reach (consumer counts an order of magnitude over the landed migrations, non-reproducible numerics)", with "re-decide at Phase 3 pull" recorded. The verified numpy surface in the two contracts is bounded: `Board` returns `np.float32` arrays for outline polygon and bounding boxes (`board.py` lines 611/717/721) and `Netlist` builds a `np.float32` adjacency matrix and bounds array (`netlist.py` lines 224/381/424); the recorded "eigh/spectral" rationale IS present: the eigenvector-centrality method at `netlist.py:427` calls `np.linalg.eigh` (not bit-reproducible across BLAS implementations) — it is the one non-deterministic kernel, and the delegation shim keeps it in Python alongside the numpy `float32` wrappers, so it does not gate the contract's bit-parity differential.

The round-trip parity requirement: the existing `tests/test_round_trip_integrity.py` asserts structural preservation only (net-graph isomorphism) on the corpus at `power_pcb_dataset/corpus/` (temper, minimal, bitaxe_ultra, rp2040_designguide, piantor_right), so the phase must add geometric bit-parity round trips; `tests/io/` is 35 files / 5,350 LOC and `tests/io/test_dsn_kicad.py` already pins deterministic-DSN importability by KiCad's SPECCTRA importer. The DSN surface is mid-flight: `temper-io-types` landed `DSNExpression`/`DSNRect`/`DSNCircle`/`DSNPath`/`dsn_list` plus golden serializers (whose `serialize_boardstate_to_dsn` currently imports the Python `io/dsn_exporter.py`), but `io/dsn.py` (131 LOC) still defines parallel Python DSN types and `dsn_exporter.py` (559 LOC, numpy `np.argmax` rotation indices) still builds on them. The deterministic-DSN chain is contractual: `dsn_schema.py` computes a schema hash embedded as a header and `dsn_validator.py` fails closed on mismatch, so the migration must preserve the hash and the emitter's exact float formatting (the landed `temper-io-types` `format_dsn_arg` already replicates the `{:.6}`-trim convention). The loaders bind to already-migrated contracts: `netclass_loader.py` (83) produces `DesignRules` and `loop_loader.py` (319) produces loop pyclasses, both Rust since Phase 2, while `config_loader.py` (967) sits on pydantic plus the generated `_constraint_types` stubs and produces `Board`/`PlacementConstraints`/`NetGraph`.

### Key Decisions

- D1. **Phase-3 scope is the formats/IO surface as named by the program, plus the D5 re-decide** (session-settled: user-directed at the program pull — chosen over pulling only the parse surface and over deferring the loaders: the program's Phase-3 row names `kicad_parser`/`kicad_exporter`/`_parse_*`/`_write_*`/`config_loader`/`netclass_loader`/`footprint_parser` as the scope, and the Phase-2 residual record settles that board/netlist are re-decided at this pull, not deferred again). Governs R1, R8.
- D2. **The D5 re-decide: board/netlist MIGRATE in Phase 3 as the parse targets** (inferred from dependency evidence — chosen over keeping them as Python parse output with the Rust parser producing Python objects: `parse_kicad_pcb`'s output IS `Board`/`Netlist`/`ParseResult`, so a Rust parser with Python dataclass targets is the double-mapping the program's D5 rejected; the Phase-2 scorecard's counter-evidence — 100+ consumers and non-reproducible numerics — is overturned at this pull by the pure-delegation shim pattern (public API preserved with any consumer adaptations landed inside the migration PR — the `core/loop.py`/`core/design_rules.py` precedent includes `io/loop_loader.py`'s `LoopType.members()` adaptation, so candidate 1 budgets a consumer-semantics audit for iteration, dunders, and numpy dtype behavior) and by gating construction and round-trip field parity bit-identical, because float32 conversion is IEEE-deterministic per value and the non-reproducibility concern attaches to the shim-owned numpy kernels (the float32 wrappers and the line-427 centrality method), not to the Rust-held data model and construction). The measured numpy surface confirms the gate is achievable: `Board`'s float32 returns are direct conversions of its own f64 fields (`board.py` lines 611/717/721), `Netlist`'s bounds array and adjacency matrix are deterministic constructions (`netlist.py` lines 224/381/424), and the recorded "eigh/spectral" rationale IS present: the eigenvector-centrality method at `netlist.py:427` calls `np.linalg.eigh` (not bit-reproducible across BLAS implementations) — it is the one non-deterministic kernel, and the delegation shim keeps it in Python alongside the numpy `float32` wrappers, so it does not gate the contract's bit-parity differential. Governs R1.
- D3. **The kiutils boundary is REMOVE, with the program's "isolated" arm as the recorded fallback** (session-settled: the program's Phase-3 row names "kiutils leaves the boundary" — chosen over a blanket JUSTIFIED-KEEP of kiutils: Wave-3 Q6's lean is a verdict to be earned, not inherited; the strategy is inferred: the kiutils-importing modules (17 total — 10 io/ runtime, 5 io/ TYPE_CHECKING-only, 2 runtime outside io/ — see the Problem Frame inventory) migrate onto a Rust KiCad s-expression engine in `temper-io-types`, and any module that cannot clear the program R1 gates records a JUSTIFIED-KEEP with a version-locked kiutils pin and a named blocker under the program's R3). Governs R4.
- D4. **The parse chain is the critical path and board/netlist are its first link** (inferred — chosen over parser-first with Python models and over loaders-first as the spine: the parser's bit-parity differential needs the migrated target types to assert against first, and the loaders are independent of the parse engine, so they are the opportunistic parallel track, not the spine). Governs the candidate order.
- D5. **The write/export engine migrates over a duck-typed `from_py_object` boundary where its inputs are unmigrated, and entangled candidates record program-R3 verdicts** (inferred — chosen over waiting for deterministic state and router_v6 types to migrate first: the landed `temper-io-types` serializers already read attributes off Python objects (`serialize_boardstate_to_dsn` reads `state.board`/`state.netlist`), so the exporter can move before its Phase-4/5 inputs; write-path candidates whose inputs are entangled beyond the honest R1 reach (e.g. `placement_exporter.py`'s numpy `PositionState` path) record a JUSTIFIED-KEEP pending Phase 4/5). Governs R7, Q2.
- D6. **The DSN surface migrates onto the landed `temper-io-types` primitives and `io/dsn.py`'s Python types retire** (inferred — chosen over porting the Python DSN types to Rust anew and over keeping `io/dsn.py` as the exporter's types: `temper-io-types` already ships `DSNExpression`/`DSNRect`/`DSNCircle`/`DSNPath` and the golden serializers, so `dsn_exporter.py` re-points onto them and `io/dsn.py` (131 LOC) is retired under R3 after its consumers and tests re-point). Governs R6.
- D7. **Round-trip parity is re-parse parity for the writer and byte-identical parse parity for the reader** (inferred — resolves the program's Q4: the reader's differential pins byte-identical `ParseResult` against the verbatim kiutils oracle, and the writer's gate is field-equal `parse(export(x))` vs `parse(x)` on the corpus — comparing the parser's model, NOT the exported bytes, which are consumed by KiCad — paired with a per-corpus byte-golden of exported files and/or a KiCad-import assertion in the `test_dsn_kicad.py` style, so unmodeled sections (net-class names, zone fill, text, header attributes) cannot silently drop through the writer). Governs R2, R3.

### Requirements

- R1. **Board/netlist contract migration:** `core/board.py` and `core/netlist.py` migrate to Rust pyclasses in `temper-design-bundle` and keep their public API through pure-delegation shims; the differential pins construction and round-trip field parity bit-identical against the verbatim pre-migration dataclasses; every numpy `float32`-returning method stays in the shim as a thin deterministic wrapper, and the one non-deterministic kernel — the module-level `compute_eigenvector_centrality` (`netlist.py:406`, `np.linalg.eigh` at 427) — stays in the shim as the explicit non-deterministic exception, never gated by the contract's bit-parity differential.
- R2. **Parse parity:** `parse_kicad_pcb` migrates with bit-identical `ParseResult` parity against the verbatim kiutils oracle on the corpus plus `pcb/temper.kicad_pcb`, asserting field-by-field `==` with the B-catalog classes applied (B5 min/max NaN semantics, float-parse semantics, B7 operation order); the `parse_kicad_pcb_v6` wrapper is re-pointed over the migrated engine with bit-identical `ParsedPCB` parity, the wrapper and `ParsedPCB` staying Python with their verdict owned by Phase 5 under the program's R3.
- R3. **Round-trip bit-parity:** the corpus round trip (parse → export → re-parse) is bit-identical; the structural-only `tests/test_round_trip_integrity.py` is extended with geometric bit-parity fixtures.
- R4. **The kiutils gate:** no runtime `import kiutils` remains in `temper_placer` product code once the parse/export candidates close, including the two outside-io/ importers (`analysis/_violation_report.py`, `router_v6/constraints_design_rules.py` — each migrates or records an R3 verdict in its own phase); TYPE_CHECKING-only imports count against the gate only when they become runtime; `kiutils>=1.4.0` moves to a version-locked test/oracle-only dependency group in `packages/temper-placer/pyproject.toml` (it stays as the differential oracle); any module that cannot clear the gates is isolated behind a version-locked adapter with a recorded R3 JUSTIFIED-KEEP naming the blocker.
- R5. **Loader parity:** `io/netclass_loader.py`, `io/loop_loader.py`, `io/config_loader.py`, `io/footprint_library.py`, `io/reference_loader.py`, and `io/reference_aliases.py` migrate with bit-identical YAML-to-contract parity; netclass and loop loaders are the low-risk first pulls because their target contracts (DesignRules, loop) are already Rust pyclasses.
- R6. **DSN determinism:** the DSN chain (`io/dsn_exporter.py`, `io/dsn_schema.py`, `io/dsn_validator.py`, `io/dsn_normalizer.py`) migrates onto the landed `temper-io-types` DSN primitives, the KiCad-importability fixture `tests/io/test_dsn_kicad.py` stays pinned, and `io/dsn.py`'s Python types retire.
- R7. **API stability through the migration:** `parse_kicad_pcb`'s consumer chain (pipeline InputStage, `router_v6` stage0, `io/reference_loader.py`, scripts) observes no behavior change; every delegation module preserves every public name, signature, and numpy-returning method.
- R8. **Residuals decided at this pull under the program's R3 with the Step-3 evidence template:** `io/net_class_manager.py` → RETIRE (LOC 524; consumers 0 src measured; deps: none at module level; churn: low — no git-history activity); `parse_kicad_schematic` → RETIRE (LOC ~30 stub incl. `_parse_schematic_sheet` at `kicad_parser.py:122-130`, `pass` body returning an empty netlist; consumers: `tests/io/test_integration.py` only; deps: none; churn: n/a — stub) with its test updated in the same PR; `io/zone_manager.py`, `io/provenance.py`, `io/snapshot.py`, `io/via_dedup.py`, `io/boundary_registry.py`, and `io/isolation_slot_geometry.py` (8 LOC, Rust twin already in temper-io-types) → MIGRATE or JUSTIFIED-KEEP recorded at the residual candidate.
- R9. **Every candidate carries the full program gate set (R1a-R1h) and the B1-B10 bit-exactness catalog:** data-only candidates (board/netlist contracts) record structural proofs or explicit non-applicability notes per R1e; physics-gated surfaces record the R24 N/A.
- R10. **The round-trip corpus and golden fixtures stay frozen through the phase:** `power_pcb_dataset/corpus/` and `power_pcb_dataset/goldens/` are byte-stable inputs to the differential oracles, and any board change during the phase re-measures per the repo's provenance gates rather than silently moving the oracle.
