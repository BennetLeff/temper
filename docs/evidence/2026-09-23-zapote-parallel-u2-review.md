## Review corrections made before commit

- **Coupled fault:** AUX loss can both close the normally closed discharge contact onto a still live bus and stop the fan. The discharge and cooling records now carry 21.33/27.0 W intact and 32.0/40.5 W one-short continuous VB path screens at 400/450 V. Forced-air resistor ratings cannot be borrowed for the fan-off case.
- **Power-cycle restart:** The cooling fault latch is volatile. The producer contract now requires fresh self-check and deliberate interlock/Rev38 reset after complete power loss; inspection across cycles remains an unadopted policy requiring durable memory or physical service reset.
- **Stiff-bus model:** The inverter's 49.74 A low-L point is a transient-stress screen. The nominal 2240 µF bank has about 69.6 J between 390 and 300 V, so no sustained capacitor-current or thermal verdict follows without source impedance and PFC limiting.
- **F2 coupling:** The discharge fixture now tests F2-closed combined VD+VB capacitance. At a hypothetical 450 → 34 V/60 s edge with +100 µF and 5 s release, the F2-open VB result is 59.55 s, the F2-closed candidate with VD paths is 59.02 s, and omitting those VD paths is 60.03 s. Those small ideal margins do not select a circuit.
- **Mounted rating:** Vishay RH50's 40 W at 70 °C uses a 536 cm² chassis fixture. The four-resistor shared enclosure, fan-off fault, pulse energy and equivalent thermal path remain construction gates.
