# IEC 60335-1 current-edition status

The official IEC publication record identifies **IEC 60335-1:2020+AMD1:2025
CSV**, Edition 6.0, as the current consolidated publication:

<https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/61880>

The record says that Edition 6.0 cancels and replaces IEC
60335-1:2010+AMD1:2013+AMD2:2016, Edition 5.2. The older publication record is
withdrawn:

<https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/24738>

The current consolidated text is a paid IEC publication and was not available
in this worktree. Therefore this evidence establishes edition metadata only.
It does **not** verify clause numbering, protective-impedance requirements,
or any PD2/PD3 construction conclusion against the current text. Those
decisions remain deferred until the applicable current Part 2 and the current
IEC 60335-1 text are obtained and reviewed.
