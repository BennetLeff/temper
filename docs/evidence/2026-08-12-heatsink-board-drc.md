# The heatsink board is the best board of this session on every axis except one, and that one is disqualifying as the ceiling protocol is written: `clearance` +115

> **Verdict up front.** PR #1082's board **regenerates exactly** — every headline
> figure reproduces to the digit — and it is a large, real improvement: aggregate
> DRC errors **1264 → 1143** (−121), `creepage` **184 → 112** (−72),
> `solder_mask_bridge` **154 → 49** (−105), `shorting_items` **199 → 137** (−62),
> `SAF_HVL_001` **94 → 52** (−45%), `unconnected_items` **428 → 326** (−102), and
> connectivity that is not close to any prior candidate. **It is nevertheless not
> landable as-is.** The `clearance` regression that blocked the prior candidate
> **survives intact**: **386 → 501** (median, +115), against a prior candidate's
> +113. Six further categories rise past their per-type ceiling
> (`courtyards_overlap` +8, `tracks_crossing` +5, `copper_edge_clearance` +3,
> `annular_width` +2, `drill_out_of_range` +2, `via_diameter` +2). Under R27 a
> category may not exceed its `violations_by_type` entry regardless of the
> aggregate, so **seven separate Ceiling-Approval trailers** would be required.
> No ceiling paperwork was written here and none should be: that is the #1049
> failure mode, and this board is not being landed on the strength of this
> document.
>
> One genuinely encouraging structural finding: the clearance damage is
> **markedly more localised** than on the committed board — 32 occupied 20mm
> cells versus 60, with 43% of all violations in three cells and 29.5% in a
> single adjacent column band. That is consistent with a congested-region
> cause and suggests a scoped re-solve, not a placement rethink.

## 1. Method, and the two silent-truncation traps that had to be closed first

`kicad-cli` was reported missing at
`/home/bennet/.local/opt/kicad-10.0.5/bin/kicad-cli`. **It was not missing.**
Debs extract to `<prefix>/root/usr/bin/kicad-cli`; `<prefix>/bin` has never
existed. The binary was present throughout (699,760 bytes, dated Jul 24),
alongside an intact `_pcbnew.kiface` and the full OpenCASCADE set. The real
defect was that nothing had ever been placed on `PATH`, so a live install and a
genuinely wiped one failed identically for every repo tool that shells out to a
bare `kicad-cli`. Fixed durably in the same PR as this document —
`scripts/install_kicad_cli.sh`, a `~/.local/bin/kicad-cli` shim carrying
`LD_LIBRARY_PATH` and `KICAD_STOCK_DATA_HOME`, and `make drc` now depending on
`kicad-cli-check`, which fails loudly rather than skipping. See that script's
header for the full account.

A **second** trap was hit while measuring and is worth recording, because it
would have silently corrupted this entire document:

`pcb/*.kicad_dru` is **`.gitignore`d and generated** (`.gitignore:58`). The
fresh worktree used to regenerate the board therefore had no `.kicad_dru`, and
`copy_kicad_project_sidecar` copies it only *if present* — so the routed board
came out with a `.kicad_pro` beside it but **no `.kicad_dru`**, which is exactly
the configuration `docs/evidence/2026-08-08-drc-project-context-audit.md` warns
drops custom rules without erroring. The cost, measured directly on this board:

| | total errors | `creepage` | `track_width` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| routed board, **no** `.kicad_dru` beside it | **841** | absent (0) | absent (0) |
| routed board, `.kicad_dru` propagated | **1144** | 112 | 199 |

**A 303-error silent undercount, with `project resolvable: True` reported both
times** — the `.kicad_pro` check alone does not catch it. Every number in this
document was measured with the `.kicad_dru` present, regenerated byte-identical
from its SSOT generator (hash in the provenance header).

## 4. `clearance` specifically: the regression survives, but it is more localised

The prior candidate showed **+113 (386 → 499)**, attributed to one congested
region. On this placement:

**It survives, marginally larger: +115 (386 → 501 median, range 499–505).** The
heatsink co-location did not cause it and did not relieve it — the two candidates
land within 2 of each other, which strongly suggests a shared root cause in the
route stage or the reconciled component set rather than anything about U5/U6.

`clearance` is also confirmed **nondeterministic** on this board (499–505 across
130 samples, spread 6) where the committed board is near-stable (385–386, spread
1) — a wider run-to-run spread that is itself worth noting.

Spatial distribution of the clearance errors (first sample, positions binned into
20mm cells):

|---|---:|---:|
| occupied 20mm cells | 60 | **32** |

Heatsink hot cells: `x[40,60) y[180,200)` 79 (15.6%), `x[40,60) y[140,160)` 70
(13.9%), `x[160,180) y[240,260)` 68 (13.5%), `x[20,40) y[220,240)` 60 (11.9%).

The first two are **adjacent, in one column band** — 149 violations, **29.5% of

"one congested region" framing is *more* defensible on this board than on the
committed one, and the damage is concentrated enough that a scoped re-solve of
that strip is a credible path to clearing the largest single blocker, rather than
needing the placement rethought.

## 7. Assessment: is this board landable?

**No — not as it stands, and not on the strength of an aggregate improvement.**

What argues for it, and it is a lot:

* aggregate errors **−121**, comfortably under the 1266 ceiling
* `creepage` **−72**, `solder_mask_bridge` **−105**, `shorting_items` **−62**
* `SAF_HVL_001` **−45%**, the best HV/LV separation any candidate has produced
* `unconnected_items` **428 → 326**; real pad connectivity for the first time
* the heatsink constraint itself works: U5/U6 co-rotated, 0.02mm offset,
  buildable against the shared 392-120AB face — and containment PASSes

What blocks it:

* **`clearance` +115.** The single blocker that stopped the prior candidate,
  essentially unchanged (+113 → +115). Nothing in this PR addresses it.
* **Seven per-category ceiling breaches.** R27 is explicit that a category may
  not exceed its `violations_by_type` entry *regardless of the aggregate*, so
  "total errors went down" is not an answer to any of them.
* **`track_width` sits exactly at 199**, its ceiling — zero headroom.
* **A new warning class** (`holes_co_located`, 20).
* The PR's own connectivity headline **mixes two metrics** (§2).

Landing this would require seven `Ceiling-Approval:` trailers with attributed
causes. **I have written none, and none should be written for this board**, per
the #1049 failure mode. The correct next step is not paperwork but the scoped
re-solve §4 points at: 55% of the clearance damage sits in four 20mm cells and
29.5% in one adjacent column band, which is a tractable, local target. If that
strip can be re-solved without giving back the creepage and `SAF_HVL_001` wins,
this becomes the board to land.

**Recommendation: hold #1082. Do not commit `pcb/temper.kicad_pcb`. Keep the
placement constraint** — `heatsink_colocation` is sound, cheap (1.3s), and
produces measurably better HV/LV separation — **and re-solve the x[40,60)
congestion before re-measuring.**

## 8. What was NOT done, deliberately

* `power_pcb_dataset/drc_ceiling.json` **not modified**; no ceiling entry, no
  `Ceiling-Approval:` trailer, no `_march` log entry.
* `pcb/temper.kicad_pcb` **not touched** — `git status --short pcb/` empty
  before and after every step; sha256 unchanged.
* No reconciliation (`resync_pcb_netlist.py`) was run: this board is the
  committed netlist re-placed, matching PR #1082's own recipe. The prior
  candidate lineage *was* reconciled (168→169 components, 6 added / 7 removed),
  so the two are not perfectly comparable on component-count-sensitive
  categories.
* Only rotation index 1 was solved. Rot 0/2 and 1/3 are known to produce
  identical positions (the box model cannot distinguish a 180° flip), so the
  sweep yields two distinct placements; **rot 0 was not measured** and might
  differ on clearance. Outstanding.
