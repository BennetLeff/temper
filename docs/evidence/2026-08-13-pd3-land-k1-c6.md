# Landing the K1/C6 PD3 part swaps: both parts verified sound; K1's footprint does not yet fit the real board

**Task:** land the two K1/C6 part replacements PR #1152 ranked as "already-verified, zero new research" (step 1 of its remediation plan) -- confirm the parts still hold under first-hand, canonical-kernel re-measurement (not datasheet reading), land the source/footprint/BOM changes, and report whether each now clears 12.6mm PD3 reinforced creepage.

## Verdict up front

| Part | Creepage (measured this session) | Electrical | Placement on real board |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C6** (`Y_CAP_PE`) | **20.1000mm** (was 8.000mm) -- clears 12.6mm by **+7.5mm** | 5.6nF value change re-verified against the touch-current budget: 8-15% headroom, thin but real | **Clean.** Swapped alone, real `kicad-cli` DRC on a scratch-resynced candidate board reproduces the baseline's category counts exactly -- zero new violations of any kind. |
| **K1** (`K_BYPASS`) | **17.8000mm** (was 8.000mm) -- clears 12.6mm by **+5.2mm** | Coil is a different value (360ohm vs. 160ohm); `R_RLY_DROP` retuned 39ohm->91ohm to restore correct drive (99.8% of rated coil voltage, was 112.7%) | **Does not fit.** Every one of 4 cardinal rotations tested at the resync's chosen site produces genuine new electrical shorts against existing routed copper. A pure-courtyard CP-SAT free-solve (both fixed-copper and domain-clearance constraint families disabled) is also UNSAT -- but that check turned out to be unreliable (see Section 4) and is not the basis for this conclusion; the real `kicad-cli` DRC result is. |

**Both parts are real, orderable, and creepage-sound.** `elec/src/modules.ato`, the new footprint library file, and `docs/hardware/BOM.md` are updated for both. **`pcb/temper.kicad_pcb` is deliberately NOT resynced by this change** -- for C6 because a board rework was already the established pattern for this exact node (see the 2026-07-28 note this BOM entry sits under) and there is nothing left to flag; for K1 because resyncing it today would manufacture new electrical shorts, which this task's own hard constraints forbid ("if it does not fit, report that rather than forcing it").

## 4. Placement -- checked, not assumed

Per this task's explicit warning ("a different part may have a different footprint size, which can make it unplaceable... check that each replacement still fits at its current location"):

**Method.** `elec/src/modules.ato` was updated and `make netlist` re-run (exit 0, all assertions pass). `scripts/resync_pcb_netlist.py` was run against a **scratch copy** of `pcb/temper.kicad_pcb` (never the tracked file) to produce a candidate board with both new footprints synced in at their existing anchor positions ("keeps the OLD board's position/orientation," per that script's own docstring). Real `kicad-cli 10.0.5` DRC was run on that scratch candidate, compared against an identically-built scratch copy of the unmodified baseline board.

**A sanity check on the diff method mattered.** A raw before/after violation-set diff on the combined (K1+C6) candidate showed ~80 additional "unrelated" violations touching neither K1 nor C6. Re-running `kicad-cli` DRC twice on the *identical, unmodified* candidate board showed the violation set is not perfectly deterministic run-to-run (1236 vs. 1238 unique signatures) -- consistent with this session's environment brief warning about capped/near-cap DRC categories. To get a trustworthy signal, K1 and C6 were isolated into **separate single-swap scratch boards** (K1-new-footprint/C6-original, and C6-new-footprint/K1-original, built by direct S-expression footprint-block splicing) and the courtyard/short-class violations specifically attributable to each part were checked for reproducibility across repeated runs.

**C6 alone: clean.** The C6-only candidate reproduces the baseline's DRC category counts **exactly**, across all 20 reported categories (clearance, shorting_items, courtyards_overlap, hole_clearance, etc.) -- zero new violations of any kind, confirmed reproducible.

**K1 alone: does not fit.** The K1-only candidate shows, reproducibly:
- 5 new `shorting_items` violations: K1 pad `4` (net `w1_2`) shorts against a `rtd_pan.high_window-out` track; K1 pad `3` (net `power_in.ntc-no`) shorts against 4 separate segments of a `safety.fault_or-b2` track.
- 1 new `courtyards_overlap` violation against `C27`.

A rotation sweep (0/90/180/270 degrees, same anchor point) confirms this is not fixable by simply re-orienting the part in place -- every rotation produces multiple new shorts against different nets (`WDT_KICK`/`GATE_LS` at 0 degrees; `hb.gate_hs.driver-p1-1`/`safety.thermal-line`/`power_in.bypass_relay-coil2` at 90 degrees; the two above at 180 degrees; `+3V3`/`gnd`/UVLO/OCP/an SPI bus at 270 degrees). The root cause: the incumbent Omron footprint's contact pins (`13`/`14`) are Faston tabs on `F.Fab` only -- **zero real PCB copper** (`temper:Relay_SPST_Omron-G4A-E`'s own `descr`, corrected 2026-07-29 after they were found shorting two nets as fictitious copper). Nearby traces were routed through what has always been, on the real board, copper-free space at that footprint. `RT33K012` is a conventional THT relay with real copper at the equivalent physical location, so it collides with exactly that routing regardless of orientation.

**A CP-SAT-based cross-check (`temper-placer repair-unplaced`, PR #1144/branch `fix/t2-repair-entrypoint`, not yet merged to this branch) was also tried**, temporarily borrowed into this worktree for verification and reverted before committing (not part of this change's diff). Its pure-courtyard free-solve (fixed-copper and domain-clearance constraint families both disabled) reported K1 UNSAT anywhere on the board, and Phase 2 with the 7 nearest neighbours displaceable up to 15mm was also UNSAT. **This result is not relied on**, because a sanity check -- running the identical free-solve for K1's *current, already-fitting* Omron footprint on the *unmodified* board -- also reported UNSAT, which is known-false (the incumbent footprint is on the committed board today with no courtyard violation against it). The tool's Phase-1 "solve from scratch anywhere on the board" search evidently has some default-domain or configuration limitation that makes it an unreliable oracle for this specific use (a component that already has a good, working position), separate from its evident correctness for the T2 "genuinely unplaced" case it was built for. The **real `kicad-cli` DRC result above -- the actual, ground-truth check, run twice for reproducibility -- is the basis for this document's K1 placement conclusion, not the CP-SAT tool.**

## 5. What was landed and what was not

**Landed** (this document's own diff):
- `elec/src/modules.ato`: `K_BYPASS`/`bypass_relay` MPN+footprint (`G4A-1A-E DC12` -> `RT33K012`), `R_RLY_DROP` value+MPN retune (39ohm -> 91ohm), `Y_CAP_PE`/`y_cap_pe` value+MPN+footprint (2.2nF `VY1222M47Y5UQ6TV0` -> 5.6nF `B81123C1562M000`).
- `pcb/libs/temper.pretty/Relay_SPST_Schrack-RT33K012.kicad_mod`: new hand-authored footprint, pad-for-pad from KiCad 10 stock, full provenance in its own `descr`.
- `docs/hardware/BOM.md`: both rows updated, explanatory notes added following this document's own established convention.

**Not landed:**
- `pcb/temper.kicad_pcb` -- neither part's footprint change was resynced onto the tracked board. For C6 this is a safe, inert gap (the resync is verified clean whenever someone does it). For K1 this is a real, open blocker: landing it as-is would introduce genuine electrical shorts. A follow-up reroute of the `safety.fault_or-b2` and `rtd_pan.high_window-out` nets (or a placement pass moving K1 and/or its neighbours) is needed before K1's new footprint can be written to the real board -- out of scope for "land the already-verified swap."
- `HV_CREEPAGE_ENFORCED_MM` / any DRU threshold -- untouched.
- `power_pcb_dataset/drc_ceiling.json` -- untouched; this document's changes never touched `pcb/temper.kicad_pcb`, so no real board DRC count changed.
