# R43 enclosure DFMA review: fabrication, fit and mechanisms

**Scope and stage.** This is an engineering-prototype planning review of the frozen R43 enclosure package at `input/enclosure-r43/`. The authoritative saved assembly is `STEP/assembly.step`, SHA-256 `0a6fcd0fe8dfa03477ffc170df59d2ab32faee8e67ea38e94059fa7066be86ce`; `CURRENT.json` calls it a design candidate pending integration review. The source manifest was verified at intake. I read the current R43/R32 decisions, source geometry, quote packet and check outputs, and inspected `renders/R43-{front,interior,rear,underside}.png`. This is a digital review. I did not regenerate CAD, inspect physical parts or assess supplier capability. R43's `evidence/native21-pose-check.json` explicitly checks an older **stage-13** native-21 STEP. Its board-dependent spaces and interfaces must be rechecked against the selected **stage21b** board before they are credited in an integrated assessment. This report covers only DFMA-25–36 and 55–60; all 18 are scored. Scores follow the new DFMA rubric: 0, 1, 2, 3, U or NA. No average expresses readiness.

**Evidence authority.** The six-part R32 shell remains the current R43 architecture: one-piece P1 seat ring, P2 fence, bent P3 fascia, bent P4 U-wrapper, P5 chassis, and C-section P6 door (`design/r32/DECISIONS.md:41-64`; `src/r32_shell.py:6-17,221-260`). R43 adds the J13 sheet barrier and floor slots (`design/r43/DECISIONS.md:19-31`). The source drawings in `design/fabrication/R43-*.dxf` and `evidence/fabrication/r32-flats.json` cover P1, P2, P5 and the louver. Bent P3/P4/P6 are quoted from STEP. The older `design/fabrication/formed-parts-review.md`, `evidence/fabrication/cover-unfold-audit.json`, `nominal-flats-audit.json`, and `design/enclosure-flat-pattern-candidate.json` concern the **retired one-piece folded cover or older door/chassis** and cannot establish R43 forming readiness. `evidence/assembly-sequence.json` does match the R43 STEP hash and reports `PASS_SCOPED_SEQUENCE_SCREEN`; its scope excludes tool access, flexible harness sweep and compliant snap/seal deformation. The rendered views show a plausible product and internal layout, but not final fit or ingress performance.

## Findings and improvement angles

**ENC-01 — Confirmed documentation contradiction, high priority.** `quote-package/QUOTE-REQUEST.md:39-48` mostly describes the current six-part shell, but its final questions at lines 53-57 ask a shop about a retired full-cover blank, 2 mm rear bend-end web, CD stud welding and anodize masking. `design/fabrication/formed-parts-review.md` and `evidence/fabrication/cover-unfold-audit.json` are R11/R31 cover records even though they reside in R43. R39 consolidated fasteners to CLS/FH4. This is confirmed by the current `catalog.json`: four `enclosure_shell_nut_*` entries are CLS-M4-2 and `enclosure_door_hook_studs` are FH4-M4-12, but the quote still specifies CLS-M4-1 and FHA-M4-12; its EMI stand row also mentions CLA-M4 despite the catalog's CLS-M4-2. This is not a proven geometric defect of R43; it is a current manufacturing-packet defect. **Angle:** one generated, revision-checked process/BOM callout per custom part; remove retired questions and reconcile insert families. This avoids a supplier clarification/requote cycle and cutting/clinching to an obsolete design, with no claimed amount of money saved. **Check:** compare every quote row and attached file to `catalog.json`, the R43 STEP and current decisions; reject mismatches before any issue to a shop.

**ENC-02 — Forming route plausible, supplier feasibility open.** Current P1/P2 are cut flats, and current P5/louver nominal flats exist, but P3/P4/P6 are quoted from STEP. The old 2 mm bend-end web belongs to the retired one-piece cover; it must not be used to fail the current six-part shell. Conversely, current bent-sheet cutouts, U-wrapper flanges, door C-section and louver lap/slat assembly still need a real bend sequence, die access, relief and as-formed checks. **Angle:** ask for one compensated flat and bend simulation per bent part; examine whether a riveted louver split or simpler hood loses less time than a highly formed single piece while preserving spill and airflow functions. **Trade-off:** more blanks/joins can simplify forming but add fastening and cleaning seams. **Check:** one first article with named critical interfaces, not a generic 4T pass.

**ENC-03 — Custom CNC and polymer route carry yield risk.** The G11 former is a two-sided tool and winding jig; thin notches, close ferrite channels and the angled NTC bore need delivered-bar and cutter trials (`quote-package/QUOTE-REQUEST.md:16`). The carrier adds many jets and an oblique lead bore (`:17`). The boot has a 0.4 mm web; the production PPS former is not tooled (`:26`). **Angle:** first machine a short arc and one spoke with the worst tooth/bore, then wind a few turns and inspect Litz abrasion; use this to set the CNC corner radius and actual production molding envelope. **Trade-off:** larger radii or thicker teeth may alter wire packing, coil loss and cooling.

**ENC-04 — R43 barrier is a useful assembly simplification but its snap is unqualified.** One flat Formex flag with two floor snaps avoids a driver in a 6.4 mm strip, and the R43 rigid sequence passes with its base pair classified compliant (`design/r43/DECISIONS.md:21-31,79-80`; `evidence/assembly-sequence.json:step 27`). Barbs, strap fit and service removal are design estimates. **Angle:** laser/knife-cut two gauges and try installed clips with the real base coupon; record insertion/retention forces, barrier tilt and tie loads hot and cold. If retention fails, compare an integral base lance or a separately retained flag by whole assembly operations, not part count alone. **Trade-off:** a stiffer flag can improve retention but narrow both board-side gaps.

**ENC-05 — Finish and electrical contact specification needs one controlled sequence.** Chem film is specified for prototype; the black P2 top needs a conductive underside. The quote alternates PEM before versus after finish and carries obsolete masking questions. **Angle:** specify exact finish, masking, insert and inspection sequence per part, with the finished bearing/PE land on drawing. **Trade-off:** masked/bare lands cost handling and can corrode; broad conductive finish eases contact but may miss cosmetic requirements. **Check:** coated sample thickness/fit and four-wire bond after assembly and thermal cycling.

**ENC-06 — Enclosure attachment is nominally coherent; later board fit is not yet integrated.** Current R43 has a full shell/door/glass/coil/PS3 part tree and a rigid sampled build sequence. It does not show a stage21b board. **Angle:** reconcile the stage21b model and BOM into the same R43 frame before judging board support, mount access, sink or wiring; retain a matrix of part → locator → retainer → chassis. **Trade-off:** moving a support may simplify tool reach but change PE/thermal paths.

**ENC-07 — Critical datums and tolerance chains are incomplete.** The P1 flatness callout and modeled 0.52 mm seat stack are useful; P2/glass/sensor/door alignment and hot movement need a common datum map. **Angle:** choose P1 ledge, base and glass bore as candidate functional datums, then allocate measured part/joint/finish variation; retain intentional radial play where glass expands. **Check:** first-article cold/hot displacement and proudness at multiple perimeter points.

**ENC-08 — Tight R43 clearances need endpoint budgets.** The barrier's own 2.0 mm digital rule has only 0.20–0.22 mm nominal geometric excess to controller/native-21. Its spring tabs and floor slots are estimates. **Angle:** test the smallest current stage21b/controls gap at limits including board, standoff, sheet, hole and tab variation; if margin fails, relocate the barrier or change its thickness/stiffness without transferring the loose-lead hazard. **Trade-off:** more spacing may consume service and routing room.

**ENC-09 — Hot and repeated-use behavior remains model-only.** R32/R16 plans reserve sensor stroke, glass bore impact, tabs, door bow and set-down tests; no physical R43 results are in the package. **Angle:** use small coupons for barrier, seal, twist tab and sensor mechanism before an integrated powered prototype. **Trade-off:** strengthening one joint may increase glass stress or block service.

**ENC-10 — Ingress paths are identified but only narrow geometry screens pass.** The old cassette ray screen is normal-direction only and source-provenanced to an earlier louver, while the R43 rear has a different three-slat cassette (`quote-package/QUOTE-REQUEST.md:48`; `evidence/fabrication/r32-flats.json`). A6 and sensor wet tests remain planned. **Angle:** trace liquid at glass edge/bore, fascia controls, door/inlet gasket, rear outlet and underside in a cold dummy with visible dye; choose drainage, seal or service access based on observed path. **Trade-off:** tightening louvers can lower airflow and worsen thermal/noise performance.

**ENC-11 — Seal and cleaning durability are open.** Novasil glass bed, inlet HT-800 and outlet/door foam exist as allocations, but installed compression, adhesion to chem-filmed aluminum and hot/oily cleaning durability are unmeasured. The old cassette report's detailed washer/foam stack uses an older geometry and should not be carried forward. **Angle:** measure free seal thickness, door gap under torque, contact witness, wet leak and cleaning residue after repeated service; compare replaceable gasket to bond only after the path and service frequency are known. **Trade-off:** permanent bonding can reduce leak paths while increasing repair cost.

**ENC-12 — Current controls and sensor have separate evidence needs.** R43 retains front-clamped Grayhill/push-on knob and sprung sensor geometry; its own R44 note delegates a future magnetic knob. Do not score that future control as present. **Angle:** assess push load and encoder nut, sensor force/stroke/contact, and replacement sequence on the actual current assembly, then compare R44 only as a future alternative.

**ENC-13 — Stage21b interface evidence is unknown.** R43's native-21 fit gate cites stage-13; R43's 2.20–2.22 mm barrier gaps, tie path, and older board attachment cannot be transplanted to stage21b. **Exit:** pair the exact stage21b STEP/board/BOM/connector poses with R43, rerun all constrained fit and separation checks, then inspect one cold physical stack. This is an evidence gap, not a demonstrated stage21b collision.

The next sensible physical artifact is a **cold fit and process coupon set**: P3/P4/P6 formed samples, P1/P2 flatness, the R43 barrier/base snap, one former/spoke, selected fastener joints, and glass/door seal witnesses. An integrated powered/hot/wet prototype requires the separate electrical, thermal and ingress evidence in `design/r32/BENCH-PLAN.md` and the stage21b geometry reconciliation first.
