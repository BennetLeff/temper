# R43 thermal, coil, airflow and wiring review against DFMA-37–48

**Scope and identity.** This is a read-only assessment of the frozen R43 enclosure package under `input/enclosure-r43/` (manifest checked by the parent; `STEP/assembly.step` SHA-256 `0a6fcd0fe8dfa03477ffc170df59d2ab32faee8e67ea38e94059fa7066be86ce`). R43 declares `DESIGN_CANDIDATE_PENDING_INTEGRATION_REVIEW` in `CURRENT.json:1–7`. I inspected `renders/R43-interior.png`, `R43-underside.png`, and `R43-rear.png`, the R43/R41/R40/R33/R32 design notes, saved evidence JSON, the harness list and the relevant modeled part names. These images and saved checks support geometry only. No physical thermal, harness pull, blower flow, earth-continuity, EMI, supplier or service test was run for this review.

The enclosure's native-21 pose gate is explicitly an R31 **stage-13** STEP match (`evidence/native21-pose-check.json`); R41 says the native-21 J11 connector is absent from its R40 envelope (`design/r41/DECISIONS.md:25–35`). Its R43 PS3 tests use this carried enclosure geometry. Until the verified **stage21b** board, selected parts and transform are compared to R43, scores for dependent integrated interfaces are **U**. This is an identity gap, not evidence that stage21b has a defect. Older R32 airflow and coil estimates are inherited analyses and do not become current-board or physical measurements by copying them into R43.

**Scoring basis:** DFMA rubric's 0/1/2/3/U/NA anchors, for the R43 digital engineering-prototype candidate integrated with the requested stage21b board. No average or readiness grade. A score of 2 credits a coherent R43 allocation while retaining the open verification stated below; it does not verify stage21b fit. The design can support focused cold fit samples, with powered or production suitability unverified.

## Findings and improvement angles

1. **TWI-01 — stage21b integration identity, review gap.** R43's saved native-21 pose is a stage-13 gate and R41's J11 was added as a mated envelope. Build a revision-bound comparison of stage21b PCB STEP, connectors, device bodies, mount/PE locations and R43 world transform before carrying any old geometry check into this board. This could reveal opportunities to reduce brackets or leads, but those are hypotheses until the true fit is known.
2. **TWI-02 — J13 retention depends on one unqualified physical detail, review gap with demonstrated failure if omitted.** R43 itself shows the barrier without the fourth tie cannot contain the loose DC end; the tie/flag's stiffness, snap barb, material grade/rating and re-tie after service are estimates (`design/r43/DECISIONS.md:10–29,35–54,80–96`). A captive cable guide integrated into an already retained insulating feature might eliminate a loose tie and a separate sheet part, while retaining terminal reach and separation. Compare installation time, heat aging, access and actual dielectric performance; do not remove the protective function on part-count grounds.
3. **TWI-03 — long PS3 mains span, review gap.** R43 defers physical sag/abrasion; its 4.99 mm modeled nearest clearance is to HOT-class geometry and 11.10 mm remaining to SELV assumes only that amount of droop (`design/r43/DECISIONS.md:55–72`). A reroute or formed support could gain abrasion robustness but add part/operation count or disturb airflow. Select only after testing full-length leads, vibration/heat and actual stage21b clearance.
4. **TWI-04 — thermal interfaces and assembled airflow, review gap.** The source-to-sink stack and push/bleed paths are plausible models, but grease/pad application, force variation, flow loss, wall re-ingestion and hot service states lack current physical correlation. A fewer-piece sink/cradle or reworked duct should be evaluated by total installed cost and the same temperature/flow tests, not just CAD fit.
5. **TWI-05 — terminations and off-board assembly, review gap.** The harness list has cut lengths and some terminal families but expressly is not released; coil lugs are envelopes. Preterminated standardized subassemblies might cut operations and error risk, yet may raise purchase price or consume more routing volume. Compare supplied drawing, crimp inspection, strain and thermal data against hand termination.
6. **TWI-06 — PE joints and finish, review gap.** Candidate bonds and earth paths are modeled; inspectability and post-service continuity need physical proof. If joint families are consolidated, preserve bare/contact-controlled interfaces and test every path. Supplier material/finish rules and the selected appliance obligations set acceptance, not this review.
7. **TWI-07 — coil variation, review gap.** Ferrite, Litz, potting and sensor are defined as a prototype route. The R32 low-transfer sensitivity and the later R37/R40 coil definition are not a measured common envelope; correlate assembled R_ac, installed L, glass and winding temperatures before trading coil geometry or ferrite count against manufacturing cost.
8. **TWI-08 — harness/EMI interaction, review gap.** Removing ties, shortening leads or moving the PS3/EMI module might improve assembly but alter common-mode loops, shielding and return geometry. Route variants need layout/PE review and installed precompliance EMI evidence.

No physical part, PCB, enclosure source, supplier record or benchmark was changed. All numerical clearances and thermal predictions above are **inherited model outputs for the frozen R43 package**, bounded by its older input identities; they are not new stage21b results.
