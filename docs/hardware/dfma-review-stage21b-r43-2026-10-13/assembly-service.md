# R43 assembly, inspection and service review for the stage21b DFMA study

**Scope and identity.** I assessed the frozen R43 mechanical package under `input/enclosure-r43/`, specifically DFMA-49–54, 61–66 and 69–70. `STEP/assembly.step` hashes to `0a6fcd0fe8dfa03477ffc170df59d2ab32faee8e67ea38e94059fa7066be86ce`; the saved 39-step `evidence/assembly-sequence.json` and `evidence/cover-lift-checks.json` carry that hash. `CURRENT.json` calls R43 a design candidate pending integration review. The R43 electrical import predates the user's selected **stage21b** board; the integrated stage21b enclosure has therefore **not** been demonstrated by this package. This is a review of a candidate assembly at the **engineering-prototype design** stage, not a physical build or released production process. Source mentions of stage 13, R32 and R43 are retained with their actual scope.

**Evidence limits that change interpretation.** The 39-step sequence reports `PASS_SCOPED_SEQUENCE_SCREEN` with no unassigned parts and no blocked sampled rigid insertion path, but its own scope expressly omits flexible harness insertion, snap deformation, screw-and-nut placement, and tool reach (`src/check_assembly_sequence.py:1-11,145-169,185-190`; `evidence/assembly-sequence.json:1-3,348-359`). The render `renders/R43-interior.png` shows a dense assembly with the coil over the PCB and a hand-routed harness; `R43-rear.png` and `R43-underside.png` show the rear opening and underside closures. These are visual context, not reach or operation trials. The `rear-inlet-checks.json` door result carries assembly hash `d46611ec…0d9`, and `final-validation.json` carries `ebbcebb9…2081`; neither matches the saved R43 STEP. Door withdrawal and overall-final claims thus remain **inherited/unverified on R43** even though the saved source checker describes a useful nominal path (`src/check_rear_inlet.py:1-4,46-76`). The R43-matching cover lift is also a sampled rigid screen with flexible wiring excluded (`src/check_cover_lift.py:1-7,43-80`). I did not rerun a CAD kernel or perform physical trials.

Scores follow the project's 0/1/2/3/U/NA anchors. `U` means the evidence needed to tell whether the operation actually works is absent. No score 3 is claimed from a CAD path screen.

## Improvement angles to compare later (no design changes made)

1. **Build sequence and tool access:** use a coil holding fixture or temporary stop that exposes J2/J5 torque and allows the sink PE joint after coil drop; compare extra fixture/handling work with a route that moves the joint or subassembly boundary. The smallest discriminator is an actual tool/hand reach trial through operations 29–35, including the stage21b board's component heights. (`ASY-01`, `ASY-03`.)
2. **Mistake proofing at protection interfaces:** standardize visual/physical differentiation for H1 versus H2–H7, make tie omission at J13 visible to final inspection, and require PE continuity after the door and cover close. This adds inspection or small locating features but can reduce latent wrong-build risk. Try deliberately wrong assemblies before selecting a mechanism. (`ASY-05`, `ASY-08`, `ASY-14`.)
3. **Single-use service work:** measure how often J13 tie/barrier, glass/sensor sealing, door gasket and cover PE lug are disturbed for plausible repairs. Compare the present low-part-count snap/tie arrangement with a reusable retainer only if service frequency and electrical clearances justify it. (`ASY-04`, `ASY-13`, `ASY-14`.)
4. **Controlled bonding:** preform or fixture the ferrite/coil bond and NTC fill so dose and position are inspectable; compare cure time, scrap and dielectric results with the hand method. Keep this a process experiment until real compound and test data exist. (`ASY-06`, `ASY-07`, `ASY-10`.)
5. **One revision-bound assembly and test traveler:** attach stage21b board identity, R43 (or a later integrated enclosure) geometry, selected BOM, torque/compound settings, first-article measurements and closure tests. The immediate check is whether each saved result hashes to its claimed current input; currently two do not. (`ASY-07`, `ASY-09`, `ASY-12`.)

The digitally coherent 39-step sequence is a useful starting point. The key unknowns for the intended prototype are tool reach, physical snap and harness behavior, PE restoration, rear-opening service, and the stage21b/R43 fit. There is no evidence here for an integrated powered build or a repeatable production line.
