## Improvement angles to test against the corrected benchmark

1. **Control the 1.6 mm terminal interface at minimum material thickness.** First seek a measured finished-board minimum/accepted stack. If the chosen route cannot guarantee it, compare a thicker approved stack or a different termination at the same electrical/current/thermal and enclosure requirements. Added thickness can change plated holes, impedance, insulation distances, stud fit and cost; it is not a free tweak.
2. **Treat via covering as a selectable process cost with function constraints.** The native board has 737 small vias and intentional in-pad fields. Compare blanket POFV with a supplier-supported selective approach only if cost, flat solder lands, shunt current/thermal paths and qualification improve. Keep R5 and U26 process evidence; an unsupported tent-only variant is not an equivalent design.
3. **Spend tiny local area where it improves assembly yield.** C31/C24 courtyard spacing is narrow. Test a small placement variant with a realistic nozzle/AOI/rework envelope and preserve U6 comparator input/return behavior. Accept it only if the electrical and insulation checks still pass and the physical route is less fragile.
4. **Compare L-outline panel routes at the installed-system level.** Nesting, rails, rout tabs and fixture support may change material utilization, handling, depanel damage and the enclosure mating edge. Prototype array and loaded-board separation before claiming an area saving.
5. **Target the manual outliers rather than all THT indiscriminately.** The four removed REDCUBE rail links already reduce operations; measure where F1 clips, radial capacitors, THR studs, lead trim, BR1 and connector work now consume time. A replacement must preserve current, isolation, heat, service and inspectability. Estimate real assembly labor/fixture/rework alongside part prices.
6. **Keep the assembly packet self-checking.** The final raw and corrected CPL variants now agree on all coordinates, after the producer fixed an earlier C30/C31 mismatch. Enforce board-hash, 353-designator/MPN/coordinate parity and controlled rotation-delta gates on every upload candidate, including C30/C31 witnesses. This reduces wrong-build risk; any labor saving is contingent on measured order preparation and incoming inspection time.
