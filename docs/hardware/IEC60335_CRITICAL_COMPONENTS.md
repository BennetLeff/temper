## 1. What this is, and why

IEC 60335-1 (household appliance safety) does not accept "this part works" as sufficient for components whose failure can cause fire, electric shock, or injury. Those components — fuses, X/Y capacitors, mains-switching relays, isolation transformers/SMPS modules, reinforced-insulation isolators, thermal cutoffs, and the materials the board and enclosure are made of — must be **separately approved**: tested by a recognized body (UL, VDE, CSA, CQC, TÜV, etc.) to the relevant component standard, at the specific voltage/current/temperature rating the part is used at in *this* design.

This document is the first pass at that list for Temper. It has not been done before — every other BOM axis assessed so far (functional values, availability, ripple) asked "does it work / can we buy it," not "can we legally sell it." This axis asks the second question, and the answer is not yet "yes."

**Method:** for every category IEC 60335-1 cares about, the design was searched for the real ref designator and MPN doing that job (not a generic checklist). Each MPN's approval was then checked against a datasheet or agency database fetched directly — not inferred from the part's family, and not invented. Where no approval could be confirmed, the cell says **UNVERIFIED**. An UNVERIFIED cell is a correct, honest answer, not a failure of this document.

## 2. The isolation-barrier finding — star join and ZCD path FIXED 2026-07-26; a narrower finding remains open (read this before the table)

> **STATUS, re-audited 2026-07-29.** The finding as originally written (§2.1 below, preserved verbatim per this repo's re-date-rather-than-delete convention) described two hazards: a `power_return ~ gnd` star join that shorted the AuxSupply's 4.2kVAC isolation barrier, and an uninsulated resistive ZCD path from raw AC Line into the same SELV ground. **Both are fixed** — see §2.2 for the commits and current source lines, and §2.3 for how to verify it yourself. **What is NOT fixed, and is the live finding as of this audit:** two IEC 60335-1 protective-impedance resistor dividers (OVP-01's comparator-sense and ADC-sense dividers) still tie the HV half-bus to `gnd` at high impedance, by design, and the new `gnd ~ pe` bond is ordinary PCB copper doing an EMI/noise-reference job, not a certified, continuity-tested protective-earth conductor. Neither of those is the 4.2kVAC-barrier-defeating hazard §2.1 describes — see §2.4 for why they're real but materially smaller, and why they don't reopen this document's original "single highest-consequence finding" ranking.

### 2.1 Original finding, as recorded 2026-07-26 (HISTORICAL — read §2.2-§2.4 for current status before relying on anything below)

The task brief for this document specifically warned: verify where the isolation barrier actually is before classifying anything as SELV, because getting this wrong is the exact error class that already bit this project once (the OCP-02 common-mode miscalculation). It happened again, here, in a different circuit.

**The physical isolation component exists and is well-approved.** `AuxSupply` (`modules.ato:1075-1139`) uses a Mean Well IRM-10-15 encapsulated AC/DC module (`PS1`) to take ~170VDC off the half-bus and produce an isolated 15V rail. That module is genuinely certified for this role: Class II, IEC 62368-1, IEC/EN 61558-1/-2-16, UL 62368-1, TÜV EN 62368-1 and EN 61558-1/-2-16, EAC TP TC 004, BSMI CNS15598-1, with a 4.2kVAC input-output withstand rating (confirmed directly from Mean Well's own `IRM-10-SPEC.PDF`). Every downstream logic rail (`vcc_15v` → `vcc_3v3` → MCU, safety interlock, RTD ADC) is powered through this module's secondary.

**But the module's isolation is defeated by how its output is grounded.** `main.ato:273`: `aux_supply.power_out.gnd ~ gnd`. `main.ato:299`: `power_return ~ gnd  # Single-star-point ground join near doubler caps`. And `power_return` is not earth — it is the voltage-doubler midpoint (`main.ato:263`: `power_return ~ power_in.dc_bus.gnd_ref`), which is tied directly to AC Neutral through the common-mode choke (`modules.ato:620-621`: `ac_n ~ cmc.W2_1; cmc.W2_2 ~ dc_bus.gnd_ref`). So the "SELV" secondary of the isolated supply is deliberately strapped back to a node that tracks AC Neutral. The transformer isolates the *power path*; it does not isolate the *reference* the entire logic domain is measured against.

**There is also a second, independent conductive path from AC Line to the same node**, with no isolation device in it at all. `PowerInput`'s zero-crossing divider (`modules.ato:683-689`) runs `ac_l ~ r_zcd_top1.p1 → r_zcd_top2 → zcd → r_zcd_bot.p2 ~ dc_bus.gnd_ref`, and `zcd` is wired straight to `mcu.zcd_in` (`main.ato:456`). That is a resistive (not capacitive) 450kΩ path from raw AC_L to the same star-ground node the MCU, safety logic, and RTD ADC all reference — through ordinary Yageo 1206 resistors, not a certified safety-rated impedance-protection component. The module's own source comment already flags this as a TODO ("ZCD signal crosses isolation barrier"), but it has not been fixed.

**Net effect:** the "SELV control domain" (MCU, safety interlock, RTD sensing/ADC, and any USB or I2C connector eventually populated on the `usb_dn`/`usb_dp`/`i2c_sda_ui`/`i2c_scl_ui` nets, which today are bare, unconnectored signals per `main.ato:106-109,459-465` — no physical USB or I2C connector is currently instantiated in `elec/src`) is referenced to AC Neutral in normal operation, and would be referenced to AC **Line** in an L/N-reversed fault (non-polarized plug, miswired receptacle, or a floating/open neutral upstream — conditions IEC 60335-1 requires the appliance to remain safe under). This is most consequential for the RTD probe: `RTDSensing`'s own docstring (`modules.ato:1259-1265`) asserts "the user-touchable RTD food probe is therefore separated from AC mains potential" — that claim is not fully supported by the actual ground topology traced above. No component certificate fixes this; it is a wiring/topology decision, and it was, at the time this was written, the single highest-consequence finding in this document.

**What was and wasn't mains-referenced, concretely, as of 2026-07-26 (superseded by §2.2-§2.4):**
- Mains-referenced (by design, via the star point and the CMC/AC_N tie): `dc_bus_plus`, `dc_bus_minus`, `power_return`, and — because of the star join — `gnd`, `vcc_15v`, `vcc_3v3`, and every net hung off them (MCU, safety interlock logic, RTD ADC, CT bias, USB/I2C nets).
- Additionally Line-referenced via the ZCD divider, independent of the star join: the `zcd` net and, through it, one MCU ADC pin.
- Isolated only in the sense of "floats on the half-bus, not on Neutral": `power_15v_ls` (low-side gate-driver supply, explicitly referenced to `dc_bus.hv_minus`, not `gnd` — `modules.ato:380-381`), and the UCC21550's secondary-side (high-side) gate-drive output, which floats on `switch_node`.
- True chassis/earth: `pe`, bonded to `power_return` only through the 2.2nF Y1 capacitor (`modules.ato:645-646`) — by design a high-impedance EMI path, explicitly *not* a DC safety bond (per the module's own comment).

### 2.2 The fix (2026-07-26): what changed, and how to verify it

Commits `6976ef443` ("fix(hardware): float SELV control domain, remove star-point ground join") and `1390e807` (re-verification after a same-day rebase), full record in `docs/hardware/SELV_ISOLATION_REDESIGN.md`:

- **The star join is gone.** `elec/src/main.ato:714` now reads: `# REMOVED: the star join (`power_return ~ gnd`) that used to sit here.` `gnd` is instead bonded directly to protective earth: `main.ato:754`: `` gnd ~ pe  # SELV ground reference: bonded to protective earth, NOT to power_return ``. `power_return` and `gnd` are verified-distinct compiled nets (`SELV_ISOLATION_REDESIGN.md` §1's falsifier: 80 pins on `gnd`, 17 on `PWR_RTN`, re-confirmed on this branch — see §2.3 below).
- **The ZCD path is now opto-isolated.** `elec/src/modules.ato:1035-1053` instantiates `zcd_opto = new H11L1` (onsemi `H11L1TVM`): the LED (pins A/K) stays on the HV side referenced to `dc_bus.gnd_ref`/`power_return`, and the Schmitt-trigger output (`VO`/`GND`/`VCC`) is powered from and referenced to `vcc_3v3`/`gnd` — the SELV side. This resolves the "ZCD signal crosses isolation barrier" TODO §2.1 quoted. See §3's new table row for this part's own agency approvals.
- **`RTDSensing`'s docstring was corrected in the same change**, not left asserting the old unqualified claim. `elec/src/modules.ato:1736-1765` now states the RTD probe's SELV reference is PE (not AC Neutral), qualifies that this only holds if every other HV/SELV crossing is isolated, and explicitly names the OVP-01 divider crossing (§2.4) as the reason the domain is not yet at *zero* galvanic connection to HV — "**Do not restate the old unqualified 'separated from AC mains potential' claim until the OVP crossing is also resolved**," which this document's own conclusion (§2.4) now follows.

### 2.4 What remains open, as of 2026-07-29

Two items are real and unresolved. Neither is the 4.2kVAC-isolation-defeating hazard §2.1 described; both are declared, analyzed constructions, not undetected shorts.

1. **The OVP-01 protective-impedance divider bridges still tie the HV half-bus to `gnd` at high impedance, by design.** `OVPComparator` (`elec/src/modules.ato:2085-2180`) instantiates two independent resistor dividers from `+170V_BUS`/`dc_bus_plus` into `power.gnd` (which chains to `gnd`): the comparator-sense divider (`r_div_top1-3`, 3×430kΩ, `modules.ato:2204-2223`) and the ADC-sense divider (`r_adc_top1-3`, 3×169kΩ, `modules.ato:2361-2385`). Both are declared in `elec/domain_manifest.yaml:673-691` as `protective_impedance_chains` — IEC 60335-1's recognized alternative to insulation for a mains-derived-to-PE-bonded-earth bridge, provided (a) no single component failure removes the current-limiting function and (b) touch current stays under the applicable limit in both normal and single-fault operation. The manifest's own arithmetic (re-derived, not merely copied, in this audit): normal current 130.1µA/328.8µA, worst two-shorted-resistor case 380.4µA/949.7µA, against a 1.35mA touch-current limit (IEC 60335-2-6, 0.75mA/kW capped at 5mA, at 1.8kW) — 3.5×-10× margin even under a double fault the standard does not require evaluating. `scripts/check_domain_partition.py`'s `check_chain_integrity` (§2.3) verifies the *construction* — every declared resistor is present, wired in series, and untapped — every time it runs; it is not a one-time hand-check.
2. **The `gnd ~ pe` bond is ordinary PCB copper doing an EMI/reference job, not a certified, continuity-tested protective-earth conductor.** `docs/hardware/SELV_ISOLATION_REDESIGN.md` §3 documents its actual purpose: giving the floated SELV domain a stable low-impedance reference (an EMI/stability decision), not a Class I protective-earthing scheme for this specific boundary. `docs/evidence/2026-07-30-insulation-tier-audit.md` §2.2 (a later, independent audit of a different question — insulation *tier*, not this document's component-approval axis — reaching the same underlying fact) confirms this product's own test plan (`docs/specs/REQUIREMENTS.md` §4, "Ground Continuity: Measure resistance from PE to all exposed metal (<0.1Ω)") applies continuity testing to **exposed metal**, never to the internal `gnd` net `LV_CONTROL` copper sits on — no document in this repo claims or verifies that `gnd`'s trace/via construction is sized or rated to carry and clear a full DC-bus-to-`gnd` prospective fault current the way an appliance's protective-earth conductor to its chassis is required to.

**Why these two don't reopen the "single highest-consequence finding" ranking §2.1 gave the original star join.** The star join shorted a *certified 4.2kVAC transformer barrier* outright, with no current-limiting element at all — any single insulation fault anywhere on the HV side put the full fault current straight onto the RTD probe. The two items above are engineered, redundant, current-limited constructions that have been arithmetically checked against the applicable touch-current standard, not an unbounded short. They remain real open items — item 2 in particular means this design's protective strategy for `LV_CONTROL` rests on certified galvanic isolators (the Mean Well module, the H11L1 opto, the UCC21550 gate driver), not on Class I PE earthing, and the document should not imply otherwise — but they are a narrower, lower-severity finding than what this section originally reported, and the gap list in §4 is re-ranked accordingly.

## 4. Gap list, ranked by consequence

**Re-ranked 2026-07-29.** Two items that led this list in the 2026-07-26 original — the isolation-barrier star join/ZCD path and the fabricated X2/Y1 MPNs — are now fixed or narrowed (see below and §2). 1. **Discharge relay contacts (G5LE-1 DC12) are being operated above their agency-approved voltage rating** (170VDC vs. 125VDC/30VDC-UL max). The relay itself is well-certified; this specific application is not covered by that certification. Needs either a relay rated for the actual voltage or a documented engineering-judgment deviation accepted by the certification body — an RC snubber alone does not restore rating coverage. **Unchanged as of 2026-07-29** — a same-day replacement attempt (Finder 40.52.7.012.0000) was tried and reverted 2026-07-28 (`docs/evidence/2026-07-28-relay-board-resync-decision.md`; the candidate part's own coil-to-contact pitch failed both the isolation and the DC-break requirement, so the revert restored G5LE-1 rather than trade one non-compliance for another) — `elec/src/modules.ato:1068-1123` documents the full trail and states plainly that a real fix needs a relay with a coil-to-nearest-contact pitch roughly double what's been evaluated so far, not yet found.
2. **No AC mains inlet connector, cordset, or strain relief exists in the electrical source.** Every plug-in appliance needs an approved answer here; the design currently has none, and the one document that claims otherwise is stale and contradicted by source. **Unchanged as of 2026-07-29** — re-confirmed via `grep -rn "4798\|Schurter" elec/src/*.ato`, still no hits for a connector part.

### Is a gate feasible for this document's central claim? Yes — one already exists.

`scripts/check_domain_partition.py` already derives, from the compiled netlist and `elec/domain_manifest.yaml`, exactly the claim this document's §2 makes: that the declared HV and SELV domains are galvanically disjoint except through declared isolators and protective-impedance chains. It is wired into CI (`.github/workflows/python-tests.yml:706` runs its test suite, line `718` runs the gate itself) and fails closed (exit 5) on a missing, empty, or stale netlist or manifest — never silently exit 0 on incomplete data.

**Not gateable, and why:** the second item in §2.4 (the `gnd ~ pe` bond is not a *continuity-tested* protective-earth conductor) is a manufacturing/test-floor fact — whether the as-built PE path from the AC inlet to the `gnd` bond point measures under the required resistance on a physical, assembled board — not a netlist-derivable one. A decorative gate that merely re-asserted "this is a known limitation" was not built.
