## Prioritized findings and decisions

1. **P1, high confidence — coil/pan and coil thermal closure (MAG-02/22/24, with MAG-23).** The 28 A, 85% pooled full-power yield, bank/coil heat and 35–60 kHz operating points depend on assumed pan coupling and an unwound/uncalibrated coil. This is already acknowledged in TANK and SPEC; it is not a newly discovered board error. A received-coil sweep on representative no-pan, centered/offset, material/size and hot cases should establish complex Z, R_ac and temperature before increasing the commissioned 22 A ceiling. If R_ac or loaded resonance misses SPEC, the practical trade is coil winding/cooling or lower current; changing bank/turns can improve yield while worsening capacitor ripple and heat. Model bank 1.12× its 85 °C curve at 28 A makes bank hot-spot capture part of the same gate.
2. **P1, high confidence — the off-board filter is a modeled dependency, not a qualified part (MAG-06/13–17/19/21).** STAGE19B's October 13 EMI model now passes its own 6 dB sensitivity rule with 31 pF modeled floating-pan capacitance (plus a +20 pF copper bound), but its essential ≥100 µH DM module inductor lacks a selected, hot-biased impedance and thermal record. The older 18 µH-only case misses the modeled low band by 5.1 dB; the board filter alone misses severely. Select and characterize the module, measure the assembled coil→PE capacitance split, source/load impedances and LISN CM/DM spectra from 9 kHz to 30 MHz. The pan-earthed scenario's 1.7–2.6 dB **modeled** margin is a robustness watch item, not a compliance violation. A stronger CM choke or coil-side shield is a reserved remedy, trading volume, heating, resonance and leakage for margin. The ~16 kHz DM/X-cap/damper-open transient remains an already-known startup check (MAG-14).
3. **P1, medium-high confidence — CT fault accuracy and insulation (MAG-11/25–27).** Direct netlist inspection confirms local termination, so routine burden-open cable risk is reduced; a solder/open burden fault still creates the modeled hundreds-of-volts secondary excursion and saturation. The cited model is older than the 28 A settings and is **not** a present measured failure. Confirm the current fault envelope and clamp energy, then inject safe primary fault-range waveforms and measure amplitude, phase, reset/droop and both trip polarities. Manufacturer confirmation of T1's working-voltage/high-frequency reinforced-insulation basis remains necessary despite its 5 kV one-minute test and stage18 clearance reroute. Burden/clamp changes trade protection headroom against distortion and calibration.
4. **P2, medium confidence — thermal and test access (MAG-09/29).** The latest converged body model removes the old mesh artifact and estimates L1 near 69 °C, but its package path/limit is assumed and no enclosed equilibrium IR exists. The EMI procedure is detailed while PE/probe access is deferred. Measure L1 winding and local air, coil/ferrite, filter module, and bank at final airflow/ambient; stage any later footprint/test-point changes with a fresh EMI mode scan because an added PE/Y path can move resonance.
