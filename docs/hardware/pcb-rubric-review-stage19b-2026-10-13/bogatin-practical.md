## Independent checks and limits

1. **Identity/source:** `identity-check.txt` PASS (61 pins); independently read `B/provenance/final-manifest.json` full board, copper, schematic/netlist and Gerber SHA-256. `shasum -a 256 P/validation-results/08-manufacturing-package/native-21/fab/native-21-gerbers.zip` reproduced `c9eaf0d96091662cd25d0ef5a2d78e972f6123e1a1b4cb1c6d85804922e80ea3`. The report uses this identity only, never root stage15b.
2. **KiCad-native placement versus source BOM:** `kicad-cli pcb export pos --format csv --units mm --side front` on the exact board produced `R/bogatin-positions.csv`. C153–C156 are at board y 128.78, 128.68, 157.38, 175.00 mm (negative Y in KiCad export); R5 is Bourns CSS2H; J2/J5 center pitch is 15.0 mm. `B/provenance/resolved-components.json` has 357 unique designators and all four C153–C156 resolve to YAGEO `CC0603KRX7R9BB104`. The manufacturing CSV's positive Y is a real frame discrepancy requiring assembler preview, not proof of wrong assembly.
3. **Copper/stackup geometry:** parsed `B/provenance/copper.json` directly: 53 zones, 1532 track objects, 753 vias. Around C153–C156, the exported via objects include both `v3v3` and `selv_gnd`; approximate center-to-nearest-via distances are 0.81–1.76 mm (the producer's pad-to-via metric is more precise, 0.85–1.51 mm). The raw board stackup at lines 35–75 is 4 layers, 0.07/0.061/0.061/0.07 mm copper, 0.4355/0.5/0.4355 mm dielectric. Thus neither proximity nor an assumed generic 1 oz stackup was inferred from prose alone.
4. **Layer figures:** generated `R/bogatin-copper.pdf` from the exact board with `kicad-cli pcb export pdf -l F.Cu,In1.Cu,In2.Cu,B.Cu --mode-multipage`, then inspected all four raster pages (`R/bogatin-copper-1.png` through `-4.png`). They visibly show the four-layer high-current/SELV layout and separated plane regions. A full-resolution numerical clearance check and current-path extraction remain the pinned gates' job; a figure cannot prove an electrical margin.
5. **Evidence inheritance:** stage19b `DECISIONS.md:786–791` states only the four new transfer caps/plane vias and a small unrelated `ocp_thresh` In2 reroute changed from 19; Kelvin and B3 were rerun, G6 was not. Thermal convergence at `:808–827` replaces the older peak-cell model and explicitly retains bench dependencies. The stage19b return-path and PI JSON files were read as model results, not represented as our independent measurements.

**Verification limit.** `kicad-cli` plots and position export interrogated the pinned PCB without altering it. I did not rerun expensive electromagnetic, thermal, or router solvers, nor claim bench or vendor results. This review cannot turn the digital candidate into a fabrication or mains release.
