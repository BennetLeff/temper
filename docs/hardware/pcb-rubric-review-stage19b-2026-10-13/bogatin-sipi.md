## Prioritized findings and actions

1. **P1 — Negative gate-rail and high-energy loop performance still need direct validation (known, high confidence in evidence gap; model-dependent severity).** The `−1.6 V` minimum is a functional off-gate condition, not simply a regulator nominal setpoint. The real local U1/U2 100 nF loop is ~2.2 mm, but the combined mounted rail impedance and droop at the switching edge have not been shown. C5/C6 bulk-path inductance is estimated ~20 nH ±50% while S4 model results can fail around 28–32 nH. Extract the exact 19b gate/bulk ports, then use synchronized S4/S5 double pulses with pin-local negative-rail, VGS and VDS probes. A larger bulk capacitor alone does not address access inductance and may move resonances; a local bus film/C0G part would need stress, heating, insulation and antiresonance re-analysis.
2. **P2 — `bias_bad` reference path is deliberately open (known, high confidence geometry; medium confidence functional impact).** Stage19b resolved `pwm_hb` transitions; it did not resolve `bias_bad`'s seven changes or 22.25 mm no-plane segment at x≈44–66,y=127.2. The producer argues the fault line only adds a fail-safe disable. That is not enough to establish benign behavior if a brief asserted DIS truncates a pulse or creates an unexpected next edge. Capture U40 output and both driver DIS inputs through controlled fault transitions and power switching. If susceptibility appears, first compare local reference transfer/stitching or a routed return with the existing J4 fan; extra caps can raise V3V3–SELV_GND AC coupling and shift rail impedance.
3. **P2 — Geometry gates do not measure PDN peaks (known, high confidence).** C153–C156 correctly connect the two planes and C75/C82/C99 reach the pins, but pad distance is not mounting impedance. The 10/1/0.1 µF banks plus damped 220 µF reservoir and added V3V3/SELV_GND 100 nF parts could form peaks; no impedance sweep or load-step evidence exists. Keep these as supported geometry improvements and obtain rail-by-rail sweep or validated ESR/ESL/stackup model and edge-time voltage response before asserting rail margin. Damping should follow the peak location and load spectrum, not be added generically.
4. **P2 — Kelvin/reference interaction needs a switching capture (known, medium confidence risk).** R5's Kelvin fingers are independent of the current pads, and stage19 moved `bus_fault_hot` from the n_ls crossing to the Kelvin reference; this is a real improvement, corroborated on frozen copper. The shared high-frequency reference and coil magnetic pickup can still perturb the comparator threshold. Measure U8/U9 and OCP comparator pin-to-pin disturbances with the actual coil/pan, 22/28 A and switching faults; distinguish R5 DC resistance from fast common impedance.
5. **P3 — PWM transfer repair is present (resolved versus stage19, high confidence geometry).** The four new caps are on the correct nets with pad-to-plane vias 0.85–1.51 mm; the historical exact-build route gate reports all seven `pwm_hb` changes 1.12–2.72 mm from a transfer capacitor. Do not carry stage19's four missing J4-side capacitors forward as a stage19b defect. The remaining evidence is a receiver waveform, not another placement correction.
