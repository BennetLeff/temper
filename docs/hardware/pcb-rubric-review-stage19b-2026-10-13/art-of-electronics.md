## Prioritized findings

1. **High; known/open; high confidence — DC fault interruption is still unqualified.** `P/validation-results/01-switching-parasitics/round17/delegation/out-D31/README.md:8-22` calculates a comparator-output-to-DIS allocation of 787.5/814.9 ns, but Q1/Q4 turn-off lacks a tight bound and the worst data-sheet charge bound can take the chain to about 1.5 µs. It expressly does not credit this chain for hard shoot-through, which may reach the Q2/Q3/Q5/Q6 495 A pulse headline in about 55 ns. The separate FC1 DC clearing I²t/arc voltage, HS400 hot or repeated pulse and LC1D18BD DC make/break remain vendor questions; `DEC:78`, `P/prototype-closure/round5/protection/README.md:123-175`. Gate disable also cannot extinguish a shorted FET or discharge the tank/bus. **Fix/check:** instrument threshold, PERMIT, DIS, VGS, tank/bus current and VDS through normal and fault turn-off; resolve fuse/holder and contactor DC data at installed L/R and temperature; test single-fault paths and show current extinction and residual energy. The current single loaded precharge per cool-down/contacts-last rule is a procedural containment, not proof of silicon or cable survival.
2. **High; known/open; high confidence — electrothermal conclusions must use the converged STAGE19B body model.** `BODY:4-17,34-93` supersedes older 136/149 °C single-cell claims. At 28 A ceiling, 50 °C inlet, h25, it estimates R5 element 140.1 °C and pad max 129.5 °C, with 18.8 K to the project 20% derating-margin rule. U41/U42 junction estimate 105.1 °C and U26 88.9 °C. The five B32654 bank capacitors, C38–C41 HF film, C5/C6, L1 and IRM-20 PS1/PS2 have assumed thermal paths or ambient-versus-case limits and require bench IR; C38–C41 margin is only about 5 K in the model. Fan-stall h10 at 28 A is **unsupported**; the 14 A derate is an essential control. **Fix/check:** first-article thermocouples/IR at the named BODY measurement points, source-specific capacitor ripple/hot-spot data and fan-stall timing. Do not transfer stage17b G6's 50.8% margin to the converged model without reconciling its thermal definition.
3. **High; known/open; high confidence — sensing and external control validity remain system-dependent.** The actual Kelvin shunt is Bourns CSS2H, not WSK (`ATO:997`; `BOM:12`; board R5). The stage19b Kelvin calculation gives approximately 158 mΩ of shared return and −2.17 A trip shift; shunt band 67.72–116.44 A (`P/validation-results/08-manufacturing-package/native-21/stage19-pi-si/README.md:80-85`; `P/validation-results/02-protection-timing/kelvin/budget-native21s19b.md`). The CT path and firmware are needed for the narrower 64.55–76.09 A limit (`DEC:551-552`); controller-interface review still calls PERMIT and BUS_FAULT integration indeterminate (`P/validation-results/06-controller-interface/README.md:50-71,147-160,260-273`). **Fix/check:** freeze actual controller/harness/interlock pin contract, power-loss defaults and firmware threshold/source-freshness behavior; inject CT open, reference drift, broken Kelvin lead and stuck-low fault output, then measure detector and current-extinction timing. High-side bias is independent RECOM supply, so bootstrap hold-up is inapplicable.
4. **Medium/high; known/open; high confidence — exact build parts are not automatically qualified substitutes.** The frozen BOM has 9 µF `B32676G1905K000` C5/C6, two `30KPA150CA` D3/D17, Bourns CSS2H R5 and four `IPW65R018CFD7` FETs (`BOM:10-12,35,60`). Earlier sourcing `P/validation-results/08-manufacturing-package/native-21/sourcing/ALTERNATES-2026-10-09.md:548-553` discusses *previous* B32656/MRT130KP selections; its “no same-footprint alternate” statements do not qualify the later 9 µF and series 30KPA choices. `FAB:12-15,50-59` still lists consigned parts and required JLC preview for R5's custom Kelvin land. **Fix/check:** maintain a STAGE19B exact-MPN procurement/approved-alternate register with changed bus/clamp calculations, selected manufacturer curves, sample fit and solder inspection; a supplier's fabrication substitution requires its own electrical and mechanical evidence.
5. **Medium; known/open; medium confidence — component temperature and insulation boundaries depend on assembly conditions.** `FAB:17-33` leaves CTI confirmation, F1 open-clip certification, and the RES_A inner-layer 9.694 mm path's solid-insulation interpretation to the fabricator/lab. `DEC:745-758` records 297 V rms/656 V peak RES_A and 12.6 mm outer-layer rule; `P/native-21/BRINGUP-PROBES.md:54-69` requires all-nylon H2–H7 fixing chains near HOT. **Fix/check:** obtain the written laminate/clip/insulation decisions and inspect first-article hardware, leads and enclosure routes. A 0-error KiCad DRC is a check of configured rules, not a product-standard determination.
