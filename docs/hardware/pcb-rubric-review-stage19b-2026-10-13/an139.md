## State and geometry cross-checks

The frozen source connects Q2/Q3 as leg A and Q5/Q6 as leg B; C38–C41 are `BUS_P`–`HV_RET`, while both low-side sources join `LEG_RET` and return to `HV_RET` through R5 (`S:222–236, 380–425, 715–725`). T1, the external coil and C21–C23/C151/C152 form `SW_A → CT → coil → RES_A → series C → SW_B` (`S:425–448`). Thus these are the **topological** state paths, with direction reversing on resonant-current reversal:

| Adjacent state | Current path at positive `SW_A → SW_B` tank current | Abruptly changed branches |
|---|---|---|
| Q2 + Q6 diagonal | C38–C41 `BUS_P` → Q2 → `SW_A` → tank → `SW_B` → Q6 → `LEG_RET` → R5 → `HV_RET` → capacitor | Q2/Q6, both bus rails, R5 and local capacitor closure |
| Q3 + Q5 diagonal | For opposite tank-current sign: capacitor `BUS_P` → Q5 → `SW_B` → tank reverse → `SW_A` → Q3 → `LEG_RET` → R5 → `HV_RET` | Q5/Q3 and corresponding rail/capacitor closure |
| Dead time/freewheel | Current sign, body-diode conduction and Coss/snubber charge determine which diode/channel receives current; C12/C13/C19/C20 span each device (`S:231–236`). | The state **difference** includes commutating device and capacitor/diode branches; it is not the entire high-RMS tank path. |
| Startup, burst edge, fault disable | Controller gating can remove gate drive, but residual tank and bus energy still requires diode/snubber paths. | Exact signed current and transition sequence require a waveform/validated state map; the source alone cannot resolve them. |

Three independent checks against the selected board, made with KiCad 10.0.4 `pcbnew` plus the frozen copper polygons, constrain the review:

1. **Pads and topology:** Q2/Q3/Q5/Q6 drain/source pad centres lie on `y=4.215`; C38–C41 terminal centres lie on `y=28.2`. Q2 drain `(68.05,4.215)`, C38 `BUS_P` `(61,28.2)`, C39 `BUS_P` `(82.8,28.2)`; R5's power terminals are `LEG_RET` `(38.5,10.0)` and `HV_RET` `(38.5,15.2)`. The Q2-drain–C38-positive **centre** separation is 25.0 mm (rectangular pad edge gap approximately 21 mm); this is one leg of a loop, not a measured loop length or inductance. C12/C13 snubber pad centres are `y=9.7`, much closer to the FET row. Likewise Q5-drain `(9.95,4.215)` to C41-positive `(2.4,28.2)` is 25.1 mm centre separation. These are independent `pcbnew` readings from `B`, matching `S:1074–1080,1117–1120`.
2. **Routed tracks/vias:** `pcbnew` counted `sw_a` 159 segments/12 vias and `sw_b` 99/13 across their routes; `leg_ret` 135/26, `bus_p` 7/1 (large pours carry much of the current and are not represented by track-length totals). The four series gate-net branches Q-gate to R-gate each have 31.8 mm *aggregate track length* on F.Cu; the driver-output nets range from 43.5 to 69.8 mm aggregate, with one output using two vias. These aggregate sums include branches, so cannot be mistaken for a device-to-driver loop metric. U1/U2 output pins are around `y=46.125`; gate resistors around `y=14.062`; FET gates `y=4.215`. This is a real spatial constraint on gate-path coupling (`B` pad and track objects; `S:204–229`).
3. **Actual filled inner copper:** Point-in-polygon checks on exact-board `P/copper-dump.json` put `In2.Cu=bus_p` below `(68,8)` and `(10,8)`, with no `In1.Cu` copper at those sample points; `(68,20)` and `(10,20)` have `In1.Cu=hv_ret` and `In2.Cu=bus_p`; `(50,11)` has `In1.Cu=leg_ret` and `In2.Cu=bus_p`. `B:35–75` gives F–In1 dielectric 0.4355 mm and In1–In2 core 0.5 mm. A near-plane image/intentional return cannot be asserted continuously at the FET row from a four-layer label. The image-current path and its RF voltage require field/impedance analysis.

The image of `P/copper.png` and fresh F/In1/In2/B SVG layers was inspected with these coordinate checks. The reported STAGE19B change is confined to four SELV transfer capacitors and an `ocp_thresh` reroute; producer `D:785–791` says power copper is unchanged from STAGE19. Its older native-21 FEM/model screens can therefore inform the power-loop trade, but are not a measured STAGE19B margin.

## Prioritized findings and actions

1. **Power-loop trade accepted on a provisional model — high confidence in geometry, medium in predicted margin.** The capacitor row is about 24 mm from the FET row, and the `HV_RET` return crosses R5 to `LEG_RET`; the native-21 provisional FEM says leg-A parallel loop increased from 19.4 to 32.0 nH relative to native-19, but F6 model cases retained internal gate/VDS screens (`E/01-switching-parasitics/round17/d2/README.md:520–559`). This is **already known and consciously accepted**, not a new discovered defect. Its physical mechanism is increased shared commutation inductance and partner-gate coupling. The next decision needs exact-board loop extraction with mounted-cap/shunt/package closure and pin-level VDS/VGS waveforms. Pulling the film row toward the FETs would reduce loop inductance but crowd the F6 gate network and thermal/spacing allocation.
2. **Filter is an enclosure system, not just the board filter — high confidence in missing physical integration, medium in EMI consequence.** The producer's model predicts board-only failure and requires an external ≥100 µH DM/CM damped module (`D:70,792–807`). Its real choke/DM parts, pad-through cap path, earth/quiet boundary, source impedance and coil coupling are not frozen in STAGE19B. The board's C2/C3/C4 placements make HF return evaluation particularly useful, though their offsets do not prove bypass. Qualify the built module and harness with impedance, thermal/bias, DM/CM and LISN tests before attributing any spectrum to PCB copper.
3. **Bench document is behind the latest CM input — high confidence, new documentation correction.** The latest enclosure study uses 31.0 pF floating-pan coil→PE and changes nominal model margin to 10.5 dB, while `BENCH-PRECOMPLIANCE.md` B1/B5/B6 still directs comparison to 50 pF and 7.8 dB. Update predictions and B1 decision thresholds before first power; otherwise a valid measurement could be labelled a model miss. The earthed-pan 1.7–2.6 dB informational case remains a robustness question, not a compliance failure.
4. **Return-path repairs are real but not an RF immunity proof — high confidence in route improvement, medium in residual effect.** The four new C153–C156 transfer caps close every `pwm_hb` reference change to ≤3 mm; the 11 selected bypass checks pass. `bias_bad` reference changes remain deliberately open because it acts in the safe disable direction (`D:785–790`), and quiet-attachment placement under the bridge's image current is unsolved. Scope DIS/PWM/comparator references at pins through worst switching and fault events before considering more copper/transfer capacitors.

**Limits of the review.** Centre separations and aggregate trace lengths above are clearly labelled; no centroid bounding box is a loop measurement. The prior PI/SI/bypass gates, historical DRC, converged thermal *model* and conducted-EMI *model* are useful design evidence but do not verify RF impedance, return-current distribution, waveforms or a physical EMC/thermal margin. No full FEM rerun, CAD mutation or chain/regate was performed for this read-only review. The AN-139 buck examples supply physics and review questions, not Temper full-bridge limits.
