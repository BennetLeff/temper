## Priority findings

1. **Installed inlet filter remains the dominant dependency (known, high confidence).** The board source places C1 before L1 and C2/C3/C4 after L1 (`source/elec/src/power_stage_120v.ato:317-354`), but the successful calculation includes a *separate enclosure module* with an assumed 100 µH differential-mode branch, specified as two 41 µH inductors plus 18 µH choke leakage (`producer/validation-results/07-conducted-emi/s19b/README.md`, §§3, 5–7). `results.json` predicts board-only margins of −39.3 dB in 9–150 kHz and −38.4 dB in 150–500 kHz; the old 18 µH module misses the low band by 5.1 dB. The discrete inductors' exact part, 15 A bias curve, temperature/saturation, winding capacitance, filter case/bond, and inlet-to-load harness separation are not installed-part evidence. In the frozen copper, J1 L/N are at (281,7.5)/(286,7.5) mm, L1 is around (232–242,31–56), while BR1 is at x≈82–107 mm; filtered `l_filt` tracks span 350 mm total. That geometry makes filter bypass by the module and board harness a credible **installation** risk, not a proven failing coupling. Select and characterize the module, then measure insertion and terminal spectra with the actual enclosure, cord, PE bond, and lead dressing. Increasing inductance/bonding can cost heat, volume, leakage, and surge robustness.

2. **Modeled pass depends on an enclosure and pan state, with slim sensitivity margin (updated on 13 Oct, high confidence in arithmetic; moderate in physical transfer).** The R43 finite-volume study (`study16/REPORT.md`, `capacitance.json`, `_tables.md`) computes 31.0 pF winding/lead/lug-to-PE for a floating pan (28.5–35.1), split 15.3/15.7 pF at J2/J5; an earthed pan computes 91.4 pF (88.5–101.7). Its 18.14-million-cell result agrees with coarser grids at about 1% and records input STEP hashes. The stage19b `results.json:verdict` then reports **modeled PASS**: nominal minimum 10.48 dB, 22 A sensitivity 8.39 dB, all one-factor sensitivities 6.50 dB versus its 6 dB *internal planning* target. It does **not** establish a measured 0.5 dB EMC cushion. The study omits board, EMI-module, PS3, controls metal and several wires, treating them as air; its full-PE-plane bound adds up to 20 pF. Earthed-pan results are informational and predict only 1.7–2.6 dB at the 60 kHz CM fundamental, although the author says that is outside the intended compliance setup. Measure J2/J5-to-PE capacitance and split in the assembled R43-equivalent geometry, both pan states and variants, then sweep 35–60 kHz with the LISN. Keep a coil-side shield or stronger CM choke as *evaluated options*, not adopted fixes: the former can alter hot-side return and insulation; the latter adds heat and package burden. The stage16 report's old 6.6 dB 60 kHz interpolation is a preliminary mapping, superseded for the stage19b model by its full rerun and different coil-node placement.

3. **Return and sensor paths need physical probing (known geometry; effect unmeasured).** Native KiCad 10.0.4 reports R5 current-pad anchors `leg_ret` (38.5,10.0) and `hv_ret` (38.5,15.2) mm, with separate Kelvin-pad anchors `ocp_kelvin_p` (37.0,12.1) and `ocp_kelvin_n` (37.0,13.1) mm. This is a deliberate improvement, not a common-source failure. My earlier (37.43,11.67/13.53) values were the arithmetic mean of vertices in each real, asymmetric custom-pad polygon in the copper dump; the polygon geometry is sound, but that mean is not KiCad's pad anchor. The same dump contains ~269 mm aggregate `ocp_kelvin_p` track across four copper layers and ~203 mm `ct_s2` track over three. Aggregate segment length is not point-to-point cable length or proof of an EMC problem; the producer's D4 and coil-pickup notes explicitly require `ocp_node` and CT validation under switching and coil field. Separately, the exact-build `return-path.json` and `producer/validation-results/08-manufacturing-package/native-21/stage19-pi-si/return-path19b.txt` show the *repaired* `pwm_hb` and `ct_zc` reference transitions within 3 mm of identified transfer capacitors, while `bias_bad` has a 22.25 mm unreferenced run and six open reference changes; `pwm_ha`/`pwm_la` also retain 7.59/5.11 mm transfer-cap distances. This is a concrete return-path concern, not a demonstrated noise failure. Capture Kelvin differential/common-mode error, CT phase, bias-fault integrity and fault response at 22 A then the commissioned upper current, pan/no-pan, and worst coil field. Any added filtering must preserve the protection latency budget.

4. **Connector return and chassis bond are defined electrically but not at RF (observed, moderate confidence).** J4's two-row 16-pin map in `prior-build-evidence/copper-dump.json` puts four PWM outputs in adjacent positions x46.52–55.52,y180.08 with grounds at x43.52,y180.08 and x52.52,y183.08; the mating harness pairing/length is not fixed in this package. J6 PE pads lie around (150.63/165.87,134.5), whereas the removable R38 PE-to-SELV functional bond is at (24.6/26.25,132.87); the copper dump lists 260 mm of PE track segments and no PE plane zone. This does not imply a safety fault: `DECISIONS.md` assigns cord PE directly to the chassis stud and J6 only a branch, and R38 is a functional bond. It does leave high-frequency return inductance and cable common-mode distribution dependent on real wire/bond topology. Draw the assembled PE/chassis/R38/filter/Y-cap current paths, measure bond impedance and cable CM currents, and keep PE continuity independent of any EMC experiment.

5. **The model omits important operating and frequency behavior (known limitation).** `README.md` states a fixed rectifier conducting pair, one-way source, no 120 Hz cycle, burst/startup/damper-open events, independently solved leg B, or predictive wiring/board behavior above about 1–3 MHz. The companion `BENCH-PRECOMPLIANCE.md` is a plan; no LISN, near-field, clamp, ESD, EFT, surge, or immunity result is supplied. PS1–PS3 emissions are bounded from assumed class levels; their isolation capacitances and possible filter bypass are unmeasured. Use the plan's held-frequency, burst, auxiliary-only, line/neutral and CM/DM tests as the first empirical discrimination. Confirm the actual 2026 market/product requirement and lab setup before treating the document's FCC transcription or Ott's 2009 examples as acceptance limits.
