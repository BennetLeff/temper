## Improvements confirmed on stage19b

The four new **C153–C156** are present on `v3v3`/`selv_gnd`, with pad-to-plane-via distances about 0.85–1.51 mm. All seven checked `pwm_hb` transitions are within 1.12–2.72 mm of a transfer capacitor. The earlier missing J4-side PWM transfers must not be carried forward as current defects.

The bus-fault path was re-referenced to its intended Kelvin domain; the selected route gate reports no samples over another net. R5 has separate current pads and Kelvin anchors, independently checked with native `pcbnew`: pad 1 `(38.5,10.0)` `leg_ret`, pad 2 `(37.0,12.1)` `ocp_kelvin_p`, pad 3 `(37.0,13.1)` `ocp_kelvin_n`, pad 4 `(38.5,15.2)` `hv_ret`. [Native pad results](evidence/r5-native-pads.json) distinguish these anchors from vertex averages of the asymmetric custom copper shapes. This is not evidence that auxiliary Kelvin current or switching pickup is zero.

RECOM/LDO HF caps and driver-local reservoirs have specific stage19 geometry improvements. The high-side bias uses independent monitored RECOM supplies, so bootstrap recharge criteria are inapplicable. Remaining rail hold-up, droop, CMTI and brownout criteria still apply.
