# Temper mechanical application review — both enclosure packages

Reviewed 2026-10-03 against the corrected `temper-mechanical-review` skill, the snapshot manifest/baseline, and the original package sources and saved validation. This is a review of existing prototype evidence, not a CAD rebuild or physical test. The reviewed electrical candidate is the newer full bridge without a native PCB layout; every prior-board envelope fit is only historical packaging context.

## Release position and priority decisions

**P0 — retain prototype-only status for both packages.** The folded-aluminum study reports zero rigid overlaps and valid STEP reimport, while explicitly lacking structural, thermal, electrical, bend-shop and physical qualification (`output/temper-folded-aluminum/verification.json`). R2's `CURRENT.json` likewise says `PROTOTYPE_REVIEW_ONLY`; `output/temper-manufacturing-r2/README.md` says click, thermal, glass, seal, controls and cooling remain open. Neither is a fabrication or powered-use release. This is a **verified package-status/result**; it is not evidence the design will fail physically.

**P0 — resolve the inherited knob click before choosing a stop, switch or sealing architecture.** The earlier recorded test explicitly fails (`output/temper-cad-revision/validation.json:212`); R2 retains that failure (`output/temper-manufacturing-r2/evidence/validation.json:384`). With stop `0.55±0.05 mm`, air gap `0.20±0.10 mm`, and electrical trip `[0.15,0.40] mm`, switch travel at the stop is `[0.20,0.50] mm`. Minimum travel misses maximum trip by `0.20 mm`; maximum travel after minimum trip is `0.35 mm`, but supplier safe mechanical travel is unknown. These are **computed from declared assumptions**, not measured build variation. The R2 detent/plunger redesign changes rotational feel, not this axial switch stack. **Design options:** (a) establish a controlled actuator datum and adjust/shim the gap and stop so both trip and safe-overtravel inequalities hold with margin; (b) choose a switch with documented trip and safe stroke compatible with the controlled stack; (c) decouple feel from electrical actuation or use noncontact press sensing if the mechanical stack cannot be made robust. Closure requires a supplier maximum safe travel/force, selected real switch, datum chain and tolerance drawing, worst-case two-sided calculation including thermal/reassembly offsets, and instrumented cold/hot/cycled samples that trip before stop without exceeding safe travel/force. Do not claim a click merely from the sampled rigid-motion pass.

**P0 — glass support and liquid barrier are still unqualified.** The folded design puts a `338×326×4 mm` glass panel through a `339.2×327.2 mm` opening (nominal `0.6 mm` per side), on a separate `6 mm` nominal carrier ledge with a `2 mm` bedding envelope (`output/temper-folded-aluminum/model.py:111`, `:162-169`; `geometry-checks.json`). The opening, glass edge, bedding and carrier are a single functional chain; their tolerance, hot relative motion and cookware-load deflection are absent. R2 inherits that geometry (`output/temper-manufacturing-r2/src/enclosure.py:162-169`) and moves top screw axes to improve a carrier edge web, but that does not establish glass stress or sealing. **New mechanical inference:** rigidly locating the glass at multiple edges, or letting cover/carrier bow under screw preload, can change contact pressure and local edge loading during heat cycling. This is a risk to test, not a demonstrated failure. **Design options:** use a controlled compliant bedding with hard load/height stops and intentional in-plane movement; or separate a structural cookware-load support path from the liquid seal so seal squeeze is not set by screw torque. Closure requires the chosen glass supplier's edge/hole/load limits, bedding/seal material and installation specification, an explicit datum and clearance/compression stack at cold/hot extremes, cookware and impact load paths with measured glass/support deflection, and spill/leak plus cycling/reassembly tests with pass criteria set by product requirements. The model's 2 mm bedding is an allocation, not a gasket specification.

**P0 — sensor contact and seal need one coupled stroke/load budget.** The inherited face-seal check has a `1.50 mm` nominal cross-section and nominal squeeze `23.33%`, but declared corner combinations (`1.45–1.55 mm` cross-section, `1.10–1.20 mm` gland height) give **17.24–29.03%** squeeze (`output/temper-cad-revision/validation.json:177`). This is a dimensional interval only: no hot compression set, bond/diaphragm fatigue or leakage result. R2's thicker sensor cap, formed lip and annular glass shim improve model geometry but mark end-stop strength, heat transfer, glass preload and seal performance unqualified (`output/temper-manufacturing-r2/src/mechanisms.py:113-134`; `evidence/validation.json:48`). The R2 sensor motion pass samples `0–1.2 mm` without hard-body collision; it does not establish a stable contact force or maximum safe travel (`output/temper-manufacturing-r2/evidence/validation.json:352`). **Design options:** set a mechanical travel stop and calibrated spring/compliant element with the seal gland independent from glass contact force; or redesign the sensor cartridge/contact architecture if temperature response and leak barrier cannot coexist. Closure requires the selected elastomer's allowable squeeze and compression-set range at actual temperature/exposure, a tolerance stack for gland and contact height, force-versus-stroke and temperature response measurements against a defined pan/glass load case, hot/wet leakage and cycle life, and a repeatable service reassembly procedure.

**P1 — establish the coil support's locating and load path in R2.** Four tube legs at `x=±157 mm`, `y=190/330 mm` join a 3 mm insulating laminate plate to the tray with M4×90 fasteners (`output/temper-manufacturing-r2/src/support.py:7-39`). Saved validation confirms nominal contact at all four leg ends, explicitly not stiffness or strength (`evidence/validation.json:66`). Four rigid length/position constraints plus dissimilar laminate, aluminum tray, tube and glass temperatures are a plausible thermal/preload fight; how much it moves is uncomputed because grade, expansion, operating temperatures, cut-length tolerance and clamp load are unselected. The sensor stack is located through the central laminate spacer/adapter and three M2.5 bolts (`support.py:21-32`); coil-to-glass and sensor-to-glass positions therefore depend on tray bends, leg lengths, plate flex and glass carrier separately. **Design options:** define one primary in-plane datum and directional compliance at other supports while preserving vertical/load support, or keep rigid legs only after analysis and hot-cycle measurements show acceptable stress and registration. Closure requires a datum-flow drawing from cookware/glass through carrier, coil and sensor to tray; measured leg, plate and bend variation; selected material CTE/temperature limits; relevant hot ΔT and load cases; deflection/preload/clearance analysis; and prototype coil-to-glass registration, sensor contact and joint-retention data after cycling/transport. No numeric expansion or force value is defensible yet.

**P1 — close folded-sheet and joint process capability before cutting either design.** Folded study assumes `5052-H32`, `2 mm` sheet, `3 mm` inside radius and `K=0.42`; five bends and all flats remain provisional (`geometry-checks.json`; `output/temper-folded-aluminum/model.py:107-161`). R2 uses the same sheet development and adds flat laminate support, tubes, riveted hatch strips, formed cap, threaded hardware and purchased feet (`output/temper-manufacturing-r2/README.md`). The top ring mounts use four stock angles and nominal 2.4 mm spacer stacks; heads are visual envelopes, with no bolt preload or thread strength calculation (`output/temper-folded-aluminum/model.py:170-185`). The cover/tray/glass datum chain can shift with springback and reassembly. **Design options:** obtain a shop-approved bend development and datum/inspection drawing with formed dimensions, then fixture or shim carrier height; simplify/adjust bracket scheme if the measured bend process cannot hold the glass/sensor relationship. Closure requires selected stock angle, actual screws/nuts/finish, shop bend coupon and forming sequence, produced-part measurements and process capability for critical formed dimensions, torque/preload and vibration/thermal retention tests, hardware access/service trials, and revised flat DXFs approved by the fabricator. Do not use the provisional cutting files unchanged.

**P1 — replace historical PCB envelope checks with a new physical interface review.** The folded model imports prior exported PCB/coil/sensor/cooling bounds (`output/temper-folded-aluminum/model.py:213-233`) and reports no overlap; R2 composes inherited context and replaces only selected support items (`output/temper-manufacturing-r2/src/assembly.py:57-70`). Those are actual computed results for that older model. The selected full-bridge source has no native PCB, so neither package proves mounting, connector access, heat sink airflow, cable bend/routing, earthing or service fit for it. Closure requires an electrical revision with actual board outline, component heights/keepouts, thermal path and harness/connectors, then refreshed enclosure CAD, clearance/load paths and assembly inspection with that exact revision.

## Minimum functional datum map to draw

1. **Cookware and glass:** cookware contact → glass thickness/flatness → bedding/carrier ledge → four bracket/spacer stacks → folded cover/tray → feet/countertop. Assign the actual vertical and lateral locating surfaces; fasteners should retain the chain without silently setting glass squeeze by torque.
2. **Coil and sensor:** glass/cookware datum → coil gap/centering and sensor protrusion/contact → R2 plate, spacer and adapter → four legs → tray, while keeping the cover/carrier chain separate until their relative hot motion is measured.
3. **Knob:** front fascia datum → knob seat/stop and switch mounting → real switch plunger; include temperature, formed-sheet variation and service reassembly in the axial stack.
4. **Barriers:** glass perimeter bedding and sensor diaphragm/face seal each need their own compression/contact map, leak path and replaceability plan.

The **MIT course contribution** is the method: select variation-sensitive product characteristics, map datums and constraints before tolerance arithmetic, budget geometric/thermal/load/process error, and test assembly plus service. The package-specific failures and proposed options above are **Temper inferences** unless marked as saved computed results. No physical hot, wet, glass-load, joint-retention or actual new-PCB fit result was present in the reviewed evidence.
