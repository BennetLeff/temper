# Product and manufacturing application to both Temper enclosure packages

Reviewed 2026-10-03 using the updated `temper-product-taste` and `temper-manufacturing-review` skills. This is a design decision review of existing artifacts, not a build, compliance assessment, or physical qualification. The two packages are related revisions: the folded-aluminum study establishes the bent-sheet exterior; R2 keeps that exterior and revises the knob, sensor, support, hatch, feet, and build records. Neither should be described as a proven current enclosure for the new full-bridge `zapote/power-stage-120v` electrical source; its own README states that the native schematic/PCB and physical tests have not started ([power-stage README.md lines 1–11](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/zapote/power-stage-120v/README.md:1)). A native board and current component envelopes must be integrated before fit conclusions transfer. The shared [baseline](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/worktrees/mit-product-guidance/docs/research/mit-product-design/application/baseline.md) identifies the dirty/untracked source state; all 21 live files selected in its [input manifest](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/worktrees/mit-product-guidance/docs/research/mit-product-design/application/input-manifest.json) matched their SHA-256 snapshots at review time.

## Product decisions to retain, change, and test

**Retain for the next physical mockup:** the broad cooking surface, angled control area, removable knob, and accessible cover plus underside sensor hatch are coherent starting points for seeing, touching, cleaning, and servicing the product. Both packages retain the basic layout, and R2 deliberately keeps a replaceable wear cam and improves hatch access ([folded fabrication notes](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-folded-aluminum/fabrication-notes.html:1), [R2 README.md lines 13, 18, 22](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/README.md:13)). This is a recommendation to retain them as **hypotheses**, not a claim that users prefer the geometry or that a service technician can actually extract the parts.

**Change before committing finish or tooling:** make the control carrier, button/display sealing, and feedback state an engineered module. R2 still labels the buttons/display as floating appearance pieces ([change-register.csv line 5](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/change-register.csv:5)). Define what the cook sees and feels for off, pan present/absent, command accepted, active heating, hot surface, sensor fault, and safe shutdown. The exact vocabulary and controls are product decisions to validate with the firmware and electrical teams; the MIT course material supplies a user-observation method, not these states or an appliance UI standard. **Existing known gap**, not a new geometry discovery.

**Keep the visible glass joint and screw/seam language open:** the folded package explicitly accepts visible seams and four top screw heads for a prototype, with a nominal 0.6 mm glass-edge gap and allocated bedding ([geometry-checks.json lines 33–49](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-folded-aluminum/geometry-checks.json:33), [fabrication notes](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-folded-aluminum/fabrication-notes.html:1)). R2 carries a 0.6 mm sealant allocation but no glass supplier approval ([make-buy-review.csv line 62](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/make-buy-review.csv:62)) and records open finish/cleanability work ([change-register.csv line 15](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/change-register.csv:15)). Compare the present screw/seam arrangement with a concealed but reversible fastener variant on full-size process samples. Evaluate hand wipe, residue trapping, liquid path, fastener access, assembly time, and finish yield together. Do not adopt permanent bonding or a tighter flush gap solely for a render; no owner taste preference or supplier gap capability is recorded. **Existing gap plus proposed alternative**, not evidence that the current seam fails.

**Protect serviceability while measuring it:** R2's captive hatch strips and cam accessibility are promising; the actual rivet, joint seal, screw torque, driver reach, harness slack, and extraction path remain open ([README.md lines 18, 35–42](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/README.md:18), [change-register.csv lines 11, 14](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/change-register.csv:11)). Assemble and disassemble with real tools and selected components. Record damaged seals/wires, inaccessible steps, time, and whether reassembly restores sensor/coil and glass key characteristics. **Existing known gap**; the evaluation protocol is proposed.

**Control variation at the assembly level:** do not promote R2's improved nominal hatch/carrier webs into supplier acceptance or infer seam yield from the CAD. The folded package calls its bend allowance provisional; R2 calls for a shop bend coupon and retains an unresolved formed-sheet stack ([folded geometry-checks.json lines 81–86](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-folded-aluminum/geometry-checks.json:81), [R2 README.md lines 31–38](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/README.md:31), [change-register.csv line 13](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/change-register.csv:13)). Draw datum/tolerance chains for glass-to-frame seam, pan/glass-to-coil/sensor geometry, support-to-tray height, and panel-to-control-module location. Include bends, finish, fasteners, selected glass/bedding, heat, and reassembly. Use worst-case limits for must-fit/must-clear conditions; only use statistical capability after supplier process data show centering, spread, and correlation. **Existing gap**; exact chain and critical-characteristic selection are proposed engineering work.

**Do not carry prior electrical packaging into this choice:** the folded check used a prior packaging bounding box ([geometry-checks.json lines 51–53, 88](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-folded-aluminum/geometry-checks.json:51)); R2 still has blank inlet/probe parts and says the fan, heat sink, and harness need selection before powered integration ([README.md lines 41–42](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/README.md:41), [make-buy-review.csv lines 32–33](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/make-buy-review.csv:32), [change-register.csv lines 10, 22](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/change-register.csv:10)). The new full-bridge source needs its own native board, components, isolation/thermal/airflow interfaces, and matched revision identity before a mechanical fit verdict. This is an **integration finding from comparing current source identity with the package scope**, not proof of a physical collision.

## Stage decision

**Folded aluminum:** sufficient for design comparison, an unpowered full-size appearance/ergonomics mockup, supplier DFM/quote requests, and bend/seam coupons. **R2:** sufficient for a controlled engineering prototype of the revised nominal mechanics after selected materials, hardware, process instructions, and safe test planning are in place. The first package's calculated no-overlap result and R2's saved-solid/motion results do **not** authorize cutting their provisional DXFs as production drawings, powered-use release, a learning pilot presented as qualified product, or production release. This agrees with each package's own status statements ([folded verification.json lines 9–12](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-folded-aluminum/verification.json:9), [R2 CURRENT.json lines 2–11](/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-manufacturing-r2/CURRENT.json:2)).

To decide on a **learning pilot**, the mechanical and manufacturing owners need released supplier-reviewed enclosure drawings and process samples; electrical/thermal/safety owners need a current integrated board, selected components, controlled integration plan, requirements and test methods; product/design research needs observed use and cleaning data; quality needs critical-characteristic measurement and build traceability. A **production release** needs qualification across production-intent parts, assembly, lots, thermal/use stresses and faults, plus validated production screens and supplier capability. These are proposed closure categories, not claims that MIT provided appliance thresholds or that one 20-unit build would prove a 20,000-unit process.

No newly proven physical defect was found from reading these artifacts alone. The most useful immediate design action is to retain the reversible architecture, select an actual control/cooling/electrical stack, and test the glass/seam and pan/coil/sensor stack with real process samples before any exterior finish or tooling choice is frozen.
