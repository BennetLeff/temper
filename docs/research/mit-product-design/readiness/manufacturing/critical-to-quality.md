# Inspection, qualification and production screens

Every record uses [release-records.md](release-records.md): requirement/version, numeric limit or observable rule, raw result, uncertainty, instrument/calibration, exact sample/revision/lot and disposition. **All physical results are NOT_RUN.** An unresolved limit is a HOLD, not an inspector's judgment call. Suggested methods below become executable tests only after the owner approves fixtures, conditions and acceptance criteria.

G1 is a characterization build: inspect all critical features of the single unit and retain failed data. G2 plan must distinguish within-unit repeat measurement, repeated assembly, between-unit variation, independent machine setup, and material/supplier lot changes. These are not interchangeable replicates. Choose sample count from the failure risk and measurement distribution; record why it is enough for the proposed claim. Do not compute a credible capability claim from one unit or from uncontrolled hand rework. Validate a candidate EOL screen against known-good and representative defective samples before treating a pass as evidence that the defect would be caught.
