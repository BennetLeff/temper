## Packs needed before requesting fabrication/assembly release

**PCBA pack — electrical owner:** one matched native PCB and schematic/netlist revision including HOT5 and any current-trip correction; ERC/DRC and connectivity results; fab Gerbers/drill/map plus approved stackup/copper/laminate/material evidence and tolerances; assembly drawings with pin1/polarity and DNP marks; exact MPN/approved alternates BOM; CPL with origins/rotation/board-side conventions independently checked; paste/stencil guidance; programming/test-point/fixture interface; board serialization, incoming/assembly inspection and test acceptance. Ask assembler to identify parts that cannot be sourced, ambiguous footprints/orientations, through-hole/hand assembly steps, cleaning/residue control, rework limitations, test access, minimum order and excess-component needs. Quote **the first engineering unit**, additional prototype units, and setup separately. Native-18 and the changed source are not interchangeable manufacturing inputs.

**Mechanical pack — mechanical/process owner:** formed STEP, controlled dimensioned drawings with functional datums/tolerances, supplier-accepted flats, materials/finish/masking, fastener/thread/insert/torque schedule, bond/seal work instructions, fixtures and witness coupons, actual display/header/gland/cable drawings and approved top-cover-first service sequence. Name every unselected envelope. Require written exceptions before changes; retain the continuous R4 flush front.

**Harness pack — electrical/mechanical owner:** L/N/PE diagram; direct cord-to-chassis protective bond and sink bond separate from J6 branch; exact inlet/cord/strain relief/terminal/fuse/wire MPNs and ratings, lengths, pin map, bend/clamp route, crimp tooling, torque/locking and inspection. Include fan supply, coil leads, display/button/probe connectors. A generic M12 gland or4mm cable envelope does not settle conductor count, sealing or strain relief.

The PCB readiness inspection adds two concrete mechanical/fab holds: native-18 has **no dedicated board-mounting-hole footprints** (component NPTHs cannot be silently reused), and its persisted copper/dielectric stack totals **1.653mm**, with outer copper70µm and inner copper61µm. The electrical/mechanical owners must agree board retention and the supplier-tested stackup; a nominal1.6mm order note alone does not settle either. HOT5 integration also changes U8 from a five-pin to six-pin package, so retaining the old U8 placement/footprint would not implement the new source.

Every request needs pack revision/hash, units, quantity, intended stage, buyer-supplied versus supplier-supplied parts, approved substitution policy (none without engineering review), required certificates/inspection records, packaging and quote validity. Supplier claims become evidence only when tied to the proposed process and drawing.

| Task | Hardware/sample preparation | Measurement resources and purchase hold |

| Coil/pan characterization | Selected coil/ferrite sample and labeled intended pans; fixed pan position/gap jig; power team's measurement protocol | Impedance analyzer or equivalent validated four-terminal fixture spanning the intended20–60kHz band; temperature and geometry records; de-embedding/open/short checks. | Low-energy electronics | Source/layout-matched PCBA only after pack release; harness/protection fixtures, programmer, known test loads | Current-limited bench supplies, DMM, scope with appropriate probes, signal/stimulus tools specified by electrical owner. | Mains switching/fault work | Qualified guarded lab station, accepted power module, coil/pan fixture and independent disconnect/discharge provisions | Line source with approved range/current capability, power analyzer, rated differential/isolated voltage probes and current probe, scope acquisition suited to fault timing and switching edges. | Closed-unit thermal/airflow | Actual selected sink, insulating interfaces/clamps, fan/supply/duct/guard, real enclosure and thermally relevant materials | Calibrated multichannel temperature logger with isolation appropriate to attachment points; thermocouples on each switch/bridge case, local sink, inlet/exhaust, R5, supply, sensor/UI/bonds; flow/pressure fixture; power/frequency/current record. | Safety/EMC/qualification | Defined specimens from frozen build, actual cord/PE/barriers, approved fault samples and packaging | Qualified lab provides calibrated protective-bond/dielectric/touch-current/EMC/environmental resources and exact procedures. Tektronix's [probe guidance](https://www.tek.com/en/documents/whitepaper/abcs-probes-primer) requires respecting probe ratings/derating and grounding; retain protective earth on grounded instruments and use suitable differential/isolated measurement systems for floating nodes. A mains isolation transformer is not blanket permission to attach grounded probes to a hazardous switching circuit. Instrument ownership, condition and calibration are still unknown; inventory or rent before committing the powered-test date.
