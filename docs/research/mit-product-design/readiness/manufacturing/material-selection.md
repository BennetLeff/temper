## Three-button electrical implementation for the cold mockup

Use three **C&K PTS645SH43SMTR92LFS** candidate tactile switches, one each for BACK, SELECT/START and STOP, on an unpowered fixture board with actual mounting datum. The [C&K sheet](https://www.ckswitches.com/media/1471/pts645.pdf) gives2.0±0.5N operating force and0.30+0.10/−0.15mm electrical travel for this exact order code. This allows comparison to the existing knob candidate; it does not settle maximum safe stroke/force or membrane feel. Cooling/mechanical worker owns that coupon and both travel bounds. Do not assume the switch itself seals the panel.

Route **six independently labeled contact wires**, two per switch, to a removable bench test connector/terminal strip. Identify the two poles from the manufacturer's footprint/drawing and verify with a meter—four switch pads are not four independent contacts. Labels: `BACK_A/B`, `SELECT_A/B`, `STOP_A/B`. Initial test only measures open/closed resistance and force/travel; no power-stage connection. This isolated-pair scheme leaves the final controller pullup/common arrangement undecided and avoids an accidental connection to hot-ground circuitry. Permanent connector/package and ESD/filtering are held for the controller pin-map review.

The available `firmware/main/ui.c` reads **one encoder button**, and its normal-screen release toggles start/stop using `state_machine_force_state`; three independent R4 buttons are **not integrated in this candidate**. `state_handlers.c` names separate buttons but that alone does not prove the HAL mapping. The separate read-only `worktrees/zapote-programming-ui` at `0a77e4f7efb8aa2e4116ef32e0567f197ce1e342` has a source-conflict ledger, not a ready service connector: IO19/20 USB versus relay/fault, IO17 fault versus LED, IO16 relay versus reserved RTD and GPIO0 download-strap versus reset semantics disagree. Its README explicitly blocks service hardware. D20 in `ps-oracle` likewise leaves actual controller GPIO selection to the controller schematic. This proposal does not supersede those workstreams.

Before wiring an energized cooker, firmware/electrical owners must freeze actual controller GPIOs and reference domain, active levels/pullups/filtering/debounce, event/state mapping, simultaneous/stuck/disconnected-button behavior and STOP behavior in every state. A passive continuity test cannot qualify those interactions. Keep START separate from STOP; do not parallel the three buttons into the existing toggle input. Service reset and USB/programmer access also need accepted isolation/backfeed and stop/rearm behavior before connecting test equipment.

Retain **Newhaven NHD-3.12-25664UCW2** for the display mockup. Its [manufacturer specification](https://newhavendisplay.com/content/specs/NHD-3.12-25664UCW2.pdf) identifies3.3V, SSD1322, serial/parallel options and1×20 2.54mm header. Choose and document the serial/parallel straps and all20 pin functions before a cable is made. The R4 rear connector/bend is an envelope, not a selected cable; ESP32 presence does not establish firmware or voltage-domain compatibility. The independent low-voltage UI mockup may be powered only after its own pin/power review; do not infer isolation through a USB cable.
