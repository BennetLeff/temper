# Engineering prototype traveler

Copy this traveler into a directory named for the physical build serial. All rows begin **NOT_RUN**. The builder records date/name/result/evidence and the responsible owner signs each hold release. Rework creates a new revision or deviation record; it never erases a failed result. A procedure requiring an unset torque, force, temperature, electrical limit or dwell stops until its owner approves that value and source.

| Step | Entry and operation | Required record / hold | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 00 | Assign build serial, intended stage and operators. Freeze source, native PCB, firmware/config, assembled STEP, drawings, BOM and test plan hashes. | [Manifest](release-records.md); no historical board substituted for current PCB. List every deviation from intended production materials. | Integration |
| 01 | Review supplier exceptions; inspect incoming part identity and condition before assembly. Segregate cold-fit prints from intended energized parts. | Exact MPN/lot/revision, PCB stackup/copper/laminate certificates, enclosure alloy/temper/finish, glass and adhesive/seal batches, shelf life. Unapproved substitutions HOLD. | Purchasing + quality |
| 02 | Cold-fit current populated PCB surrogate, selected sink/fans/duct, terminals, harness bends, display and control carrier. Practice installation and top-cover-first service sequence. | Dated photographs, measured datums, interference/clearance log, actual tool access. Mechanical owner accepts revised assembly, including protection compartment and live-to-metal construction. | Mechanical + electrical |
| 03 | Inspect first formed front/tray/cover against supplier-accepted drawing. Inspect finish and apertures before bonding. | Flatness, bend/aperture data, burr condition and coating/bond contact areas. Do not compensate a bad bend with adhesive thickness. | Mechanical + supplier |
| 04 | Build separate lens/ring/stud and seal process coupons using intended preparation/cure. Review retention and cleaning results before committing the only enclosure. | Batch, preparation, bondline, cure history, test fixture/load/temperature and observations. Bond/seal procedure remains provisional until actual supplier limits and tests close it. | Process + mechanical |
| 05 | Assemble lens/ring/studs in exterior-datum fixture; record cure. Inspect bonds before carrier conceals them. | Measured flushness, bondline/coverage, void/rework photographs, stud location and approved witness-coupon result. Distinguish inward support from outward glass retention. | Assembly + quality |
| 06 | Assemble actual button board, membrane, knob switch/stop, display and cable/gland. Exercise the stack cold before sealing. | No missed actuation, no preload, both maximum safe travel/force bounds, return, button identity, display alignment and connector clearance to approved limits. No invented membrane modulus. | Mechanical + firmware |
| 07 | PCBA incoming inspection and unpowered electrical check at qualified electronics bench; firmware/serial provisioning; low-energy rail and fault-chain tests using electrical team's procedure. | Board serial/revision, component/polarity/solder inspection, continuity/short results, rail/current captures, firmware hash. **HOLD before hazardous energization** until PCB/protection owner accepts. | Electrical + test |
| 08 | Fit sink/device electrical insulators and clamps, coil/ferrite supports, protected compartment and fan supply. Install PE bonds and all harnesses. | Exact insulator/contact preparation, clamp torque and locking per approved specification; conductor/terminal/crimp tool ID; direct cord-to-chassis PE and sink bond photos. Check probe/USB/debug access isolation. | Electrical + assembly |
| 09 | Inspect joints that cover will hide; check harness chafe/pinch, strain relief, coil terminals, barriers, sensor contact, fan direction and cable restraint. | Independent second inspection, torque witness records, mechanical/PE/insulation pretests to reviewer-issued procedure. Record sensor positions before closure. **HOLD** on missing criteria. | Quality + safety/test |
| 10 | Qualified test lead accepts guarded station and test-specific power/current/duration/temperature stop limits. Connect instruments while de-energized under station procedure. | Probe ratings/derating, calibration, isolation/grounding diagram, emergency stop/discharge procedure, raw-data path. No generic scope ground clip on floating switching nodes. | Test lead |
| 11 | Follow electrical owner's staged bring-up: rails and inhibit behavior, controlled switching then operating-envelope tests. Verify HOT5, OCP/OVP and gate/current extinction at approved conditions. | Time-aligned voltage/current/fault captures, actual stimulus, threshold/delay/residual energy, expected vs observed. Shorted-switch interruption and other destructive tests use separate approved specimens/procedures, not improvised faults on a shared functional-test unit. | Electrical + test |
| 12 | Close assembly to production-intent configuration for thermal/airflow and controls tests. | Installed flow/pressure, local temperatures and uncertainty, pan identity/position, line voltage/input current, frequency/power command, every active limit. Apply [CTQ](critical-to-quality.md) and power team's approved matrix. | Thermal + firmware |
| 13 | After normal-operation acceptance, run separately approved abnormal-operation/aging/spill/service cases. Inspect afterward. | Sample allocation, test order, pre/post measurements and damage; quarantine stressed samples from ordinary demonstration. Qualification failures feed revision, not merely a repeat until pass. | Safety + mechanical + test |
| 14 | De-energize and verify discharge; top cover comes off before front rear lid. Remove/reinstall serviceable carrier and agreed modules without disturbing permanent glass bond. | Tool access, replaced consumables, pinched-wire/bond damage check and repeated PE/insulation/function/seal checks to approved service scope. | Mechanical + quality |
| 15 | Review complete evidence and deviations. Decide next revision or G2 pilot proposal. | Remaining defects, affected retest matrix, proposed pilot coverage/quantity rationale and role signoffs. Current outcome remains NOT_RUN. | Integration + quality |

**Dependency rules:** 02 precedes ordering final enclosure/PCBA interfaces; 04 precedes irreversible bonding; 07 precedes integrated energization; 09–10 precede every new hazardous test configuration; 11 precedes rated sustained heat testing; normal-operation evidence precedes controlled fault/abuse qualification. Sensor instrumentation can change airflow or insulation: document it, and remove/retest any alteration affecting the claim. Changing board/copper, coil/capacitors, sink/interface/duct, harness, firmware protection, adhesive/finish or supplier invalidates the associated evidence until an owner records the affected retests.
