# Proposed interlock aggregate-gate substitution

**Proposal only; foreign interlock source/PCB unchanged.** Replace `CD74HC30PWR` with **Nexperia `74AHC30PW,118`** after interlock-owner review. It supplies an explicit 3.0–3.6 V propagation-delay row, removing the need to extrapolate the old gate's 2 V timing table. The primary [product page](https://www.nexperia.com/product/74AHC30PW) marks Production; DigiKey's [exact-suffix listing in its substitute table](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/nexperia-usa-inc/74LVC30APWJ/5169947) lists the orderable `74AHC30PW,118`. Stock is not reserved or ordered.

The [Nexperia Rev 8 datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74AHC_AHCT30.pdf), pp. 1,3–6,8–9,11, gives TSSOP14/SOT402-1, 4.4 ×5 mm body and 0.65 mm pitch. Pin mapping matches: A–F 1–6, GND7, Y8, NC9/10/13, G11, H12, VCC14. Eight-input NAND polarity is unchanged. At 3.0–3.6 V and −40…125 °C, delay is 1–15.5 ns with 50 pF load, or 1–12 ns at 15 pF. Inputs allow voltages above VCC; **there is no output IOFF guarantee to import from LVC parts**. AHC, not AHCT, is the candidate for the 3.3 V rail.

## Applicability and remaining checks

- The surrounding devices are already **SN74LVC14ADR** and **SN74LVC1G74DCUR**, not HC-family parts. Their [inverter](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc14a.pdf) and [latch](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g74.pdf) tables explicitly cover 3.3 V ±0.3 V. The stated-condition sum for two inverter stages, candidate aggregate, and async clear is **4.7–39.4 ns**. It excludes loaded PERMIT edge formation and harness propagation.
- Each NAND input has one local logic producer. Check its leakage/load against the source output specifications, and total NAND output load against 50 pF including pads, trace and probe. No harness should terminate on the internal NAND output. The latch output does drive the two AO3400A gates through 100 Ω resistors; its ≤50 pF datasheet timing cannot be extended to those gates without a loaded calculation/measurement.
- At the AHC's tabulated 3.0 V/50 µA output condition, VOH≥2.9 V and VOL≤0.1 V are compatible with the next LVC14A's tabulated 3.0 V thresholds. AHC's input threshold table has discrete rail points: the conventional 0.7/0.3·VCC interpretation over the actual 3.135–3.465 V rail is an engineering inference, requiring confirmation before this is a fully qualified substitution. Source logic has substantial rail-to-rail margin, but do not call that inference a new guaranteed table row.
- Verify actual transition slew against the timing fixture and recommended operating conditions. Re-run Boolean/source/native parity after adoption, then low-energy latch assertion/reset/power-ramp tests; powered-down output and common-rail collapse remain explicit system cases. The existing SENSOR_LIVE and AUX producer gaps are unaffected.

The initially considered **74LVC30APWJ** has faster specified timing and IOFF behavior, but [DigiKey marks that exact part obsolete](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/nexperia-usa-inc/74LVC30APWJ/5169947). It is rejected as the new BOM recommendation. No unverified modern lifecycle status is inferred from its still-accessible PDF.
