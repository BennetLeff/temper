## What changed in the evidence

The [reproducible experiment](../../../../../zapote/power-stage-120v/prototype-closure/power/README.md) copies the qualified leg-A deck and matrix from `ps-oracle` commit `fda5ab9ece24ef1ee6f2317604c5ca73367d5201`. It first reproduces the historical 348 ns S1 baseline exactly, then disables both commands and explores imposed current, command latency and capacitor ESL. This replaces the old assumed 150 ns gate-discharge number with simulated gate behavior for specified cases. It does not model the complete detector, physical tank, short circuit or current extinction.

The actual paths differ:

- **Tank CT:** U10/U11 → U13 three-input OR → J4.10 BUS_FAULT → interlock → PERMIT → Q1/Q4 receiver → UCC21550 DIS → gate discharge.
- **Shunt:** R5/filter/U7 → U8 NAND → U9 ISO7710 → U13 → the same downstream chain. HOT5 supervision adds another U8 input. CT faults do **not** traverse U8/U9.

These paths were checked against the frozen source and `FAULT-INTERFACE.md`, with the HOT5 source replacing the historical U8 two-input NAND. The interlock's source-build-02 traces a fault input through SN74LVC14A, CD74HC30, a second SN74LVC14A, and asynchronous clear on SN74LVC1G74. There is a real circuit to qualify; the whole interlock is not an unspecified black box.

## Decision

Do not retune R34/R35 from these results. D17 remains incomplete for actual threshold-to-current-extinction, hard shoot-through/shorted-load response, device energy/SOA, and returned bus energy. A device already shorted cannot be interrupted by its gate command. Use the staged first-unit traveler only after the responsible engineer has set its energy/current/abort limits from the matched design and station.

Completion requires the actual detector/logic/loaded PERMIT waveform and a physical fault circuit, followed by semiconductor stress and bus-energy analysis on the same-time shutdown state. The new commutation cases are a reusable endpoint of that chain, not a replacement for its missing upstream and energy portions.
