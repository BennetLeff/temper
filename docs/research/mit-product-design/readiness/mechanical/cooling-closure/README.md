# Contact-bearing cooling candidate for the first prototype

2026-10-04. **Cold mockup candidate; integrated powered cooling is not closed.** The new STEP connects the modeled five-device backs to a common sink through explicit 1 mm ceramic allocations. It preserves the R4 exterior and front controls, the 240 × 160 mm board outline, device positions and mains-left/coil-right orientation. It is more specific than the previous disconnected rear-sink box, but supplier thermal curves, real clamp geometry/force, electrical insulation and the complete airflow path remain release blockers. Corrected manufacturer drawing bounds place the generic MOS rear face inside the possible interval, with −0.345..+0.310 mm signed variation at the fixed interface: either interference or gap is possible. The common rigid interfaces are not ready to machine. The initial half-lead-thickness sign error is withdrawn in the [drawing erratum](contact-plane-erratum.md).

The reviewable inputs and replay adapter are [interface.json](../../../../../../zapote/power-stage-120v/prototype-closure/cooling/interface.json) and [build_study.py](../../../../../../zapote/power-stage-120v/prototype-closure/cooling/build_study.py). Generated [STEP](../../../../../../output/temper-prototype-closure/cooling/contact-bearing-cold-study.step) and [checks](../../../../../../output/temper-prototype-closure/cooling/checks.json) belong to this study, not the canonical R4 assembly. Use [candidate parts and closure work](parts-and-validation.md), [assembly and retention](assembly.md), and [control coupon](control-coupon.md) with it.

## Geometry that changed

R4 coordinates are X left/right, Y front/rear, Z upward, millimetres. Move the complete board to X −110.5..129.5, Y 162..322; top copper datum Z=32 and underside Z=30.347. No individual power device or PCB trace moves in this study. A custom 200 mm long sink occupies X −112..88, Y 94.515..162.515, Z 12..72. Its 8 mm base is vertical, facing the transistor backs; the fins project toward the front. One 60 × 60 × 25 mm fan at the right end pushes toward the left. The short catalog 153 mm sink was not selected: the modeled body/contact span is 150.3 mm, leaving only 2.7 mm total before insulator edge and hardware allowances. The original 305 mm two-fan example leaves only 5.5 mm per side inside R4, without usable end-turn plenums.

The generic KiCad TO-247 model has rear planar faces at local board Y=1.515 for all four devices, world Y=163.515. Each has two coplanar faces totaling approximately 119.62 mm². This is a **library-model face observation**, not verified Infineon metallization or the 151.2 mm² thermal area assumed in D18. BR1's provisional box rear face is at local Y=2.2, world Y=164.2. A modeled 0.685 mm raised land plus the same 1 mm ceramic connects BR1; that machining dimension must not be released from a provisional box. The corrected bridge interval is local Y=1.300..1.800, implying −0.900..−0.400 mm signed interference against the fixed model interface under the straight-lead assumptions. The actual Infineon p12 drawing gives A1=2.20..2.60 mm and lead thickness c=0.38..0.89 mm; the rear-to-lead-center distance is A1+c/2, or 2.390..3.045 mm. The generic model’s 2.7 mm offset lies inside that interval; the drawing does not prove it is the actual assembled value. Neither model establishes the actual seated-height tolerance or contact finish. Manufacturer drawings and a physical package-to-board fixture must establish those datums before machining.

The base now has a 200 × 2.015 × 6 mm rear-face board-height recess (Y 160.5..162.515, Z 28..34) so the sink does not cut through the PCB edge. It leaves 5.985 mm of continuous base so the sink is not severed. This is a manufacturability concept for supplier review; it changes base stiffness/spreading and is not included in a catalog thermal curve. The recess's 1.5 mm nominal board gap does not establish an electrical clearance rating. A new insulating chamber remains necessary: the historical covered PCB tray and chamber lid are explicitly removed, along with the old board/cooling allocations. All front lids, controls, coil support, sensor and exterior solids remain included.

The four-shoe cold PCB carrier uses the left edge and one front stop as its in-plane datums, with 1 mm nominal float at the right and rear edges. Its 3 × 6 mm contact rectangles avoid the copper rejected at the former right-rear proposal. The removable caps, outside-board M3 fastener envelopes and carrier rails are included in the STEP. Final native-19 copper screens, real material/thermal-growth tolerances, vertical capture adjustment, handling loads and rail-to-chassis attachment remain open; see [assembly and retention](assembly.md).

An EMI **body allocation**, X −140..−30, Y 335..415, Z 12..62, represents the current 110 × 80 × 50 mm / 8 W D22 module. It does not include its upstream fuse/holder/rocker, terminals, wiring bends or insulating cover. Its ≤60°C local choke ambient is unproven; route sink exhaust separately.

## What was actually verified

CadQuery 2.6.1/OpenCascade imported all 135 native-18 component models, the bare board, and 250 retained R4 named parts. The final study has **zero positive-volume cooling/PCB or new-part/retained-R4 intersections** and exports/reimports 539 valid solids. Native-18 board hash is `fb113d95819f1ea7cd8c27f929f7e88308eff44b663af85f71cd6f2bca5a0002`; R4 assembly hash is `2e89923f65e466831395a10a2ac7e307ded48fbb8b49e5c19e87b4da1df21291`. The base and all 20 fins also fuse into one valid connected solid. A negative replay of the original full-depth cut fails that guard. The receipt hashes every imported model/part and the adapter/input data.

The first study found nine intersections with front-lid parts and six cooling/PCB intersections. They were addressed explicitly: move the board/sink 12 mm farther rearward, cut the board-height base relief, reduce the hypothetical MOS clamp projection from 12 to 8 mm, and reserve only 2 mm projection for a bespoke bridge clamp before C2. **Shrinking an allocation is not verification that a commercial clip fits.** Real clip drawings must fit those spaces; otherwise use the screw-mount alternative or change the layout after reviewing parasitics. A subsequent structural check replaced the original full-depth slot, which would have severed the base, with the shallow rear-face recess. No collision was hidden by omitting front-lid parts or capacitors.

This is nominal geometry, not a tolerance/insulation test. Twenty-four board models are provisional; the HOT5 additions and revised R5 package/copper in native-19 are absent. Reconcile its final inventory and board hash before claiming combined fit. The 135-model evidence must not be relabeled native-19. Unchanged copper preserves some board-only FEM inputs, but moving the board beneath the coil and adding a nearby grounded sink/chassis changes environmental magnetic and capacitive coupling. Installed EMI and coil/sink proximity need fresh analysis and correlation.

## Stage supported

An unpowered, full-size package/contact/retention mockup with real selected samples. No powered integrated release and no claim that the custom sink achieves ≤0.15°C/W or 20 CFM. The next irreversible drawing choices are the package metal-face datums, clamp stack and complete duct. The comparison remains anchored to folded R4: manufacturing-r2 predates the retained flush-front and board integration, and its old internal cooling arrangement supplies no missing proof for these parts. This study does not silently replace either package.
