# Cold click-cartridge fixture and measurement

A new [STEP holding plate](../../../../../../output/temper-prototype-closure/cooling/click-cartridge-cold-fixture.step) supports the actual R4 removable switch cartridge in a force/displacement test stand. It is **not** a corrected production click mechanism. [Replay source](../../../../../../zapote/power-stage-120v/prototype-closure/cooling/build_control_coupon.py) and [geometry receipt](../../../../../../output/temper-prototype-closure/cooling/click-fixture-checks.json) accompany it.

Machine one 40 × 30 × 5 mm flat plate for an unpowered bench fixture. Four Ø3.4 mm through holes at (±15,±10) fasten it to the test stand; two blind M1.4 × 0.3 threads at (±4.8,0), 3 mm pilot depth, retain the existing 12 × 8 × 0.6 mm cartridge seat. The STEP contains Ø1.1 mm pilot holes, not modeled threads. The shop confirms tapping allowance and marks the plate “COLD TEST FIXTURE”. Deburr both seat faces and verify their flatness before interpreting displacement. Use screws that do not bottom and a supported real electrical switch PCB; a CAD seat alone is not a routed circuit board. This fixture loads the board through its seat and does not establish the product's final stop load path.

Use the exact C&K PTS645SH43SMTR92 LFS, manufacturer alias Y97HT23B4EAFP. The existing source review gives electrical stroke 0.15–0.40 mm, operating force 1.5–2.5 N and installed-height tolerance ±0.1 mm. The sheet does not establish maximum safe overtravel or abuse force. The proposed product's 0.44–0.52 mm delivered stroke remains **unapproved**. Do not run a fixed 0.52 mm stroke or an overtravel life test merely because a fixture can move that far.

First measure closure/release and rest-open behavior with a calibrated displacement/force stand and continuity logging. Reference displacement to the **actual actuator's rest position**, not to nominal package height. Set an independent travel/force stop on the test ram from the manufacturer-approved limits before exercising travel beyond closure. A force gauge range or the normal actuation force is not a safe-force limit. If closure is absent within the specified electrical-travel range, stop and inspect setup/part identity rather than pressing farther to obtain a result.

Record at least five independently seated/reseated specimens across available lots for initial characterization; this is a chosen engineering sample, not capability or life qualification. Keep serial/lot identity and measurement uncertainty with every curve. Record closure, release, peak force to closure, rest-open state and reseating shift. Use those observations to gauge the actual cartridge gap and stop budget. Then obtain the supplier's safe stroke/force/preload/side-load limits before accepting both inequalities from [the prior knob review](../knob.md): minimum motion closes with margin; maximum motion/force remains safe. Thermal and life conditions are still set by the intended product and selected materials.

The production mechanism must carry abuse/stop force through carriage/frame instead of switch or solder joints. A force-limiting spring proposal still needs its own curve and the switch's limits. This cold holding fixture does not silently implement either a spring mechanism or a new production stop.

[Blank measurement record](click-coupon-record.csv) is intentionally NOT_RUN. No hardware exists in this task, so no displacement, force or durability result is claimed.
