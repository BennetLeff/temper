# US/Mexico product-safety and earthing basis — classification pending

This is an engineering dossier for qualified safety review. It is **not** a declaration of conformity or a substitute for the purchased standard text. Temper's precise intended use, fixed versus portable installation, cord/plug, power rating, consumer versus professional market, radio functions and target countries must be fixed on the product specification before selecting a certification route or PCB spacing. The current R4 cooker appears countertop/portable in the CAD, but no standards classification has been approved.

These public scope pages support **candidate classification only**. They do not expose full clauses or give Temper's allowable creepage, clearance, touch current, leakage, protective conductor impedance, abnormal-operation outcome or accessible-surface temperature. A qualified reviewer must use licensed/current full standards, market deviations and the final material/voltage/pollution/insulation-class construction to derive numbers. The native-18 D5 **8.0 mm placement target** and laminate group IIIa-or-better conditional decision are design allocations, not a certification rule; verified CTI and real laminate order remain open.

## Earthing and insulation construction record

1. Draw a single physical diagram from cord L/N/PE and inlet/strain relief through chassis stud, direct accessible aluminum bond, sink bond and J6 branch. Keep the controller-ground to PE R38 functional link separate from protective-earth continuity. Identify every removable/painted/bonded joint and service operation. The approved source architecture has cord PE directly to the chassis stud with a branch to J6, and electrically insulated Q2/Q3/Q5/Q6 and BR1 on a PE-bonded sink.
2. Mark hazardous-live, accessible conductive, SELV/control, coil/tank and PE domains on the *native-18* board and whole assembly. Include PCB edges, mounting screws, heatsink/insulator puncture paths, coil studs and cables, fan supply, glass moisture/soil paths, probe port and R4 bond hardware. The old `pcb/temper.kicad_pcb` distances and R4 imported PCB placement are historical.
3. Select actual cord/inlet, protective conductor gauge/color/termination, stud/lug/washer/locking sequence, coating removal or plated contact method, metal thickness and corrosion system. Provide torque and inspection access without removing a safety barrier in normal use. Define first-build continuity and service-reassembly checks with instruments, uncertainty and market-derived limit.
4. For each insulation barrier, record working voltage/waveform (including high-frequency tank stress), transient environment, insulation class, CTI material proof, component certification scope, pollution/contamination, solder/assembly variation, mechanical retention and measured worst-case geometry. DRC 0 under a provisional rule is not the final clause check.
5. Plan dielectric/touch-current/protective-bond, abnormal operation (including shorted switch, fan stall, blocked vents, sensor loss and spill), accessible temperature, mechanical impact and post-aging tests using the selected product standard. Establish power limits and qualified station before any energized prototype test. Capture traceable samples and failure analysis, not merely a pass word.

**Current gate:** native-18 has bounded connectivity/placement/DRC evidence and an 8 mm conditional layout target; R4 has corrected its historical-board compartment collision in nominal CAD, but current-board integration and the PE stud process remain unselected. The required construction drawing, material certificate, market classification and physical tests are missing. Safety/PCB owners can close the design basis on paper, but only qualified test of the final assembly can close compliance.
