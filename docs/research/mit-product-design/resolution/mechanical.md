# R4 mechanical closure and remaining qualification

The R4 flush-front package was copied from the existing local package before edits. The original remains unchanged. This change closes its ten **nominal** intersections between the front carrier/rear lid/hardware and the inherited PCB chamber, while retaining the flush 108 × 38 mm lens, three flush buttons, actual selected display envelope, removable control-carrier lid, chamber walls and a continuous roof over the imported PCB. It does not establish fit for the current full-bridge electronics or physical protection.

## Geometry change and evidence

The source in `output/temper-flush-front-r4/src/reference/packaging.py` now trims the chamber's front ~34.5 mm down to a shallow roof parallel to the 35° control face. In pre-placement coordinates, the roof rises from z=49.35 mm at y=65.5 mm to z=73.5 mm at y=100 mm; the lower surface at the rear joins the original chamber lid at z=72 mm. Its existing front and side walls are trimmed to meet the new roof. The common `baseline_engine` -4 mm PCB/barrier correction places the corresponding front edge at y=61.5 mm in the complete cooker. The change is a provisional folded/sealed enclosure allocation, not a released flat pattern or construction detail.

The original saved `front-panel-validation.json` contained **10 front-to-chamber collision hits**. The rebuilt 406-part complete assembly STEP was exported and independently reimported as 434 valid solids. The updated `checks/front_panel.py` reports zero modeled panel/complete-cooker hits and zero panel/historical-PCB hits, including the display and button allocations. The historical imported PCB has zero overlap with the new tray and lid; minimum nominal CAD distances are 1.5 mm to the tray and 4.74 mm to the roof. These are geometric separation readings, not insulation or creepage limits. The former panel source itself did not require a change.

The suspected check at y=150 mm was **correct**: `reference/packaging.py` places the old board at y=154 mm and `baseline_engine.context_parts()` moves PCB/barrier parts -4 mm. The panel checker now uses `historical_pcb_world()` and compares all 149 transformed STEP solids with the saved assembly catalog. The largest bound difference from STEP/OCC representation is below 0.1 mm, while an erroneous 154 mm final placement differs by 4 mm. This correspondence check prevents a future false clearance from a stale transform.

The control lid cannot be pulled directly rearward through the assembled cooker: its nominal motion first intersects the PCB chamber roof at 5 mm travel. The plausible service order is to isolate power, remove the outer/top cover with its attached controls, then open the exposed control lid. At +1, +5 and +26 mm vertical cover/panel travel, the saved formed cover, carrier and rear lid have zero nominal overlap with the chamber, its roof, or the historical PCB. Once removed from the cooker, the existing isolated-cassette lid-removal check passes. This is a **sampled rigid path**; a service mockup must still confirm hand/tool access, harness disconnect, cable bend and seal renewal after opening. The rear control lid should not be described as directly accessible in the assembled cooker.

## Current-board integration boundary

The board imported by R4 is historical `inputs/current-pcb.step` (SHA-256 `7b5206e0b4f035f4042f4a9857e9b5a96dfb11977f8de97572c3598688e799a2`). Despite its filename it is not the current power stage. The active `ps-oracle/.../native-18/section.kicad_pcb` Edge.Cuts polygon is 240 × 160 mm and D18 reserves a shared sink class about 305 × 60 × 68 mm plus fans and a dedicated duct. Its nominal board rectangle might fit within the historical chamber's 265 × 177 mm plan allocation in one orientation, but mounting, copper/edge insulation, component heights, connectors, harness, separate protection and heat removal cannot be inferred from plan dimensions. A 305 mm sink cannot fit within the 265 mm chamber width; the current system needs a revision-matched mechanical/electrical/thermal packaging model. Do not reuse these R4 historical fit results to accept native-18.

## Two-sided human-interface and seal budgets

The inherited knob switch still has stop `0.55 ± 0.05 mm`, air gap `0.20 ± 0.10 mm`, and switch trip `0.15–0.40 mm`. The declared travel at stop is `[0.20, 0.50] mm`; at one legal corner the switch needs 0.40 mm but only gets 0.20 mm, a **-0.20 mm actuation margin** before any required positive margin. At the opposite corner, travel after an early 0.15 mm trip can reach 0.35 mm. A supplier maximum safe stroke/force is unavailable, so neither actuation nor damage avoidance can be accepted. Choose the real switch and controlled datums/stop, then prove both `minimum delivered travel ≥ maximum trip + margin` and `maximum delivered travel/force ≤ supplier safe limit` through tolerance, thermal, load and reassembly corners. The knob detent's radial preload test does not close the axial click path.

The sensor face seal's existing dimensional interval is 17.24–29.03% squeeze for 1.45–1.55 mm section and 1.10–1.20 mm gland height. This is only geometric cold squeeze. The selected elastomer's allowable squeeze, compression set at hot/oil exposure, sensor contact force/stroke, glass load path and leakage after cycling remain unknown. R4's flush lens perimeter 0.4 mm joint, 0.3 mm bond, membrane nominal 20% compression, concealed studs and OLED closed-housing temperature similarly need selected materials, stack corners and physical tests. Define independent glass support and liquid barriers so button actuation, cookware load and fastener torque cannot silently set seal pressure. Test cold/hot/reassembled extremes with a stuck or noncontacting sensor while pan detection remains true.

## Release boundary

This is a digital geometry closure for the inherited R4 arrangement. The front chamber's sloped fold, roof-to-lid seam, fabrication method, service tools, barrier insulation, bonding, glass retention, button force/overtravel, seal ageing, thermal behavior and current-board fit still require supplier choices and physical validation. The CAD package remains prototype-only. No energized or physical hardware was available in this task.
