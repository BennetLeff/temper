# Native-21 power stage: bench bring-up and commissioning

This procedure takes the integrated SELV controller (`zapote/controller`) and native-21
(`zapote/power-stage-120v`) from first power to the point where firmware may set
`commissioned=true`. It is written for the power board's **board of record, native-21 stage 53** (board SHA-256
`82af7bd93e62…`, copper key `b7a0bc5790b9`; DECISIONS 75). Neither board has been built or powered yet.

Every board-specific number is in the **board table** below (rows B1–B17, J4, probe points, ceiling ladder). It is
generated from the stage 53 sources by `firmware/tools/commissioning_board_table.py`, and CI fails when this file and the
board disagree, so the steps cite its rows instead of repeating the numbers. Every other number is cited to its source or
marked **provisional**, and the bench measurement replaces it. The controller is the **J13-merge board** (controller
DECISIONS C49 / C50 on branch `controller-j13-merge`, power-stage DECISIONS 79, firmware `0550fffcf`): its logic 15 V
comes in on J13, J4.1 is the power board's BIAS_GOOD read on GPIO35, and RAIL_OK_RB is on TCA9555 P02 (ground rules,
"Controller revision"). The firmware matches only that controller.

The firmware pieces are:
- `n21_signal.{c,h}` and `n21_binding.{c,h}`: the logic, host-tested by `firmware/test/test_n21_binding.c`.
- `tank_freq.{c,h}`: the commissioned tank ceiling, the trips derived from it, the no-pan clamp and the per-pan frequency loop (stage 15, DECISIONS 10), host-tested by `firmware/test/test_tank_freq.c` against a first-harmonic RLC tank.
- `n21_hw_esp32.c`: the ESP32-S3 peripheral code.
- `Kconfig`: the switches and calibration fields.

## Ground rules

- **Default is off.** `CONFIG_TEMPER_POWER_HW_BINDING=n` leaves the power service unbound. With the binding on, the default mode is `INHIBITED`: PWM pads are held low and acquisition runs for telemetry only. Nothing in this document changes a default in the repository. Bench settings live in a local `sdkconfig`, never in `sdkconfig.defaults`.
- **Firmware never asserts PERMIT.** PERMIT (J4.9) comes only from the interlock latch. Firmware can only request a reset edge on IL_RESET_N (GPIO17), through `n21_request_interlock_reset(…, operator_confirmed=true)`.
  - That call is refused while PWM is live, while any interlock source reads faulted, or within 1 s of the previous request.
  - It is never called from a fault, sample or tick path.
- **Faults latch until MCU reset.** Any input fault, or a stale sample, makes `sample()` return false. `power_service` then forces the pads low and trips; it does not rebind. Hardware trips latch PERMIT independently and need the operator reset.
- **Isolation.** Every measurement on the HOT side uses isolated or differential probes. Use the isolation transformer for every AC step, and the power analyser in the inlet path.
- **Never weaken a check to make a step pass.** If a step needs a lower trip to demonstrate it, lower it in the bench `sdkconfig` only, and restore it before the next step.
- **Board revision.** Everything here is for stage 53 (board table). Before a board is powered, confirm its fitted parts against B1, B2, B9, B13 and B14 (the bank part and its fit mark) and enter them in the record. A board of any other stage needs its own table, rendered from that board's sources; nothing here transfers. Two known traps on earlier boards: before s50 BIAS_GOOD is high at idle (no option P), so step 2 item 6's check 1 latches `BIAS GOOD STUCK`; before s49 the OCP divider and band differ from B2–B5.
- **Bring-up power plan: BR1 out until step 4 passes** (owner decision 2026-10-18). PS2's output + is wired straight to V24_RAW, with no series diode (B18), and back-feeding an unpowered MEAN WELL output is unqualified. BR1 is the only part joining mains (l_filt / n_filt) to the bus (bus_p / hv_ret; B18). So the bring-up boards leave BR1 unfitted (the JLC order deselects it, as RV1), PS2 runs from its own input in steps 2–4, and the owner fits BR1 in step 4b.
  - **Mains is on the HOT input side from step 2:** the inlet, l_filt / n_filt, F2 / J11, the J3 loop and PS2's input. Use isolated or differential probes only, everywhere, and keep the hand-operable mains cut in reach.
  - **Reference:** with BR1 out, the bus and the HOT domain have no galvanic path to mains; in steps 3–4 they float on the isolated DC supply. Tie them to PE at one point, a single labelled lead from hv_ret (the B19 hv_ret lead, which is also the DC supply's negative) to the bench PE stud, in steps 2–4, so the HOT domain sits at PE instead of floating on PS2's and the supply's leakage. Make no other HOT-to-PE or HOT-to-scope-ground connection. **Remove the tie before BR1 is fitted** (step 4b): with BR1 in, hv_ret is at mains potential and the tie is a line-to-earth short.
  - The DC supply goes onto the bus at the B19 leads (the board has no bus test pad): solder short tails to the two through-hole leads before power instead of clipping on. They are the bus_p / hv_ret pair on one part that sits farthest from any other net's pad, live mains included.
- **Mains cut.** A bench unit always runs behind an external, hand-operable series mains cut. Nothing on the board opens mains (`08-manufacturing-package/native-21/PROTOTYPE-LOT.md`, bench plan).
- **Controller revision: the C49 (J13-merge) board only** (controller DECISIONS C49 / C50, branch `controller-j13-merge`; power-stage DECISIONS 79; `firmware/README.md` "Fitted (2026-10-18; J13 rail merge)").
  - Its logic rail `v15_selv` is fed from J13 (`V15_BLW`) through D9 (SS14); in the product PS3 feeds J13.
  - J4.1 is the power board's BIAS_GOOD output (board table J4, B15), read high-Z on GPIO35 (`CTRL_GPIO_BIAS_GOOD_RB`) through controller R124 10 k, with C97 1 nF C0G and controller R123 1 MΩ to `selv_gnd` at the pin. **J4.1 never sees 15 V.**
  - RAIL_OK_RB is TCA9555 P02 (`CTRL_EXP_RAIL_OK_RB`), read from the expander snapshot with a freshness check, and latched in hardware as RAIL_FAULT.
  - The C48 controller (J4.1 its 15 V input, RAIL_OK on GPIO35) does not match this firmware and is never connected to a stage-53 board (C50). C50's accepted bench risks are checked in steps 2 and 8c: GPIO35 and D9 have not been on hardware, and a blower fault that drives PS3 into hiccup resets the controller.
- **J4 breakout.** The J4 harness runs through an inline breakout that brings out every pin and can open each line; the J4.1 / J4.9 / J4.10 manipulations below are made there. The power-board-only items (step 2 items 1 and 5, the B8 / H2 levels of item 6, and step 3) may also run with the controller unplugged from the breakout: J4.3 from a bench 3.30 V (inside J4.3's 3.135–3.465 V contract, power-stage README), current-limited to 0.2 A (**provisional**: there is no SELV-side current budget on record), J4.9 driven to v3v3 through 1 kΩ, and J4.10–J4.14 to the scope.

## Modes and what flips `commissioned=true`

| Kconfig | Effect |
|---|---|
| `TEMPER_POWER_MODE_INHIBITED` (default) | Pads low; no binding; acquisition telemetry available |
| `TEMPER_POWER_MODE_BENCH` | `n21_bench_start(ms, confirmed)` runs bounded (≤ 60 s) live-PWM sessions without the adapter. Every acquisition fault check and the capture-timing check (same window as `fullbridge_apply`) stop the session. Never ship |
| `TEMPER_POWER_MODE_COMMISSIONED` | `n21_binding_init` builds `bridge_config_t` with `commissioned=true` and the power service binds. **Only** if all three conditions below hold; otherwise it returns `N21_INIT_NOT_CALIBRATED` or `N21_INIT_NO_RUN_GATE` and the pads stay low |

The three conditions for `commissioned=true`:
1. `CONFIG_TEMPER_POWER_CALIBRATED=y`, set in the same reviewed change that enters the commissioning record (end of this document);
2. the hardware has a firmware run gate (open question **H1**). Controller layout L2 adds it (RUN_EN on GPIO14, PERMIT = latch AND RUN_EN through controller U37, DECISIONS C23), and `n21_hw_esp32.c` now passes `run_gate` on it. On an earlier controller capture without U37 the gate does nothing, so do not select COMMISSIONED there;
3. steps 1–9 below have passed, with their evidence filed.

**When:** only after step 10's sign-off. Burst mode stays disabled (`burst_enabled=false`) after that too. Enabling it is a separate gate (DECISIONS 2026-10-06: LINE_ZC scheduler, F6 capture, B2).

## Step 0: build gate (desk)

1. Run the host suite. Every test must pass, including `n21_binding_tests`.
   ```sh
   cmake -S firmware/test -B build-host
   cmake --build build-host
   ctest --test-dir build-host
   ```
2. Build with ESP-IDF v5.2.x, `CONFIG_TEMPER_POWER_HW_BINDING=y`, mode INHIBITED. `n21_hw_esp32.c` has so far only been syntax-checked against stub headers; this is its first real compile. Fix any API drift without changing the logic in `n21_binding.c`.
3. **H3** is resolved for layout L2: the RTD service uses `controller_pins.h` (SCK 11, SDI 13, SDO 12, CS 10, mode 3) and samples DRDY from TCA9555 P07. Run `python3 firmware/tools/check_controller_pinmap.py` before flashing; it checks the firmware map against the controller ato.

## Step 1: controller alone (no native-21)

Setup: a bench 15 V, current-limited to 1 A, into the controller's J13 (`V15_BLW`; D9 feeds the logic rail from it), with the blower (J10) unplugged. J4 goes to the breakout with 10 kΩ loads on the PWM lines and J4.1 open.
**Controller revision.** The C49 controller only (ground rules). The bench 15 V goes into J13 and **J4.1 must never see 15 V**: it is GPIO35's input (controller R124 10 k series, controller R123 1 MΩ and C97 1 nF to ground). Confirm before power that the board carries D9 next to J13 and the three parts beside U4 (C49 layout addendum).

1. **Rails:**
   - v3v3 is 3.242–3.367 V.
   - RAIL_OK reads high: TCA9555 P02 (`CTRL_EXP_RAIL_OK_RB`), read from the expander snapshot.
   - BIAS_GOOD_RB (GPIO35, `CTRL_GPIO_BIAS_GOOD_RB`) reads low with J4.1 open (the 1 MΩ pull-down; C49 `bias_good`: open reads low).
   - SUP_RUN_OK (TCA9555 P14 since R36, DECISIONS C27; read from the expander snapshot) reads high while the WDI (GPIO18) heartbeat runs. It drops within 0.9–1.6 s when the heartbeat stops (halt the control task).
2. **Pads low at every boot phase.** Scope all four PWM lines through reset, boot ROM, app start and `n21_hw_start`. Expected: low throughout, with no pulse over 0.8 V (D-25, controller misc checks). Repeat with the MCU held in reset and with it unpowered (10 kΩ pull-downs).
3. **Interlock with J4.10 open:**
   - BUS_FAULT_RB reads high (10 kΩ pull-up) and PERMIT_RB stays low.
   - `n21_request_interlock_reset(true)` is refused (bus_fault), and the IL_RESET_N scope shows no edge.
   - Tie J4.10 low on the breakout and request again: one falling edge, and PERMIT_RB goes high.
   - Lift the tie: PERMIT drops with no firmware action.
4. **Analog front ends.** These set the VBUS and CT_MON trims (enter them in the record):
   - **VBUS:** drive J4.11–J4.12 differentially with a calibrated source at `k·V`, with k from B11 (D-11). Use 0 V, k × 170 V and k × 198 V (1.417 V and 1.650 V at B11's k).
     - Expected readings: 247.8 / 956.2 / 1072.9 mV (`controller_calcs.d11_receiver`).
     - `TEMPER_POWER_VBUS_OFFSET_UV` is the 0 V reading minus 247.77 mV.
     - `TEMPER_POWER_VBUS_GAIN_PPM` is the ratio that maps 170 V exactly.
     - D-11 allows ±6 %.
   - **VBUS thresholds:** 95 mV equivalent must give "invalid", and 1215 mV, just above the over-range threshold (B11), the latched `N21_FAULT_VBUS_OVERRANGE`.
   - **CT_MON:** with J4.14 driven at 1.650 V DC, record the reading as `TEMPER_POWER_CT_MON_ZERO_MV`. With J4.14 open, the reading is < 50 mV ("unplugged").
   - **Tank RMS:** drive J4.14 with 1.650 V DC plus a 40 kHz sine of 0.330 V rms (22 A at B12's 15.0 mV/A).
     - Expected: telemetry `tank_rms_a` = 22.0 A and `v3v3` within 3.242–3.367 V.
     - Set `TEMPER_POWER_RMS_GAIN_PPM` from the error.
     - Repeat at 31 and 55 kHz; the error must be ≤ 1 % (AD8436 65 kHz figure, C19).
     - Step the sine on and off and measure settling to 0.1 %. It must be ≤ 3.4 ms (assumed in C19, bench item 5).
   - **SPI sharing:** run the MAX31865 conversion loop at the same time and confirm both devices read correctly for 10 min with no `N21_FAULT_RMS_CHANNEL` (mode 3, bus lock).
5. **ADC pattern (C12 flag):** check the logged sample rates: VBUS ≥ 20 kS/s with a gap ≤ 150 µs, and CT_MON 20 kS/s with the repeated-channel pattern on this IDF version.

## Step 2: native-21 logic powered, HV bus dead, gates disconnected

Setup:
- Native-21 SELV and HOT logic and gate supplies powered; **BR1 not fitted** (B18, the bring-up power plan in the ground rules). The inlet is fed at 120 V from the isolation transformer through the power analyser (no variac: PS2's input range is 80–264 VAC, MPM-20-SPEC p.2, datasheet ledger), so PS2 runs from its own input through the J3 loop with the bench TCO (DF110S on the bench sink, `zapote/power-stage-120v/validation-results/11-candidates/bench-sink-r1/README.md` D5). V24_RAW, V15_LS, HOT5 and the HS-A / HS-B bias (U41 / U42) come up from PS2 on the board. The DC bus is dead: nothing feeds bus_p / hv_ret with BR1 out; tie it through 10 kΩ across the B19 leads anyway, with the hv_ret–PE tie of the ground rules in place. First check +15 / −2 V on every domain at the TP pairs and the monitor windows (−2.206 / −1.799 V; PROTOTYPE-LOT.md bench plan item 2).
- **FET gates disconnected:** gate resistors not fitted, or the driver outputs lifted, and each driver output loaded with 10 nF to its source return.
- The controller is powered as in step 1 (the bench 15 V into J13, J10 unplugged), with the real J4 harness through the breakout. PS3 first feeds J13 at step 5's mains sequencing.
- Mode BENCH, `TEMPER_POWER_F_MAX_HZ=50000` (a test value, as in round 5; bench sessions run at F_MAX, the loop runs only in COMMISSIONED).

1. **Interlock:**
   - With all sources healthy, BUS_FAULT_RB reads low.
   - Request a reset (operator-confirmed); PERMIT goes high.
   - Open the J3 loop (unplug the TCO lead at J3; PS2 loses its input): V15_LS and HOT5 collapse, BUS_FAULT goes high and PERMIT drops (FAULT-INTERFACE.md). Record the latency from the V15_LS collapse (v15_ls pad) to DIS high (leg B DIS pad; leg A at the pad listed in the board table).
2. **PWM verification, PERMIT low** (drivers disabled by DIS):
   - Run `n21_bench_start(2000, true)`.
   - At the J4 pads, measure:
     - period 20.00 µs (1600 ticks at 80 MHz);
     - HA/LA and HB/LB complementary;
     - leg B 180° from leg A;
     - MCU non-overlap ≥ 200 ns (16 ticks) on all four transitions (DECISIONS 2026-10-03 O07). Record the measured value: the capture check accepts 16–18 ticks.
   - The driver outputs must stay low throughout (DIS).
   - The session must end at 2 s with the pads low and no fault.
3. **PWM verification, PERMIT high** (drivers enabled, gates still disconnected):
   - Repeat item 2 and measure at the driver outputs (into the 10 nF loads).
   - **Driver dead time** (2EDR8259X STP/DTC, B13): record the value per transition. **Pass:** inside B13's simulated window on every transition. A value outside B13's datasheet-table estimate but inside the window passes and goes to the record as an estimator finding (c25-driver).

## Step 6: tank current ramp with a pan

1. Raise the variac 108 → 120 → 132 V with nominal, low-R and high-R pans. Per point, record:
   - tank RMS (current probe through the coil lead) against telemetry `tank_rms_a`: within 2 % after the step 1 trim;
   - cos φ;
   - bus voltage;
   - tank and heatsink temperatures.
2. **Tank envelope at the commissioned ceiling** (17.5 A ships; power-stage OPEN-ITEMS S17, hot-copper B3 basis): the per-pan loop (step 9a) *regulates* the half-cycle tank RMS to the ceiling; the binding *trips* (`N21_FAULT_TANK_RMS`) at `tank_rms_trip_a` = 1.05 × ceiling **on the half-cycle RMS**, and on any single AD8436 sample above the crest guard 1.5 × ceiling (both in the ceiling ladder). The per-sample reading is the 120 Hz envelope crest, about 1.41 × the half-cycle RMS (round 6), so it is not the trip quantity.
   - Demonstrate the half-cycle trip with `tank_rms_trip_a` temporarily lowered in the bench config to 10 A, then restore it.
   - Record per pan and line case the frequency the loop settles at, the telemetry reason (`freq_reason`) and the half-cycle RMS.
3. **AD8436 envelope smoothing** (C19 open item): compare the per-half-cycle mean of telemetry against the current probe's 120 Hz envelope. The resulting error feeds step 7.
4. **Half-cycle RMS estimator and stuck-low check** (evidence `build-evidence/rms-estimator/`). The half-cycle RMS is time weighted (each 625 us reading counts for its share of the half, the end readings held to the zero crossings), which removes the slot-phase scatter of the old equal-weight mean (-3.1 .. +2.7 % in the host model, now -0.03 .. +0.27 %). The trip, ceiling and loop thresholds are unchanged. A tank-RMS reading below `max|CT_MON| / (1.20 x 2.17 = 2.60; wide because at the ADC lock frequencies the CT_MON samples sit on the same carrier phases every half and can hit the crest six halves running)` for 6 consecutive halves while the bridge runs (CT_MON peak >= 6 A) latches `N21_FAULT_RMS_CHANNEL`. Bench check: with the AD8436 OUT pin shorted to ground at 4 to 10 A the bridge must inhibit within about 60 ms; with a healthy chain, record the largest `last_half.ct_abs_max_a / last_half.est.tank_rms_a` seen over 31-55 kHz and all pans. It must stay below 2.60 (the latch), and a value above 2.17 still means `TANK_PEAK_PER_RMS` is too low. A real ratio above 2.17 means `TANK_PEAK_PER_RMS` itself is too low (it also sets the peak trip), not that this check should be loosened.

**Step 6.1a (optional comparison bench, never ships): shadow RMS.** Build with `CONFIG_TEMPER_POWER_SHADOW_RMS=y` (Kconfig, default off). The firmware then computes a second half-cycle tank RMS from the raw CT_MON ADC1 samples, on an ADC clock re-selected from 72.1-80 kS/s so the samples never phase-lock to the carrier (40 kHz and k x 8.889 kHz at the as-built 80 kS/s), and logs it once a second next to the AD8436 value (`tank rms: ad8436 .. A, shadow .. A`), also in `n21_telemetry_t.shadow_*`. It never feeds a limit or trip. Compare it with the AD8436 and a current probe over 0-70 C and 31-55 kHz; this is the bench check DECISIONS C35 asks for before the AD8436 chain is deleted. Each clock re-selection restarts the ADC1 stream for about one frame, and a stale snapshot inhibits the bridge, so expect an occasional inhibit on this build and do not use it for anything else.

## Step 7: inlet-current estimator error (sets e_est and the hold)

This step qualifies the firmware estimate that replaces a line-current sensor (DECISIONS 2026-10-08, "input current ≤ 15 A RMS", option (a)).

1. **Instruments:** a true-RMS power analyser (≥ 0.2 % basic accuracy, bandwidth ≥ 100 kHz) at the inlet. A separate measurement of the auxiliary current I_aux with the bridge idle at each line voltage.
2. **Grid:** line 108 / 120 / 132 V × pans (low-R, nominal, high-R; cold and at temperature) × at least 3 power points up to the highest power that stays ≤ 15 A at the inlet.
3. **Per point, over ≥ 10 s:**
   - I_line (analyser true RMS);
   - I_aux;
   - telemetry `last_half.est.i_ps_a` (the unmargined estimate), averaged over the same window;
   - `p_inv_w` and the analyser's input power, which also checks `eta_min = 0.93`.
4. **Error:** e = (I_line − I_aux) / i_ps_a − 1 at each point. Only under-estimation is unsafe. Take e_est = max(0, max e) + the analyser's uncertainty, rounded up to 0.5 %.
   - If any point shows efficiency below 0.93, lower `eta_min` instead of folding it into e_est.
   - If e_est > 0.5 the estimator is unusable (`input_limits_config_ok` rejects it), and the board needs a hardware line-current sense (DECISIONS option (b)).
5. **Set** `TEMPER_POWER_EST_ERROR_PPM = e_est × 10⁶`. The hold is then (13.5 − I_aux) / (1 + e_est) and the trip, where the bound reaches 15 A, (15 − I_aux) / (1 + e_est). Since stage 16, I_aux is scheduled with the blower (step 8c): 0.31 A with the blower off (11.99 / 13.36 A at the provisional 10 %), 0.65 A at typical blower (11.69 A hold), 0.887 A at full blower or whenever the drive is unknown (11.47 / 12.83 A). Recompute them and record them.
   - I_aux in this step is the firmware's scheduled allowance at the blower state of each point (`blower_aux_level_a()`), NOT the auxiliaries' own RMS measured alone: they add to the line RMS only by their increment (temper-aux-load-r1), so subtracting their stand-alone RMS would understate e. Run the grid with the blower off and again at full blower; temper-aux-load-r1 `BENCH-RMS.md` is the same check at the hold.
6. **Re-run** the grid's highest-current points with the new value. Confirm that `inlet_bound_a` ≥ the analyser's I_line at every point.

## Step 8b: controller L3 thermal and blower (firmware side of C28 / C32)

Firmware: `components/control/thermal_mgmt.{c,h}` (host-tested by `test/test_thermal_mgmt.c`), glue in `main/thermal_io.c` and `components/hal/esp32/controller_board_esp32.c`. Decisions recorded here (coordinator, 2026-10-12):

## Step 8c: blower supply PS3 inlet current against blower drive (replaces the model constants)

Stage 16 (power-stage DECISIONS 16) feeds the blower from PS3 on the mains tap F2 / J11; since s38 PS3 is a Mean Well MPM-65-15ST that also carries the controller logic (J13 merge), PS1 is deleted and PS2 is an MPM-20-24. The inlet estimate counts the auxiliaries by their increment to the inlet RMS at the hold point (temper-aux-load-r1 `aux_load.py` → `aux-load.json`), through an allowance scheduled with the commanded blower drive (`components/power/blower_aux.{c,h}`, host-tested by `test/test_blower_aux.c`):
- I_aux = PS2 + PS3 logic (0.26 A static, `aux_min_a`; worst-corner increment 0.254 A) + PS3 blower(drive).
- PS3 blower(drive) = P_blower / η_buck / (η_PS3 × PF_eff × 108 V), with P_blower = 32.4 W × (V / 12 V)³ (Delta's maximum at 12 V, fan law), V the C28 drive map's upper envelope, η_buck 0.90, η_PS3 0.875 (MPM-65-SPEC 2024-10-30 p.2, 92.5 % typ. − 5 points) and PF_eff 0.67 (the effective power factor of the inlet-RMS increment, its lowest over duty and line).
- Below full speed the allowance adds 15 % plus 0.05 A.
- Full speed, unknown drive, failed blower write, stall, boot and an unconfirmed off all use the worst case, 0.887 A (modelled 0.882 A).
- Speed-ups take the allowance first (4 A/s, then 300 ms in force before the blower moves; the binding ramps it 0.05 A per half). A fault goes to full speed at once.

All of it is PROVISIONAL. This step replaces the model with measurements.

Setup:
- Bridge idle (no power request), so the inlet carries PS2 / PS3 only (steps 1–4); the increment at the hold point is checked in temper-aux-load-r1 `BENCH-RMS.md`; the inlet fed from the variac through the power analyser (true RMS, ≥ 0.2 %, bandwidth ≥ 100 kHz).
- A bench-config hook forces the blower drive (fan law bypassed); log `V_BLOWER` (J10), the 15 V rail at J13, the tach rpm and the duty.

Procedure:
1. **Baseline:** blower off with FAN_OFF confirmed (tach 0): I_PS2+PS3 at 108 / 120 / 132 V. This sets `base_a` + `idle_a`.
2. **Sweep:** drive 0 (duty 0.85), 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.8 and 1.0 (duty 0), each settled ≥ 30 s, at 108 / 120 / 132 V. Record the true-RMS inlet current, real power, PF, V_BLOWER and rpm. Do it on two blowers if available (Delta's maximum vs typical spread).
3. **Model check:** at each point, the scheduled allowance (`blower_aux_level_a()`, printed by `test_blower_aux`) must be ≥ the measured auxiliary current. Report the worst ratio.
4. **Replace the constants** in `blower_aux_native21()` from the data, keeping each one conservative:
   - `vout_max_v` / `vout_slope_min_v`: the measured V_BLOWER envelope against duty;
   - `fan_law_exp` and `p_rated_max_w`: a fit of P_blower against V_BLOWER, through the highest unit; if the exponent fits below 3, use the fitted value (a lower exponent gives a larger allowance at partial drive);
   - `ps3_eta × ps3_pf`: P_out / (108 V × the measured inlet-RMS increment above the blower-off point, at the hold), taking the lowest product over load;
   - `idle_a` from step 1, and `margin` reduced only to cover the unit-to-unit spread seen.
   Re-run `test_blower_aux`; update its pinned numbers and the hold table in DECISIONS.
5. **Step-up on the bench (COMMISSIONED, after 9a):** at the inlet hold on the 120 V nominal pan, force the fan law from off to 0.8 drive, then a fault (full speed at once). With the analyser at 10 ms resolution, the inlet stays ≤ 13.5 A on the normal step and < 15 A on the fault step, with no `freq_reason` other than UP_INLET / HOLD_LIMIT and no adapter trip.
6. **Blower start (G-B1):** at a full-voltage enable, record PS3's output and input peak, and confirm no MPM-65 hiccup.
7. **BLW_PG (expander P16, controller C33) and PS3 loss.** On the C49 controller J13 also carries the logic 15 V, so "PS3 absent" means no controller: PERMIT and the PWM fail low with it (the power board's R7 / R15 pull permit_gate down, and the drivers' VDDI is v3v3, so input UVLO holds both outputs low; `CONTROLLER-REQUEST.md` item 3). The two "unplug J13" items of the C48 procedure become PS3-loss checks.
   - With PS3 on, P16 reads 1 and the diagnosis is clear.
   - Unplug J13 (PS3 output) with the bridge idle, then again during a bench session: the controller powers down, the pads and PERMIT go low, DIS goes high, and nothing restarts on its own when J13 is replugged (the controller boots to IDLE). Scope v3v3, J4.9 and leg B DIS; record the time from the unplug to DIS high.
   - **Blower start and PS3 hiccup (C50 accepted risk 1; `CONTROLLER-REQUEST.md` item 5):** scope the logic rail (`v15_selv` after D9) and v3v3 during item 6's blower start and at the PS3 low corner. ## Step 9a: per-pan frequency loop and no-pan clamp (COMMISSIONED, needs H1)

The loop (`tank_freq.h`) starts at F_MAX (60 kHz), steps down 250 Hz per closed half line cycle while every limit holds with 2 % to spare, holds in the band, and retreats (1 kHz, or 250 Hz for a marginal excess) when one is exceeded, blocking that frequency for 1 s. Limits: lag ≥ 45° and f ≥ 1.05 f_res (ZVS), f ≥ F_MIN, half-cycle RMS ≤ effective ceiling, inlet bound ≤ `inlet_target_a`, peak estimate ≤ peak trip − 3 A. A missing CT_ZC lag or a stale half steps up. Each live period change goes through `pwm_update` (MCPWM shadow registers, loaded at timer zero) and is ≤ 1 kHz per control tick.

1. **Live retiming (HIL, first):** 120 V variac, nominal pan, isolated probes on both switch nodes and all four gate commands. Force a 1 kHz step (bench config `step_up_hz`) down and up at 45 kHz and at 36 kHz, 20 times each, with persistence on. Pass: no truncated or merged period, MCU non-overlap ≥ 200 ns on every edge, ZVS kept on every edge of the transition period. Confirm `pwm_program` still starts the timer at the written period (`update_period_on_empty`).
2. **Descent:** at 120 V per pan class, log `freq_hz`, `floor_hz`, `f_res_hz`, `q`, `freq_reason` and the half RMS from gate-on to settle. Pass: monotone descent from 60 kHz, settle within 2 s, no trip, ZVS on the scope at the settled point, and `f_res_hz` within 3 % of step 4.3's measured resonance where a fit exists.
3. **Each limit, demonstrated:** with bench-lowered limits one at a time (ceiling via `TANK_CEILING_DA`=150, inlet via `inlet_target_a`, peak via `tank_peak_trip_a`), the loop settles on that limit (`freq_reason` HOLD_LIMIT / UP_x) without tripping. Remove CT_ZC (lift the lead at low power): `N21_FAULT_CT_ZC_STALE` latches above 2 A.
4. **Pan-detect tuning (no-pan clamp):** for the empty coil and every pan class (cold and hot), record at the settled point the telemetry `q` and p_inv / I² (`last_half.est.p_inv_w / tank_rms_a²`). Set `TEMPER_POWER_PAN_Q_MAX_X10` and `TEMPER_POWER_PAN_R_MIN_MOHM` between the empty-coil and the lowest-R pan values with ≥ 30 % margin each side. **Provisional:** Q ≤ 10, R ≥ 0.8 Ω (empty coil ~0.12–0.25 Ω, Q 50–100; a pan needing 28 A: ~1.4 Ω, Q 4–8).
5. **Power tracking:** per pan class at 120 V, `power_set_level` 15 / 35 / 60 / 100 %, and steps 20 → 60 → 15 % while running. Pass: analyser input power within ±6 % of the request (plus the e_est error) where the limits allow it, `freq_reason` HOLD_POWER, no frequency change over 60 s once settled; at 100 % the limit reason shows instead. Record the 60 kHz minimum power per pan (H12).
6. **No-pan clamp:** empty coil, ceiling set in the bench config to **17.5 A until a ladder step has passed its thermal gate** (gate 0 of the ceiling procedure), then to the highest step that has passed (never above B14's stop), with the no-pan stop off (`no_pan_stop` = false) in the bench config, 120 V and 132 V. At 17.5 A the clamp and the ceiling coincide, so this run shows only that the tank stays ≤ 17.5 A; repeat it at each new ladder step to show the clamp acting. Pass: `pan_present` stays false, `ceiling_eff_a` = 17.5 and the half RMS ≤ 17.5 A throughout; then lift a pan off mid-run at that ceiling: `pan_present` drops on the first half and the tank is back ≤ 17.5 A within 100 ms (otherwise the overrun latch `N21_FAULT_TANK_RMS` must fire). Measure the ferrite bar temperature in the no-pan case (enclosure item).
7. **No-pan stop (UL 858 47.8: off within 30 s of vessel removal, manual reset; OPEN-ITEMS B10):** shipped config, 120 V and 132 V, levels 1 % and 100 %. Empty coil, START: the gate drops 2.0 s (240 halves, `TANK_NO_PAN_STOP_HALVES`) after RUN_EN rises, `pan_refusal` = `N21_PAN_NO_PAN_STOP`, no fault latched, and the gate stays off with the request held, also after a pan is put back; only withdrawing the request (OFF) and a new START restarts. Then per pan class, run 60 s and lift the pan off mid-cook: the gate drops ≤ 2.1 s after the lift. Record the no-pan telemetry next to step 4's: lag (> 84°), `0.9 × line RMS / half RMS × cos(lag)` (< 0.5 Ω) and half RMS (≥ 2.5 A) for the empty coil, and confirm no pan class meets all three for 240 consecutive halves. **Provisional thresholds** from the plant table (config.yaml): empty coil 0.13 Ω, lag ~89°; lowest-R pan 1.0 Ω, lag ≤ 81°.
8. **No pre-start pan probe (coil study F3):** the loop's first half at 60 kHz is the only pan test. A small aluminium pan reaches the CT band (≈ 64.6 A) or the 41 A firmware trip within 40 µs of the first drive, before any firmware read (`test_start_transient_evidence`), so it latches a trip instead of a no-pan refusal. Keep aluminium off the coil for steps 9a.1–9a.7; the comparator latch is proven by step 3's injected-current test, so do not provoke it with an aluminium start. At 132 V capture the CT on a carbon-steel µ-low pan's first 50 µs: the model puts the start overshoot ~6–8 µs above 41 A (a possible nuisance TANK_PEAK latch).

## Raising the commissioned tank ceiling (17.5 → 18 → 20 → 22 → 24 A; 26 → 28 A on a B board only)

The stage 53 protection parts are sized for 28 A (stage 15, DECISIONS 10), but the prototype bank stops the ladder at **24 A** (B14), and every step above 17.5 A needs board-face airflow or a cooler bay (gate 0). The firmware ships at **17.5 A** (`TEMPER_POWER_TANK_CEILING_DA=175`). The release basis is the hot-copper B3 thermal model (power-stage OPEN-ITEMS S17: copper loss evaluated at the copper's solved temperature). On board s45 in still air at the worst bay air (61 °C) its binding row, R10's 2512 terminal derating, keeps +1.0 K at 17.5 A and fails by 0.7 K at 18.0 A, so the earlier 18.0 A release (DECISIONS 55 / 56, on 20 °C copper) no longer holds. Evidence: `zapote/power-stage-120v/validation-results/04-board-current-thermal/round5-native21/b3-ratings/hot-copper-2026-10-15/`. On stage 53 the release case (17.5 A) keeps +1.3 K on the same R10 row (`scorecard-native21s53.json` `b3_release_margin_K`); 18 A has not been re-solved on it. The ceiling is one parameter; the RMS trip (1.05 ×), crest guard (1.5 ×) and peak trip (min(2.17 × + 3, 62) A) derive from it and cannot be set above their derived values (`config_ok`); the board table's ceiling ladder lists them per step, with the low-trip floor and the bank stop. With no pan established the loop holds the tank at or below 17.5 A (`TANK_CEILING_DEFAULT_A`) whatever the ceiling.

At 28 A (a B board only, B14) the 62 A cap binds: with the peak estimate at 2.17 × RMS, the loop's peak limit (59 A) holds the line RMS to **27.2 A** until the bench calibrates the peak ratio (gate 5 below).

**Precondition for any raise above 17.5 A (once):**
- Stage-53 parts confirmed on the unit (MPN marking or measurement): the OCP divider (B2), the CT references (B9) and the dead-time resistors (B13).
- Steps 0–9a above passed at 17.5 A, with the record filed.
- The bench R_on gate-row reading (DECISIONS 55 open item: the R_on power basis, 0.217 W charge split against ~0.17 W simulated), filed.
- **Fitted bank part, per board** (power-stage DECISIONS 74; OPEN-ITEMS S28; B14). Read the bank-variant fit mark on F.SilkS beside the bank column (C21–C23 / C151 / C152) and the parts' own marking, and enter the fitted part in the record:
  - **H** = TDK B32643H8104J000 (the stage 53 prototype bank): the ladder **stops at 24 A** (B14).
  - **A board marked H, or with no mark ticked, is never commissioned above 24 A**, whatever its other gates show.
  - **B** = TDK B32643B8104J000 (the 28 A part, same 22.5 mm hole): 26 and 28 A stay **bench-gated** like every step (gates 0–7, gate 1 on this part); the mark alone raises nothing, and the firmware ceiling for a B board is set only by the per-step record.
  - A board with the in-stock fallback KNSCHA MMK104J3DF1KN208G2 (27.5 mm hole, stage 51; no tick box) stops at **22 A** (24 A only with the bench gap-flow credit, S25).

**Per step N = 18, then 20, 22, 24 A (26 and 28 A only on a B board, B14) — never skip a step.** Each step first needs the hot-copper B3 thermal basis at N (gate 0); without it the ceiling stays where it is. Set N only in the bench config, run every gate at N A on the worst-case pan for the gate (and the 132 V line where it matters), and file the evidence:

   - board-face airflow per `zapote/power-stage-120v/validation-results/11-candidates/airflow28-2026-10-16/README.md` §7 (the cold-copper `ENCLOSURE-REQUEST.md` is superseded), with h **measured on the board** by `11-candidates/bench-h-2026-10-16/results.md` (power-stage DECISIONS 69): step N passes only if the fitted h − U95 at the worst condition (clogged filter + aged fan) is ≥ the step's h_req and the nominal h ≥ 1.2 × h_req, and the gate-0 B3 case at that lower bound passes on the board fitted. ## Open hardware questions

- **H14. Firmware peak-trip cap against the CT band corner: 0.05 A (B17).** `TANK_PEAK_TRIP_MAX_A` 64.5 A sits 0.05 A below B9's low corner, computed with the CT network at a uniform 85 °C. Only R39 has a B3 row (B10); R40–R46 have none, and a hotter network lowers the corner. **Recommended:** add B3 thermal rows for R40–R46 (power-stage OPEN-ITEMS S29) and re-render the table. No firmware cap changes until that row exists.

- **H1. No firmware run gate (blocked COMMISSIONED; hardware closed by controller layout L2, DECISIONS C23: PERMIT = latch Q AND RUN_EN, RUN_EN on GPIO14 with a 4.7 kΩ pull-down; firmware `run_gate` implemented, bench step 9 still open).**
  - **The conflict:** `fullbridge_apply` qualifies the four PWM captures on every tick, idle included, and gates the drivers through a separate request. That is round 5's REQUEST → supervisor → SUP_RUN_OK path.
  - **This controller:** PERMIT comes only from the interlock latch, and the latch is granted by operator reset. So with PERMIT high, live pads mean a running bridge, and the pads cannot be qualified while idle.
  - **Proposed ECO:** native-21's PERMIT = latch Q AND RUN_EN. Firmware can only *remove* permission with it, never grant it. RUN_EN is pulled down, with a read-back through the LVC244.
  - **GPIO:** none is free. The candidates are TCA9555 P06/P07 (slow, adequate for an enable that is not a trip path), or freeing a GPIO.
  - **Rejected:** turning MAX31865 BIAS off to trip the runaway comparator as a stop. That makes a firmware reset request the "run" action, i.e. firmware asserting PERMIT.
- **H2. No power actuator at a fixed frequency.**
  - The adapter computes a conductance from the inlet hold (11.1 A pre-stage-16; 11.47–11.99 A with the blower-scheduled allowance on the s38 supplies) and min(pan, tank ceiling), but `fullbridge_apply` uses it only as on/off. Phase shift is removed (DECISIONS 2026-10-05) and frequency is fixed (`power_service_frequency` accepts only the configured value).
  - **Stage 15 (partly closed):** the per-pan frequency loop (`tank_freq.h`) now regulates the tank ceiling, the inlet hold and the peak limit by frequency, from 60 kHz down. The trips stay as the backstop (adapter 15 A inlet bound; binding 1.05 × ceiling on the half-cycle RMS).
  - **Power level:** the adapter's `requested_w` (`power_set_level`) is the loop's power target: input power = min(request, the limit-bound maximum), in a ±6 % dead band (wider than the ~5.7 % power change of one 250 Hz step, so it cannot chatter). Every limit wins over the request; level 0 stops the bridge.
  - **Still open (H12):** below the power the pan draws at 60 kHz the loop caps at 60 kHz and over-delivers.
- **H3. The RTD service used the legacy pin map (resolved for layout L2).** It now uses `controller_pins.h`: SCK 11, SDI 13, SDO 12, CS 10, mode 3 (C20). DRDY moved from GPIO14 to TCA9555 P07 (C23); the control task samples it once per 10 ms tick and the service takes a high-to-low transition as completion. A DRDY low pulse shorter than one tick is missed and ends in the 100 ms fail-silent timeout (bench: confirm the MAX31865 DRDY stays low until the data registers are read).
- **H4. CT_ZC.** Its polarity and the T1 + TLV3201 delay across 31–55 kHz are unspecified, and so is the CT's phase error.
- **H5. Capture independence and channel count.**
  - The PWM feedback is a loop-back capture of the MCU's own pads. Round 5 used an external capture over JFAST, so this is not independent of the MCU. Confirm it is acceptable to the safety review.
  - It uses all six MCPWM capture channels (both groups), with HA captured in each group as the phase reference.
- **H6. C12 / C19 bench items:** repeated ADC1 channels in one continuous pattern; the AD8436 settling with CAVG = 100 nF and its envelope smoothing (steps 1 and 6).
- **H7. CT_MON raw path.** It is sampled asynchronously at 20 kS/s, so its peak trip (derived from the ceiling, 40.975 A at 17.5 A) is supplementary. The qualified over-current protection is the hardware CT comparators and the shunt OCP.
- **H8. Precharge and contactors.** `energy_supervisor` (precharge, K1/K2/KB) runs on an AUX-powered owner. Native-21 and this controller have no such owner. Confirm the inrush path, so this binding's assumption that the bus is simply present holds.
- **H9. LINE_ZC on J4.16.** The pin took a return contact; recheck it against the D-10/D-20 return allocation (DECISIONS 2026-10-06).
- **H10. Live MCPWM retiming.** `hw_pwm_update` relies on `update_period_on_empty` and `update_cmp_on_tez` shadow loading; never compiled against ESP-IDF or scoped (step 9a.1). Per-leg non-overlap does not depend on the load timing (one comparator per leg), but a period and its compares landing on different timer zeros gives one period of up to half-step duty error.
- **H11. Peak and f_res estimates.** The peak estimate is max(2.17 × half RMS, 1.485 × crest AD8436 sample, max |CT_MON| sample) and the f_res fit assumes a series RLC with constant L and R across a 2 kHz span; both are pre-measurement (ceiling gate 5, step 9a.2).
- **H12. Low-power operation.** The loop's lowest continuous power is what the pan draws at 60 kHz. The FHA model (120 V, 55 µH / 0.5 µF) gives about 73 W into a 1.4 Ω pan, 104 W at 2.0 Ω and 175 W at 3.5 Ω (×1.21 at 132 V). Requests below that are capped at 60 kHz and over-delivered (`TF_POWER_AT_FMAX`). Pulse-skipping or LINE_ZC burst (`burst_scheduler`, disabled) is out of scope here; the temperature PID above it sees the over-delivery.
- **H13. Blower off through an MCU-only hang (controller L3, accepted 2026-10-12).** Expander P15 FAN_OFF is a TCA9555 output latch, which an MCU reset or hang does not clear, so a hang while the fan law had the blower off leaves it off. Accepted: the TPS3823 watchdog drops PERMIT, so the tank is off too; a hang means blower off and tank off, which is safe (only residual heat). Open hardware options if that is ever not enough: a TCA9539 (the TCA9555 with a RESET pin) with RESET tied to the MCU's EN, so every MCU reset returns FAN_OFF to an input (blower on); or a hardware tach-loss trip (retriggerable monostable on FG into a spare interlock input, DECISIONS C28).
