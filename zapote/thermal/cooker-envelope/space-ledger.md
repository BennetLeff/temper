## Air path and mechanical concept screen

**Independent bridge path:** the GBU 395-1AB control can be packaged as a dedicated bridge duct, keeping unrelated PFC/inverter heat out of its 40 °C inlet target. Its two 41 CFM free-air fans have 44.8 Pa shutoff pressure each. Parallel fans widen available flow, not pressure. The 500 LFM gross-face target corresponds to about 43.4 CFM before fin blockage; final grille, duct and fin pressure must intersect the actual parallel fan curve. If an upstream source heats the inlet, its temperature rise must be measured and charged to this model.

**Shared PFC path:** the 392-120AB/Sanyo concept is useful only if all concurrent heat sources have physical sink paths and the fan/system intersection establishes roughly 100 CFM through the fins. Its 120 CFM free-air and 100 Pa shutoff endpoints do not occur together. The initial ~30 Pa budget at 100 CFM is a design screen, not an installed result. An inverter source or hot exhaust recirculating into this inlet invalidates the 40 °C assumed inlet; a shared whole-cooker duct also needs separate inverter and auxiliary loss/geometry data.

Both concepts need independent chassis load paths, not PCB-supported sinks. Do not credit an enclosure design lacking a bracket, clamp, insulation/fastener detail, intake and exhaust clearances, and access to fuses/connectors. A nominal box around a sink is not a completed duct or electrical clearance volume.

## Minimum enclosure data for a fit decision

Provide a dated 3-D enclosure and assembly coordinate system; internal wall and cooktop/pan plane; power-entry and inverter board placement; chassis mounting points and allowed mass/loads; air inlet/outlet/grille geometry; wire and fuse service volumes; live-metal barriers, creepage and accessible-surface requirements; fan acoustic/contamination constraints; and control/SELV partition. Collision checks must cover installation/removal motion and cable bend, not only static solid overlap. Thermal checks need the same exact panel/grille/duct revision used for pressure/flow measurements.
