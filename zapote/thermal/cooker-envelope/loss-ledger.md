## Minimum next measurement set

Identify board/source revision and operating state for each run. Measure bridge and converter electrical losses at low line, high line, full and reduced load, including chosen PFC gate bias and switching frequency. Capture fan electrical input and actual fin-path flow/pressure with final grille and enclosure; record inlet temperature at each heat source, outlet temperature, recirculation, contact temperatures and coil/pan state. Separate heat reaching a shared sink from heat entering PCB, air, pan and enclosure. A total loss with an unknown destination cannot be assigned to a sink resistance.
