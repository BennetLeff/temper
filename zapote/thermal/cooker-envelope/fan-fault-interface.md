# Fan and thermal fault dependency contract

This is a producer-side U1 interface inventory for a future cooling circuit. It is **not** a built, tested or joined protective circuit. Fan choice, protected supply and mechanical mounting remain open; electrical thresholds and timing are not selected by this document.

## Consumer and required default

The accepted standalone interlock's `zapote/interlock/INTERFACES.md` assigns J1 pin 4 to active-high **Heatsink fault**. A powered, valid remote producer must actively sink this line to ≤0.3 V when healthy and let it reach ≥2.7 V for a fault. Its local 10 kΩ ±1% pullup makes an open fault conductor assert under the stated 20 µA leakage budget. The proposed cooling producer must be high-impedance or fault-asserted when its supply, sensor or self-check is unavailable; an unpowered output that clamps the pullup low would defeat the claimed disconnect behavior. This power-off electrical property needs circuit-level proof. `SENSOR_LIVE` at J2 pin 5 must separately go low when relevant sensing is invalid; its producer is not yet qualified. The interlock's PERMIT receiver requires a driven high and a local pulldown, so loss of the interlock supply itself must disable heating at the receiving stage.

Fan fault is a **heating inhibit** for both power entry and inverter once integrated. Rev38's source-permission path and the inverter permit receiver must each be checked against this actual interlock PERMIT connection; standalone interlock acceptance alone does not prove the joined stop path. A cooling trip remains latched; reset requires fault removal, inspection and a fresh authorized reset edge, not an automatic loop after tach returns. The standalone interlock's reset timing and watchdog contract continue to apply.

## Producer inputs and faults to distinguish

| Input or state | Required interpretation | Evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fan supply at startup | Healthy only after selected supply reaches its valid window and each fan demonstrates the required response; a missing rail cannot read healthy. | Selected regulated rail, startup/stall current, allowed spin-up time and brownout behavior. The GBU two-Sunon concept requires regulated 12 V and at least 0.276 A combined maximum running current; do not connect it directly to 15 V (`bridge-cooling.md`). The GBJ Sanyo supply remains to be bound to its exact electrical data. |
| Each fan tach | Missing, implausible or stuck tach asserts a cooling fault after a justified startup grace interval. Two-fan concept needs independently monitored tach channels. | Fan tach electrical levels, pullup supply and power-off behavior, pulse-rate window, noise filter and fault latency. A two-wire legacy fan circuit cannot provide this by assumption. |
| Fin-path airflow | Tach spinning does not prove an open inlet or useful fin flow. Blocked inlet, clogged grille, broken blade and recirculation must be detected by independent airflow or thermal evidence. | Installed pressure/flow curve and selected airflow sensor or demonstrated temperature supervision with transient bound. |
| Bridge case/spreader and hottest board contact | Overtemperature, open/short sensor or implausible reading asserts fault. The GBU legacy screen would require case trip below 75 °C *minus* sensor error and shutdown overshoot; with illustrative 2 K + 3 K it is ≤70 °C, not a chosen setting. The modeled PCB neck is 107.86 °C against 110 °C, leaving only 2.14 K. | Actual sensor location, thermal lag/error, material ratings and transient shutdown overshoot. A nonempty full-power trip window is not established. GBJ's different package model requires its own sensor calibration and limits. |
| Host/power loss and restart | Missing sensing rail, MCU reset or disconnected cooling harness cannot be considered healthy; PERMIT must fall through the joined hardware path. Recovery of tach or temperature alone must not restart heat. | Electrical power-off and open-wire tests; joined Rev38/inverter permit wiring; latch/reset tests on the assembled board. |

The historical `elec/src/modules.ato::ThermalSystem` has a 60 mm, two-wire Sunon fan and 39 Ω dropper from 15 V. It cannot be used as the power/tach circuit for either current cooling candidate. Selecting a fan and rail precedes a native circuit; the standby, running, startup, stalled and rail-fault loads must be handed to the auxiliary supply owner.

Physical qualification remains **NOT RUN**. The GBJ protocol at `zapote/power-entry/passive-reva/cooling/qualification-protocol.md` identifies the needed installed assembly and records: calibrated flow/pressure with final grille/enclosure, synchronized bridge electrical loss, case/spreader/board temperatures, fan tach, and separate controlled fan-stop or airflow-loss capture. Repeat with each fan lost, tach disconnected, blocked inlet and high inlet air as applicable. Prove the stop latency and peak temperature before assigning any trip delay or claiming cooling protection.
