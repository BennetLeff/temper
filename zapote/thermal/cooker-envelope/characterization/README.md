# Fan, airflow, and thermal bench intake (no measurements yet)

The [run register](fan-run-register.tsv) separates the retained `GBU395-SUNON2`, `GBU392-SANYO1` and `GBJ392-SANYO1` concepts. All rows are `NOT_RUN` or explicitly held. The three header-only capture files contain no physical observations. The parent [candidate gate](../candidate-inputs.tsv) retains 17 `UNKNOWN` fields per candidate and remains `INDETERMINATE`.

## Freeze each physical assembly

Before a powered run, identify fan MPN, lot/serial and count; fin/sink, clamp, bracket, heat-source package, duct, grille and enclosure drawing revisions; orientation; inlet/outlet locations; fan rail/controller/harness revision; tach and independent flow/sensor locations. Photograph the assembled air path and record any bypass or recirculation. The `GBU392` and `GBJ392` cases may share sink and fan part numbers, but have **different package, contact, lead/board heat paths and assumed loads**. Do not merge their thermal results. Use the matching heat-source map from [loss-ledger.md](../loss-ledger.md) and mechanical constraints from [space-ledger.md](../space-ledger.md).

Record instrument model/serial, valid calibration certificates, electrical probe factor, flow/pressure sensor calibration, temperature sensor placement and contact method, sample rate/time alignment, ambient condition, uncertainty method and raw file digest for every run. A raw digest only identifies bytes; the reviewer must inspect the data and its applicability. Keep aborted and failed runs with their stop reason, and link any retest to the original.

Capture tables use decimal values in the units named by each column. A required observation cannot be blank, `UNKNOWN`, or silently zero. Use `NA` only for a field genuinely outside a run kind, with the reason in the raw record; `NA` cannot fill a required candidate-input field. Preserve each uncertainty budget and calibration source with the raw packet. Every fan instance gets its own electrical row, especially the two Sunon fans.

## Staged sequence and stop rules

1. **Unpowered fit and airflow route:** check fan spin clearance, support, lead strain relief, blocked-path access and distinct tach wiring for each of the two Sunon fans. The heavy sink must be carried by its own chassis support, not by PCB leads. Record exact physical assembly identity.
2. **Isolated low-voltage fan bench:** use a current-limited source and separately reviewed fan terminal limits. Capture cold and hot start, steady states across the intended terminal-voltage range, bounded brownout, restart, and a controlled stall only after its duration/energy limit is approved. Measure each fan's terminal voltage/current, startup and stall peaks, start time, tach frequency/jitter and power-off leakage. A catalog 12 V and running-current figure does not supply startup/stall bounds. Stop on unexpected current, smoke/odor, rotor obstruction, overtemperature, unstable source or invalid instrument range. For two fans, independently open or stop one and retain both tach traces.
3. **Installed air path:** with the exact sink/duct/grille/enclosure assembly, measure static pressure, fin-path flow and spatial velocity distribution at the actual fan operating point; record inlet and exhaust temperature and recirculation. A free-air CFM endpoint or tach count alone is never an installed-flow result. A calibrated dummy heat source can screen the air path if its power and contact geometry are recorded, but it cannot qualify Rev38 or inverter semiconductor junction temperatures.
4. **Thermal and fault chain:** only after heat-source electrical loss, package/contact path, sensor positions, rail and interlock circuit are frozen, capture hot spots, sensor error/lag, loss of one fan, blocked inlet with plausible tach, fan rail loss, sensor invalidity, fan-off heat soak and J1/J2 behavior. Synchronize fan/flow/sensor, J1-4, J2-5, PFC RUN, inverter PERMIT, gate outputs and measured tank-current-zero where present. Measure restart only after an explicit new authorization; fan or tach self-recovery must not re-arm heating. Do not run an energized cooker or staged fault until separately reviewed source/current/energy/temperature limits and protection are available.

Every stage records numeric preflight limits and the person approving them in header-only [run-approval.tsv](run-approval.tsv) before power is applied. Stop and preserve evidence on loss of the independent flow/sensor channel, protection failure, out-of-range temperature/current, missing synchronized channel, J1 fault with either stage still enabled, unexpected restart, or exhausted test limit. No numeric safe limit is invented by this packet.

Once a protected cooker exists, the product-behavior campaign also needs set/hold temperature recovery and step tests, probe and through-glass sensor agreement, intensity and preset changes, and pan/placement variation while recording inlet flow and the hottest relevant hardware temperatures. Competitor functionality motivates those cases but supplies no Temper thermal limit or hardware measurement.

## Transfer to selection and discharge

The [electrical capture](fan-electrical-points.tsv) supplies terminal low/high, startup/stall current, start time and tach-window evidence. The [installed-air capture](airflow-thermal-points.tsv) supplies pressure, fin flow, inlet rise, source/sensor temperatures, sensor error and lag at the *same* assembly/heat map. The [fault capture](fault-timing-points.tsv) supplies blocked-inlet detection, J1 low/high/leak, J2 contribution, both stage-stop times and measured current-zero. The trip window is a separate worst-case analysis combining detection, sensor lag/error, control/output delay, current-zero and post-stop heat rise with uncertainty; it cannot be filled from one timestamp or the retained 0.361 K model margin. An independent reviewer assigns the evidence ID only after raw review and source matching.

Fan electrical input loads the auxiliary rail. Its heat goes where the motor/driver exhausts, and supply-conversion losses go where the supply sits; do not count fan power twice. Discharge's fan-off case must retain continuous/one-short resistor heat even when auxiliary loss has removed fan power; fan cooling is creditable only if its independent supply and measured flow are proved. Full-cooker heat assignment also needs measured PFC, inverter, capacitor, coil and pan destinations. The fan gate cannot select a native fan-control PCB before terminal range, selected fan/rail, tach/flow sensor and J1/J2 electrical implementation are defined.

## Negative controls

The parent cooling Rust checker leaves plausible tach with blocked flow `INDETERMINATE` until physical detection timing is measured. It rejects J1 stuck healthy, held reset or tach recovery falsely re-arming, invalid sensing with J2 still live, and altered source files. Repeat those tests before promoting capture rows. A completed electrical fan table with an empty installed-air table stays `INDETERMINATE`; the three candidate identities must never be averaged.
