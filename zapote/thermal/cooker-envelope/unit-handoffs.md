# Cooling handoffs and decision gate

This is the cooling unit's digital U2/U3 handoff at source snapshot `cbfcc2ebc` (2026-09-23). The active Rev38 checkout is moving separately; its uncommitted source is provisional and outside this decision. `concept-screen.md` gives reproducible conditional arithmetic. `fan-fault-interface.md` gives the producer behavior to construct and test. There is no installed fan operating point, enclosure fit or physical qualification.

## Cross-unit inputs and outputs

| From → to | Exact handoff needed | Why it changes the decision |

| Rev38 power entry → cooling | Frozen GBJ board/native revision and coordinates of bridge, STW, C3D diode, 760800301 inductor, VD reservoir, F2, VB bank, shunt and AC-entry parts; losses at low/high line and load, gate-bias/frequency envelope, junction/package limits and each heat destination. | The passive GBJ study assumed 40 W bridge + 65 W other PFC + 5.6 W fan but did not prove Rev38's simultaneous loss or sink contacts. The frozen Rev38 source has no native PCB and cannot establish actual package-to-sink geometry. |
| Auxiliary → cooling | A **regulated 12 V candidate fan rail**, electrical return/isolation, voltage at each fan under startup, continuous and stalled load, loss/brownout and cable drop, plus a separately valid sensing/logic rail. | Two Sunon fans are 0.276 A combined *maximum running* from the exact sheet; their startup/stall current is not supplied by that figure. Sanyo is 0.47 A/5.6 W **rated free-air**, not a guaranteed start/stall maximum. Both fan thermal catalog points are at 12 V. The auxiliary source must budget fan power and its own conversion waste by physical heat location. |
| Cooling → auxiliary | Selected fan MPN/quantity; rail operating range for thermal rating; measured startup/stall current and waveform, tach pullup/cable current, control/sensor load, run-on/cool-down policy and fault response. | The auxiliary rail ledger currently lists alternative fans. Do not add 0.276 A and 0.47 A as simultaneous loads or reuse the legacy 60 mm fan/39 Ω dropper. A smaller minimum operating voltage does not prove adequate airflow at reduced voltage. |
| Inverter → cooling | Switch/tank/capacitor/coil/ferrite loss versus bus, power, frequency and pan state; exact heat-source locations, sink contact and coil-to-pot transfer; local fan/sensor needs; enclosure/pan stack geometry and forbidden air paths. | Inverter loss and physical volume are unknown. | Cooling → inverter | Candidate sink/duct occupancy, flow direction, allowed inlet temperature, expected fan-valid and overtemperature producers, and the requirement that an active-high interlock `PERMIT` be physically joined to the inverter's default-disabled gate-drive permit. | A separate inverter must stop on cooling fault and cannot infer it from PFC RUN. The accepted gate-drive interface alone has no physical cooling producer. |
| Enclosure/integration → cooling | Dated 3-D enclosure and board placement, cooktop/pan plane, intake/outlet/grille, chassis mounting strength, service clearances, barriers, insulation/PE and accessible-surface targets. | The 230 × 210 mm power-entry section outline and 170 × 160 × 95 or 170 × 160 × 175 mm concept allowances are not collision checks. Pressure drop and recirculation depend on the final enclosure. |
| Discharge → cooling/auxiliary | The conditional NC VB discharge candidate dissipates 21.33 W continuously at 400 V or 27.0 W at the 450 V rating-edge screen if AUX fails while mains remains; one shorted 7.5 kΩ bank resistor raises total fast-path heat to 32.0/40.5 W. ## Current conditional decision

| Candidate | Digital disposition | Reason to retain or reject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GBU-395-control` | **Retain only as historical control.** | Source-bound local FEM and selected assembly exist, but the 40 W/40 °C/500 LFM screen leaves 5 K junction and only 2.14 K local PCB margin. It is tied to the saved GBU board, not Rev38's GBJ. |
| `GBU-392-shared-screen` | **Retain as GBU comparison, no promotion.** | The Sanyo fan could in principle reach the 100 CFM catalog point only at a sufficiently low system pressure. The 105 W distributed load is provisional and the bridge-zone thermal resistance has not been measured. |
| `GBJ-392-passive-checkpoint` | **Preferred package-aligned investigation for Rev38, not selected cooling.** | Rev38 source selects GBJ2510-F, and the retained four-diode model is the relevant package family. But its 59.639 °C sink screen is only 0.361 K below the imposed 60 °C FEM boundary; its weak-assembly case crosses the 110 °C board/joint screen and its fan-loss transient is unmodeled. No Rev38 native geometry or actual loss is bound. |
| GBU 396 compact and GBU 392/two-Sunon | **Do not lead further design on current evidence.** | Compact is 142.8 °C on the matched legacy 40 W/500 LFM screen. Two Sunon free-air endpoints total 82 CFM, below the 392 sink's 100 CFM catalog point before system resistance. |

The meaningful decision now is to **develop a chassis-supported GBJ/392/Sanyo assembly model for the Rev38 package while preserving the GBU/395 control**. This selects the next evidence-gathering target, not a product part or operating limit. Fan control hardware selection remains gated by the final fan/rail and a nonempty thermal trip window. A shared inverter/PFC airflow path remains indeterminate until the inverter supplies loss and geometry.

## Exact missing observations for promotion

1. **Electrical loss:** synchronized bridge voltage/current waveform and real loss at 25/50/75/100% of the 15 A RMS design branch, 120 Vrms and the product's low/high line; STW, diode, inductor, shunt, bus ripple and gate losses at the selected PFC bias/frequency and hot temperature. The passive 112.90–157.44 W STW sensitivity is a warning, not a Rev38 measurement.
2. **Fan/system operating point:** pressure and actual fin-path volume flow at the final 12 V fan terminals, with final grille, duct, sink, board and closed enclosure. Capture minimum supply, fan aging/one-fan loss, blocked inlet and heated/recirculated inlet; verify 100 CFM for 392 or 500 LFM for 395 as applicable. Capture flow distribution, not just aggregate CFM.
3. **Thermal path:** measured bridge case/spreader/sink/inlet temperatures; contact pressure, pad/spreader geometry and package-to-lead/PCB heat split; board contact and solder temperatures; selected insulation and fastener path. Reconcile the measured boundary with the GBJ model, then rerun it; do not transplant the GBU neck result.
4. **Mechanical fit:** released 3-D board/enclosure, heat-source coordinates, bracket and support strength, duct and service volumes, live-metal clearances/creepage, chassis bonding and cooktop/pan clearance. Record dimensions and tolerance stacks of the GBJ mounting assembly.
5. **Protection timing:** selected tach/sensor thresholds and error, loaded fan spin-up/stall waveform, actual sensor lag, worst-case stop latency and post-stop temperature overshoot. Demonstrate a nonempty case/PCB trip window at rated heat, including high inlet temperature. Verify fault output high on power loss and low only in qualified running state, then trace interlock PERMIT removal through both power stages.

Digital source/numerical acceptance can falsify wrong model identities, missing heat terms, free-air-as-installed-flow and unsupported sink load paths. It cannot establish these observations. Physical cooling, fault-injection and whole-appliance qualification remain **NOT RUN**.
