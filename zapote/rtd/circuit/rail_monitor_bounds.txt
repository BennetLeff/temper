RTD_AVDD monitor bounded-divider receipt

Source: TPS3700 SBVS187G Rev G (TI official datasheet), which specifies
VIT- = 387..400 mV, VIT+ = 396..404 mV over temperature and INA+ input
current = +/-25 nA. Divider: Panasonic ERA-6AEB6983V 698 kOhm +/-0.1%,
25 ppm/C, 0805 over ERA-3AEB104V 100 kOhm +/-0.1%, 25 ppm/C, 60 C span.

For each independent corner, using Rtop/Rbottom at tolerance and opposing
temperature-coefficient endpoints, Rth = Rtop || Rbottom, and solving

    Vnode = Vrail*Rbottom/(Rtop+Rbottom) + Iin*Rth

at Vnode = VIT gives:

    Vrail_trip = (VIT - Iin*Rth)*(Rtop+Rbottom)/Rbottom

Python command used:

    python3 - <<'PY'
    from itertools import product
    for vt in ([.387,.400], [.396,.404]):
        out=[]
        for v,r1,r2,t1,t2,i in product(vt,[.999,1.001],[.999,1.001],[-.0015,.0015],[-.0015,.0015],[-25e-9,25e-9]):
            a=698000*r1*(1+t1); b=100000*r2*(1+t2); th=a*b/(a+b)
            out.append((v-i*th)*(a+b)/b)
        print(min(out),max(out))
    PY

Recorded output (V):

    VIT- falling trip: 3.057381000530 .. 3.223488617599
    VIT+ rising clear: 3.128887684290 .. 3.255548567263

The exact corner script used for the contract additionally prints the falling
maximum as 3.223 and rising minimum as 3.129 after decimal rounding. The
falling minimum is above the MAX31865 3.0 V supply minimum, and the rising
maximum is below nominal 3.3 V. This is a bounded resistor/reference/input
current calculation; it does not model TPS output transients, V(POR), rail
ramp shape, PCB parasitics, or physical fault-latch behavior.

Source-rail minimum and load drop
---------------------------------

The authoritative WIP buck module uses LMR51430XDDCR with 100 kOhm / 22.1
kOhm feedback resistors and declares `power_out.voltage within 3.3 V +/- 5%`
and `v_out_calculated within 3.135 V to 3.465 V`. A direct feedback corner
calculation using LMR51430 VFB=0.591..0.609 V, +/-1% resistor tolerance,
opposing +/-100 ppm/C feedback-resistor TCR over 60 C, and +/-50 nA maximum FB
leakage gives 3.176066..3.459346 V. The lower source contract bound, 3.135 V,
is therefore the conservative rail floor because it also covers regulation
and load behavior not represented by the static feedback equation.

Feedback-corner command output:

    nominal Vout = 0.6 * (1 + 100000/22100) = 3.314932 V
    corner Vout (including +/-50 nA FB leakage) = 3.176066 .. 3.459346 V
    declared source envelope = 3.135 .. 3.465 V

The RTD branch is not assigned a source current limit, so this artifact uses a
10 mA bounded allocation: MAX31865 active-conversion maximum 3.5 mA, REF2025
quiescent current approximately 0.43 mA, comparator/logic current, and margin.
The exact Murata BLM18AG121SN1D ferrite has maximum DC resistance 0.18 Ohm;
at 10 mA the post-ferrite drop is at most 1.8 mV. The local RTD_AVDD floor used
for the monitor comparison is therefore `3.135 - 0.0018 = 3.1332 V`. This is
a bounded engineering allocation, not a measured branch-current result.

Valid-minimum release verdict
-----------------------------

At 3.1332 V local rail, the TPS3700 rising-clear worst corner is 3.255549 V,
so the selected 698 kOhm / 100 kOhm guard does **not** guarantee release at the
declared valid-low rail. It does clear at nominal 3.3 V, with 44.451 mV of
worst-corner margin. The low-valid-rail case is a deterministic nuisance UV
fault, not an indeterminate result to be hidden as physical validation.

For the same high-impedance divider family, even ideal ratio selection leaves
no robust full-corner window: falling trip above the 3.0 V MAX minimum requires
`Rtop > 100 kOhm * (3.000/.387 - 1) = 675.194 kOhm`; rising clear below the
3.1332 V local minimum requires `Rtop < 100 kOhm * (3.1332/.404 - 1) =
675.545 kOhm`, before resistor tolerance, TCR, and +/-25 nA input-current
error. The approximately 351 Ohm ideal interval is consumed by those errors.
The 698 kOhm part is retained as the fail-safe guard because it guarantees
permission removal before the ADC's 3.0 V minimum, while the product/interface
contract must choose a higher valid rail floor, a lower-impedance divider
redesign, or an active supervisor if release at every 3.135 V-valid rail is
required.

Precision-supervisor check
--------------------------

The proposed TI TPS3808G01DBVR is an exact, orderable 6-pin SOT-23 (DBV)
adjustable supervisor. Its official Rev N datasheet gives the pin map RESET=1,
GND=2, MR=3, CT=4, SENSE=5, VDD=6; RESET is open-drain active-low, MR has an
internal 90 kOhm pullup, and leaving CT open selects a 20 ms reset delay. VDD
is specified from 1.7 to 6.5 V, the G01 SENSE threshold reference is 0.405 V,
negative threshold accuracy is -2/+2% over temperature, hysteresis is 1.5..3%,
and SENSE input current is +/-25 nA. TI recommends 1..10 nF at SENSE.

This part is **not a drop-in closure** for the present rail contract. To ensure
falling trip above 3.0 V, an adjustable nominal threshold must be at least
3.0/0.98 = 3.0612 V. Its worst rising release then reaches at least
3.0612 * 1.02 * 1.03 = 3.155 V, already above the 3.1332 V valid local floor;
divider tolerance and SENSE current widen the gap. The fixed TPS3808G33
(3.07 V nominal, +/-1.5% threshold and up to 2.5% hysteresis) also has a
worst rising release above 3.1332 V. Therefore no TPS3808G01DBVR source patch
or pin substitution is claimed here. A lower-impedance precision comparator
or supervisor with explicitly bounded threshold/hysteresis, or an increased
valid rail floor, is required to close both sides of the contract.

TPS3808 source: TI [TPS3808 Rev N datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3808.pdf),
pp. 1, 4-7, 10-12; exact orderable part and DBV package are listed on the
[TI TPS3808G01DBVR product page](https://www.ti.com/product/TPS3808/part-details/TPS3808G01DBVR).

Official references:

* https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3700.pdf
* https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/high-precision-chip-resistors/models/ERA6AEB6983V

Superseding monitor: TPS389001DSER + low-impedance divider
------------------------------------------------------------

The TPS3700 high-impedance divider is superseded by TI TPS389001DSER. The
official TPS3890 Rev A datasheet gives DSE top-view pins SENSE=1, GND=2,
MR=3, VDD=4, CT=5, RESET=6; 1% maximum threshold accuracy, maximum 0.825%
hysteresis, 10..100 nA SENSE input current, and approximately 25 us minimum
no-CT delay. CT is left open in the source proposal. RESET is open-drain,
active-low, and its 10 kohm pullup is on upstream power.vcc.

The exact divider is Panasonic ERA-3AEB1652V, 16.5 kohm, 0.1%, +/-25 ppm/C,
0603 over ERA-3AEB103V, 10 kohm, 0.1%, +/-25 ppm/C, 0603. The nominal falling
trip is 1.15 V * (1 + 16.5k/10k) = 3.0475 V. At the declared 3.1332 V local
minimum, divider current is approximately 118.2 uA, more than 100 times the
100 nA maximum SENSE current as TI recommends.

For each corner, I used Rth=Rtop||Rbottom and

    Vrail_trip = (Vthreshold - Iin*Rth) * (Rtop+Rbottom)/Rbottom

with independent +/-0.1% resistor tolerance, opposing +/-25 ppm/C drift over
60 C, +/-100 nA input current, and +/-1% threshold accuracy. For the rising
bound I conservatively used Vthreshold = 1.15*1.01*1.00825 = 1.171082375 V,
combining maximum threshold accuracy and maximum hysteresis. The exhaustive
corner output is:

    falling trip: 3.006009934549 .. 3.089235504007 V
    rising clear: 3.092085094442 .. 3.114708050363 V

Thus falling trip remains 6.0099 mV above the MAX31865 3.0 V minimum, and the
worst rising clear is 18.4919 mV below the bounded 3.1332 V local rail floor.
This is a resistor/reference/current bounded calculation, not a PVT, rail-ramp,
transient, or physical fault-latch proof.

Corner command (Python 3):

    python3 - <<'PY'
    from itertools import product
    for vt, label in [([1.15*.99,1.15*1.01], 'falling'),
                      ([1.15*1.01*1.00825]*2, 'rising')]:
        out=[]
        for v,a,b,ta,tb,i in product(vt,[.999,1.001],[.999,1.001],[-.0015,.0015],[-.0015,.0015],[-100e-9,100e-9]):
            r1=16500*a*(1+ta); r2=10000*b*(1+tb); rth=r1*r2/(r1+r2)
            out.append((v-i*rth)*(r1+r2)/r2)
        print(label, min(out), max(out))
    PY

    rising 3.092085094441816 3.114708050363152
