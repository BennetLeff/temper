# RTDIN runtime-open transient model receipt

Command: /opt/homebrew/bin/python3 zapote/rtd/circuit/rtdin_transient_model.py
Date: 2026-09-10

The model solves the actual passive resistor network after a runtime open.
RTDIN_P and RTDIN_N are the dynamic nodes represented by `sensep` and `sensem`;
the protected 100 kohm comparator branch, LOW threshold, and HIGH threshold
are solved algebraically at every step. It uses implicit Euler at 1 us for the stiff
network, 1.10 nF differential C (1 nF C0G +5% tolerance plus separate 50 pF allowance), and 100 pF
to ground at each RTDIN node (200 pF harness/board allowance). The initial
state is a healthy 100 or 194.1 ohm PT100 with 1 ohm per conductor; the final
state opens exactly one FORCE+/FORCE-/SENSE+/SENSE- conductor or steps to a
10/0 ohm RTD short. `cross_ms` is
the first comparator threshold crossing and `unit_detect_ms` adds the sourced
TLV3201 55 ns maximum propagation and a conservative 10 ns AND/NAND logic
bound. No MAX ADC or SINC assumption is used.
The crossing criterion is 20 mV comparator overdrive, below the static corner sweep
open-fault minimum margin of 166.7 mV; sourced propagation is applied only after
that overdrive. An analytic monotonic-passive-RC envelope uses Rdiag(max)=1.057 Mohm,
Rwindow(max)=102 kohm, Cbound=1.30 nF, and the retained corner endpoint margins;
it bounds worst RC crossing at 1.323511 ms and detector time at 1.323576 ms.
The output-load assumption is 15 pF (the TLV propagation test-load class); this is
an engineering timing bound, not a vendor guarantee for an arbitrary load.

The standalone unit transient contract is passive-harness only: each probe
conductor must remain in -0.2..+2.5 V relative to unit ground, source
impedance >=1 kohm, edge time >=10 us, pulse width <=1 ms, and repetition
<=1 Hz. This span is 2.7 V, equal to the TLV3201 minimum 2.7 V supply, so the
computed comparator differential remains within the supply and no input clamp
is required. The MAX31865 +/-45 V RTDIN absolute maximum is recorded as a
device limit only and is not a unit transient immunity claim. At local
post-ferrite loss the external source is forbidden; the rail monitor/HW fault
owns shutdown and the powered-comparator model is not asserted.

The capacitor-energy and current lines below use four nominal 100 nF post-rail
bypasses with an explicit acceptance minimum of 45 nF each (180 nF aggregate).
This is a passive energy bound, not a rail-ramp or sink measurement. Physical harness, EMC, and shutdown validation
remain NOT RUN.
