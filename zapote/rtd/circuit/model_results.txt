# Bounded RTD model receipt

Commands (ngspice 45.2):

    ngspice -b zapote/rtd/circuit/rtd_window_bounded.cir
    ngspice -b zapote/rtd/circuit/rtd_sense_open_rc.cir
    ngspice -b zapote/rtd/circuit/rtd_sense_minus_open_rc.cir
    ngspice -b zapote/rtd/circuit/rtd_diagnostic_loading.cir

Complete stdout is retained in [model_results_raw.txt](model_results_raw.txt).
The decks are isolated passive/Boolean models. They are not vendor macro-models
and do not establish PVT, EMC, common-mode, internal MAX switch, cable, or
physical performance.

The DC deck reports: 0/10 ohm fault, 100/194.1 ohm clear, 300 ohm/open
approximation fault, and RTD_AVDD=0 fault. The hardware window is a local
REFIN-/ISENSOR detector; it is not used to classify an isolated RTDIN sense
open.

The selected source values are paired 1 Mohm pullups (Yageo RC0603JR-071ML)
and a 1 nF C0G differential capacitor (KEMET C0603C102J5GACTU). The RC deck
sweeps 950k/1M/1.05M, 950p/1n/1.05n, 0/200pF added harness/PCB capacitance,
and explicit MAX input-leakage corners of -14/+14 nA. At the slowest corner
(1.05 Mohm, 1.05 nF + 200 pF, +14 nA), the positive SENSE+ deck measures
1.607941 V and the matched SENSE- deck measures -1.607941 V at 30, 85, and
95 ms. The 30 ms point is the earliest continuous-conversion launch after
20 ms BIAS startup and the one-time automatic fault cycle.

The worst temperature-adjusted RC is 1.322753 ms using +100 ppm/C pullup TCR,
+30 ppm/C C0G coefficient, +/-5% values, and <=200 pF parasitic. The MAX note
requires 10.5 tau for 15-bit settling, giving 13.8889 ms. It is settled before
the 30 ms startup launch; runtime classification uses the separate phase model
and does not incorrectly substitute the endpoint voltage for a converter sample.
The MAX gives 16.7..17.6 ms continuous periods and 52..55 ms first-continuous
(single-conversion) time, labels the block a digital SINC filter, but publishes
no order or step response. The phase model is therefore conditional.

The paired healthy-load deck includes the 430 ohm RREF/RTD path, 1 ohm sense
lead/contact resistance, 1 Mohm pullup corners, and independent +/-14 nA input
leakage. At RTD=194.1 ohm its worst recorded differential error is 0.1845 mV,
about 0.084 ohm using the local voltage slope (0.22 °C). This added network
error is included in the normal 100 °C accuracy ledger; it is not excluded as
a diagnostic-only term. The selected RREF is Susumu RG2012V-431-W-T1 (430 ohm,
0.05%, 5 ppm/°C, 0805, 1/8 W). At the required steady-state 100 °C, the
declared linear allocation is Class A probe 0.35 °C + MAX ADC 0.50 °C + RREF
initial 0.18 °C + RREF drift over 60 °C 0.11 °C + network 0.22 °C +
self-heating/contact 0.10 °C = 1.46 °C. The bounded extended 250 °C total is
1.87 °C; that range is outside the product accuracy criterion. MAX gain/offset
correlation, temperature correlation, connector effects, and EMC are outside
this model and remain acceptance items.

## Phase-sampled continuous-filter model result

The vendor facts are MAX31865 continuous 60 Hz period 16.7..17.6 ms, first
continuous conversion 52..55 ms, and a block labeled digital SINC filter. The
vendor does not state filter order, step response, phase reset, or transition
sample acceptance. The companion `rtd_sinc_phase_model.txt` therefore applies a
conditional causal third-order boxcar model with T=17.6 ms.

The force/RREF path remains intact for an isolated sense break, so normalized
ADC ratio `r=RRTD/RREF` is used, with conservative clipping to 0..1. SENSE+
from 100 ohm (`r0=100/430`) to clipped full scale crosses 300 ohm (`r=300/430`)
at 28.9116 ms. At 194.1 ohm the corresponding crossing is 25.1988 ms. SENSE-
from 194.1 ohm (`r0=194.1/430`) to clipped code 0 crosses the 10 ohm low
boundary at 40.8998 ms; the datasheet's negative-input code-0 rule is also
applied. With one <=17.6 ms continuous sample phase, <=10 ms poll including
scheduling jitter, <=5 ms sink, and 13.8889 ms RC accounting, the conditional
runtime envelope is 87.3887 ms. This is a model result, not vendor-guaranteed
timing or physical proof.
