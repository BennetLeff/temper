# Zapote passive loss/cooling data (2026-09-16)

Corrected profile: 120 Vrms input, 15 A true-RMS, about 390 V DC bus, Würth 760800301 at 180 uH; FREQ resistor 16.2 kohm sets approximately 130 kHz. The initial research brief incorrectly said 330 uH; that value is not used. Values below are manufacturer ratings/typicals, not guarantees at this operating point. No extrapolation of curves was made.

## Major heat terms still unquantified

* 760800301 core loss and hot copper loss at actual 130 kHz ripple/current waveform (datasheet has no loss curves).
* Bridge rectifier forward-drop/conduction loss and boost switch/diode switching and conduction losses.
* B82726 CMC high-frequency copper/core loss and temperature rise when carrying the rectified 15 Arms.
* Nichicon bank ESR and ripple-current sharing at the PFC switching envelope; film-capacitor ESR/AC loss for B32672P6474K000.
* RT33K012 contact resistance/heating at 15 A and relay terminal losses (coil 0.4 W is quantified).
* PCB copper, fuse/holder, NTC hot-state loss beyond the 0.05 Ω test point, and shunt temperature coefficient/pulse SOA.
