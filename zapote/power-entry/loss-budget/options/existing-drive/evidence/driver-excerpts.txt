TI UCC28180D, printed p.17, section 8.3.15:
  internal clamp limits MOSFET gate to 15.2 V typical; external RGATE is
  selected for rise/fall control, ringing and EMI; 10 kOhm gate-to-ground
  resistor discharges stray capacitance and prevents dv/dt turn-on.

TI UCC28180D, printed p.26, section 9.2.2.8:
  design example uses 3.3 Ohm RGATE and a standard 40 V, 1 A Schottky diode
  anti-parallel with RGATE to facilitate fast turn-off; 10 kOhm gate-ground
  resistor is retained.
