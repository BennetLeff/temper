4. WHY R*C IS NOT THE CONDITION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At the envelope corner R = 5 mOhm, L = 20 uH (C = 2240.47 uF):
    R*C  = 1.120e-5 s = 11.20 us
    L/R  = 4.000e-3 s = 4.00  ms       (L/R : R*C = 357x)
    2L/R = 8.000e-3 s = 8.00  ms       (2L/R : R*C = 714x)
    zeta = (R/2)*sqrt(C/L) = 0.02646   (heavily underdamped -- not an RC decay)

The dispatch and the AR-COORD receipt cite a 714x difference at this corner; that
factor corresponds to 2L/R, not L/R. Either way R*C understates the manufacturer's
L/R time constant by more than two orders of magnitude, and the damping factor
shows the discharge is oscillatory, so an RC-decay reading does not describe the
waveform. AR-COORD's R*C <= 1.43 ms comparison against the 2.5 ms figure is
withdrawn.
