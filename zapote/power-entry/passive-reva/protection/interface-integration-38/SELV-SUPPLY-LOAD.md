# Rev38 SELV 3.3 V load and startup inventory

**Status:** engineering screen, 2026-09-24. The existing cooker ESP32-S3 is the selected Rev38 **command source**. Its present SELV 3.3 V rail is an unqualified supply candidate, not part of that command-source decision. The Rev38-side 16-contact header is joined in `PowerEntryIntegrated38`; a separate `cooker-mate` Atopile derivative joins its mate to the existing cooker ESP and rail. Neither native board nor harness is integrated. The `source-build-03` Rev38 export has no second ESP. This record does not establish spare capacity on the existing cooker rail. Its Rev38 inventory is the frozen `source-build-03` source and resolved export; the complete cooker load still needs a measured budget.

`SELV-PORT-CONTRACT.md` records the candidate 3.0–3.6 V board-input envelope
and a **100 mA Rev38-only design allocation**. That allocation is not a
measured maximum or proof of spare current on the cooker rail.

## Joined Rev38 load

The figures below are **supply-design inputs**, not an accepted whole-board maximum. Device-current test conditions differ, and output pull resistors, switching capacitance, RF bursts, internal module capacitance, power sequencing and the cooker-mate reset/interlock producers still need a complete corner sum.

The cooker-mate derivative adds one TPS389001DSER, one SN74LVC1G17,
one SN74LVC1G04 and two SN74LVC1G08 packages on the **existing cooker**
3.3 V rail. Five local 100 nF bypass capacitors add **0.5 µF nominal**
direct rail capacitance, so the Rev38 plus mate increment is 1.9 µF
nominal before harness and effective-capacitance corrections. The
supervisor's 100 nF CT capacitor is on its timing pin, not directly across
the rail. At nominal 3.3 V the 16 kΩ/10 kΩ sense divider draws about
0.127 mA; the 10 kΩ reset pull-up draws up to 0.33 mA while RESET is low,
and the GPIO15 pull-down draws up to 0.33 mA while runaway cut is high.
Each Rev38 10 kΩ bias draws about 0.33 mA when its cooker-side producer is
high. These are separate state-dependent resistor figures, not a coincident
maximum. Gate supply current, switching power and output loading still need
the selected datasheet corners and a measured load-state sum; see
[`SOURCE-RESET-INTERLOCK.md`](SOURCE-RESET-INTERLOCK.md).

The existing cooker ESP still requires a supply capable of at least 0.5 A under its specified conditions, independent of the Rev38 connector current. [Espressif module datasheet, §§6.2–6.4](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-s3-wroom-1_wroom-1u_datasheet_en.pdf) At the data-sheet 1 Mbps/15 pF isolator example, the two Rev38 SELV isolator sides alone add 9.5 mA. This is not a certified simultaneous peak for the Rev38 port. Measure cooker rail and port current and minimum voltage during RF transmit, normal cooking, startup, brownout/recovery, one-shot activity, I²C traffic and maximum asserted pull loads; then replace these inputs with an accepted envelope.

## Selected reuse of the existing cooker supply, pending integration

`elec/src/main.ato` already connects an IRM-10-15 isolated 15 V source through `PowerManagement`'s LMR51430XDDCR buck to the cooker `+3V3` net, and bonds its SELV return `gnd` to PE. Mean Well rates IRM-10-15 at **15 V, 0.67 A, 10.05 W** before installation/ambient derating; TI rates the LMR51430 for **up to 3 A output**, which does not increase the available upstream 15 V power. [Mean Well IRM-10 series data sheet](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/IRM-10/IRM-10-SPEC.PDF); [TI LMR51430 product and data sheet](https://www.ti.com/product/LMR51430).

The existing `+3V3` already feeds **one cooker ESP32-S3**, half-bridge 3.3 V controls, CT bias, RTD sensing/reference, and `SafetyInterlock` (OCP/OVP/thermal/coil-thermal comparators, second OCP, TPS3823 watchdog, logic UVLO and several HC logic packages) through `main.ato`. The same IRM 15 V output also feeds the half-bridge/driver and fan/thermal circuitry and relay controls. Their complete maximum and coincident startup demands are **not** bounded by this document. The cooker-board mate and its physical producers are compiled as a source candidate but remain unbuilt, so the two source trees are not yet an integrated assembly BOM. The current circuits do not prove spare power.

The reuse gate is an actual per-rail current/energy worksheet and capture: `P_15V,total = P_15V,existing direct + (P_3V3,existing + P_3V3,Rev38)/η_buck`, including the one cooker ESP, and the IRM's ambient/line/installation derating. Check 15 V sag, buck current limit, thermal rise, soft-start and recovery while relays/fan/gate-drive and RF transients overlap. Use measured efficiency at the selected load, not a blanket efficiency assumption. The existing 3.3 V output capacitors (2 × 22 µF nominal) and MCU local capacitance contribute to shared startup, but their effective values, IRM startup and buck soft-start must be checked together. A data-sheet output-current label alone is not a startup proof.

## Interface and rail-order requirements

**Selected reuse path:** Specify a rated board-to-board connector and pinout for `+3V3`, SELV return/PE reference, GPIO13/21/48/18/40/41/42, I²C GPIO38/39, and the joined reset-good and interlock signals. Include return pins and current/temperature/contact-resistance derating, retention, mis-mate protection, harness length, filter/decoupling at the Rev38 end, inrush, and an accessible current-measurement point. The connector must preserve the declared isolation and creepage barrier. The existing ESP is the Rev38 source; `PIN-INTERFACE-CONTRACT.md` records the GPIO and bus ownership reconciliation still required. `SOURCE_RESET_GOOD` is now driven in the cooker-mate source by supervised rail/EN release; it does not detect internal CPU-only reset, which remains under the separate TPS3431 watchdog contract. `SOURCE_INTERLOCK_N` is derived from the cooker fault latch with reset-request qualification. Both need physical open/short and startup qualification.

**Independent path:** Select and join a dedicated isolated SELV supply/regulator or a specified external isolated SELV input, with its own fuse/protection, rated connector, return path and PE-reference decision. Its output must meet the ESP 3.0–3.6 V range and all other device limits at the Rev38 pins over startup, line, load and fault transients. A second isolation module is not automatically needed if the existing qualified SELV rail is intentionally shared; if separate, verify isolation from mains/HOT and avoid a second uncontrolled PE bond or ground loop.

For either path, define and measure order of `SELV3V3`, ESP EN/reset, expander VCCI/VCCP and RESET_N, both isolators' VCC1 versus HOT VCC2, watchdog/supervisor release, `SOURCE_RESET_GOOD`, `SOURCE_INTERLOCK_N`, and HOT rail readiness. The expander can retain outputs through an ESP-only reset, and powered I/O can back-power an unpowered device; bound those partial-power states from each data sheet and test them. All PERMIT, START and relay-request outputs must remain physically inactive during every ramp, drop, partial-power and reset path. No 3.3 V supply selection or authorization claim follows from this load inventory until those checks pass.
