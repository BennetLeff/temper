# Rev38 SELV 3.3 V load and startup inventory

**Status:** engineering screen, 2026-09-23. No `SELV3V3` producer or power connector is joined in `PowerEntryIntegrated38`; this record does not select a supply or establish spare capacity on the existing cooker rail. The source inventory is the live Rev38 `elec/src/{power_entry_integrated_38,source_mcu,source_authority,receiver_isolation}.ato`, not the frozen `source-build-01` copy. A refreshed generated BOM/netlist must be used before release.

## Joined Rev38 load

The figures below are **supply-design inputs**, not an accepted whole-board maximum. Device-current test conditions differ, and output pull resistors, switching capacitance, RF bursts, internal module capacitance, power sequencing and the unjoined reset/interlock producers still need a complete corner sum.

The ESP module's internal capacitance, any connector/input filter, and capacitor tolerance/effective capacitance are not bounded here. Initial capacitive current is `C_effective × dV/dt`; for the 1.4 µF explicit direct bank alone, a linear 3.3 V rise in 1 ms would average about 4.6 mA. That illustration is not a startup-current limit. |

The first **output-capacity floor** is therefore the ESP's 0.5 A requirement **plus** the other loads, rather than 0.5 A for the complete candidate. At the data-sheet 1 Mbps/15 pF isolator example, the two SELV isolator sides alone add 9.5 mA; the deliberately all-low rail-pull illustration adds about 5.3 mA. Those figures cannot be summed into a certified peak because the ESP has no all-condition current maximum here, output and switching loads are incomplete, and their timing may coincide. A 0.55 A provisional allocation for the Rev38 portion is a useful **test point**, not a rated requirement or a claim of headroom. Measure rail current and minimum voltage during RF transmit, normal cooking, startup, brownout/recovery, one-shot activity, I²C traffic and maximum asserted pull loads; then replace it with an accepted envelope.

## Candidate reuse of the existing cooker supply

`elec/src/main.ato` already connects an IRM-10-15 isolated 15 V source through `PowerManagement`'s LMR51430XDDCR buck to the cooker `+3V3` net, and bonds its SELV return `gnd` to PE. Mean Well rates IRM-10-15 at **15 V, 0.67 A, 10.05 W** before installation/ambient derating; TI rates the LMR51430 for **up to 3 A output**, which does not increase the available upstream 15 V power. [Mean Well IRM-10 series data sheet](https://www.meanwell.com/Upload/PDF/IRM-10/IRM-10-SPEC.PDF); [TI LMR51430 product and data sheet](https://www.ti.com/product/LMR51430).

The existing `+3V3` already feeds **one cooker ESP32-S3**, half-bridge 3.3 V controls, CT bias, RTD sensing/reference, and `SafetyInterlock` (OCP/OVP/thermal/coil-thermal comparators, second OCP, TPS3823 watchdog, logic UVLO and several HC logic packages) through `main.ato` lines 798, 831, 838, 858 and 919. The same IRM 15 V output also feeds the half-bridge/driver and fan/thermal circuitry and relay controls. Their complete maximum and coincident startup demands are **not** bounded by this document. The Rev38 `source_mcu.esp` is currently a *separate instantiated module*; if the product integration reuses the cooker ESP, prove it is one physical device and reconcile pins before counting its 0.5 A capacity reservation once. If both modules remain physical, reserve for **two** ESP supplies and both associated operating loads. The current circuits do not prove spare power under either interpretation.

The reuse gate is an actual per-rail current/energy worksheet and capture: `P_15V,total = P_15V,existing direct + P_3V3,existing/η_buck + P_3V3,Rev38/η_buck`, including any second ESP, and the IRM's ambient/line/installation derating. Check 15 V sag, buck current limit, thermal rise, soft-start and recovery while relays/fan/gate-drive and RF transients overlap. Use measured efficiency at the selected load, not a blanket efficiency assumption. The existing 3.3 V output capacitors (2 × 22 µF nominal) and MCU local capacitance contribute to shared startup, but their effective values, IRM startup and buck soft-start must be checked together. A data-sheet output-current label alone is not a startup proof.

## Interface and rail-order requirements

**Reuse path:** Specify a rated board-to-board connector and pinout for `+3V3`, SELV return/PE reference, and any intentionally shared reset-good and interlock signals. Include return pins and current/temperature/contact-resistance derating, retention, mis-mate protection, harness length, filter/decoupling at the Rev38 end, inrush, and an accessible current-measurement point. The connector must preserve the declared isolation and creepage barrier. Resolve whether the existing ESP is the Rev38 source before connecting either GPIO bank. A conductor named `SOURCE_RESET_GOOD` must be driven by a physical CPU/reset-valid producer; EN high or WDO high alone is not that proof. `SOURCE_INTERLOCK_N` likewise needs a selected independent producer and defined open/short behavior.

**Independent path:** Select and join a dedicated isolated SELV supply/regulator or a specified external isolated SELV input, with its own fuse/protection, rated connector, return path and PE-reference decision. Its output must meet the ESP 3.0–3.6 V range and all other device limits at the Rev38 pins over startup, line, load and fault transients. A second isolation module is not automatically needed if the existing qualified SELV rail is intentionally shared; if separate, verify isolation from mains/HOT and avoid a second uncontrolled PE bond or ground loop.

For either path, define and measure order of `SELV3V3`, ESP EN/reset, expander VCCI/VCCP and RESET_N, both isolators' VCC1 versus HOT VCC2, watchdog/supervisor release, `SOURCE_RESET_GOOD`, `SOURCE_INTERLOCK_N`, and HOT rail readiness. The expander can retain outputs through an ESP-only reset, and powered I/O can back-power an unpowered device; bound those partial-power states from each data sheet and test them. All PERMIT, START and relay-request outputs must remain physically inactive during every ramp, drop, partial-power and reset path. No 3.3 V supply selection or authorization claim follows from this load inventory until those checks pass.
