# Rev38 footprint-library parity diagnostic

**Status: diagnostic only, 2026-09-24. U7 native acceptance OPEN.** This
repeats the temporary `source-build-05` generation with the grid-corrected
flat schematic from `NATIVE-DIAGNOSTIC-06.md`, the same unreviewed shelf poses,
and a candidate bridge repair for library footprint serialization. It does
not release a placed or routed section board.

KiCad 10.0.4 identified two source-to-board serialization differences behind
all 14 prior `lib_footprint_mismatch` reports. For 12 through-hole instances,
kiutils interpreted explicit library `(remove_unused_layers no)` as true and
serialized bare `(remove_unused_layers)` on the board. For two DWW isolators
placed at 180°, the imported child pad angles remained at their unrotated
values. The strict candidate bridge now preserves the explicit false pad
flag and writes absolute child pad angles on rotated footprints. Focused
tests cover a through-hole pad and asymmetric 180° pad angles.
An independent KiCad 10.0.4 `pcbnew` check on a temporary asymmetric
45° placement with local pad angles 0° and 30° found board pad angles
45° and 75° and `FootprintNeedsUpdate == false`. This probes a
non-orthogonal case that the 180° regression alone would miss.

KiCad's `pcbnew.FOOTPRINT.FootprintNeedsUpdate` now reports **0 of 295**
footprints needing a library update on the temporary board. `kicad-cli pcb
drc --schematic-parity --severity-all` reports **zero schematic-parity
issues**, **22 DRC violations** and **499 unconnected items** (the report
cap). The DRC types are 10 `clearance`, 8 `drill_out_of_range`, 3
`silk_over_copper` and 1 `silk_overlap`. The 14 library mismatches were
eliminated without altering source nets or accepting a lower board rule.
The grid-corrected schematic still has 383 ERC warnings; neither ERC nor
DRC passes. No functional route or insulation construction is present.
