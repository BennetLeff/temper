# Rev38 small-package footprint closeout

This record checks the selected MPN against manufacturer package/pin drawings and the installed KiCad 10 footprint geometry. It is a schematic-to-land-pattern decision, not assembly or electrical qualification.

| Integrated ref | MPN | Decision | Evidence and remaining check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U72, U137 | TPS3431SDRBR | `Package_DFN_QFN:Texas_DRB0008A` | TI specifies DRB0008A, 3 × 3 mm VSON, eight 0.65 mm-pitch perimeter contacts and exposed pad 9. Installed KiCad `Texas_DRB0008A` has pins 1–8 at 0.65 mm pitch, a 1.5 × 1.75 mm center copper pad numbered 9, plus pad-9 thermal vias. Pin 1 VDD through pin 8 ENOUT and EP9 match the Atopile declaration. Via fill/plug choice, solder paste, and assembly inspection remain for the native board. |
| U291 | TCA6408AQPWRQ1 | `Package_SO:TSSOP-16_4.4x5mm_P0.65mm` | TI lists exact order code as 16-pin PW0016A. Its package drawing has 4.3–4.5 mm body width, 4.9–5.1 mm body length, 0.65 mm lead pitch; installed KiCad footprint explicitly tags `Texas_PW0016A`, with pins 1–16 in correct counterclockwise top-view order. TI pin table agrees with all 16 Atopile pins. Board orientation and assembly inspection remain. |
| U134 | RC1206FR-0710RL | `Resistor_SMD:R_1206_3216Metric_Pad1.30x1.75mm_HandSolder` | Yageo's exact part is a two-terminal 10 Ω, 1%, 0.25 W, 1206/3216 chip, body 3.1 ± 0.1 × 1.6 ± 0.1 mm. KiCad's 1206 hand-solder land has two 1.30 × 1.75 mm pads centered ±1.55 mm, compatible with that package. This distinct valid land variant is necessary because Atopile 0.2.69 aliases unlike MPNs on the same standard 1206 footprint (the 10 kΩ EN pull-up otherwise absorbs this 10 Ω part). Pulse power, gate-loop impedance, and thermal qualification remain open. |
| U130 | UCC27624DDAR | `Package_SO:SOIC-8-1EP_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm_EP2.95x4.9mm_Mask2.71x3.4mm` | TI specifies DDA0008B, 8-pin PowerPAD SOIC with EP9, 3.8–4.0 × 4.8–5.0 mm body and 1.27 mm lead pitch. The installed KiCad footprint's official generator source `data/package/gullwing/soic.yaml` names **TI DDA0008B** as its basis. It uses TI's 2.95 × 4.9 mm copper land under a 2.71 × 3.4 mm mask opening, four paste windows, and pins 1–8 at 1.27 mm pitch; EP9 matches Atopile. Native board still needs creepage, thermal-via and stencil/assembly review. |
