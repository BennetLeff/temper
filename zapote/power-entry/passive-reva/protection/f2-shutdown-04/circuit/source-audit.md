# F2 default-OFF Rev B audit (2026-09-19)

This is a read-only review of `/private/tmp/temper-f2b-circuit/elec/src/power_entry_f2_shutdown_revb.ato`.  The part-level claims below use the manufacturers' primary data sheets, especially [UCC27511A](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ucc27511a.pdf), [TPS3890](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3890.pdf), [SN74HCS74](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74hcs74.pdf), [TLV3202](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3202.pdf), [SN74LVC1G08](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g08.pdf), and [BC817](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/BC817_SER.pdf).

## What is sound

The revised choice of UCC27511A is materially better than direct UCC27624 ENA control.  Its IN+ is active-high and IN- is active-low; the data sheet specifies internal biasing so a floating input holds the output low.  A transistor pulling IN- low while RUN is asserted therefore gives a specified gate-driver interface, and a dead logic rail cannot command a high output.  Keep that architecture.

The eight 22-kohm comparator input resistors are a credible first clamp-current limit for a charged bus with logic5 at zero.  TLV3202 input clamp current is rated at +/-10 mA; even a 2.4-V input with VCC=0 gives approximately (2.4-0.5)/22k = 86 uA per input.  This bounds the fault, but the sum of all input and LM4040 paths still needs a regulator/back-power measurement.  Do not call the unpowered comparator harmless solely from the resistor value.

## Blocking corrections in the source

* `driver.OUTL` (UCC27511A pin 3) is unconnected.  The gate is connected only to OUTH through 10 ohms and a 10-kohm pulldown.  OUTL is the specified sink path; leaving it open makes turn-off depend on a 10-kohm resistor and bus/gate charge.  Connect OUTL to `gate` through an explicit turn-off resistor (and retain the pulldown as a backup), then measure the actual gate fall and F2 current decay.  A gate-driver propagation number alone is not a shutdown-time guarantee.
* `driver.INP ~ pwm` creates a direct net alias in Atopile.  The subsequent 1-kohm `pwm_iso` is therefore bypassed.  Remove the direct connection and retain only `pwm -> pwm_iso -> INP` (with the 10-kohm INP pulldown).  If the PWM source can remain high while aux is absent, add a source-side current limit and verify UCC27511A input injection against its absolute maximum.
* Add a real external pull-up from `INN` to aux (10 kohm is a reasonable starting value) and keep the BC817 collector on INN.  The internal pull-up establishes the logic function but has no guaranteed minimum resistance/current in the table; an external pull-up bounds release leakage and the BC817 sink current (~1.5 mA at 15 V).  Confirm the chosen BC817 hFE and VCE(sat) at that current.
* The two TPS3890 CT capacitors are now wired correctly to ground, but `1 uF` is not a 26-us delay.  TI specifies `tPD(r)(s) = CCT( uF ) * 1.07 + 25 us`; 1 uF is about **1.070 s**.  Choose the hold intentionally: approximately 100 pF gives 132 us and 1 nF gives 1.095 ms (subject to tolerance/leakage), or leave CT open for the minimum delay.  Update the comment and verify the selected startup/brownout budget.
* The TPS3890 sense dividers are now ratio-correct: 294k/100k gives about 4.53 V at the 1.15-V nominal threshold, and 1.03 Mohm/100k gives about 12.9 V.  The earlier extra 100-kohm lower-leg mistake is absent in this revision.  Keep the 47-kohm SENSE series resistors and verify their effect over the full SENSE leakage/threshold-current limits.
* `PRE1 ~ logic5` and `CLR1 ~ clear_ok` are simultaneously low during early logic5 ramp.  SN74HCS74 prohibits asserting PRE and CLR together and does not guarantee Q at power-up.  Sequence PRE high before releasing CLR (for example, a defined RC/Schmitt qualification or a supervisor-derived preset inhibit), or produce bench evidence that Q cannot enable the BC817 during this overlap.  A 100-kohm base pulldown is not a proof of the HCS74 output state.
* `arm` is wired directly to a clock input and `permit` directly to an HCS21 input.  If either signal can be high while logic5 is absent, its input clamp can back-power the unpowered CMOS.  Either make the source contract explicit (both are guaranteed low unless logic5 is valid) or add series resistors and calculate the worst-case clamp current.  The same review applies to any external PWM high during aux-only operation.

## Minimal closure recommendation

Keep UCC27511A + BC817.  Correct the OUTL and PWM net errors, add the explicit INN pull-up, add bounded ARM/permit/PWM injection paths, and fix the TPS3890 CT value/comment.  Then choose one explicit dropout policy: either add a fast sticky aux-fault latch that asynchronously clears RUN, or state and verify an aux-off/hold-up interval long enough for TPS3890 to assert RESET before aux can return.  In both cases, qualify the HCS74 so PRE is high before CLR is released and demonstrate that a held ARM cannot restart the gate; only a fresh ARM edge may do so.

The report does not certify F2 shutdown timing.  With OUTL open and the PWM resistor bypassed, the current source does not yet provide a bounded turn-off path or a bounded input-isolation path.

## Addendum: bounded fast aux-loss reset

The remaining auto-rearm hole can be closed without pretending that a TPS3890 catches a one-microsecond event.  Define the event electrically as: **aux falls below 4.3 V (below the UCC27511A UVLO range) while logic5 remains above 4.5 V for at least 1 us**.  A dedicated fast comparator powered from logic5 can detect that event and asynchronously clear RUN; its clear is retained by the HCS74 until a new ARM edge.

A concrete small circuit is:

* Add one TLV3202 channel (or a pin-compatible single-channel TI comparator) powered from logic5.  Make a logic5-powered 0.8-V reference from a second LM4040A25 and a resistor divider; do not use the existing aux-powered reference, which disappears exactly when aux is lost.
* Divide aux with 430 kohm over 100 kohm (approximately 0.189 ratio).  The comparator trips at about 0.8/0.189 = 4.2 V, while the divider is only 2.84 V at aux=15 V and remains inside the TLV3202 input common-mode range.  Put the 0.8-V reference on IN+ and aux sense on IN-.  Comparator OUT goes high when aux is low.  Do not add a large filter capacitor: the divider's roughly 70-kohm source resistance and comparator input capacitance give sub-microsecond response; qualify the actual 1-us pulse on the bench.
* Drive a small NMOS gate from OUT through 1 kohm with a 100-kohm gate pulldown.  NMOS source goes to ground and drain goes to a new `latch_clr` node.  Feed existing `clear_ok` to `latch.CLR1` through 10 kohm, and connect the NMOS drain to `latch.CLR1`.  The resistor limits the HCS21 output sink current to about 0.5 mA while the fast path is active.  On aux loss, the NMOS clamps CLR low in tens of nanoseconds; when aux returns it turns off, but Q is already cleared, so held ARM/PWM cannot restart the driver.
* Keep the TPS3890 `sup_aux` path as the slow rail qualification/startup debounce.  It is still needed for the normal `clear_ok` level, but it is no longer the only mechanism expected to catch a short aux dropout.  Tie the unused comparator channel's inputs to defined rails and leave its output unconnected.

This adds one comparator package, one logic5 reference, one NMOS, and the divider/resistors, but gives a finite, testable contract: an aux excursion that crosses 4.2 V and lasts 1 us clears RUN; no fresh ARM edge means no restart.  Measure the trip threshold over tolerances, comparator propagation, NMOS clamp voltage, and the minimum pulse width that reliably clears HCS74 CLR.

### PRE/CLR qualification wording

The PRE1=logic5 connection is not automatically a datasheet violation during ordinary operation: PRE is an unused asynchronous input and tying it high is the normal hook-up once VCC is valid.  The concern is the **power-up interval**, where PRE and CLR can both be low while VCC is below the guaranteed operating range.  SN74HCS74 does not promise Q during that interval, and its truth table prohibits simultaneous assertion of PRE and CLR.  Therefore this must be handled as a sequencing/test requirement, not stated as a guaranteed violation: ensure PRE is high before releasing CLR, or demonstrate with the chosen ramp and a base-gate current probe that Q cannot turn the disable FET on during overlap.

## Fresh review of canonical source (hash `8dd5271c070493a5cfdbbda92810cda7b72ff976bbed563baf9e5bf37f14cf5a`)

The canonical file now has the expected manufacturer pin maps: UCC27511A pins 1/2/3/4/5/6 = VDD/OUTH/OUTL/GND/IN-/IN+; SN74LVC1G08 and SN74LVC1G17 DBVR pinouts; BSS138 pin 1=G, 2=S, 3=D; TPS3890 WSON-6; TLV3202 SOIC-8; LM4040 DBZ; and HCS21/HCS74 TSSOP maps.  OUTL is connected through 10 ohms, PWM is isolated only through its 1-kohm resistor, INN has an aux 10-kohm pull-up, and the reference is powered from logic5.  The `health.D1` connection is still `cmp_vb.OUT2`; the fast comparator is separately connected to `health.D2`, so those outputs are not shorted.  (An earlier note in this report misread D1 as D2; that finding is withdrawn.)

The fast comparator now has the intended polarity: its IN1+ is the 430-kohm/100-kohm aux divider and IN1- is the 2.5-V reference, so its output is high only for a healthy aux rail.  The nominal threshold is 2.5/(100/(430+100)) = 13.25 V.  At aux=15 V the input is about 2.83 V, below the TLV3202 5-V common-mode ceiling; the divider source resistance is about 81 kohm and the 22-kohm series resistor still permits a sub-microsecond transition for a 1-us aux loss.  This catches an aux excursion long before the UCC27511A UVLO and clears the retained latch through the existing HCS21 path.  After aux returns, Q remains cleared and a fresh ARM edge is required.

One startup/brownout hardening remains advisable.  The BSS138 gate is driven from raw `run`, while HCS74 Q is unspecified during the interval where PRE tracks logic5 and CLR is held low, and during a slow logic5 brownout.  Make `enable_good` an Ioff-rated AND of `run` and `clear_ok`, then drive the BSS138 gate from `enable_good`.  This keeps INN high whenever rails, detector, permit, or fast-aux health are not qualified and prevents a Q glitch from enabling an aux-powered driver.  It adds no new timing assumption; verify the resulting gate and INN waveforms with held ARM/PWM during both rail orders and a slow logic5 ramp.

The 100-pF TPS3890 CT capacitors correspond to roughly 132 us nominal reset release by TI's timing law.  They are a startup qualification delay, not a fast-dropout detector; the 13.25-V comparator is the mechanism that gives the finite 1-us aux-loss contract.  Keep the comparator's unused channel inputs tied to defined rails and leave its output unconnected.

### Preferred implementation of the fast-loss path

The simpler implementation proposed after this review supersedes the NMOS clamp above: add one TLV3202 channel powered by logic5, move the existing LM4040A25 bias resistor from aux to logic5 (10 kohm gives about 250 uA), and use a 430-kohm/100-kohm aux divider against the 2.5-V reference.  This comparator is **healthy-high**: at aux=15 V its sense node is about 2.83 V, and its output falls when aux drops below about 13.25 V.  Route that output into the existing HCS21 qualification input currently tied to logic5 (health.D2).  Then a 1-us aux excursion that crosses the UCC UVLO range necessarily creates a low `clear_ok` pulse through the comparator and HCS21; the HCS74 CLR minimum pulse is much shorter than the comparator-plus-gate propagation.  Q is cleared and remains cleared after aux recovery, so held ARM/PWM cannot auto-restart.  This also avoids an NMOS pulling against the HCS21 output.

The 13.25-V threshold is intentionally early: it catches any dropout capable of reaching the driver's approximately 4-V UVLO, rather than trying to place a threshold at the UVLO edge with resistor and comparator tolerances.  It may require a fresh ARM after a benign aux sag, which is the safe failure mode.  At aux=15 V the 2.83-V input remains below the TLV3202's 5-V common-mode ceiling; at aux=0 the comparator output is low.  Define the second comparator channel's inputs and leave its output unconnected.  Bench-check the threshold across resistor/reference tolerances, the actual comparator output polarity, the HCS21 propagation/pulse width, and the resulting fresh-edge behavior.
