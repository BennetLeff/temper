# F2 power-stage experiment

The authoritative13 cases are generated by `run_cases.sh`: one healthy run,
11 F2-open/current/phase/gate-load cases, and one intentionally slow-driver
negative control. Twelve cases pass and the negative control fails the same
2µs/500V checks. The separate `../fault-tests/` owns channel/supply/rearm tests;
legacy ineffective startup/state plant stimuli are excluded from acceptance.

The baseline current labels describe target bands. **Actual current at F2
opening**, not the initial condition, is the evidence:41.658A at100µH,
46.228A at180µH, and51.095A at216µH. Initial conditions are90/74/75A because
the model first establishes an armed switching interval. F2 opens at220µs.
The fixture includes the rising bus voltage from this interval; it does not
silently hold the local node at410V when calculating remaining headroom.

The model initializes prequalified supervisor/divider states, with ARM at21µs.
This is an already-running energy fixture, not a startup qualification test.
Normal nominal rows deliberately use a39ns driver response time constant
(~27ns50% crossing), slower than the isolated fixture's18.75ns (~13ns).
The negative control uses5µs and48nF gate load; doubled_gate_charge uses24nF.

## Timing and voltage observations

The extractor measures the first actual **unfiltered bus threshold crossing**
to sustained controlled channel current below10mA, with Q/enable/gate off.
It requires prior healthy switching, causal F2→threshold→fault→clear→off events,
then retained off state. The fault indicator is active HIGH in this controller.
A normal PWM-off sample cannot satisfy shutdown while the latch remains set.

The high-current baseline delay is about0.505µs. The doubled gate-load case
has gate9.04V and channel48.94A when Q clears, then completes controlled
turnoff0.870µs after threshold. This explicitly exercises a loaded turnoff.
Peak bus voltage remains below478V across accepted cases. The deliberately
slow driver takes about5.074µs and fails. The CSV is the exact-value authority.

Two cases are rerun at1ns rather than2ns maximum step. Delay changes by at
most0.001768µs and peak voltage by0.003743V. `refinement.txt` records both.
The extractor fails if refinement changes delay>0.02µs or peak>0.1V.

## Current and energy definitions

`i(vg_sense)` measures the finite MOS channel; its intrinsic body diode is
suppressed. A separate explicit body diode and current probe own reverse
conduction. The total source-terminal current is measured independently from
voltage across the1mΩ return shunt, including capacitive/body current.

The source terminal continues small passive reverse oscillations after the
channel is off. Their observed peaks are retained in the CSV; terminal-settled
time is null when the10mA criterion is not sustained by the end of the run.
**Channel shutdown does not mean every passive circuit current is zero.**
This idealized low-loss ring is not an avalanche or EMI qualification.

Energy balance starts at F2 opening and includes line-source work, gate-port
work, inductor/bus/explicit-capacitance stored energy, resistor loss, MOS
terminal dissipation and diode net terminal absorption. Diode absorption
includes internal junction storage; it is not a heat-only measurement.
Logic/aux circuits upstream of the gate port are outside this energy boundary.
The residual includes omitted small divider/input capacitor terms and numerical
integration error. It must remain below0.1% of **local19.8µF** headroom to500V.
The disconnected2240µF bank is not credited as an absorption path.

The full-interval energy columns are diagnostic: they omit pre-opening F2
on-state dissipation. Only `fault_energy_residual_J` and its local denominators
are used for closure. A small residual establishes numerical accounting within
the model, not correctness of its device dynamics or real hardware limits.

## Reproduce

From repository root, run:

```sh
sh zapote/power-entry/passive-reva/protection/f2-shutdown-04/plant/run_cases.sh
```

`extract.rs` requires all13 exact scenarios, finite ordered full-span traces,
correct causal events, retained channel shutdown and expected verdicts. It
returns failure if the slow negative unexpectedly passes. `tests.txt` contains
regressions for causal ordering and passive-current/controlled-current separation.
`attempt-initial-model/` contains rejected diagnostic archives.
