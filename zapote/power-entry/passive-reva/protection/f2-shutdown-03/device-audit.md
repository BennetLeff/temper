# F2 emergency-shutdown device audit

Date: 2026-09-19. Read-only review of the retained sources in
`zapote/power-entry/passive-reva/protection/f2-timing-02`. No PCB or repository
files were changed. This is a circuit recommendation and simulation-input
audit; it is not a protection qualification or a physical guarantee.

## Recommended signal path

Use all four comparator channels as independent, push-pull `DET_OK_i` signals
(high means the corresponding voltage/current condition is healthy). Do not
wire comparator outputs together. A channel's input polarity can be chosen so
that the healthy state is high.

| Device | Pin connections (SOIC/TSSOP pin numbers) | Function |
|---|---|---|
| each TLV3202 | 1OUT=1, 1IN-=2, 1IN+=3, GND=4, 2IN+=5, 2IN-=6, 2OUT=7, VCC=8 | Two independent detector channels; VCC is regulated logic 5 V |
| SN74HCS21 gate 1 | 1A=1, 1B=2, 1C=4, 1D=5; 1Y=6 | `DET_ALL_OK = DET_OK_1 & ... & DET_OK_4` |
| SN74HCS21 gate 2 | 2A=9, 2B=10, 2C=12, 2D=13; 2Y=8 | `CLR_N = DET_ALL_OK & rails_ok & permit & 1` (active-low HCS74 clear; the fourth input is tied to logic 5 V because the external `rails_ok` contract includes 5-V/15-V validity and startup reset hold) |
| SN74HCS74 channel 1 | 1CLR=1, 1D=2, 1CLK=3, 1PRE=4, 1Q=5, 1Qbar=6; GND=7, VCC=14 | D=VCC, PRE=VCC, CLK=`fresh_arm`, CLR=`DET_ALL_OK & rails_ok & permit` (active low), Q is retained arm state |
| SN74HCS74 channel 2 | 2CLR=13, 2D=12, 2CLK=11, 2PRE=10 | Tie PRE/CLR high and D/CLK low if unused; leave outputs open |
| UCC27624 channel A | ENA=1, INA=2, GND=3, INB=4, OUTB=5, VDD=6, OUTA=7, ENB=8 | VDD=aux 15 V; INA=controller PWM; ENA is enabled only from retained Q through an isolation stage; INB/ENB=GND; OUTA drives MOSFET |

The raw `fresh_arm` input must be a real low-to-high pulse after all rails are
valid. Keep it separate from the rails/permit gating of the clock. If it stays
high through a fault or an auxiliary-rail return, Q remains cleared because no
new clock edge occurs. This is the key property that prevents held-arm or
power-return auto-restart.

`CLR` is asynchronous and dominant. A detector fault, loss of `rails_ok`, or
loss of `permit` therefore clears Q. When the fault
disappears, Q stays low until a new arm edge. With only two HCS21 gates there
is no third AND gate available to form `Q & DET_ALL_OK & rails_ok & permit`;
do not silently reuse the CLR gate for ENA, because that would let the driver
restart as soon as a fault clears while Q is still low.
Use the second HCS74 channel only if a separately reviewed power-on/arm
handshake is needed; do not use a clock generated by `fresh_arm & rails_ok`,
because a held-high arm would then create a false edge when rails return.

### ENA isolation and default-off details

UCC27624 ENA/ENB have internal pullups (about 200 kOhm typical; no min/max
pullup resistance is specified in the table) and floating EN pins are enabled.
Do not leave ENA floating and do not wire Q directly to ENA. A practical
fail-closed interface is:

* Preferred: use a small NPN/NMOS clamp driven by HCS74 `Qbar` (pin 6) to
  pull ENA hard to UCC GND whenever Q is low, and use a dedicated pullup to a
  qualified 5-V rail only when Q is high. Include a base/gate pulldown and a
  aggregate `rails_ok`-controlled default-off clamp so an unpowered HCS74 cannot leave
  ENA at the UCC internal pullup level. This adds a discrete stage but avoids
  relying on the unspecified EN pullup resistance.
* If the diode-plus-pulldown interface is retained for the prototype, use a
  2.2 kOhm pulldown (not 10 kOhm) and label the low-state calculation as
  typical-only: with REN=200 kOhm typical, ENA is about
  `15 V * 2.2 k / (200 k + 2.2 k) = 0.16 V`; the data sheet gives no REN
  minimum, so this is not a guaranteed low threshold. The Schottky still
  blocks 15-V back-power into the HCS74 output when VCC5 is absent.
* Drive INA from PWM through a small series resistor (about 1 kOhm) and add a
  local 10 kOhm pulldown. The driver's internal 120-kOhm pulldown is not a
  substitute for a board-level default.
* OUTA pin 7 -> 10 Ohm external gate resistor -> STW65N65DM2AG gate. Place a
  10 kOhm gate-to-source pulldown at the MOSFET. Decouple UCC VDD locally with
  at least 1 uF and 0.1 uF. Keep the gate loop short and use a Kelvin source
  return if the layout permits.
* Tie UCC INB and ENB to GND and leave OUTB open when channel B is unused.
  UCC UVLO holds outputs low during VDD startup/shutdown, but this must be
  treated as a second safeguard, not as the retained fault latch.

The HCS21/HCS74 outputs are 0-to-VCC devices. `rails_ok` and `permit` must be
5-V-safe logic signals (or divided/Schmitt-buffered); never apply aux15
directly to their pins.

## Startup, dropout, and back-power review

The HCS74 data sheet does not promise a power-up Q state. The stated external
`rails_ok` contract must hold `CLR` low until logic 5 V is valid, aux15 is
valid, comparator outputs are valid, and the controller has held reset/arm low.
The controller must keep `fresh_arm` low during this interval and issue a new
pulse only after reset release. If the actual `rails_ok` implementation does
not provide that aggregate startup behavior, add a real reset supervisor; this
is a candidate blocker for any no-auto-restart claim.

With a charged F2-side bus and VCC5=0, TLV3202 input pins have rail clamps.
The retained divider is roughly 1 MOhm over the high side and 5.62 kOhm at the
bottom; around a 2.45-V sense node, a dead comparator rail can receive about
`(2.45 - 0.5)/5.6 k = 0.35 mA` through its input clamp. This is below the
TLV3202's 10-mA input-clamp current limit, but it is still back-powering and
the comparator is outside its recommended supply condition. Add an explicit
series/isolation resistor or a low-leakage clamp/buffer and verify the actual
threshold shift. The same check is required for any externally powered
`rails_ok`, `permit`, PWM, or comparator output entering an unpowered HCS
device. HCS input/output absolute clamp limits are not an operating mode.

Recommended fault/dropout simulations (all with PWM high where relevant):

1. VCC5=VDD=0, bulk VD already charged, and `fresh_arm` held high: gate remains
   off; Q and ENA must be low.
2. Ramp VCC5 then aux15 with `fresh_arm` still high: no gate output; only a
   subsequent low-to-high arm pulse may set Q.
3. Nominal PWM and Q=1, then assert one comparator fault: `DET_ALL_OK` falls,
   `CLR_N` falls, Q clears, the retained-state ENA isolation stage disables
   the driver, and VGS discharges.
   Clear the fault while PWM and arm remain high: Q/ENA must stay low.
4. Drop aux15 or `rails_ok` while PWM is high, then restore it with arm held
   high: no restart.
5. Assert each detector individually while the bus follows the F2-open
   VD/inductor trajectory; confirm no push-pull output contention and that a
   fault pulse meets HCS74 CLR's minimum pulse width (11 ns at 4.5 V).

These tests establish logic behavior only. They do not establish the F2
voltage envelope or a guaranteed MOSFET current-clearing time.

## Published limits and timing applicability

The retained manufacturer PDFs are under
`zapote/power-entry/passive-reva/protection/f2-timing-02/sources/`:

* TLV3202: VCC 2.7--5.5 V; input common-mode extends 0.2 V beyond rails;
  inputs are rail-clamped. At VCC=5 V, tPD max is 55 ns over -40..125 C for
  the 20-mV overdrive/15-pF table. This is a comparator-only maximum under its
  test fixture.
* SN74HCS21: VCC 2--6 V; two independent positive 4-input AND gates with
  Schmitt inputs. At VCC=4.5 V and 50 pF, tPD max is 22 ns over temperature.
  Inputs and outputs have clamps; recommended input range is 0..VCC.
* SN74HCS74: VCC 2--6 V; active-low asynchronous PRE/CLR and positive-edge
  clock. At 4.5 V/50 pF, PRE/CLR-to-Q and CLK-to-Q tPD max are 19 ns; CLR/PRE
  low pulse minimum is 11 ns. The function table explicitly says PRE=CLR=low
  is nonstable. Keep PRE high and ensure the reset pulse is valid.
* UCC27624: VDD 4.5--26 V, UVLO rising max 4.4 V and falling min 3.5 V;
  INA/INB and ENA/ENB accept -10..26 V. Inputs have about 120-kOhm pulldowns;
  EN pins have about 200-kOhm pullups and therefore float enabled. At VDD=12 V
  and 1.8-nF load, disable propagation max is 27 ns and output fall max is
  14 ns. The output-current headline (5 A) is not a specified end-to-end
  gate-discharge time.
* STW65N65DM2AG: VDS 650 V; ID 60 A at Tcase=25 C and 38 A at Tcase=100 C;
  IDM 240 A but pulse width is SOA-limited; VGS +/-25 V; Ptot 446 W at
  Tcase=25 C; avalanche IAR 8 A and single-pulse EAS 1100 mJ under the
  stated 25-C/50-V test. Ciss=5500 pF, Coss=210 pF, Crss=3 pF, Qg=120 nC
  and Qgd=58 nC are typical values under the listed test conditions, not
  maximums. Switching td(off)=114 ns and tf=11.5 ns are typical at 325 V,
  30 A, 4.7-Ohm gate resistor, and 10-V drive. They do not bound a 432--500 V,
  50-A, 15-V-drive F2 event.

The published logic numbers can show that the IC-only portion is on the order
of a few hundred ns, but their conditions differ and must not be added as a
qualified 2-us end-to-end limit. Divider/filter delay, comparator overdrive,
fault pulse width, loaded UCC output, MOSFET Miller charge, source inductance,
and current cessation remain open. The existing 50-A / 100--216-uH / 19.8-uF
screen therefore remains conditional: approximately 3.0 us from the chosen
static-threshold point, approximately 2.53 us after the stated 20-mV reserve,
and 2 us as a provisional target only.

PSpice ZIP availability is not the same as a plain-text ngspice model. Before
claiming a simulation result, retain each archive and extracted subcircuit,
record whether `.SUBCKT` text is encrypted, run a minimal pin-order test, and
compare model supply/startup behavior against the datasheet. If the exact ST
model is encrypted or PSpice-only, use an explicit behavioral MOSFET model for
the logic exercise and report the MOSFET result as illustrative; do not infer a
physical F2 guarantee from it.

## Acceptance blockers

1. Implement and verify the aggregate `rails_ok`/startup-reset contract; HCS74
   has no stated power-up Q guarantee.
2. Specify input isolation/clamp behavior with a 432-V charged bus and all
   logic supplies unpowered; otherwise back-power can invalidate the default
   state and comparator threshold.
3. Obtain and inspect the exact ST PSpice archive (and the TI archives if used)
   before treating a waveform as a manufacturer-model result.
4. Simulate comparator/filter corners and the actual loaded 10-Ohm gate path,
   then hardware-verify synchronized VD, inductor current, detector output,
   CLR/Q, ENA, VGS, and drain current. The 2-us target remains provisional.
