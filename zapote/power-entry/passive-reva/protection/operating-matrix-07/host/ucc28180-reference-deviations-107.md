The EVM is a 360 W, 390 V, 85–265 VAC design with 120 kHz average-current control and documented UVLO, soft/cycle current limits, open-loop detection, OVP hysteresis, and soft-start (guide pp. 2–5). TIDA-00779 Rev D has a stated full-load input range of **190–270 VAC** in the [current design guide](https://www.ti.com/lit/pdf/tidube1). The schematic notes separately say 156–265 VRMS; that note does not establish 3.5 kW operation at 156 V. It is a nominal 390 V CCM reference, using a 47 kΩ FREQ resistor and 45 kHz schematic nominal, 180 µH choke, onboard UCC28881 bias, and UCC27524 gate driver (Rev D sheets 1–2). Neither establishes the local 1.8 kW, 108/120 VAC induction-load behavior.

The current product benchmark is Breville Control Freak CMC850, listed as 120 V, 60 Hz, 1800 W mains input in the manufacturer-authored, distributor-hosted [specification sheet](https://savagebros.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Breville-ControlFreak-CMC850_USA.pdf). That is an input benchmark, not guaranteed DC/pan output. The local 15 A screen remains unchanged; low-line derating and efficiency must be resolved independently.
