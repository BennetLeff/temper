# Functional protection requirements (model-to-hardware boundary)

This is a requirements synthesis from the frozen fault graph and the parent-reviewed reports. It does not select parts, change a deck, or claim that any simulated fault is acceptable. The 1.8 kW figure is the Control Freak **mains-input** benchmark; no PMP efficiency is applied to turn it into a DC-output requirement.

| Function | Implementable requirement | Model verification | Hardware verification / boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| F2 energy isolation | F2 must have an explicitly verified open state and a defined indication of that state. Test F2 open during an established run, startup before charge, and restart with charged, discharged, and residual VD/VB energy. Do not assume an open command removes stored energy instantly. | `fault_checks` requires an F2-open transition before the detector event, a prefault healthy/armed interval, declared event timing, and complete event/retained-off observation. This is a modeled fault-opening witness, not proof that the product has a controlled safety disconnect. | Measure F2 voltage and current in both polarities and confirm isolation after command; instrument VD local capacitance and VB bank energy. A control signal alone is not proof of galvanic or energy isolation. |
| Independent failed-MOS interruption | A switch-short or both-short state must be treated as a failed power-path device whose channel may remain conductive after gate removal. The hardware requirement is an actuator or interrupting path independent of the failed gate command and independent of F2 bank isolation, with clearing time, interrupted energy, safe failure, and restart behavior specified by a later hardware study. | `fault_checks` returns `ProtectionGap` for switch-short/both-short when channel current in the post-detector slice (`rows[fault_idx..]`, beginning at the detector rising edge) exceeds its frozen model limit. Parent evidence shows source-fed `Lboost` and bank-discharge paths are distinct; the detector does not directly measure either path. | Use isolated VDS/VGS probes plus calibrated line, inductor, F2, diode/body, and channel-current measurements. Prove source-current interruption, stored-energy handling, clearing time, device survival, and non-restart under the actual source and charged-bank states; no part or existing trace proves this requirement. |
| VD local capacitor versus VB bank energy | Treat `Clocal` at VD and `Cbank` at VB as separate energy reservoirs. With F2 closed, verify the complete modeled discharge loop before assigning current limits or interruption timing. | The reviewed diode-short graph permits `Cbank → F2 → VD → shorted diode terminal → MOS channel → node 0 → Cbank−`; `Clocal` can discharge through the same terminal/MOS path. Voltage detectors report VD/VB mismatch/overvoltage, not direct storage-current allocation. | Measure each capacitor voltage, ESR/ESL, current path, and temperature independently. Do not infer bank-current or local-capacitor-current limits from detector timing or source-return ISENSE. |
| Detector coverage and ISENSE gaps | Voltage detectors must be shown to cover the fault modes they are credited for. The source-return shunt/ISENSE path is not a complete measurement of F2-to-MOS stored-energy discharge. Any negative-ISENSE clamp is an explicit assumption requiring qualification. | Preserve detector timing and direct node traces; report detector event separately from current cessation. Local `DCLAMP 0→isense BAV23C_ASSUMED_V2` is a model assumption. The EVM has no external ISENSE clamp shown; TIDA Rev D uses D4 BAT54T1G with its R11/C15 network. | Characterize low-current forward voltage, leakage, dynamic behavior, PCL interaction, and probe burden of the actual clamp and shunt. Verify detector thresholds/timing against injected VD/VB faults and direct current probes. Reference parts do not qualify a local clamp. |
| Restart interlock | After a fault, gate drive, latch state, and enable must remain off until all required rails and ARM/PERMIT conditions are re-qualified. Repeated mains or rail recovery must not silently rearm a failed-short path. | Verify `q`, `en`, gate, fault, ARM, PERMIT, `f2ctl`, and rail states across fault and restart windows. `fault_checks` rejects rearm after the off witness and rejects missing/unstable prefault health. | Exercise rail undervoltage/overvoltage, ARM/PERMIT loss, controller standby, repeated mains, and residual-energy restarts with synchronized captures. Firmware/supervisor timing and hardware latch behavior must be checked independently of the behavioral model. |
| Inrush bypass graph | Any adopted reference D2 inrush bypass must be represented as a new power graph and re-qualified; it may bypass the boost inductor and MOS-controlled conversion path when forward biased. | Current local `cold.cir` has no D2 bypass path. EVM and TIDA Rev D schematics show D2=1N5406 in an inrush-bypass connection. Existing fault traces cannot be transferred unchanged to a graph containing that path. | Verify D2 forward/reverse behavior, inrush current, relay sequencing, thermal stress, and fault interruption with current probes and voltage probes. No fuse-clearing or interruption result follows from the reference schematic alone. |

The frozen checker screens (VD 500 V, VB 450 V, switch node 650 V, gate 25 V, `|Lboost|` 100 A; gate/channel-off and passive-report limits) are model-screen values in `faults/fault_checks.rs`, not component ratings or hardware qualification. The completed diode-short diagnostic recorded passive-path witnesses and a modeled -326.5 kA branch extreme, but explicitly states that the mechanism and physical survival are unproven. The BOTH-SHORT review remains screen-failed (`Lboost` 435.79 A) and protection is not accepted.

Later hardware evidence should follow the observation set in `checker/hardware-validation-spec.md`: synchronized line/bridge/inductor/shunt, VD/VB, VDS/VGS, gate-enable, ARM/PERMIT/fault, controller and rail measurements; calibrated instruments, probe settings, source identity, temperatures, and raw traces; cycle-window comparison with uncertainty budget; and separate fault fixtures after a normal point is characterized. That document is an observation/comparison specification, not an energization procedure or authorization. No current result establishes hardware acceptance.
