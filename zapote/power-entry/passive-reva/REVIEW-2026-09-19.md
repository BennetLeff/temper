## Loss and cooling

The retained [STW loss report](../loss-budget/evidence/correction-02/loss-report.json)
at 120 V line decomposes its selected sensitivity endpoints as follows:

| Assumed condition | Switching | Conduction | Gate charge | Subtotal |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 11 V gate, 25 °C, 50 mΩ | 105.643 W | 7.091 W | 0.170 W | 112.905 W |
| 9 V gate, 125 °C, 100 mΩ | 143.114 W | 14.183 W | 0.139 W | 157.437 W |

These are conditional model outputs, not actual losses or worst-case limits.
The dominant overlap term depends on assumed plateau voltage, transfer charge,
gate voltage and driver representation. The [existing-drive review](../loss-budget/options/existing-drive/REPORT.md)
already records that U11 receives external `AUX_15V_IN` through U43; a label
does not establish loaded gate voltage or output impedance. The 9–11 V points
are not measurements or schematic-derived operating points.

The earlier approximately 33.38 W result belongs to **IPW65R045C7**, a different
device and gate network. It combines independent modeled switching with
analytic conduction and gate terms. The [accounting audit](../loss-budget/campaign/LOSS-ACCOUNTING-AUDIT.md)
explicitly prohibits transferring that subtotal or a correction factor to STW.
The repository retains the exact STW datasheet, but the review found no retained
independent exact-STW switching model. This is a repository finding, not a claim
that such a model cannot exist externally.

Consequently, the [cooling budget](cooling/thermal-budget.json) cannot establish
installed temperatures. Its 110.6 W allowance and roughly 112.63 W calculated
capacity depend on catalog airflow and assumed heat. The STW sensitivity does
not prove the assembly overheats; it shows why the old 65 W other-PFC allowance
has not been justified. A further sweep of the same assumed inputs would not
resolve this.

**Next deliverable:** bind the actual auxiliary supply and gate network to one
operating specification, then obtain applicable independent switching evidence
or a qualified device-level measurement at those conditions. Keep measured
Eon/Eoff accounting explicit, including whether Eon includes Coss. Installed
cooling still needs an actual duct, interfaces, airflow and temperature record.
The [existing qualification protocol](cooling/qualification-protocol.md) owns
that work; this review does not authorize powered testing.

## Protection topology and the next ECO

The [native netlist](native-01/native.net.xml) puts U10.2, U20.1, the four bulk
capacitors, U40, the output connector and the bleeder on `PFC_BUS_PLUS_390V`.
There is no F2 and no spare dedicated HV fuse interconnect on this board.

The proposed series split is definite: U10 cathode → diode-side node → F2 →
bulk-bank positive. It needs a dedicated rated interconnect for the offboard
holder; the existing output connector is already occupied. The sourced
US141/Z331153 holder remains a candidate installation component, not proof of
fault interruption. See [protection disposition](protection/DISPOSITION.md).

Opening F2 creates two separately energized nodes. Keeping VSENSE on the bank
does not detect a rising diode-side node. Moving it to the diode side removes
that blind spot but does not by itself establish bank-ready, residual-charge
or restart behavior. The proposed independent detector is still an experimental
circuit whose timing and energy response are not established.

The [retained timed model](../active-rectifier/experiments/f2-open/f2_open_timed.rs)
includes continued mains energy and a simplified controller gate law. The
Luna review added three scratch-only 22/47 µF cases. The coordinator reran the
review probe and confirmed its numerical self-checks and reported outputs.
This scratch work is not a retained qualification receipt. Larger C
reduced the modeled first peak, but this did not supply a real controller
large-signal model, detector latency, capacitor pulse rating, restart policy,
or failed-short protection. No capacitor was selected and these scratch results
are not an acceptance record or a reason to order parts.

The healthy/failed-short distinction remains necessary. Healthy U9 can be
commanded off only with an established detection-and-response argument. A
failed-short U9 cannot. Healthy U10 blocks the bank for a U9-only short;
shorted U10 plus conducting/shorted U9 opens the internal discharge path.
The proposed F2 needs applicable clearing and loop-withstand evidence for that
path. Existing F1 and U12 remain outside it.

**Next deliverable:** one prototype ECO specifying both voltage-observation
functions, inhibit/restart behavior, the exact local reservoir or clamp, and
the holder/interconnect. Review first peak, continuing input and restart as one
circuit before applying the ECO to source and CAD. Prototype CAD can precede
physical qualification; it must preserve experimental status. Fuse application
data and the actual fault envelope remain required for coordination. This is
not reduced to one missing resistance measurement or one email.
