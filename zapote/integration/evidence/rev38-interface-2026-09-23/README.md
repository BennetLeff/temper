## Interface and energy map

```text
fused L,N -> CMC/NTC+relay -> bridge -> 180 uH boost + diode -> VD_LOCAL
                                                                | 22 uF + 0.47 uF to HOT0
                                                                | VD sense/VSENSE ladders
                                                        F2 (candidate)
                                                                |
                                                             VB_BANK -> inverter input and local HF capacitor
                                                                | 4 x 560 uF + bleeder + VB sense to HOT0

HOT0 = hazardous power return; SELV/CTRL_GND, protective earth, and the old
voltage-doubler midpoint are separate domains unless an engineered join says otherwise.
```

The provisional capacitor inventory from the committed Rev38 source is VD 22.47 µF nominal, 20.223–24.717 µF initial tolerance, and VB 2.24 mF nominal, 1.792–2.688 mF initial tolerance, before any inverter capacitor. At 390 V, nominal VB energy is **170.35 J**, upper-tolerance VB energy **204.42 J**, and upper-tolerance VD energy **1.88 J**. At 400 V these become 179.2, 215.04 and 1.98 J. A *rating-edge sensitivity only* at 450 V yields 272.16 J upper-tolerance VB and 2.50 J upper-tolerance VD. Each additional 100 µF directly on VB adds 7.61 J at 390 V or 10.13 J at 450 V. These are `½CV²` inventories; temperature, age, ripple, tolerance of future parts and fault replenishment still need bounds. The Nichicon [LGX series data](https://www.nichicon.co.jp/english/series_items/catalog_pdf/e-lgx.pdf) identifies the 560 µF/450 V part and its ±20% initial capacitance tolerance; TDK identifies the [22 µF film](https://product.tdk.com/en/search/capacitor/film/dc-link/info?part_no=B32776P6226K000) and [470 nF film](https://product.tdk.com/en/search/capacitor/film/snubbering_pfc/info?part_no=B32672P6474K000) candidates. **A 450 V component rating is not an operating or transient bus ceiling.** The conditional 500 V VD F2-open screen cannot be applied to the VB bank.

The only source-supported **provisional voltage cases** are: an intended regulated nominal near 390 V; ideal rectified line peaks of about 153–187 V over 108–132 Vac before boost (actual sag, charging and transients are unbounded); a 400 V arithmetic comparison case; and a 450 V capacitor-rating-edge sensitivity. The existing F2 audit additionally screens a conditional 500 V **VD-only** event. None is a selected permissible VB operating maximum, minimum loaded VB, ripple amplitude, or fault limit. A ramp may traverse low voltage, but neither a usable inverter minimum nor a bank-ready threshold has been established. The next Rev38 freeze must turn these cases into separately derated VD, VB, switch and diode envelopes with measured/source-backed transients.

For the proposed 108–132 Vac, 15 Arms input class, `Vac × Arms` is **1620–1980 VA** over that range and **1800 VA at 120 V**. Real delivered inverter power is lower after power factor and conversion losses. Thus a full 1800 W *delivered* at 120 V and 15 Arms is not established; the cooking-power claim and line-current limit must be reconciled with measured efficiency, power factor and line waveform. The nominated Eaton [LP-CC-20](https://www.eaton.com/us/en-us/skuPage.LP-CC-20.html) has a 20 A nameplate and published interruption rating, but it does not electronically cap current at 15 A. The [F1 screen](../../../power-entry/passive-reva/protection/interface-integration-38/F1-SCREEN.md) proposes a dedicated 20 A residential branch and a 10 kA prospective fault-current review envelope; plug/cord/branch compatibility, installed thermal behavior, upstream coordination, inrush, and a sustained overload through the 15 A NTC / 16 A CMC and relay remain **unresolved**. These are not a passed home-installation envelope.

## Discharge decision recommendation, not a requirement

Ask the product safety/service owner to classify enclosure and connector access, jurisdiction and appliance form factor first, then adopt a node-specific maximum residual voltage **and** time, measured after verified mains isolation, for VD, VB and any detached inverter capacitor. The applicable standard/edition and test clause must be recorded with that adoption. IEC lists [60335-1](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/61880) for household appliances, [60335-2-9](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/61318) for portable cooking appliances including hotplates and induction wok hotplates, and [60335-2-6](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/65424) for stationary cooking appliances. Their scope listings alone do not establish this cooker's residual-voltage limit or validate the historical 34 V/60 s number. Other applicable North American product requirements require the same explicit review.

Until adoption, use **400/450 → 34 V within 60 s** only as a transparent engineering *feasibility scenario* inherited from the older split-bus study, with a separately allowed 5 s actuation delay. At 450 V and high-tolerance 2.688 mF VB with no inverter capacitor, an ideal single RC path would need `R <= 7.92 kΩ` after that delay; adding 100 µF makes it `<= 7.64 kΩ`. Its initial resistor-bank power would be roughly `450²/7.92k = 25.6 W` and its initial bank energy is 272 J. These computations ignore resistor tolerance and hot/fan-off temperature, control/contact faults, source replenishment, and any detached island. They **cannot** select a resistor or attest compliance. The committed [discharge candidate decision](../../../discharge/evidence/board-candidate-01/DECISION.md) already holds the PCB because its screened active path misses fault cases. Do not count down a service deadline while mains remains connected: the bridge/NTC/boost-diode route can recharge VD and, with F2 conducting, VB even when the boost gate is off. Qualify any engaged resistor for continuous energized stress in that state or prove a real upstream isolation element.

**Stop conditions:** any unbounded VB maximum, unknown direct/detached inverter capacitance, no independently observable charged island, unresolved mains-attached discharge stress, absent stop producer, or unreviewed F1/F2 assembly keeps the combined design provisional.
