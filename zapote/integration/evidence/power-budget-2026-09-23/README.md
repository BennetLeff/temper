## Four distinct power boundaries

For single-phase RMS quantities, `S_in = V_ac,rms × I_ac,rms` in VA and `P_in = S_in × PF` in W. PF here is **true** real-power factor, including waveform distortion, and must be measured over representative cycles rather than assumed from current phase alone. The U.S. [Department of Energy electrical handbook](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/DOE-HDBK-1011-92_VOL3.pdf) defines PF as real/apparent power. At 15 Arms, apparent input is **1620 VA at 108 V, 1800 VA at 120 V, 1980 VA at 132 V**. Even ideal unity PF, zero auxiliary draw and lossless conversion cannot deliver 1800 W into a pan at 120 V/15 A; realistic output is lower.

For the illustrative [Rust screen](budget.rs), `P_aux,AC` is the **AC-input-equivalent** power taken by all control, supply and cooling branches outside the modeled PFC path. This boundary choice matches the active source's separate AUX branch. `P_PFCfeed = max(0, P_in − P_aux,AC)`, `P_VB = P_PFCfeed × η_PFC`, `P_inverter,out = P_VB × η_inv`, and `P_pan,absorbed = P_inverter,out × η_pan`. `η_pan` folds coil, ferrite, field and cookware coupling losses into a single illustrative factor. Pan absorbed heat is still different from heat delivered to food. If the actual auxiliary architecture draws from VB, reassign that loss at its measured boundary; do not count it twice. These equations are steady-state energy bookkeeping and say nothing about switch, capacitor, shunt, wiring or thermal ratings.

At 120 V, the middle hypothesis produces 1800 VA apparent, 1764 W real input, 1724 W PFC feed, 1638 W VB, 1507 W inverter output and **1281 W absorbed by the pan**. The inverse arithmetic needs **20.94 Arms** for a hypothetical 1800 W absorbed by the pan with those same factors; even the optimistic hypothesis needs 18.44 Arms. These inverse values are *infeasibility markers*, not proposed operating currents. At low line the pan bound falls, while fixed auxiliary draw consumes a larger fraction. No scenario is a probability distribution or a measured performance guarantee. The entire grid and inverse values are saved in [scenario-output.csv](scenario-output.csv).

The VB bank can supply a short deficit without lifting the continuous line limit. The prior [interface inventory](../rev38-interface-2026-09-23/README.md) calculates about 170 J at 390 V for nominal 2.24 mF and about 48 J between 390 and 330 V. Neither 330 V nor that usable energy interval is an adopted enable threshold. Startup, pan changes and transient requests need measured VB trajectory, ripple, recharge, and loss; repeated borrowing from the bank cannot be treated as sustained cooking power. The [inverter screen](../../../inverter/U2-CANDIDATE-SCREEN.md) shows possible high tank current even for weak/no-pan conditions, so line-power arithmetic alone cannot clear inverter stress.

## Recommended provisional control design

1. **Default-off admission.** Start with PFC and inverter gate permissions low. Admit PFC only through Rev38's qualified start/protection path; admit inverter PWM only after independently valid absolute VB, healthy HOT and SELV supplies, closed interlocks, healthy sensing, known gate-rail order and an explicit new start epoch. A VD/VB match or a dead VD reading does not prove VB discharged or ready. Establish coil/pan presence with a *limited-energy* qualification sequence before high power. Its excitation, threshold and timeout are to be designed from measured impedance and current response; there is no blanket 44–50 kHz or 390 V enable grant.
2. **Sustained input limit.** An outer controller clamps requested power using measured rolling line RMS current and true input watts, corrected for line voltage, measured auxiliary demand, loss and thermal limits. The final limit must be **below** the reviewed 15 Arms maximum by a validated measurement/tolerance margin. Separate hardware protection must cover abnormal instantaneous, cycle and overload behavior, including relay stuck open; the outer loop cannot replace it. Specify response time and trip coordination from measured source, NTC, CMC, relay and F1/F2 withstand, not from this table.
3. **VB reserve and load qualification.** Reduce inverter request when VB approaches a validated loaded floor, when ripple/sag breaches a validated envelope, or when the PFC cannot replenish energy. No catch-up burst may defeat the line-current or tank-current limit. Weakly coupled, off-center or absent pan produces lower available power or loss of heat mode; temperature error is never permission to keep increasing tank current. Requalify after pan removal, pan exchange, reset, F2 event or charged-bank interruption.
4. **Thermal derating.** Use bounded sensor validity and monitored temperatures at the coil/ferrite, inverter switch/heat spreader, tank capacitor, PFC magnetics/switch/diode, bridge, F1/F2 contacts, inlet path and enclosure air. Derate to the minimum independently established component and cooling limit; a stopped or degraded fan can force reduced power or stop. The standalone thermal unit and unmeasured cooling path do not currently set numerical thresholds.
5. **Two-stage stop plus stored-energy state.** A power-budget fault, invalid sensor, interlock opening, rail loss, fan failure, overtemperature or controller reset must reach **both** PFC stop and the inverter's separate hardware PERMIT. Observe sustained input/tank current cessation, not merely a low logic bit. F2 is upstream of VB and cannot interrupt a direct VB-bank-to-failed-short-inverter fault. Containment for that path and independent discharge verification of VD, VB and any detached capacitor remain separate design requirements. Hold rearm through fault clearance, rail stabilization, energy-state verification and a new start epoch; never infer safe service from a stopped PWM or auto-restart after an unknown-state fault.

**Signal gap to close:** current Rev38 has a PFC switch shunt at `RECT_MINUS` and VD/VB detector ladders; the standalone inverter tank CT senses a different current. None is a proven isolated, calibrated whole-appliance `V_ac(t), I_ac(t)` wattmeter or continuous VB-energy measurement. Add or prove a safe measurement chain for line RMS/real watts/PF, absolute VD and VB with open-sense detection, fast PFC/input and tank current protection, F2 status as distinct from voltage equality, and temperatures/airflow. Keep hazardous HOT0 measurement and SELV user control separated by evaluated isolation. Record bandwidth, sample alignment, calibration, saturation, stale-data and loss behavior before firmware depends on a number.

## Validation matrix and promotion gates

| Case | Input/load condition | Required observations and pass decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source corners | 108/120/132 Vac; source impedance and frequency bounds; steady heat at several commands | Measure `V(t)`, `I(t)`, RMS, true W/PF, AUX W, VD/VB waveform, tank V/I/phase and calorimetric pan heat. Verify line current including tolerance and physical component temperatures. Any delivered-power rating comes from repeated measured results, not the sensitivity table. |
| Startup and precharge | Cold/part-charged VB, repeated starts, NTC bypass delayed/stuck-open, fan and controls load | Record inrush, relay/NTC temperature, source/AUX/HOT rail order, VB/VD, both gate permissions and no unqualified inverter PWM. Verify F1 no-nuisance and contact/trace withstand against physical assembly. |
| Cookware envelope | Selected coil with no pan, weak/small/offset pan, cold/hot reference pan, pan removal/exchange | Sweep paired complex L/R and protected current/phase response. Verify limited-energy recognition, tank/cap/switch stress, no uncontrolled current chase, and requalification before heat resumes. |
| Dynamic line and demand | Low-line sag/recovery, step power request, pan load step, sustained high demand | Measure controller clamp delay, line RMS and cycle peaks, VB droop/ripple, energy recovery and clamp stability. A transient VB draw must return to reserve without repeated limit violation. |
| Cooling/thermal | Hot ambient, blocked/reduced airflow, fan-stop, cold and heat-soaked PCB | Measure all limiting component temperatures and derating/stop latency; verify sensor-open, short, stale and implausible readings lead to the defined stop. |
| Fault and stop | PFC overcurrent, inverter OCP, over/under VB, F2 open, lost rail, source reset, interlock, stale command | Capture both stage disable pins, actual switching/tank/input current cessation, VB/VD energy islands, fault latch and reboot behavior. A stuck-short path requires separate containment evidence, not just permission logic. |
| Service/restart | Mains isolation and mains-present stop, failed discharge element, detached inverter capacitance, sense-open | Apply the later adopted node-specific voltage/time/access rule. Verify each island independently and prohibit rearm/service indication on unknown charge. No numerical safe-to-touch claim is adopted here. |

**Promotion gate:** freeze one Rev38 source identity and its input/auxiliary, VD/VB, stop and F1/F2 envelopes; measure the selected coil/pans and cooling assembly; select a valid measurement and independent fast-protection chain; then set numeric current, reserve, thermal and stop timing requirements and retest the whole matrix on protected hardware. Until then the recommended policy is an interface requirement, not implemented firmware or a product rating. No energized testing or purchase is claimed by this artifact.
