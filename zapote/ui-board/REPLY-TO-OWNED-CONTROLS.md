## 1. Sensor, range, filter and update schedule (U23, U24, U25)

- **Confirmed: TMAG5170A1QDGKR** (C5220642; 4 342 in stock, USD 2.31 at qty 1 on 2026-10-12), SPI mode 0, 3.3 V, CRC on, 32-clock frames, conversion status checked on every response. **SCK 1 MHz first, 2.5 MHz as the cap; 5 MHz is possible (13 ns of MISO margin with the sensor's 390 ohm series resistor) only after a scope check.**
- **Range +/-100 mT** (the only range above your 55.7 mT; +/-50 mT clips at nominal). Largest field with a 0.5 mm push, a 0.2 mm tighter gap, -40 C and +5 % magnet: 73.0 mT (27 % headroom, inside 80 % of full scale). Weakest seated field: 47.3 mT.
- **Conversion: 16x averaging, X, Y, Z, continuous mode, SmCo temperature coefficient** (DEVICE_CONFIG 0x422C, SENSOR_CONFIG 0x01EA). 1.25 ms per set; read every 5 ms (200 Hz); three frames per update, each its own select/release pair, accepted only if the three responses share the set count and no error or status flag is set. Bus time 311 us per update at 1 MHz; worst data age 2.1 ms. Noise: 0.049 degree 1 sigma, **0.34 degree p-p over 10 s** (8x gives 0.47, which leaves no room for the stray field).
- **Stray field, worst case, no filtering credit:** angle error = asin(sqrt(2) x B_rms / 52.7 mT): **0.173 degree** (60 kHz, no pan, bare), 0.051 (31 kHz, pan, bare), 0.027 (no pan, base and roof), 0.0015 (pan, base and roof). The non-repeatable error is at most 0.40 degree, leaving at least 0.6 degree of your 1 degree for calibration residual, magnet and mechanism.
- **The 0.5 degree p-p jitter holds (0.35 to 0.45) with a pan or with the base and roof, and does not hold (0.69) in the bare no-pan 60 kHz transient.** It stays under the 1 degree step hysteresis everywhere. Averaging cannot fix that case at 200 Hz; inhibiting no-pan operation or the base and roof does.
- **The 55 kHz gap:** I do not rely on filtering. A 16-sample average passes a coil tone at 50 % or more between 38.5 and 41.5 kHz (the sensor's ~40 ksps sampling aliases it to DC) and at 0.13 or less elsewhere in 31 to 60 kHz. The error is therefore bounded by the unfiltered amplitude. Bounds assumed at 55 kHz: with a pan, no more than your 31 kHz pan case; without a pan, no more than your 60 kHz bare case at the same 45 A peak. Please close it on the bench (raw X, Y, Z with the coil running through 31 to 55 kHz and harmonics) and say whether 28 A is peak or rms.
- **|B| for push and removal:** removal threshold 23.7 mT (reached at a 2.9 mm lift); push as a relative change of |B| (+15.3 % for 0.5 mm) against a slow baseline, threshold about 8 %. Absolute |B| cannot separate push from gap tolerance and temperature; tilt and the retention steel are not in the model.

## 2. Freed-GPIO and CS scheme; MISO contention resolved (U20, U21, U22)

- **GP3 = sensor select (low = select), GP4 = sensor ALERT input (10 kohm pull-up), GP5 = spare input (pull-up), GP7 = TMP122 select as before.** Selects pass through gates: `BUS_BUSY = NAND(CS_EXP#, CS_OLED#)`, `CS_dev# = GPx OR BUS_BUSY` (SN74LVC1G00 and a dual SN74LVC2G32; three gates).
- **A device is selected only when its GP is low AND CS_EXP# is high AND CS_OLED# is high** (one row in eight, per device; table in U20, evaluated from the netlist). At reset the GPs are inputs, pulled high: both devices deselected whatever the chip selects do; device-side pull-ups hold CS# high if a gate is unpowered or the harness is unplugged.
- **MISO:** the sensor, the TMP122 and the MCP23S08 are each high impedance unless qualified-selected (16-row table). At most one driver is enabled except if firmware drives GP3 and GP7 low together; then a 390 ohm resistor in the sensor's SDO limits the short to 8.9 mA (10 mA abs max). A sensor left armed during an expander or OLED frame is deselected 18.8 ns after that frame's chip select falls, 31 ns before its first clock.
- **Firmware sequence (U21):** write OLAT before IODIR; expander device `cs_ena_posttrans` >= 3 (CS_EXP# stays low >= 600 ns after the last clock so the GP output settles; otherwise a 355 ns zero-clock select glitch is possible); lock the whole bus; write GP low; wait >= 1 us; clock exactly 32 (TMP122: 16) bits with **no hardware chip select**; wait >= 1 us; write GP high; unlock. One select/release pair per sensor frame. Timing: select margin 936 ns against tsu_cs 45 ns, hold margin 957 ns against th_cs 45 ns, MISO handover margin 3.1 us.
- **Header:** `ui_board_map.h` at 780eaab89 still names GP3-GP5 ENC_A/B/SW; firmware must replace them. IODIR reads 0x37 when initialised.
