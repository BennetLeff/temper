## Intake and fixture freeze

1. Assign physical coil and pan serial labels. Photograph item labels, coil winding/ferrite/termination, and each pan underside; record dimensions, mass, visible flatness/warp, magnetic area and condition. Separate C1's coil from the powered kit only under a reviewed de-energized procedure; the kit's PCB is not a Temper power source.
2. Freeze the fixture drawing, glass/ceramic article and thickness, coil-to-pan *effective* gap, ferrite and bracket, lead length/routing, terminal connections, pan center reference and repeatable offset/lift spacers. The kit guide's 5–10 mm is its evaluation condition, **not** the as-yet-undefined Temper production gap. Start a new fixture revision whenever these change.
3. Record calibration, open/short compensation at the coil terminals, cable parasitics, instrument model/serial/range and raw-file hash using [run-manifest.tsv](../../characterization/run-manifest.tsv). Record correction residual and uncertainty at each frequency; repeat a centered no-pan point before and after each pan block to detect drift.
4. Before excitation, isolate the coil from all inverter connections and independently verify every nearby stored-energy node discharged with a rated instrument. The [run-approval.tsv](../../characterization/run-approval.tsv) must contain reviewed limits for source type, excitation V/I, temperature, energy and stop method. Missing limits mean `HOLD_INPUT`; they cannot be inferred from an LCR meter's dial setting.

## Measurement sequence and handoff

Execute [planned-cases.tsv](planned-cases.tsv) on **the same C1 coil and fixture revision**. For each static state, take complex impedance and signed phase at 20, 30, 40, 47 and 50 kHz plus the eventually proposed control-band edges; use at least two *reviewed* low-energy excitation levels within instrument and article limits. Keep raw `Z_re` and `Z_im`; calculate series L as `Z_im/(2πf)` only at points with a valid inductive series-equivalent model. Do not subtract no-pan `R` and call the remainder pure pan heating: pan proximity can alter winding AC loss.

At the measured installed gap, compare P1, P2 and P3 centered, then P1 and the empirically weakest centered article at mechanically approved offset and lift. Repeat cold points after each move. Hot states require a separate, bounded preheat method and actual winding/ferrite/pan temperatures; a setpoint alone is insufficient. Record individual samples, repeated points, uncertainty components, pan contact geometry and instrument settings in [coil-points.tsv](../../characterization/coil-points.tsv). The per-run manifest binds each raw file by SHA-256 and reviewer. Do not combine extrema across different C1 coil, ferrite or fixture revisions as though they were one article.

This is a **de-energized, low-energy or instrument-current-limited impedance stage only**. Static lift positions are allowed after excitation stops and energy is verified absent. Live pan removal, high-current drive, thermal soak at cooking power, no-pan switching, open/short faults and gate-stop timing stay at `HOLD_ENERGIZED` until a protected common inverter article, bounded source/energy, approved motion/fault fixture and synchronized waveform capture exist. Later powered captures must prove current cessation independently of a gate-off command; they must not reuse an LCR result as that proof.

## Analysis and decision rules

- **Receipt complete:** the article IDs and fixture drawing are frozen; all required raw files, calibrations, corrections, uncertainties and repeat points are present; an independent reviewer can trace every reported `(Z_re, Z_im, f, current, temperature, gap, offset)` tuple to one coil/pan/fixture article. Missing or invalid provenance is `HOLD_DATA`.
- **Pilot envelope:** report observed joint `(L, R)` pairs and their uncertainty by pan, geometry, temperature, current and frequency. List independent observed extrema and which article/condition produced each. Keep an explicit unmeasured/extrapolated column. A rank of “weakest” is established from measured coupling/damping behavior, never from pan names or material alone.
- **Historical incoming screen:** for an eventual supplier coil, the old spec's 40 kHz free-air `79.2–96.8 µH`, `R_ac ≤0.40 Ω`, `R_dc ≤0.12 Ω`, and single-reference-pan loaded `L ≥53.43 µH` and ratio `≥0.60` may be reported as separate **legacy-screen** pass/fail rows when their exact test geometry is met. C1 need not pass a custom supplier acceptance test, and passing it does not qualify Rev38, the full cookware cohort, coil thermal behavior or the 390 V inverter.
- **Design promotion:** replace synthetic rows in [provisional-pan-cases.csv](../provisional-pan-cases.csv) only with measured, source-bound rows after reviewer acceptance. Run the coupled inverter/source/stop and part-stress checks separately. The existing U3 gate requires data from the same physical protected article; these pilot results inform design but do not become its hardware qualification by relabeling.
- **Distribution:** three deliberately chosen pans are a *coverage cohort*, not a random sample of home cookware. If a usage-weighted model is wanted later, first declare the target cookware population and subjective class weights, then collect independent pans and multiple coil builds per class, across controlled gap/temperature/current states. Fit **joint** `L,R` conditional behavior and retain sampling and instrument uncertainty. Mark any prior as subjective; percentile results rank expected behavior only. Protection, device and thermal limits use the adverse measured plus justified extrapolation envelope, including no-pan/removal/fault modes regardless of prior weight. No distribution fit or numerical probability is accepted at this milestone.

The next concrete gate is to approve the exact purchase/fixture/low-energy limits and receive articles. Neither purchase nor any physical result has occurred here.
