## Inhibit and stop requirements to prove

1. **Before any START:** require fresh, valid absolute `VD_LOCAL/HOT0` and `VB_BANK/HOT0` readings, F2 state bounded by its own evidence, known tank-cap voltage and current-zero state, qualified load/coil presence, valid 3V3/15V/bootstrap conditions, working OCP/latch path, an explicit new command inside its timeout, and no latched fault. The exact voltage windows and capacitance/charge thresholds remain unselected until Rev38, inverter and safety owners freeze them. Neither equal VD/VB nor VD≈0 is proof of F2 continuity or a discharged bank.
2. **At an injected fault or loss:** deassert inverter PERMIT and both PWMs, command the PFC stop, latch the cause through reboot/brownout, and prevent automatic resume. Observe `t_fault`, `t_PERMIT↓`, both gate-emitter voltages, `t_|I|≤I_zero`, VD, VB and tank-cap voltage on a common timebase. Define `I_zero` above the measured probe noise and require it to remain below threshold throughout an adopted dwell; a single zero crossing is insufficient. A latched bit or a low gate voltage alone is not current-zero evidence.
3. **For timeout:** a hardware or independently checked watchdog must deassert PERMIT when commands stop advancing, even if PWM output is stuck. Freeze maximum age, clock failure behavior and watchdog power domain; the Rust `pwm` alias does not implement this circuit.
4. **For AUX/gate rail loss:** require default-off under every supply order and partial-rail state. The model's `rail` alias does not bound collapse timing, parasitic gate charge or bootstrap hold-up. When 3V3 is invalid, ignore an apparently low `OCP_FAULT` and keep PERMIT disabled.
5. **For failed-short switch or bus-side wiring fault:** classify gate-off as ineffective. F2 is upstream of the 2240 µF VB bank and cannot clear its direct fault loop. Assign a separately rated bank-side interruption/containment and discharge path, with loop impedance, fuse/interrupt coordination, energy and thermal proof before any full-bank test. This engine has no failed-short solution.
6. **For restart after any fault:** manual/explicit rearm requires fresh measurements, a verified zero-current dwell, no charged/unknown detached island, valid rails and sensor integrity, and a *new* START edge. Decide whether rearm is allowed before bus discharge only after measuring the full tank-cap bias and bus-ready conditions. Service access additionally awaits adopted node-specific discharge voltage/time criteria; no criterion is selected here.

## Limited-energy bench plan requiring reviewed inputs

This is a **test proposal**, not authorization to energize a fixture. The owner must record exact instrument, source and article IDs, schematic and barriers, independent reviewer, and the values below before stage C. A blank or unknown value is a stop condition. The caps are input variables; they are intentionally not guessed from the 390 V screen.

| Reviewed input | How it bounds the campaign |
| --- | --- |
| `V_source_max`, `I_source_hard_max`, `P_source_max`, `t_source_on_max`, source output C and reverse-energy behavior | Confirm the isolated supply actually enforces these under the planned fault, including when externally driven by the bank. Bound delivered source energy by a verified integral or conservative `P_source_max × t_source_on_max`; a current-limit dial alone is not an energy cap. |
| `C_VB_max`, `C_VD_max`, `C_local_max`, `C_tank_max`, `V_VB_pre_max`, `V_VD_pre_max`, `|V_C_pre|max` | Bound initially stored energy by `½ C_VB_max V_VB_pre_max² + ½ C_VD_max V_VD_pre_max² + ½ C_local_max V_VB_pre_max² + ½ C_tank_max |V_C_pre|max²`, avoiding double count if local C is included in a measured aggregate. Also inventory probe/fixture capacitance and detached islands. |
| `E_campaign_max`, `I_tank_interrupt_max`, `I_fault_max`, `V_device_max`, `V_cap_abs_max`, `T_case_max`, `t_fault_clear_max` | Values are chosen from the actual selected components, fixture topology and independently reviewed failure budget; continuous and pulse limits need their applicable durations and temperatures. Pre-calculated energy plus source energy must remain below `E_campaign_max` under worst reviewed tolerance and single failure. |
| `I_zero`, `t_zero_dwell`, probe bandwidth/noise, scope timebase skew, current transfer calibration | These determine when *sustained* tank current cessation has actually been observed. Preserve raw synchronized waveforms and uncertainty; verify the current probe can see either polarity and the DC fault path separately. |
| Operator isolation, interlock, remote emergency isolation, discharge and recheck procedure | Prove a source-independent stop/energy-control route before connecting the tank. Define access state after power removal; measure **both** VD and VB and tank C with rated probes before touch or reset. |

Stages: **A**, power only the SELV logic/gate interface with dummy loads and bounded isolated rails; inject PERMIT loss, PWM stuck, 3V3/15V dropout, reset and command timeout, capturing pin-level outputs. **B**, measure the disconnected coil/pan complex impedance and passive capacitor initial/discharge states under the separate intake packet, without power switching. **C**, after complete reviewed values above and approved fixture drawing, run a protected limited-energy inverter article at the *reviewed* voltage/current/energy cap, first into a characterized benign load, then the identified coil/pan states. Increase no envelope without a new review. Inject one stop cause per run; record gate-off **and** sustained current zero, bus/cap waveforms, temperature and restart interlock. F2 opening/reclosing under current, live pan removal, failed-short insertion, full-bank discharge and mains operation remain outside stage C pending separate fault fixture and protection evidence.

Promotion requires actual timing and waveform captures at the selected articles and source envelope, a reconciled current-trip/timeout/rail-order budget, bank-side fault containment, adopted service-discharge criterion, and frozen Rev38 source/connector bytes. The present packet is a **digital investigation milestone**, not inverter PCB construction or appliance qualification.
