# Inverter U2 digital architecture screen

Status: **conditional topology recommendation; operating envelope and part selection open** (2026-09-23). This report uses the saved Rev38 `pfc_power.ato` bytes identified in [evidence/u1-source-snapshot.md](evidence/u1-source-snapshot.md). The concurrently active Rev38 checkout has further uncommitted work; its VB values, protection thresholds and interfaces are **provisional** until frozen and re-read. The separate [gate-drive acceptance](../gate-drive/ACCEPTANCE.md) is a digital construction result, not a loaded timing pass.

## Decision at this milestone

Carry **one VB_BANK/HOT0-fed half bridge with a series-blocking tank capacitor** as the first candidate to investigate. It is the only compared topology that uses the accepted gate unit's two outputs without inventing a power midpoint. This recommendation chooses a *circuit family for measurement and analysis*, not a switch, capacitor, frequency, gate bias or power rating. The historical series tank can inform component screening, but its `PWR_RTN` midpoint, 340 V input assumptions and embedded gate driver cannot be copied.

| Option | Electrical relation at ideal 50% duty | Interface and construction consequence | Verdict now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Half bridge, series capacitor, tank return to HOT0 | Switch node is 0/VB. In steady state the series capacitor acquires approximately VB/2 DC bias, leaving the same AC fundamental as a split-bus half bridge of equal full-bus voltage. | Uses gate J3/J4; requires new capacitor startup/bias, no-pan and fault transient analysis, VB-local HF capacitor, HOT0 current-return routing, and bank-side interruption. | **Preferred conditional investigation.** Neither steady-state equality nor the screen proves startup, ZVS or safe faults. |
| Half bridge with a fabricated VB/2 midpoint | Switch node relative to midpoint is approximately ±VB/2; series capacitor DC bias is ideally zero, with the same AC fundamental as direct HOT0 return. | Adds a current-carrying midpoint capacitor network, balancing, discharge, ripple and fault paths that Rev38 does not supply. May recreate the old return only by designing a new power section. | Defer unless direct return fails a measured constraint. It does not improve ideal fundamental power. |
| Full bridge to HOT0 | Bipolar ±VB drive doubles fundamental voltage, approximately doubles sinusoidal tank current and quadruples resistive power at unchanged impedance. | Needs four switches and at least two more gate outputs; a cross-unit gate-drive redesign plus larger stress budget. | Reject as a drop-in with the accepted two-channel gate unit; reconsider only with a different power and control requirement. |

The direct-return capacitor is **not** a guaranteed DC-blocking safety device during startup, a short or an open coil. Its initial voltage may be anywhere in the allowed restart/discharge state. The ideal VB/2 bias appears only after periodic steady switching with a connected tank.

## Reproducible first-harmonic screen

[Rust source](evidence/plant_screen.rs), [input cases](evidence/plant-cases.csv), and [saved output](evidence/plant-output.csv) form the screen. Run `rustc --edition=2021 -O zapote/inverter/evidence/plant_screen.rs -o /tmp/temper-plant-screen` and then `/tmp/temper-plant-screen zapote/inverter/evidence/plant-cases.csv`. Tests use `rustc --edition=2021 --test zapote/inverter/evidence/plant_screen.rs -o /tmp/temper-plant-screen-tests` followed by `/tmp/temper-plant-screen-tests`. No shared pyo3 cargo artifact is built.

The calculation uses an **ideal 50% square-wave switch node** and only its fundamental: `V1,rms = √2 VB/π` for either half-bridge return, twice that for a full bridge. With declared series `L_loaded`, `C`, `R_coil`, `R_pan` and provisional `R_cap = 4 mΩ/3`, `Z = (R_coil + R_pan + R_cap) + j(2πfL_loaded − 1/(2πfC))` and `I1,rms = V1,rms/|Z|`. It reports `I1,peak = √2 I1,rms`, phase, `V_C,ac,rms = I1,rms/(2πfC)`, and modeled `I²R` partitions. For the HOT0 return only, its steady capacitor voltage estimate adds VB/2 DC bias to the sinusoidal AC peak. The `1200 V − VB` column is **nominal rating subtraction**, not switching overshoot margin or a passing device screen.

The 0.34 Ω coil term is an **unloaded 40 kHz resistance chart read** from Infineon's comparison kit. The 2.91 Ω `R_pan` term is **inferred** by subtracting it from the same chart's approximately 3.25 Ω loaded total. It is not a measured separation of copper and pan loss on Temper's coil; pan proximity can also change winding AC resistance. These are example parameters at 40 kHz carried into a 47 kHz screen without frequency calibration. The historical 300 nF CDE bank/4 mΩ ESR and 88/59.84 µH figures are likewise candidate inputs, not Rev38 measurements. The [Infineon evaluation guide, Fig. 16](https://www.infineon.com/dgdl/Infineon-EVAL-IHW25N140R5L-UserManual-v01_00-EN.pdf?fileId=8ac78c8c8afe5bd0018b18c78e023a8d) explicitly calls for measuring individual properties when another coil or pot is used.

### Independent dimensional check and sensitivity

For the illustrative 390 V, 47 kHz, 59.84 µH, 300 nF, 3.25 Ω total-resistance case, an independent Python `math` calculation gives `V1=175.562 V rms`, `X_L=17.671 Ω`, `X_C=11.288 Ω`, `I1=24.506 A rms`, and `V_C,ac=276.613 V rms`. Applying `I²R` separately gives **204.185 W assigned to the 0.34 Ω coil-resistance proxy**, **1747.579 W assigned to the inferred 2.91 Ω pan-reflected resistance**, and **0.801 W assigned to ideal, equal-sharing CDE-bank ESR**. Dimensions check: `V/Ω=A`, `A²Ω=W`, `A·Ω=V`, and `½CV²=J`. These numbers are *neither an actual cooling budget nor proof of 1.8 kW heating*; the input impedance, harmonics and temperature have not been measured.

| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |

| 390 V direct HOT0, same parameters | 24.51 A rms | 276.61 V / 195 V | +14.7% voltage/current and about +31.6% modeled `I²R` heat versus 340 V at fixed impedance. Capacitor absolute sinusoidal peak estimate is 586 V, with startup still unbounded. |
| 390 V fabricated midpoint | 24.51 A rms | 276.61 V / 0 V | No ideal AC-current advantage over direct return; requires a new midpoint power network. |
| 390 V full bridge | 49.01 A rms | 553.23 V / 0 V | Shows why the old unchanged tank cannot be put behind a full bridge on these assumptions. |
| 53.43 µH loaded, 270 nF, 44 kHz | **49.74 A rms; 70.34 A sine peak** | **666.30 V / 195 V** | This stiff-bus **instantaneous operating-point** calculation flags transient capacitor/device stress to investigate. Bus droop and PFC limiting may prevent sustained operation; its RMS value cannot be used as a continuous thermal rating comparison. |
| No pan at 47 kHz | 11.94 A rms | 134.77 V / 195 V | Lower modeled resistance loss does **not** mean safe commutation or no-pan detection; switch hard-turn-on loss can dominate. |
| 450 V bus rating probe | 28.28 A rms | 319.17 V / 225 V | Sensitivity at the electrolytic **nameplate rating**, not an authorized VB operating point. |

The low-L screen implies about 8.04 kW of modeled total tank `I²R` heat/pan power at a stiff 390 V bus. The nominal 2240 µF bank contains only about 69.6 J between 390 and 300 V. If a *hypothetical* 1.8 kW source continued supplying power, that energy would cover the roughly 6.24 kW deficit for only about 11 ms, before any source dynamics or control response. Therefore the 49.74 A and 16.58 A-per-can numbers indicate a possible transient trajectory, not a sustained RMS duty or a standalone capacitor rejection. A coupled VB source-impedance, PFC limit and capacitor-energy model is required.

The saved negative controls reject zero loaded inductance and a bus at/above the historical IGBT's 1200 V rating. Loss of PERMIT or isolated 15 V produces `INHIBITED`: the screen deliberately offers **no steady switching solution** in those states. This does **not** predict residual tank current, a failed-short device, or how quickly the gate actually turns off.

## Bus, F2 and cooling handoffs

Rev38's saved source places its **470 nF HF film capacitor on VD**, while the inverter is fed from **VB** across F2. No existing VD bypass can be counted as the inverter's low-inductance local commutation capacitor. Any new VB/HOT0 capacitor belongs in the discharge inventory with exact value, tolerance, mounting and residual energy. Four nominal 560 µF cans hold 170.352 J at 390 V before tolerance. F2 between VD and VB cannot interrupt the direct VB-capacitors → failed inverter bridge → HOT0 loop. The inverter requires a separately allocated downstream fault-current and bank-discharge solution; upstream PFC gate disable does not clear it.

The accepted VB waveform is **missing**: maximum/minimum during startup, run, line ripple, load steps, PFC trip, F2 opening and discharge, with overshoot and source impedance. A 450 V capacitor rating is an absolute component datum, not a permission to operate at 450 V; a 1200 V IGBT label minus VB is not a commutation allowance. The screen treats each `bus_v` as a static sample. It does not evaluate ripple sidebands, a fast transient, high-frequency loop overshoot or bus collapse. Define `VB_min`, `VB_max`, `ΔVB_pp`, slew and `Z_source(f)` from Rev38/bench evidence before operating bounds can be issued.

## Exact gates for moving beyond this screen

1. **Freeze cross-unit input:** capture the final Rev38 source/native hash and an approved VB waveform/impedance envelope, bank-ready meaning, stop behavior and bank-side fault-energy allocation. Recheck U1 hashes against the concurrent Rev38 work. If VB can exceed a selected derated switch/cap limit, fail the candidate rather than using the 390 V name.
2. **Choose physical return and local capacitance:** produce a complete VB/HOT0 half-bridge plus series-cap circuit, current-return/CT path, local commutation-cap value and loop-inductance budget. Include startup capacitor bias, F2-open and faulted-device states. Update discharge and cooling with exact stored-energy and heat locations.
3. **Measure the installed load:** follow [coil-evidence.md](coil-evidence.md) across no pan, all allowed pans, offsets, gap and temperature, obtaining complex impedance versus frequency and large-signal current. Refit the model to independent current/phase captures. A one-pan 53.43 µH screen or unloaded 88 µH cannot establish a product-wide minimum.
4. **Select parts against actual waveforms:** compare VCE/VR, IGBT and diode current, reverse recovery, tank-capacitor AC+DC voltage/current, gate charge/overshoot and coil/connector thermal stress against **applicable** manufacturer limits at actual gate resistor, temperature, frequency and cooling. Use transient switching and fault analysis; the first-harmonic screen can only reject obvious candidates.
5. **Close protection timing and failed-short path:** current/phase/no-pan detectors, PWM and PERMIT default-off, bootstrap and rail-order tests, measured gate/tank current cessation, and a separately rated bank-side interruption/discharge path. A failed-short switch gets no gate-disable PASS.
6. **Only then** select operating frequency, switch and capacitor, construct the native standalone unit and run digital parity/ERC/DRC/Rust adverse checks. Loaded switching, EMC, heating and assembled appliance qualification remain later physical gates.

The model has no harmonic switching current, device capacitance, nonlinear L/R, core/skin/proximity temperature shift, diode recovery, PWM dead time, bus ripple, transient state, control loop, or thermal network. `SCREEN_ONLY` means equations evaluated for declared example inputs; it is never an acceptance verdict.
