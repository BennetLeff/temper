# JLC 3D placement preview of the C49 controller (2026-10-19)

Done in JLC's own viewer (project "controller-gerbers" in the owner's JLC account, drafts only: nothing saved to the cart, no order). The Gerbers
`fab/controller-gerbers.zip`, the BOM `fab/bom-jlcpcb.csv` and the CPL were uploaded. JLC matched 88 of 89 BOM lines (R39's RG2012V has no stock: set to
do-not-place in the draft, as ORDER.md's fallback says), and required **Standard PCBA**: the order has 83 extended part types (Economic allows 80) and U4 (the
ESP32-S3 module) is "Standard Only".

How the viewer shows orientation: a magenta dot on an IC's pin-1 pad, a magenta "+" / "-" on a polarized part's pads, and our own silkscreen pin-1 triangle or bracket
from the Gerbers. They should be at the same corner.

## Result 1: D9 (SS14, C2480)
JLC's cathode band and "-" marker are on the left pad, which is pad 1 in our footprint. CPL rotation 0 is correct (also D9-C2480.md).

## Result 2: the NEEDS-PREVIEW CPL was wrong for the ICs and diodes, and is now corrected
With the CPL as first uploaded (KiCad rotations), JLC's pin-1 dot was at the **opposite corner** from our silk mark on U11 and U12 (SOT-23-5) and U16 and Q1 (SOT-23),
and 90 degrees off on U3 (WSON-6). That is exactly the EasyEDA-geometry offset in `audit.csv` (180, 180, 180, 180, 270), and not the 270 the JLCKicadTools rotation database
gives for those SOT-23 parts, so the geometry was right and the database wrong for them. Placed as uploaded, these parts would have been assembled rotated.

`layout/fab.py` now applies the audit's geometry offset to every NEEDS-PREVIEW IC and diode code whose offset is not 0: U1 U2 (SOT-583, 180), U3 (WSON, 270), U14 (QSOP-20, 270),
D8 Q1 U13 U16 (SOT-23, 180), and the SOT-23-5 codes C105188 C7666 C7835 C29777121 C10096 C962078 C402161 (180). 29 CPL rotations changed from the first upload; the BOM and the Gerber zip
are unchanged. CPL sha256 `2327ec703cd241ee096b4f687d8c01d75f4d71893b833a98193fda6f53465473`.

**The THT connectors J1 J2 J3 J4 J7 are NOT rotated** (their geometry offset of 180 was tried first and reverted). For a physical connector the body must match the silk outline;
the viewer shows no pin-1 mark on them; and with +180 JLC's right-angle J4 body hung off the board edge, where our silk and ENCLOSURE-HANDOFF ("mating +X") put it inside. They stay at
KiCad's rotation and still need a look in the preview: body and keying against our silk. The JLC draft was re-uploaded with this revised CPL, but I could not re-check J4 afterwards because
the browser extension disconnected.
