# JLC 3D placement preview of the C49 controller (2026-10-19)

Done in JLC's own viewer (project "controller-gerbers" in the owner's JLC account, drafts only: nothing saved to the cart, no order). Gerbers
`fab/controller-gerbers.zip`, BOM `fab/bom-jlcpcb.csv` and the CPL below were uploaded; JLC matched 88 of 89 BOM lines (R39's RG2012V has no stock: set to do-not-place in the
draft, as ORDER.md's fallback), and required **Standard PCBA**: the order has 83 extended part types (Economic allows 80) and U4 (the ESP32-S3 module) is "Standard Only".

How the viewer shows orientation: a magenta dot on an IC's pin-1 pad, a magenta "+" / "-" on a polarized part's pads, and our own silkscreen pin-1 triangle or bracket
from the Gerbers. They should be at the same corner.

## Result 1: D9 (SS14, C2480)
JLC's cathode band and "-" marker are on the left pad, which is pad 1 in our footprint. CPL rotation 0 is correct (also D9-C2480.md).

## Result 2: the NEEDS-PREVIEW CPL was wrong for the SOT-23 families, and is now corrected
With the CPL as first uploaded (KiCad rotations, sha256 `b69720e0a0654b855936f1535b05632bf5e99ffa4ce7eb35fa7d6c233d11fb65`), JLC's pin-1 dot was at the **opposite corner** from our silk mark on U11 and U12 (SOT-23-5), U16
(SOT-23) and Q1 (SOT-23), and 90 degrees off on U3 (WSON-6). That is exactly the EasyEDA-geometry offset in `audit.csv` (180, 180, 180, 180, 270), and it is **not** the offset the
JLCKicadTools rotation database gives (270) for those SOT-23 parts, so the geometry was right and the database wrong for them. Placed as uploaded, these parts would have been
assembled rotated.

`layout/fab.py` now applies the audit's geometry offset to every NEEDS-PREVIEW LCSC code whose offset is not 0: the J1 J2 J3 J4 J7 connectors (180), U1 U2 (SOT-583, 180),
U3 (WSON, 270), U14 (QSOP-20, 270), D8 Q1 U13 U16 (SOT-23, 180), and the SOT-23-5 codes C105188 C7666 C7835 C29777121 C10096 C962078 C402161 (180). 34 CPL rotations changed;
nothing else in the CPL or BOM, and the Gerber zip is unchanged. New CPL sha256 `799bf9566663ff8f60242532c232115a95f0cc4f9ed452ed88a3d7024d6657d1`.

## Result 3: re-upload and re-check
After re-uploading the corrected CPL the viewer shows the pin-1 dot at the silk marker on **U11, U12, U16, Q1** (all clear, large view), and C9's "+" / "-" agree with its silk "+".
U3 and U2 look consistent at the viewer's resolution (magenta dot at the silk corner) but the glyphs are small.
