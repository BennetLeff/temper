# Integrated SELV controller (schematic capture)

This is the induction cooktop's low-voltage controller, captured in atopile and ready for PCB layout. **It has no layout yet.** The board integrates:
- an ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8;
- the interlock and thermal-sense circuits, ported unchanged;
- the RTD front end;
- the D-11 bus-voltage receiver;
- the buffers for the ADC channels;
- the 3.3 V and 12 V fan supplies;
- the UI and probe connectors.

It draws power from native-21's J4 header and supplies native-21's 3.3 V logic back through J4.3.

**Status:** schematic source and frozen netlist only, with no layout and no physical test. Open items and decisions are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). The connector list and placement note for the enclosure team are in [ENCLOSURE-HANDOFF.md](ENCLOSURE-HANDOFF.md).

**Requirement inputs** (read-only):
- `output/temper-manufacturing-r25/design/r25/CONTROLLER-ARCHITECTURE.md` and `CONTROLLER-SPEC.md`. They supersede r24 only in the harness landings.
- The R20 M1/M3, R21 N8 and R24 decisions.
- The native-21 J4 contract on branch `codex/power-stage-integration` @ `7c55c9ab8`, frozen into [inputs/native-21/](inputs/native-21/) with hashes in `provenance.json`. This covers `FAULT-INTERFACE.md`, DECISIONS (bus sense D-11, timing D-20, LINE_ZC, the 15 A input cap with its correction) and `firmware/components/power/include/{fullbridge_adapter,input_limits,line_zc}.h`.

**What "ported unchanged" means here:** `tools/port_blocks.py` copies `zapote/interlock/source-build-02/.../interlock_unit.ato` and `zapote/thermal-sense/source-build-02/.../thermal_sense_unit.ato` with one line filter. It drops only:
- the standalone inter-board headers (interlock J1/J2, thermal-sense J3);
- their pin joins;
- thermal-sense's `+3V3`/`gnd` net-name overrides.

`--check` proves that the committed files equal *source minus those lines*. The thermal probe connectors stay. Every value, MPN and internal connection is unchanged. `RTDSensing` is instantiated directly from the byte-identical library, so the RTD unit's 2×5 host header is not used.

## Build, freeze, verify

From `zapote/controller`. These use pinned atopile, are offline and build no Rust or workspace.

```sh
python3 tools/freeze.py            # ato build + circuit_export + normalise into frozen/ + receipt
python3 tools/freeze.py --check    # rebuild; fail if frozen/ is stale ("frozen fresh")
python3 tools/verify_controller.py # all checks on frozen/; exit 1 on any failure
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
python3 tools/controller_calcs.py  # numeric basis only
python3 tools/snapshot_native21.py # re-derive inputs/native-21 from git (read-only)
```

- **Raw build:** `uv tool run --offline --from atopile==0.2.69 ato --non-interactive build elec/src/controller.ato:SelvController`.

## Interlock trip path (hardware only)

**How the check works.** `verify_controller.py` builds a gate-level model from the captured netlist: LVC14, HC30, LVC1G74, the LVC1G08/32/38/14 gates and the LVC244 buffer, keyed by MPN pin tables. Comparators, supervisors, J4.10, the MCU and the I²C expander become free variables. The pass criterion is an **independent specification** of each source's healthy level (`tools/trip_sim.py`, `HEALTHY_LEVEL`), never the circuit's own internal nets.

**Structural checks:**
- PERMIT (J4.9) has one driver, the latch Q. It has a 10 kΩ pull-down here, and native-21 adds two 100 kΩ.
- The only firmware-driven net in PERMIT's fan-in cone is `IL_RESET_N` (GPIO17).
- No MCU or expander pin touches any other cone net. The MCU reads PERMIT, bus_fault, the supervisor output, RAIL_OK, LATCHED_FAULT and the three block faults only through the SN74LVC244A, so no GPIO misconfiguration can drive a protective net.
- The runaway comparator observes RTDIN+ through the RTD block's window branch, and its threshold comes from the BIAS divider (checked, because comparators are free variables).
- The rail supervisor senses v3v3, and the watchdog's WDI is the MCU heartbeat.

**Exhaustive check.** All 2¹¹ source combinations × latch state × every RESET_N transition (16 384 cases) were enumerated:
- every case with any source unhealthy leaves **PERMIT low**, whatever firmware does;
- all-healthy plus a fresh RESET_N falling edge grants PERMIT;
- all-healthy with no edge holds state.

**Single-cause truth table.** Each row has every other source healthy and starts from PERMIT high. The result is identical for RESET_N held low, held high, falling or rising.

| Trip source (net → interlock input) | Fault level | PERMIT | LATCHED_FAULT |
|---|---|---|---|
| J4.10 `bus_fault` (native-21 OR of OCP, OVP, HOT5, CT±; an open wire reads high via the 10 kΩ pull-up) → OCP | high/open | 0 | 1 |
| OVP input → tied to selv_gnd through a 0 Ω link (bus_fault already covers OVP) | — | — | — |
| thermal `HS_HOT` or `HS_OPEN` → HS_FAULT (LVC1G32) → heatsink | high | 0 | 1 |
| thermal `COIL_HOT` or `COIL_OPEN` → COIL_FAULT → coil | high | 0 | 1 |
| RTD low/high window out of range, or `RTD_RAIL_OK` low → RTD_HW_FAULT (open-drain NAND) → RTD | low | 0 | 1 |
| `RUNAWAY_FAULT`: pan ≥ 297–319 °C, MAX31865 BIAS off, or RTD open → runaway | high | 0 | 1 |
| `RAIL_OK` low (v3v3 outside 3.146–3.190 / 3.408–3.456 V, or within 10 ms of start-up) → RAIL_FAULT → AUX, and SENSOR_LIVE low | low | 0 | 1 |
| `RTD_RAIL_OK` low (RTD_AVDD below its 3.05 V supervisor) → SENSOR_LIVE low (and RTD_HW_FAULT) | low | 0 | 1 |
| Supervisor run-OK `WDT_RESET_N` low (TPS3823: WDI not toggled within 0.9 s minimum, or VDD below its 2.83–3.00 V threshold, SLVS165O) | low | 0 | 1 |
| All healthy, RESET_N held | — | holds | holds |
| All healthy for ≥ 1 ms, then a fresh RESET_N falling edge | — | 1 | 0 |

The simulation treats the sources as independent. Physical paths that make several assert together, like v3v3 loss, are covered by the FMEA in DECISIONS.md.
- **Loss of v3v3 or selv_gnd, or J4 unplugged:** the latch loses supply, the controller's 10 kΩ and native-21's 100 kΩ pull-downs take PERMIT low, and a broken J4.10 conductor reads as a fault.
- **Firmware reach:** it can read the latch and request a reset edge. It cannot hold the latch clear or override a trip.

## Power tree

```
native-21 PS1 IRM-20-15 (15 V ±2.5 %, 21 W) ── J4.1 v15_selv ── 100 µF + 2×(10 µF‖100 nF)
  ├─ U1 TPS62933F (FCCM, 500 kHz, EN UVLO on 10.9–12.1 V, off ≥ 9.8 V), 10 µH, 3×22 µF
  │    └─ v3v3 3.304 V nom (3.242–3.367 V, 0–85 °C)
  │         ├─ ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8, OLED (J6), encoder (J5), expander, buffers, D-11 receiver
  │         ├─ interlock / thermal-sense / RTD blocks (RTD analogue behind its ferrite → RTD_AVDD)
  │         ├─ U3 TPS3703A4330 window supervisor (SENSE via 1 k / 1 µF) → RAIL_OK
  │         └─ J4.3 → native-21 SELV logic (u_fault_or, 4× ISO7710, AMC1311 VDD2, UCC21550 VCCI …)
  └─ U2 TPS62933 (500 kHz), 22 µH, 2×22 µF → v12_fan 11.78–12.22 V → J10 fan (0.63 A)
       EN defaults on; firmware can only switch the fan off (Q1 on GPIO46)
```

**v3v3 budget**, from datasheet maxima at 3.465 V (`evidence/verify.json`, rows per part):

| Load | mA |
|---|---|
| Controller board, all parts on v3v3. Includes the ESP32 at 500 mA (Espressif's ≥ 0.5 A supply requirement; TX peak 355 mA), MAX31865 9.65 mA with BIAS, OPA2388 5.2 mA, the interlock WDI 1 kΩ pull-down 3.5 mA, and pull-ups at full current | 534.5 |
| native-21 on J4.3: 2× UCC21550 VCCI 4.8, AMC1311 IDD2 7.2, 4× ISO7710 ICC2 1.2, 2× 330 Ω DIS pull-ups 11.1 each, CT bias/references, 3× TLV3201, logic. Computed from its frozen netlist; every v3v3 part must be modelled or the check fails | 49.0 |
| native-21 loads on controller-driven J4 lines: UCC21550 INx 50 kΩ min, PERMIT gate networks | 0.35 |
| OLED NHD-3.12-25664UCW2, 100 % on (Newhaven IDD max) | 340 |
| Encoder supply allocation [UNVERIFIED part] | 30 |
| **Peak** | **954** |

- **Converter limits:** the TPS62933F is good for 3 A and SWPA6045S100MT for 2.45 A Irms (3.5 A sat).
- **PS1 at simultaneous peak:** 12.4 W, against IRM-20-15's 21 W. That is 3.9 W on the 3.3 V rail at an 85 % allocation plus 8.6 W for the fan at 90 %.
- **Own load:** with the fan excluded, the controller's own load stays under the spec's ≈ 4 W even at peak.
- **Native-21's rail:** it sees 3.237–3.389 V at its J4.3. That covers regulator tolerance, the J4 harness (≤ 1 m AWG22, two 10 mΩ contacts per conductor; an allocation, because the harness isn't specified) and ground shift from the 15 V return. This is inside the 3.135–3.465 V contract.

**Rail window** (`tools/controller_calcs.py`):
- **Thresholds:** the TPS3703A4330 trips UV at 3.146–3.190 V and OV at 3.408–3.456 V, both inside the contract.
- **Regulator corners:** the low corner clears the UV *release* threshold (≤ 3.217 V) by 21 mV, so RESET always releases after start-up.
- **Margins left** for excursions slower than the 1 ms SENSE filter: 47 mV (low) and 39 mV (high). Faster Wi-Fi/OLED load steps are filtered.
- **Bench item:** measure supervisor nuisance trips under combined ESP32-TX and OLED-on steps.

## ADC1 and sampling

| GPIO | ADC1 | Signal | Source | Attenuation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CH0 | CT_MON_ADC | TLV9062 follower of J4.14 ct_mon (≥ 10 MΩ DC load), 100 Ω / 1 nF | ATTEN3 (0–2.9 V) |
| 2 | CH1 | VBUS_ADC | D-11 OPA2388 half-gain difference receiver, 100 Ω / 100 nF | ATTEN2 (0–1.6 V) |
| 6 | CH5 | HS_ADC | TLV9062 follower of thermal-sense HS_SENSE | ATTEN3 |
| 7 | CH6 | COIL_ADC | TLV9062 follower of COIL_SENSE | ATTEN3 |
| 8 | CH7 | M12_ADC | TLV9062 follower of the M12 probe divider | ATTEN3 |

**Free ADC1 pads:** GPIO3, 4, 5, 9 and 10 are used digitally. GPIO3 is fan tach (a strap, harmless unless the JTAG eFuse is burnt). GPIO4/5 are PWM, GPIO9 is I²C SDA and GPIO10 is RTD CS. So **ADC1 is full**: a sixth analog input needs an external ADC.

**Sampling plan.** It is checked against ESP-IDF v5.2.3 `SOC_ADC_SAMPLE_FREQ_THRES_HIGH` = 83 333 S/s and the datasheet's 100 kS/s.
- **Continuous pattern** at 80 kS/s: `VBUS, CT_MON, VBUS, CT_MON, VBUS, HS, COIL, M12`.
- **Resulting rates:** VBUS 30 kS/s (≥ 20 kS/s required) with a largest gap of 50 µs (≤ 150 µs freshness), CT_MON 20 kS/s, and the three temperatures 10 kS/s each.

**D-11 receiver:**
- **At the ADC:** 0.248 V at 0 V bus, 0.956 V at 170 V and 1.073 V at 198 V.
- **Over-range:** the 1210 mV over-range latch corresponds to a 231 V bus.

## Part choices (summary; the reasons are in DECISIONS.md)

| Function | Part | Why |
|---|---|---|
| MCU | ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8 (C2913198) | Required. It has no octal PSRAM, so GPIO35–37 are free. The round-5 firmware targets it |
| 3.3 V | TPS62933FDRLR (C5219272) | 3.8–30 V, 3 A, ±1.5 % VFB. Forced-CCM gives predictable ripple. Shares a die with the fan buck |
| 12 V fan | TPS62933DRLR (C3200405) | 98 % duty with frequency foldback. 0.846 duty is needed at 14.5 V in, under the 0.923 limit without foldback |
| Rail window | TPS3703A4330DSER (C2861205) | ±0.7 % fixed ±4 % window, the only class that fits inside 3.135–3.465 V around a ±1.5 % regulator. The TPS3700 (±1.7 %) does not. **JLC stock 0**; the stocked Q1 grade C1850327 is a drop-in |
| Bus receiver | OPA2388IDR + LM4040A25 + RT0603 0.1 % | The D-11 circuit as specified |
| Analog buffers | 2× TLV9062IDR (C398355) | CMOS, 0.5 pA typical bias. Gives the thermal-sense nodes a receiver that stays within their ±1 µA budget and isolates ADC switched-capacitor kick |
| Read-back isolation | SN74LVC244APWR (C7668) | One part buffers all eight protective read-backs; Ioff |
| SENSOR_LIVE, AUX | SN74LVC1G08, SN74LVC1G14 | — |
| Runaway | TLV3201AIDBVR + RT0603 20k/10k 0.1 % | Same comparator as the RTD and thermal blocks |
| I/O expander | TCA9555PWR (C465732) | Buttons, encoder push, OLED reset, four fault read-backs |
| J4 | Molex 43045-1612 (C491447) | The same header as native-21, with a 1:1 harness |
| Encoder | TE 7-215079-6 (C306122) | Micro-MaTch female-on-board; mates the encoder lead's 7-215083-6 |
| Other connectors | JST XH (B2B/B3B/B4B/B8B) | The thermal-sense and RTD units already use XH |
