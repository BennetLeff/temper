# Integrated SELV controller (schematic capture)

This is the induction cooktop's low-voltage controller, captured in atopile and laid out (layout L3: enclosure R33 delta on the R31 outline, the R36 rim NTC, and the L3 blower supply, NTC filters and thermal trip ladder; see [LAYOUT.md](LAYOUT.md)). The board integrates:
- an ESP32-S3-WROOM-1U-N8 (U.FL to an off-board chip antenna, enclosure R33);
- the interlock and thermal-sense circuits, ported unchanged;
- the RTD front end;
- the D-11 bus-voltage receiver;
- the buffers for the ADC channels;
- the 3.3 V supply and the adjustable 5.5–12 V blower supply with tach (L3, DECISIONS C28), the blower buck fed from its own 15 V supply PS3 through J13 (stage 16, C33);
- the probe connectors and one 12-way UI bus (J7, R33) to the fascia UI board, which carries the OLED, buttons, encoder and buzzer.

It draws all of its power from PS3 on J13 (C49: the logic rail `v15_selv` is fed from `V15_BLW` through D9, SS14) and supplies native-21's 3.3 V logic back through J4.3. J4.1 is not a supply since C49: it is `bias_good_j4` from the power board, read on GPIO35.

**Status:** schematic source, frozen netlist and a routed, DRC-clean layout candidate (0 errors, 0 unconnected, schematic parity 0; DFM, enclosure checks and fab review PASS) with a JLC fab package ([LAYOUT.md](LAYOUT.md)). **Candidate, not released**: ordering is the owner's decision. Not fabricated, no physical test. Open items and decisions are in [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md). The connector list and placement note for the enclosure team are in [ENCLOSURE-HANDOFF.md](ENCLOSURE-HANDOFF.md).

**Requirement inputs** (read-only):

## Interlock trip path (hardware only)

**How the check works.** `verify_controller.py` builds a gate-level model from the captured netlist: LVC14, HC30, LVC1G74, the LVC1G08/32/38/14 gates and the LVC244 buffer, keyed by MPN pin tables. Comparators, supervisors, J4.10, the MCU and the I²C expander become free variables. The pass criterion is an **independent specification** of each source's healthy level (`tools/trip_sim.py`, `HEALTHY_LEVEL`), never the circuit's own internal nets.

**Structural checks:**
- PERMIT (J4.9) has one driver, the AND gate U37 (SN74LVC1G08): **PERMIT = latch Q AND RUN_EN** (DECISIONS C23). It has a 10 kΩ pull-down here (R103), and native-21 adds two 100 kΩ. The latch Q (`PERMIT_LATCH`) keeps the block's own 10 kΩ pull-down.
- The only firmware-driven nets in PERMIT's fan-in cone are `IL_RESET_N` (GPIO17, the reset request) and `RUN_EN` (GPIO14). RUN_EN feeds only the PERMIT gate (checked), has a 4.7 kΩ pull-down and no pull-up, and sits on a GPIO with no reset pull-up, so it reads "not permitted" at reset and with the MCU unpowered.
- No MCU or expander pin touches any other cone net. The MCU reads PERMIT, bus_fault, the supervisor output, RAIL_OK, LATCHED_FAULT and the three block faults only through the SN74LVC244A, so no GPIO misconfiguration can drive a protective net.
- The runaway comparator observes J3.2 (the connector-side S+) through the RTD block's window branch, and its threshold comes from the BIAS divider returned to J3.3 (S−, C38); the rim trip comparator compares the filtered rim NTC node with a v3v3 divider (checked, because comparators are free variables).
- The rail supervisor senses v3v3, and the watchdog's WDI is the MCU heartbeat.

**Exhaustive check.** All 2¹⁴ source combinations (C38 adds the rim trip, C39 the two capacitive-mode flip-flop outputs) × latch state × every RESET_N transition × RUN_EN (262 144 cases) were enumerated:
- every case with any source unhealthy leaves **PERMIT low**, whatever firmware does;
- PERMIT is never high while the latch is tripped or RUN_EN is low (`run_en_alone_cannot_assert_permit`): RUN_EN can only remove permission;
- all-healthy plus a fresh RESET_N falling edge with RUN_EN high grants PERMIT;
- all-healthy with no edge holds the latch state.

**Single-cause truth table.** Each row has every other source healthy and RUN_EN high, and starts from PERMIT high. The result is identical for RESET_N held low, held high, falling or rising.

| Trip source (net → interlock input) | Fault level | PERMIT | LATCHED_FAULT |
|---|---|---|---|
| J4.10 `bus_fault` (native-21 OR of OCP, OVP, HOT5, CT±; an open wire reads high via the 10 kΩ pull-up) → OCP | high/open | 0 | 1 |
| `CAP_FAULT` (C39): a capacitive-mode sample on a leg-A turn-off (U42 1Q̄ or 2Q) → OVP input | high | 0 | 1 |
| thermal `HS_HOT` or `HS_OPEN` → HS_FAULT (LVC1G32) → heatsink | high | 0 | 1 |
| thermal `COIL_HOT` or `COIL_OPEN` → COIL_FAULT → coil | high | 0 | 1 |
| RTD low/high window out of range, or `RTD_RAIL_OK` low → RTD_HW_FAULT (open-drain NAND) → RTD | low | 0 | 1 |
| `RUNAWAY_FAULT`: pan ≥ 298.8–323.1 °C (C38), MAX31865 BIAS off, or RTD open; or the rim NTC ≥ 262.1–288.3 °C or shorted (U40, C38) → runaway | high | 0 | 1 |
| `RAIL_OK` low (v3v3 outside 3.146–3.190 / 3.408–3.456 V, or within 10 ms of start-up) → RAIL_FAULT → AUX, and SENSOR_LIVE low | low | 0 | 1 |
| `RTD_RAIL_OK` low (RTD_AVDD below its 3.05 V supervisor) → SENSOR_LIVE low (and RTD_HW_FAULT) | low | 0 | 1 |

| All healthy, RESET_N held | — | holds | holds |
| All healthy for ≥ 1 ms, then a fresh RESET_N falling edge | — | 1 | 0 |

The simulation treats the sources as independent. Physical paths that make several assert together, like v3v3 loss, are covered by the FMEA in DECISIONS.md.
- **Loss of v3v3 or selv_gnd, or J4 unplugged:** the latch loses supply, the controller's 10 kΩ and native-21's 100 kΩ pull-downs take PERMIT low, and a broken J4.10 conductor reads as a fault.
- **Firmware reach:** it can read the latch, request a reset edge and drop RUN_EN (a fast stop that removes PERMIT without tripping the latch). It cannot hold the latch clear, assert PERMIT while the latch is tripped, or override a trip.

- **Converter limits:** the TPS62933F is good for 3 A and SWPA6045S100MT for 2.45 A Irms (3.5 A sat).
- **PS3 (C49, logic and blower on one supply):** merged continuous load 44.55 W against 65 W, 99 % of the 45 W 60 °C derated figure (`tools/controller_calcs.py` `merge_rail`; DECISIONS C49). The PS1 figures that stood here were for the pre-C49 board, which had a separate 21 W logic supply.
- **Own load:** with the fan excluded, the controller's own load stays under the spec's ≈ 4 W even at peak.
- **Native-21's rail:** it sees 3.237–3.389 V at its J4.3. That covers regulator tolerance, the J4 harness (≤ 1 m AWG22, two 10 mΩ contacts per conductor; an allocation, because the harness isn't specified) and ground shift from the 15 V return. This is inside the 3.135–3.465 V contract.

**Rail window** (`tools/controller_calcs.py`):
- **Thresholds:** the TPS3703A4330 trips UV at 3.146–3.190 V and OV at 3.408–3.456 V, both inside the contract.
- **Regulator corners:** the low corner clears the UV *release* threshold (≤ 3.217 V) by 21 mV, so RESET always releases after start-up.
- **Margins left** for excursions slower than the 1 ms SENSE filter: 47 mV (low) and 39 mV (high). Faster Wi-Fi/OLED load steps are filtered.
- **Bench item:** measure supervisor nuisance trips under combined ESP32-TX and OLED-on steps.

## ADC1 and sampling

| GPIO | ADC1 | Signal | Source | Attenuation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CH0 | CT_MON_ADC | TLV9062 follower of J4.14 ct_mon (≥ 10 MΩ DC load), 100 Ω / 1 nF | ATTEN3 (0–2.9 V) |
| 2 | CH1 | VBUS_ADC | D-11 OPA2388 half-gain difference receiver, 100 Ω / 100 nF | ATTEN2 (0–1.6 V) |
| 6 | CH5 | HS_ADC | TLV9062 follower of thermal-sense HS_SENSE | ATTEN3 |
| 7 | CH6 | COIL_ADC | TLV9062 follower of COIL_SENSE | ATTEN3 |
| 8 | CH7 | M12_ADC | TLV9062 follower of the M12 probe divider | ATTEN3 |

**Free ADC1 pads:** GPIO3, 4, 5, 9 and 10 are used digitally. GPIO3 is fan tach (a strap, harmless unless the JTAG eFuse is burnt). GPIO4/5 are PWM, GPIO9 is I²C SDA and GPIO10 is RTD CS. So **ADC1 is full**: the tank-RMS signal therefore goes to an external MCP3202 on the RTD SPI bus (below).

**Tank RMS (external ADC).** U14 AD8436BRQZ converts the buffered ct_mon to true RMS. Its datasheet gives 65 kHz for 1 % additional error at 300 mV rms, against a 31–55 kHz tank band.
- **Scaling:** a gain-4 output buffer puts 22 A RMS tank at 1.32 V (1606 codes) and the 55 A peak trip at 2.33 V.
- **Errors:** coupling error at 31 kHz is 0.002 %; Schottky leakage contributes ≤ 0.15 %.
- **Settling:** CAVG = 100 nF C0G gives an estimated 3.4 ms to 0.1 %, falling. That is the datasheet's 10 µF figure scaled linearly; the scaling is an assumption to bench-check. It is within the 8.3 ms half-cycle.
- **ADC:** U15 MCP3202 CH0 reads it; CH1 reads the LM4040A25 2.5 V (0.1 %) so firmware removes the v3v3-as-reference error. CS is GPIO42. The bus stays on the board; the UI board has its own SPI host (C26).
- **Why not ADC2:** the free ADC2 pads are the debug USB pins, and ADC2 cannot be read while Wi-Fi runs.

The raw ct_mon ADC1 path stays for phase and waveform checks.

**Sampling plan.** It is checked against ESP-IDF v5.2.3 `SOC_ADC_SAMPLE_FREQ_THRES_HIGH` = 83 333 S/s and the datasheet's 100 kS/s.
- **Continuous pattern** at 80 kS/s: `VBUS, CT_MON, VBUS, CT_MON, VBUS, HS, COIL, M12, RIM` (RIM: R36 rim NTC, C27).
- **Resulting rates:** VBUS 26.7 kS/s (≥ 20 kS/s required) with a largest gap of 62.5 µs (≤ 150 µs freshness), CT_MON 17.8 kS/s, and the four temperatures 8.9 kS/s each.

**D-11 receiver:**
- **At the ADC:** 0.248 V at 0 V bus, 0.956 V at 170 V and 1.073 V at 198 V.
- **Over-range:** the 1210 mV over-range latch corresponds to a 231 V bus.
