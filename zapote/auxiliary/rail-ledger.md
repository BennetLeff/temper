## Startup and return graph to resolve

The Rev38 PFC RUN path consumes `AUX_PROTECTED` and `HOT_LOGIC5`, so their production must not depend on PFC RUN. The compiled AC entry exposes fused L/N after the CMC and before the main NTC/bypass, but F1 and its harness remain unbuilt (`AC-INPUT.md`). An auxiliary AC branch there could be live before PFC RUN; its own branch protection and conductor still need design. The historical SELV auxiliary source uses a half-bus and cannot be assumed to replace the HOT-referenced source. `HOT0`, SELV `GND` and inverter `HV_RETURN` are distinct until a reviewed circuit proves their relationship. Historical SELV `GND` is PE-bonded in `elec/src/main.ato`; that fact does not decide the new board's grounding policy. A combined-source option must explicitly prove insulation and partial-power behavior.

**Failure order:** AUX may energize the UCC27624 before HOT logic5 energizes its TPS3890/TLV3202 detectors. `HOT-RAILS.md` leaves the ENA shunt/release proof open in this interval. Supply hiccup, cutoff recovery or logic5 brownout must not synthesize a new ARM or START; Rev38's explicit disarm and restart protocol owns that transition. The SELV interlock's `SENSOR_LIVE` and AUX fault inputs lack accepted producers and cannot be tied healthy merely because 3V3 has risen (`zapote/interlock/INTERFACES.md`).

**U1 boundary proposal (pending owner scope confirmation):** pursue independently accepted HOT and SELV supplies. Treat inverter low-side bias as a third isolated output requiring its own acceptance, whether eventually generated by a shared producer or a separate one. HOT bias must be available before precharge/RUN and returns to HOT0; the legacy SELV supply was fed from a different half-bus and returns to SELV GND. A single combined circuit would presently conceal an unchosen insulation crossing and startup dependency. This proposal would divide construction evidence; it does not select a producer or prevent a later shared transformer with separately proven outputs.

## U1 open decisions and evidence requests

1. Resolve the Rev38 producer itself. The live, uncommitted `AUX-SOURCE-CANDIDATE.md` proposes IRM-20-15 → LTC4368-2 with FET/shunt → TPS54202 HOT logic5, tapped after CMC/before NTC. Its own status says none is joined or accepted. Bind a committed source/netlist revision before adopting it here.
2. Specify the off-board F1 and *additional AUX branch* fuse/conductor/inrush path and the module's exact AC/DC pin/footprint identity; the nominal 20 A F1 candidate does not establish protection for the smaller branch.
3. Bound actual steady, startup, capacitor-recharge and dropout currents at `AUX_PROTECTED` and `HOT_LOGIC5`; nominal resistor arithmetic and STW typical charge are insufficient. Determine local module temperature, not just the 40 °C cooling inlet.
4. Obtain selected fan, gate-drive and inverter bias operating conditions and the new SELV supply input topology before finalizing their load and insulation contributions.
