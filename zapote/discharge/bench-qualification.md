# Discharge candidate bench qualification protocol (unperformed)

This is an experiment plan. No listed measurement has been performed and no live-mains or appliance safety claim follows from the digital screen.

1. **Freeze the article and criteria.** Record the exact schematic/netlist, F2 location, VD/VB/direct and detached inverter capacitance with tolerance/aging/voltage bias, resistor and relay order codes, mounted chassis geometry, thermal interfaces, fan dependency, coil rail and driver, firmware revision, adopted residual-voltage threshold and time, allowed single-fault behavior, and restart authorization. Hash the frozen files and retain instrument calibrations. Reject a case if any required identity or criterion is missing.
2. **Prepare an isolated, energy-limited DC fixture.** Use a current-limited HV source with independent disconnection, rated enclosure, remote controls and separately rated voltage probes at both VD–HOT0 and VB–HOT0. Use an independent discharge and verified zero-energy check before any access. Build both F2-open and F2-closed configurations; treat each as a distinct energy topology. Add an inverter-side local capacitor and, separately, a detachable capacitor without a path to expose its retained energy. This procedure requires a qualified laboratory and approved HV work controls before execution.
3. **Measure normal discharge.** At the selected minimum/nominal/maximum voltages and C/R corners, acquire both island voltage waveforms from source-off command through final threshold, including contact coil waveform, loaded release time, contact bounce and F2 voltage. Repeat at thermal steady state and after aging/cycle exposure. Compare the slowest measured island against the adopted criterion including instrumentation uncertainty and restart interlock latency. A completed timer without both measured voltages below threshold is a fail.
4. **Exercise AUX loss with the HV source still connected.** Disconnect AUX while keeping the current-limited HV source engaged to simulate mains-attached rectification. Verify the NC contact state, coil dropout, resistor currents, and whether the fan actually stops. Maintain the event for the maximum specified dwell and then repeat bounded brownout/recovery cycles; include any contact chatter and uncommanded pickup. Record the total and each resistor's power, chassis and resistor case temperatures, relay temperature, and recovery timing. Do not infer a decay deadline while a source can replenish the bus. Stop if any component, wiring, enclosure, or contact limit is reached; choose explicit limits from exact datasheets and the assembled design before testing.
5. **Inject one fault at a time.** Open each VD string, open one VB resistor branch, short one VD or VB resistor under a controlled low-energy setup, force the contact open and closed, hold the coil energized after AUX loss, open each VD/VB sense path, and open F2. Record the voltage and thermal consequences plus detection, inhibit, service indication and rearm behavior. Do not use a destructive short procedure on a populated HV board unless a separately approved safe fault-injection fixture exists. Confirm that an F2-open VB measurement cannot stand in for VD or vice versa.
6. **Qualify parts in their actual mounting.** Obtain manufacturer or measured pulse and repeated-cycle thermal impedance for the RH50 value and installation, including four resistors heating the same chassis with fan stopped and the one-short energy concentration. Verify the TNPW element voltage/power sharing, relay DC make/break life at the actual current/voltage/waveform and ambient, coil voltage maximum and worst loaded release, and required creepage/clearance. The RH50 536 cm² datasheet fixture and Coto typical release are comparison conditions, not installed guarantees.
7. **Verify restart independently.** With residual charge on either island, F2 open, one sense path open, and AUX brownout/recovery, confirm that restart stays inhibited. Service access requires independent measurement of both VD and VB and a deliberate rearm after verified safe voltage. Record negative controls where the timer expires or a single sensor reads low while the other island remains charged.

For every run retain raw waveforms, thermocouple positions, source and fan states, fault insertion method, acceptance criterion, uncertainty budget, article and source hashes, and a signed pass/fail sheet. Only then may an evidence-backed `*_verified=true` case be considered for a **conditional digital gate**; the physical qualification decision remains separate.
