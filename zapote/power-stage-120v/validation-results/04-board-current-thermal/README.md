## Summary

The required `smoke_test.py` rerun passed all 16 checks (`outputs/smoke_test.txt`). The starter solver's own strip and two-layer tests also printed `SELFTEST PASS` (`outputs/kit_selftest.txt`). Native-13 copper extraction succeeded: 31 filled-zone polygons, **zero zone holes**, 388 pad records, 642 tracks and 177 vias. The exact exports are retained in `inputs/`.

The independent probes in `outputs/kit_solver_audit.json` exposed defects that the starter tests do not cover:

| Probe | Correct result | Kit result | Consequence |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| One 0.8 mm drill, 18 µm plated through via, F.Cu to B.Cu | 0.5811 mΩ total barrel | 1.7433 mΩ | Each of three interlayer edges receives the full 1.6 mm barrel resistance; the total is 3× too high. |
| Two unequal-location 1 A sources on a uniform strip | 2.8996 mΩ power-equivalent path resistance | 3.5020 mΩ | `max(source voltage) / total current` overstates the loss-equivalent resistance by 20.8%. |
| Injection at (1.1, 0.25) mm beside a 0–1 mm copper square | Reject: point is off copper | Accepted as node 2 | Nearest-cell snapping can silently move an injection onto copper. |
| One disconnected, electrically unused copper island | Explicit topology diagnosis | `NaN` after singular-matrix warning | The solver cannot provide a trustworthy path verdict without a connectivity preflight. |

These probes use synthetic geometry with analytic answers; they do not imply any measured resistance on native-13. The kit's two-layer via test uses almost zero via resistance, so it cannot reveal the four-layer length error. The reported resistance formula is only valid for a single source potential; the board has multiple capacitor and MOSFET injection locations.

## Method and assumptions

The copper export was generated by KiCad Python directly from the unchanged active board. `copper_zones_with_holes.py` found zero holes in the 31 native-13 filled-zone polygons; therefore hole subtraction is not needed for this board revision. The regular `copper_dump.py` output is retained for census and net/pad identity, but its pad boxes do not by themselves supply the exact plated through-hole barrel geometry. A corrected solver must read the actual pad shape and drill from KiCad before any board solve.

The analytic via probe uses the starter kit's 1.6 mm length and the specified 18 µm plating. The 1 A strip probes are unit tests, not operating currents. The supplied 15 A rectifier and 19 A commutation figures remain assumptions from the task document; task 05's board-specific current distribution was not available here. No current was assigned to a pad by guessing its share.

The vendor SPICE files used by the smoke test matched the kit's pinned SHA-256 values: `cfd7-650.zip` `5a6341084202debb0f8f230b8809c090434ea9526c8e0defe3d2e07a832ff48d` and `IFX_CFD7_650V.lib` `02ac6634f47c25be04e8de3c6eec4ec8cf403b659001b7f176c8f98bb6e9ce3b`. These ignored vendor assets are not part of this result commit.

## Results and open items

`outputs/kit_solver_audit.json` is the raw result. `outputs/summary.json` records the blocked status and missing outputs. No current-density map, via-current file or temperature plot was generated because the electrical source is not validated. Consequently, the IPC-2221 width screen cannot yet be reconciled with a solved current distribution, and neither the 20 °C Joule-only copper-rise criterion nor the 3 A per-via flag can be assessed.

**Remaining thermal dependency:** task 03's `board_heat_sources.json` is absent. The authorized partial scope was Joule-only copper heating. Even after the electrical solver is corrected and the Joule-only heat model is independently checked against a uniformly heated strip, total board temperature at 50 °C ambient will remain blocked until task 03 supplies component heat sources. Do not interpret this report as a thermal PASS.

The next solver must pass a realistic four-layer via test; reject off-copper pad injections; diagnose disconnected source/sink topology before solving; calculate total loss as the sum of source current times source potential; and preserve exact pad and through-hole barrel geometry. The thermal model then needs its own analytic strip check and 0.5/0.25/0.125 mm electrical convergence check before board claims. IR-camera or thermocouple measurements at full power remain the physical confirmation.
