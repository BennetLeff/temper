## Findings (every 1 and every U)

### SI-03 (1): FEM extraction no longer describes the board.
- Mechanism/evidence: Switching verdicts (S1/S2/S4/S5, F6 252/252) come from loop matrices extracted on the first routed native-21 copper with native-19 corrections; native-21's own corrections and fine extraction never landed (`d2/README.md:559`), and DECISIONS.md:709 records the R5 rework as 'Not re-run'. Appendix A: R5.1 moved (39.13,10.12) to (38.5,10.0), R5.2 (37.23,9.17) to (37.0,12.1), bus_p In2 fill differs by 163 mm2 and hv_ret In1 by 143 mm2 in the leg-A box. Diode recovery has no physical bound (FINDINGS F2) and driver is DC resistances (M5).
- Proposed action: Run `round17/scripts/leg_region_diff.py` between the extraction board and 19b (the stage-1 `.kicad_pcb` is not in the tree; the copper dumps are, Appendix A), then rerun `campA21`/`campB21` on 19b copper and the F6 grid; bench S4 double-pulse (`out-D8`) to bound recovery.

### SI-06 (1): Narrow S4 voltage margin on a stale model.
- Mechanism/evidence: Worst die VDS 490.2 V (leg B) against 520 V is 30 V (5.8%), only with F6 at its -1.6 V bound; without F6 S4 reaches 550 V (`d2/README.md:584-587`, FINDINGS M11). Parallel commutation loop grew 24.9 to 37.6 nH (DECISIONS.md:144). The decision's reversal condition was never tested. Mounted lead ESL for TO-247/C38 is outside the model.
- Proposed action: Same re-extraction as SI-03; add a +5% self-L and lead-ESL sensitivity to the 520 V check; keep the bench bus <=150 V until S4 is measured; keep the SMD C0G bridge across the FET row as the named lever.

### SI-08 (1): Layer-change return gaps remain on leg-A PWM and PERMIT.
- Mechanism/evidence: Return-path report (`stage19-pi-si/return-path19b.txt:5-10,17`): pwm_ha 7.59 mm and pwm_la 5.11 mm to the nearest transfer cap, permit 20.89 mm with a 6.0 mm unreferenced run, bias_bad 22.25 mm unreferenced. The 19b caps were added only for pwm_hb (DECISIONS.md:785-789) and the gate asserts only pwm_hb/ct_zc/bus_fault_hot. Reference swap In1 v3v3 to In2 selv_gnd over a 0.5 mm core is the same mechanism as pwm_hb's.
- Proposed action: Extend `return_path19.py` asserts to every net into INA/INB/DIS/PERMIT with a stated threshold; add transfer caps at pwm_ha/pwm_la changes or route them on one layer like pwm_lb (0 transitions); capture TP14/TP15/TP17 at first power-up (BRINGUP-PROBES.md) against a written noise limit.

### SI-10 (1): HF film row has 5 K margin in an unvalidated model.
- Mechanism/evidence: C38-C41 at 5 K to 105 C at 28 A (DECISIONS.md:823); 10 uF X7R gate-rail bias loss 'not credited' (DECISIONS.md:155); RMS share among C38-C41 comes only from the provisional loop model. PS1/PS2 case 86/98 C against an 85 C ambient rating (same line).
- Proposed action: Bench IR on C38-C41 at 28 A (study list); compute per-cap RMS from the 5-port matrix against 5.4 A at 85 C; if >5 K margin is required, add the SMD C0G/film option already named in DECISIONS.md:144; derate the 0805 10 uF for bias.

### SI-13 (U): No rail impedance analysis.
- Mechanism/evidence: Only DC-link bulk/local anti-resonance (~1.3 MHz, assumed damping, 'indicative') exists (`ALT-C5C6.md:71,187`); gate-drive and F6 rails (v15_ls, p_h*, n_*) have no sweep.
- Proposed action: Sweep each rail with mounted ESL (loops 2.07-2.2 mm) against the 740 nC edge load; confirm with load-step or VNA on bench; set a droop target (F6 margin is 78.6 mV).

### SI-16 (U): Electrical length cannot be judged.
- Mechanism/evidence: PWM nets are 136-151 mm (Appendix B) straight to INA/INB with no R or C (`power_stage_120v.ato:81-82`), but controller edge time, drive impedance and J4 harness are unspecified (`06-controller-interface/README.md:1`).
- Proposed action: Record controller GPIO/MCPWM edge and output impedance, harness length, UCC21550 INx threshold/deglitch; simulate one line; capture at INA/INB at bring-up.

### SI-17 (U): No termination and no analysis it is unneeded.
- Mechanism/evidence: Same nets as SI-16; unterminated 140+ mm lines drive the shoot-through-critical driver inputs. Mechanism (ringing across INx threshold) is plausible but not quantified.
- Proposed action: After SI-16 data, either document margin or add a footprint-neutral series R at the controller end; verify at the receiver.

### SI-29 (U): Node-to-victim capacitance not extracted.
- Mechanism/evidence: Kelvin In1 copper lies under SW_A (F.Cu) 4.7/3.5 mm2 and SW_B (B.Cu) 8.6/5.7 mm2 for p/n (Appendix C) across 0.4355 mm; hand estimate ~0.75 pF, ~4-6 mA at 5-8.5 V/ns. EMI model covers only PE/chassis (`s19b/README.md:47-49`).
- Proposed action: Extract capacitance between sw_a/sw_b and ocp_*/bus_fault_hot/ct_*/gate-command copper; capture OCP_NODE and BUS_FAULT_HOT during switching; if material, relieve the In1 Kelvin copper under the SW pours.
