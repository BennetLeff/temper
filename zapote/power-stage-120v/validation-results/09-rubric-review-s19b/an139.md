## Findings (every 1 and U)

### F1. AN139-19 (score 1): layout improvements justified by distances; the power-loop FEM is stale for 19b
- **Mechanism.** Stage 19/19b claims (PI1 converter caps, PI2 U26 caps, SI1/SI2 return paths, C153-C156) are asserted by pad-to-pad distance <= 3.0 mm and "reference under every 0.25 mm of track" (`validation-results/08-manufacturing-package/native-21/stage19-pi-si/README.md` script table; `DECISIONS.md:775`), not by loop inductance, droop or an EMI proxy at a matched operating point. This is the rubric's own cue for 1 ("neatness/trace length"). Separately, the only power-loop extraction is the native-21 FEM on board `76ec8cca...` (`validation-results/01-switching-parasitics/round17/native21-setup/README.md:3`), "provisional" (native-19 corrections transferred, +2 nH stage-9 bound, `round17/d2/README.md:584`). `DECISIONS.md:709` records "Not re-run: the commutation-loop FEM" at stage 17, and no later FEM exists.
- **Evidence I computed.** Symmetric-difference area of copper inside the FEM leg boxes (`LEGS["A21"]` = 37.865,4.215,89.58,46.125 and `B21` = 2.4,4.215,42.58,46.125 from `round17/scripts/mesh25d_hybrid.py:71-72`, +10 mm margin) between `native21-all-copper.json.gz` and `native21s19b-all-copper.json.gz`: 559 mm2 (leg A) and 697 mm2 (leg B); with a 20 mm margin 969 and 1175 mm2. Largest contributors in leg A: sw_b B.Cu 124, bus_p In2 109, hv_ret In1 104, sw_a B.Cu 55 mm2; in leg B: bus_p In2 174, n_ls In1/In2 108/102, hv_ret In1 96. Part of that is pour-fill priority (item 17b), part is real (R5 WSK->CSS2H, stage 6 SW_B widening, stage 9 gate-output necking). The effect on L is unknown, which is the point. The SPICE deck still carries the Vishay `LSHUNT` 0.5-5 nH (`round17/d2/leg_matrix.cir:14-19`) while R5 is now a Bourns CSS2H.
- **Proposed action.** (1) Run `round17/scripts/leg_region_diff.py` between the FEM-base board and the pinned 19b board; if CHANGED (the numbers above say so), re-run `a21corr`/`campA21`/`campB21` on 19b copper and re-run the F6 252-case matrices. (2) For U41/U42 and U26 extract loop inductance or a PDN impedance before/after (stage 18 vs 19b dumps already exist) so the PI claim is more than a distance. (3) Update the SPICE shunt model for the CSS2H.

### F2. AN139-11 (score 1): Kelvin sense is tapped inside the commutation return and its self-field coupling is explicitly unexamined
- **Mechanism.** R5 sits in the power loop's return; the pinned PCB places its sense pads between the two power pads: leg_ret (38.5, 10.0), ocp_kelvin_p (37.0, 12.1), ocp_kelvin_n (37.0, 13.1), hv_ret (38.5, 15.2) (pcbnew pad coordinates). The sense pair therefore sits in the strongest field of the 32-38 nH loop; M*di/dt plus the shunt body's ESL add to the OCP signal (0.5 mV per A at OCP_NODE, so the 67.72 A trip-low is only about 34 mV; `coil-pickup/PICKUP.md:55-58`; band from `kelvin/budget-native21s19b.md`) ahead of a 5 k || 100 pF (0.5 us) filter. `validation-results/02-protection-timing/coil-pickup/PICKUP.md:142-143` states the opposite coupling is "Not covered: the shunt's own power-loop di/dt coupling into the R5 breakout". The coil-field study that exists is on stage-13b copper (PICKUP.md line 1), before the R5 swap (item 17) and the Kelvin return move to In2 (item 19, `DECISIONS.md:772`). The optional hook fix in PICKUP.md:92-97 is not recorded as done. No mutual-inductance value exists, so I do not state a glitch voltage.
- **Proposed action.** Add the Kelvin pins as a fifth FEM port (or FastHenry/Elmer mutual) on 19b; run OCP_NODE through the real filter with the CSS2H ESL for S1/S2/S4 edge rates; re-run `coil-pickup/run_loops.py` on the 19b dump; bench: capture TP5 (OCP_NODE) and TP25 in the double-pulse test (`native-21/BRINGUP-PROBES.md`).

### F3. AN139-14 (U): filter path through capacitor pads is not extracted
- **Missing evidence.** Lane-level geometry and wiring inductance of the board filter. The filter nets are pours, so I could not trace pad-through paths. Pad distances (pcbnew, computed): L1 out pins (232,56)/(242,56) to C2 pads (263.25,49.5)/(285.75,49.5) are 31.9 / 44.2 mm; to Y caps C3/C4 (225,102.8)/(225,109.1) 47.3 / 55.8 mm; L1 out to BR1.2 149.4 mm. The EMI model uses lumped 12.2 nH (X2) and 15 nH (Y, with a x3 sensitivity) with no board parasitics and calls anything above ~1-3 MHz unassessed (`validation-results/07-conducted-emi/s19b/README.md:80,127`); the board filter alone is at 1.0 dB at 26 MHz in that model.
- **Proposed action.** Extract the l_filt/n_filt loops through C2/C3/C4 from the 19b dump (FastHenry/Elmer) and add the result as series ESL in `emi_s19b.py`; measure a 2-port insertion loss of the assembled board filter (module bypassed, per BENCH step C6). Consequence is bounded because the inlet module is mandatory.

### F4. AN139-15 (U): no stray-field estimate at the on-board line choke L1
- **Missing evidence.** L1 (B82726S2203A020) sits at board (232, 31). Using the coil axis stated in `coil-pickup/PICKUP.md:17` (board (141,-25)), L1 is 106.9 mm from the axis, inside the coil's r 92-116 mm winding section (`/Users/bennet/Desktop/temper/output/temper-r33-studies/12-ps3-field/REPORT.md`: sections r26-84 and r92-116). The studies sample PS3 (453-573 uT), the mains lead (548-654 uT) and "native-21 top" (208 uT) but never L1 (`.../09-coil-210-strayfield/REPORT.md:45`, study 12 table). The 07 EMI study does not mention coupling of L1 or the module choke to the coil.
- **Proposed action.** Evaluate the study-06/09 axisymmetric field at L1 (board z), compare against the core's saturation and the winding pickup using the core axis from the datasheet orientation, and add a bench line (28 A coil current, L1 insertion loss and winding voltage). I rate it U, not 1, because magnitude and orientation are unknown; it becomes a 1 if the on-axis field is of the order of the study's 500 uT criterion.
