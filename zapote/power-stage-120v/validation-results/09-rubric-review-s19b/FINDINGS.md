# Consolidated findings: stage 19b rubric review

The 32 criteria scored 1 and the safety-relevant Us, grouped by mechanism and ranked: safety and insulation first, then switching stress, sensing, EMC, thermal, and parts/assembly. The IDs point into the per-rubric files, which hold the evidence. The "When" column says what each item blocks:
- **pre-order:** fix or confirm before the prototype fab order;
- **pre-mains:** before the first mains-powered run;
- **pre-28A:** before unlocking 28 A; the product ships at 22 A;
- **re-spin:** at the next copper revision.

## What this review did not change

- No criterion is 0. The board-of-record gates are unaffected: DRC (rerun by the practical-guide reviewer: 0 / 0 / 0), barrier, D5 (reproduced as 8.047 / 8.507 mm), DFM, fit and audit.
- Most criteria (120) sit at 2: the design rationale is documented and the bench measurement is pending. The bench plan (`BRINGUP-PROBES.md`, `s19b/BENCH-PRECOMPLIANCE.md`) is where they reach 3.
- The 21 U items are listed in each rubric file. Most need hardware: near-field scans, LISN, ESD and immunity, cable CM current, or controller-side data (J4 input injection, ADC sampling).
