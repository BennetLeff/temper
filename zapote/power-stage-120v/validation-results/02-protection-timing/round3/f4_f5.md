# F4 controller loss and F5 HOT5 slow fall

Evidence class: datasheet-backed bounded logic where all listed supplies remain
in their specified ranges; unresolved intervals are **indeterminate**. Board
identity and source hashes are in `README.md` and `outputs/thresholds.json`.
The actual series path is U6/U7 → U8 → U9 → U13 → J4.10 BUS_FAULT, then
interlock → PERMIT → Q1/Q4 → U1/U2 DIS. U10/U11 also feed U13. These
connections were checked against `frozen/default.net`, while part identities
come from `frozen/default.csv`.

**F4 verdict:** Controller death is eventually detected by the interlock's
watchdog under its supply and WDI assumptions, but 0.9–2.5 s is not an
immediate stuck-one-PWM protection bound. Driver DT interlock covers the
both-inputs-high command state. A single input stuck high and physical driver
fault need separate whole-chain analysis/bench tests.

**F5 verdict:** **BUS_FAULT is not guaranteed asserted at every point in a
slow HOT5 fall.** A sufficient REF25 operating condition ends near 4.52 V;
the isolator's explicit powered-down default-high state starts at ≤1.7 V
while SELV remains powered. Between them, neither comparator/reference
validity nor a high BUS_FAULT is established. A HOT5 supervisor in the
hardware shutdown path, or an independently qualified SENSOR_LIVE/AUX
producer with the required threshold and delay, is needed to close F5.
The driver's V15_LS UVLO cannot do so when only HOT5 falls.
