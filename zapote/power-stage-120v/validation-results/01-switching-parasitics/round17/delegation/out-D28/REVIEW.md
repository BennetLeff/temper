# D-28 review and verification record

This is a sequential main-thread review, not an independent peer-review claim. The user’s scope is limited to this output folder, so no root code, firmware, board or netlist is changed.

- **Reference-plane review:** the phase definition was checked against the actual PULSE expressions. The complementary-startup B source has a first-event delay of `(phase/360 + 0.5)/f` below 180°; after settling its high-side rising command occurs at the requested phase modulo a period. The 180° case retains the original zero SHIFT. ZVS uses the original D2/D15 ideal-command sample, not D-26’s loaded-driver threshold sample. [METHOD.md](METHOD.md), [runner](run.py), [D-15 definition](../out-D15/analyze.py#L195).
- **Measurement-window review:** an early narrow off-gate window could miss a disturbance from the other leg. The final Rust analyzer screens the entire commanded-off interval and both devices’ full-cycle VDS; the earlier commutation-only peaks remain explicitly named. A focused regression demonstrates the missed later disturbance. [physics.rs](physics.rs), [rust-tests.txt](rust-tests.txt).
- **Polarity review:** a low incoming VDS alone cannot establish inductive commutation. The final ZVS flag also requires the correct current sign. The six standalone tests include wrong-sign/zero-current rejection, incomplete screen rejection, phase symmetry and the full-off-window regression. No workspace or native extension was built. [rust-tests.txt](rust-tests.txt).
- **External-oracle review:** self-consistency is not the current-map oracle. Six independent ideal-source ngspice RLC runs reproduce transition currents within 1.703 µA, below the declared 50 µA limit. The first oracle’s incomplete start-boundary measurement is retained separately and is not counted as a pass. [oracle-check.txt](oracle-check.txt), [ideal-oracle-v2.json](ideal-oracle-v2.json), [METHOD.md](METHOD.md).
- **Source reproduction:** the matching D-22 anchor has exactly equal complex FFT arrays and cycle RMS metrics. This is distinct from qualifying the new native-19 phase sweep. [anchor-reproduction.json](anchor-reproduction.json).
- **Fail-closed reporting:** all 56 significant/weak receiver-terminal checks in both cycle and step comparisons are required, along with completed/settled captures and all four transition screens. Missing, interrupted, aborted, unsettled and step-sensitive results remain indeterminate. No row inherits a pass from a different phase, timestep, matrix or load. [qualify.py](qualify.py), [physics.rs](physics.rs), [CONVERGENCE.md](CONVERGENCE.md), [verdicts.csv](verdicts.csv).
- **Evidence identity and scope:** `verify.py` checks physical-input and raw-capture identities, final replay identity, exact FFT replay, campaign coverage and terminal status. Interrupted attempts are distinguished from captures carrying an in-run checksum. Repository import/derived-artifact checks are report-only; writes remain in this folder. [verification.json](verification.json), [repo-gates.json](repo-gates.json).
- **Limits retained:** leg B uses leg A’s matrix; the device temperature is 27 °C; the driver remains the approved-for-model-use output-resistance approximation; command dead time is one nominal 443 ns value. The 1.9 V hot screen is not hot-device qualification, and overlap energy is not measured heat. CT delay reserves and a candidate boundary are proposals, not permission to heat. [METHOD.md](METHOD.md), [INHIBIT.md](INHIBIT.md).
