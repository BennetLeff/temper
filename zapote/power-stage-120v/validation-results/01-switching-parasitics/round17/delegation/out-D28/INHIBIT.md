# Proposed CT phase inhibit — not an operating release

The existing policy applies updates at timer zero and requires inductive commutation, rejecting a current zero that precedes the switch-node transition. Burst entry/exit requires both legs off. D-27 retains only the 180° operating point pending D-28; this packet changes no policy or firmware. Sources: [`DECISIONS.md:68`](../../../../../DECISIONS.md#L68), [`DECISIONS.md:76`](../../../../../DECISIONS.md#L76).

For **50 kHz only**, propose this necessary timing screen for bench validation:

1. Label CT edges by current polarity. For A high-side turn-on the outgoing current must be negative (A→B convention); for B high-side turn-on it must be positive. Reverse those signs for low-side turn-on. Reject missing, stale, ambiguous or wrong-polarity evidence. This follows the bridge/tank connection in [D-19's circuit](../out-D19/periodic-sweep.cir) and the explicit sign convention in [METHOD.md](METHOD.md#current-and-phase-convention).
2. Correct the CT capture for the measured sensor/comparator/filter/capture delay. Relative to each **outgoing PWM off command**, require the next appropriate current zero to lie between **1.000 and 9.000 µs**, inclusive. With total timing uncertainty ε, require `measured_corrected_delay − ε ≥ 1.000 µs` and `measured_corrected_delay + ε ≤ 9.000 µs`. At the prototype's 80 MHz timer those endpoints are 80 and 720 ticks; do not round uncertainty toward acceptance. The timer source is [`mcpwm_target.c:88`](../../../../../prototype-closure/round5/firmware/esp32/mcpwm_target.c#L88).
3. Apply the check to all four proposed transitions, including their changed phase after a timer-zero update. A prior cycle's CT interval cannot automatically approve a phase jump, frequency change, startup or burst edge. Until transient prediction and CT delay bounds are validated, keep those operations inhibited. CT timing is necessary but **not sufficient**: also require a numerically qualified bus/load/phase/dead-time envelope proving that both legs discharge the incoming capacitance in time. No such continuous phase range is released here; see [README.md](README.md) and [verdicts.csv](verdicts.csv).

The 1 µs lower endpoint and 1 µs reserve before the next half-period are **proposed engineering allocations**, not measured limits or vendor guarantees. At 50 kHz the half-period is 10 µs. Relative to a putative latest 498 ns command-gap endpoint, the lower endpoint leaves 502 ns; relative to the nominal simulated 443 ns gap, it leaves 557 ns. The command-gap interval is from [D-15](../out-D15/README.md), not a calibrated switch-node transition delay. D-26 explicitly distinguishes gate-crossing and command-gap reference planes. Therefore the 502 ns arithmetic does not establish physical timing margin until the PWM-command → switch-node and CT delays are measured together. See [`DECISIONS.md:74`](../../../../../DECISIONS.md#L74).

The recorded examples explain why a second, charge-based condition is required. At 170 V / 2 Ω / 120°, the 0.5 ns capture has positive current and CT delays about 3.25/6.58 µs, yet leg A retains about 10.4 V at incoming command (above the 8.5 V ZVS screen). At 125° the provisional currents are about 10.68/10.93 A and CT delays 3.38/6.43 µs, with both voltage screens met. At 198 V / 2 Ω, 100° is still insufficient for A while 110° clears its nominal screen. These are **unqualified waveform examples**, not calibrated current thresholds. Sources: the corresponding rows in [TABLES.md](TABLES.md), with links to every final raw-waveform replay.

Bench acceptance must bound CT polarity, delay, hysteresis, jitter and switching-node pickup; validate both legs and both transition directions; and check intended phase ramps, startup and burst entry/exit. The present simulation fixes temperature at 27 °C, dead time at 443 ns, the output-resistance approximation, one tank and the same leg-A parasitic matrix for both legs. It cannot validate the complete controller inhibit. Those limitations follow directly from the [frozen method](METHOD.md) and the [D-28 brief](../D28-phase-shift-validation.md).
