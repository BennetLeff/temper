# D-15: losses and ZVS versus dead time

**At 37 A, moving from 348 to 443 ns increases the simulated overlap subtotal by 0.092–0.110 W per switch at an assumed 36 kHz; the sampled ZVS map is unchanged, with 2/5/10 A still failing and 20/30/37 A passing. These are finite-window proxies, not an efficiency measurement.**

Across the two buses and ESL corners, the separate full-load dead-time diode estimate increases by about 0.111 W per switch; it is already partly represented in the overlap subtotal and must not be added again. At 2/5 A the overlap proxy instead decreases by 1.16–2.97 W per switch, while remaining non-ZVS. Thus F7 has a load-dependent cost, not a universal loss penalty. At 498 ns, 10 A also passes the sampled ZVS screen; 2/5 A remain hard. The brief’s “cost of light-load ZVS” describes an unresolved light-load condition here, not newly lost ZVS relative to 348 ns.

This is a **27 °C simulation comparison on the best leg-A FEM matrix**, with the original D2 circuit and solver settings. It does not qualify physical switching loss or recommend a resistor change. [TABLES.md](TABLES.md) contains the ZVS map and 348/443 ns comparison; [points.csv](points.csv) has every direction-specific measurement; [cycles.csv](cycles.csv) includes all five timings and individual high-/low-side proxy powers.

## Method and definitions

The sweep uses `legA-h0-best.matrix.txt` through the unchanged `run_d2.matrix_params` mapping and copies `d2/leg_matrix.cir`, adding only probes and measurements. It covers 170/198 V, 1.06/10 nH capacitor ESL, both directions, 307/348/391/443/498 ns, and 2/5/10/20/30/37 A. The 37 A full-power peak and nominal 33–39 kHz operating band come from [POWER-SECTION.md §2](../../../../../../../docs/hardware/power-section-120v/POWER-SECTION.md). The sampled light-load magnitudes extend D2's 2/5/10 A cases with intermediate 20/30 A points; they are not claimed as an exhaustive current continuum.

Direction 0 is LS off / HS on with +IL; direction 1 is HS off / LS on with −IL. `il` is the positive magnitude. Both devices remain the installed IPW65R018CFD7 L1 vendor model. The model is held at simulator default 27 °C, not temperature-adjusted by this task. The existing gate resistors, 0.55/5 Ω driver approximation, 5 ns command ramps, 1 nF snubbers, 2 nH shunt, bulk path, and FEM couplings remain unchanged.

- **ZVS:** signed incoming die VDS at `T1+DT` ≤ 5% of Vbus, exactly the D2 `grid.py` rule. This is an instant at the start of the incoming command ramp, not actual channel onset. Unlike round-4 C1, it is not a 20 ns absolute-VDS test. Negative diode-clamped VDS passes; a large negative excursion would also pass this signed diagnostic, so the die peak and raw VDS remain reported separately.
- **Eoff / Eon / EΣ:** the D-6 positive die-terminal overlap proxy, `∫max(0,VDS_die·ID_die-terminal)dt`, with `ID=(V(ldrd)−V(dd))/74.4 µΩ` from the pinned vendor drain resistor. Eoff is the outgoing device from T1=2 µs to T1+DT. Eon is the incoming device from T1+DT through T1+DT+0.75 µs. EΣ sums **both devices across both windows**, including the two cross-terms omitted from Eoff+Eon. These integrals include capacitive energy, diode energy and finite conduction tails; clipping negative power also prevents interpretation as a net circuit energy balance.
- **Diode dwell:** incoming branch forward current `max(0,−i(V_sense2))`; reported dwell integrates the indicator `−i(V_sense2)>0.1 A` during `[T1,T1+DT]`. Charge retains all positive current, including the sub-0.1 A tail. The current comes from the vendor **body-diode branch**, not the external drain probe, which also includes Coss/snubber displacement.
- **Diode conduction estimate:** integrate `max(0,−VDS_die)·max(0,−i(V_sense2))` over that dead-time interval. This uses the model's instantaneous forward voltage and current at 27 °C, not a fixed assumed Vf. Raw outputs retain both devices and pre/post windows. It is reported **separately and never added to EΣ**, which already contains diode-related terminal energy. Postcommand diode conduction is retained as a diagnostic but is outside the requested dead-time subtotal.

## Representative-cycle normalization

The primary comparison is a hypothetical periodic sequence of one D0 and one D1 event per switching cycle at each fixed current magnitude. Each event reuses the deck's independently initialized current-source transition. At an explicitly assumed **36 kHz**, the mean per-switch subtotal is `f·(EΣ_D0+EΣ_D1)/2`; the division is by the two devices in the modeled leg. `cycles.csv` also reports each device individually by summing that device's pre/post energies across both events. This is a switching-window proxy power, not the entire MOSFET power budget. Other frequencies scale this normalization linearly **only if these event waveforms are assumed unchanged**.

For an optional illustrative operating profile, [illustrative-profile.csv](illustrative-profile.csv) assigns **equal switching-cycle count (1/6 each)** to 2/5/10/20/30/37 A, with equal D0/D1 event counts within each bin. It is a declared six-period synthetic sequence, not a measured mains-cycle or controller histogram. No bus or ESL corners are averaged together, no interpolation across a ZVS boundary occurs, and missing cases make the corresponding subtotal indeterminate instead of shrinking its denominator. The fixed-load table is the appropriate comparison when the operating distribution is unknown.

## Numerical verification and provenance

The main sweep completed **240/240 cases with no aborts**. All **32 overlapping 348 ns decision cases** reproduce the existing `grid-best` ZVS classification. Maximum absolute replay differences are **0.0001 V** for peak/incoming VDS and **0.000002 V** for off-gate voltage ([baseline-check.json](baseline-check.json)). The sampled ZVS counts are 16/48, 24/48, 24/48, 24/48 and 32/48 at 307/348/391/443/498 ns respectively; these fractions describe this grid, not an operating distribution.

Focused checks use **0.2 → 0.1 → 0.05 ns** at 198 V, 2/37 A, both directions, both ESLs, 348/443 ns; an additional 170 V, 10 A, both-direction/both-ESL set probes the 443 ns sampled ZVS boundary. Of **40 original-options refinements**, **15 abort during startup** (bus node, before the switching event). Those exact failed records remain preserved in [timestep-checks.json](timestep-checks.json) and the scalar archive; they are not classified as low loss or silently overwritten.

A separate, bounded solver-budget qualification raises only `itl4` from 200 to 1000, leaving all circuit elements, tolerances, integration method and timesteps unchanged. Its **30 cases** cover all three tiers at each affected point. All **10 coarsest-step comparisons reproduce the original energy exactly at recorded precision**, and all affected 0.1 ns points complete. Six 0.05 ns points still fail at 1000; one separately identified `itl4=10000` probe for each completes. [recovery-audit.json](recovery-audit.json) retains old/new fidelity, same-budget refinement and mixed-budget comparisons separately. This is qualification of these sampled cases, not proof that increasing `itl4` cures every D2 abort. The recovery audit requires the complete three-tier cohort for every originally failed point; any absent or failed replacement probe remains indeterminate. [check_recovery_audit.py](check_recovery_audit.py) exercises missing, failed, duplicate and unexpected evidence against the recorded cases.

Across successful original and recovery comparisons, maximum energy change is **0.1895%**, peak-VDS change **0.262%**, with **no ZVS flips**. These meet the declared focused checks of <2% energy/peak change and unchanged ZVS classification; this is numerical evidence on selected points, not a bound over the untested continuum. [full-load-convergence.csv](full-load-convergence.csv) shows the actual paired cost, including the iteration ceiling used for each constituent waveform: at 198 V/1.06 nH it changes **0.110291 → 0.111039 → 0.111231 W/switch**, and at 198 V/10 nH **0.0923375 → 0.0923407 → 0.0923510 W/switch** as the timestep is halved. The coarsest headline is therefore stable to about a milliwatt on these checked full-load corners. The 170 V full-load corners were not independently refined.

All **25 successful original refinement waveforms** are independently trapezoid-integrated from the saved raw data, with explicit window-boundary interpolation. ## Scope and limits

The positive-current favorable commutations modeled here do not cover adverse-current hard recovery, current reversal during floating dead time, a resonant tank trajectory, below-170 V mains portions, bursts, startup, or faults. The second physical leg is not separately extracted or simulated. The best leg-A matrix retains its documented extrapolation/crop-correction assumptions; applying it makes this a board-informed leg-A result, not validation of both legs or every layout variant.

The longer interval does not establish total efficiency: normal conduction outside the finite windows, driver/bias losses, device temperature, real recovery history, component spread, actual event weights, and installed cooling remain unquantified. No Eoss, snubber-energy or Vbus·Qrr correction is added on top of EΣ. The resulting subtotal must not be added uncritically to a separate conduction budget because its windows already contain some conduction. D-13 addresses hot transients, and D-14 addresses solver robustness independently. No tolerance relaxation, changed physics, circuit/PCB edit, firmware timing change or resistor change was made here. The primary sweep retains the original solver budget; the separately recorded iteration-budget qualification is described above.
