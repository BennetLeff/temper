# Future controller requirements — D-20

The controller shall implement the native-18 full-bridge interface and the accepted D-11 bus receiver, while leaving the controller-inserted dead time and unqualified protection timing as explicit owner decisions.

Document baseline: `b30b3ae35a4bd3ad5c22d8c300c4640986e1232a`, 2026-10-02. Source decision entries retain their recorded 2026-10-03 labels. This is a requirements proposal, not implementation or powered-operation approval. “Shall” denotes an acceptance condition for the future controller; it does not assert the present system passes. **Accepted** means recorded owner decision; **derived** means a source finding translated into a test; **proposed target** needs approval/qualification. Open numerical values are release gates, never implicit defaults.

## Connector schedule

Direction is relative to the power board. Default codes: **P** = power-source absent means rail absent; backfeed must be prevented; **U** = no valid signal when its supplying rail is absent, input injection/partial-power tests required; **L** = local pull-down/UVLO gives steady low/disabled with entire J4 unplugged and no alternate feed, not a transient guarantee; **G** = continuous SELV return required, broken return unqualified. Controller reset with rails present leaves interlock-owned PERMIT potentially latched, so L does not prove reset shutdown. Power-stage HOT supplies can be absent while SELV remains alive; U applies to measurement validity in that state. R01–R16 below incorporate this schedule, including defaults.

- **R15.** The controller/harness shall connect J4.15 as an allocated SELV return and satisfy the corresponding schedule level, load and default-state contract. **Source:** D10:56,245. **Verify:** inspection + bench. **Status:** derived.

- **R17.** The harness shall mate with Molex 0430451612 using a selected, rated housing/contact/wire assembly and a numbered end-to-end continuity drawing verified from mating-face and wire-entry views; 43025-1600 remains a candidate until selected (O01). **Source:** D10:80. **Verify:** inspection + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R18.** The supply design shall identify the sole 15 V source and 3.3 V converter and close total continuous, dynamic, startup, inrush, cable and derated thermal budgets; the 27.445 mA DC subtotal shall not be treated as the complete 3.3 V requirement (O02). **Source:** D10:90–110. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R19.** The assembled interface shall inhibit all four gates through power-up, MCU reset, brownout, controller/interlock power loss and entire-J4 disconnect, including partial-power injection, alternate feeds and hot-unplug tests; static pull-downs alone shall not count as proof (O03). **Source:** D10:149–160; D11:70. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R20.** The grounding design shall allocate J4.2/.4/.15/.16 current and nearby PWM/analog returns, qualify a missing contact and ground offset, and inventory every PE/USB/instrument bond against R38’s functional SELV_GND-to-PE link (O04). **Source:** D10:245–256; POWER:58. **Verify:** analysis + inspection + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R21.** The controller shall provide two complementary PWM pairs with independently controlled full-bridge phase and a documented four-pin MCU/timer allocation; GPIO4/5 alone shall not satisfy this requirement (O05). **Source:** D5:24,40–47; POWER:98–102. **Verify:** inspection + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R22.** The PWM operating policy shall cover the nominal approximately 33–39 kHz full-power pan range and the 60 kHz low-power operating point, with final allowed frequency/duty/phase limits and burst sequencing explicitly approved before enabling heating (O06). **Source:** POWER:34–44,98–102. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R23.** The controller shall never command overlapping high inputs within a leg, including reconfiguration/start/stop transients, and shall implement a capacitive-operation phase inhibit before powered qualification (O06). **Source:** TI-DT pp25–26; POWER:28. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** derived; phase policy value open.
- **R24.** The timing specification shall treat UCC21550 output dead time as the longer of DT-pin action and the propagated incoming gap, not their sum; native-18’s estimated 396.6–488.0 ns and 443 ns simulation point shall not be promoted to guaranteed gate limits. **Source:** DEC:57; D5:66–79; TI-DT pp25–26. **Verify:** analysis. **Status:** accepted board decision; derived timing rule.
- **R25.** The controller-inserted gap shall be an explicit owner-selected configuration with declared tolerance/quantization; no 0 ns, 443 ns or 500 ns controller default or minimum is selected by this document, and absence of the approved configuration shall inhibit PWM (O07). **Source:** D5:15–23,92–98; DEC:57. **Verify:** inspection + bench. **Status:** derived release gate; value open.
- **R26.** The HAL shall use a valid ESP-IDF complementary dead-time topology that assigns each available edge-delay block consistently and shall propagate every initialization/update failure without reporting a requested delay as successfully applied. **Source:** D5:26–34. **Verify:** inspection + host test + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R27.** A failed or partial PWM timing update shall leave outputs inhibited and state explicitly invalid until a complete successful reconfiguration; requested timing, applied ticks and verification status shall be distinguishable. **Source:** D5:20–21,30–34. **Verify:** host test + bench. **Status:** engineering implementation requirement derived from D5.
- **R28.** The timing guard shall reject absent/uninitialized/invalid configuration and shall not treat cached requested nanoseconds or host-test constants as hardware readback; SDK revision, clock resolution and physical verification status shall be recorded. **Source:** D5:15–23,28,92–98. **Verify:** inspection + host test + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R29.** Bring-up shall measure both transitions on both legs at INA/INB thresholds, OUT 90%-to-10% and actual MOSFET VGS crossings separately, including harness/threshold skew, load, supplies, temperature and clock tolerance; off/fault states with no next pulse shall not be assigned a finite maximum dead time (O08). **Source:** D5:51–99; TI-DT pp10,33; DEC:57. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R30.** Protection shall use active-high aggregate BUS_FAULT to latch inhibition independently of the bus ADC value; a modest bus reading shall never suppress OCP/CT/OVP faults or identify which source tripped. **Source:** D10:135; D11:118,147. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R31.** The interlock input selection shall close U13’s low-level requirement at the actual pullup/leakage/load and the high/open-wire requirement, without interpolating typical curves as guarantees (O09). **Source:** D10:135–145. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R32.** PERMIT shall stay low until watchdog, reset, SENSOR_LIVE, rails and fault/latch health are established; the controller shall supply the separate interlock branch signals and shall not bypass the latch by directly driving J4.9 (O10). **Source:** D10:66,122,149–160,260. **Verify:** inspection + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R33.** The complete fault-to-gate-off budget shall include comparator overdrive/CT threshold, U13, harness, interlock latch, PERMIT fanout, DIS network and loaded gate discharge, plus lost-supply and HOT5-brownout behavior; the maximum and acceptance method await D-17/bench owner approval (O11). **Source:** D10:234–243; D5:98. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** open numerical budget.
- **R34.** The PERMIT producer shall demonstrate adequate voltage and dynamic fanout into both AO3400A gates under resistor/rail corners; threshold voltage shall not substitute for the needed on-resistance condition (O09). **Source:** D10:122–133. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R35.** Bus measurement shall be used for line-synchronous burst timing and telemetry with accepted targets ±6% at 170/198 V, ±5 V at zero and ±250 µs zero-cross timing; the RTD/under-glass temperature loop and independent U7 OVP shall retain their roles. **Source:** DEC:59; D11:9–24. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** accepted.
- **R36.** The controller shall preserve the power-board shared divider R26–R30 and U7 reference network, accept loss of specified U4 linearity near VIN=2 V, and never claim accurate measurement through 280 V or an inclusive guaranteed 240 V endpoint. **Source:** D11:24,98–109,138–147; DEC:59. **Verify:** inspection + bench. **Status:** accepted range policy.
- **R37.** The receiving controller connector shall carry the D-11 OPA2388 differential receiver with nominal VADC=0.25+0.5×(VBUS_P−VBUS_N), 20.0 kΩ input/10.0 kΩ feedback ratios, buffered 0.25 V local reference and 100 Ω/100 nF ADC output filter; receiver resistor/reference/filter details shall follow D-11’s circuit and qualification list (O12). **Source:** D11:28–70; DEC:59. **Verify:** inspection + analysis + bench. **Status:** accepted topology; physical qualification open.
- **R38.** The bus pair shall remain differential through the harness and present at least 10 kΩ conservative receiver loading per D-11, with proposed acceptance capacitance ≤400 pF conductor-to-ground and ≤200 pF differential including parasitics; U4 common mode 1.39–1.49 V and datasheet 500/250 pF maxima shall be respected. **Source:** D11:64; TI AMC1311 SBAS786C pp6,10. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** derived plus D11 engineering harness target.
- **R39.** The controller revision shall disconnect the existing V_BUS_SENSE source from GPIO2/ADC1_CH1 and reserve that unshared ADC1 input for the receiver; ADC2 shall not substitute. **Source:** DEC:59; D10:71. **Verify:** inspection. **Status:** accepted.
- **R40.** Bus acquisition shall use 12-bit ADC1 at 6 dB attenuation and successful curve-fitting calibration, reject the current HAL’s 3100 mV fallback and propagate conversion/calibration failures. **Source:** D11:74–78; ESP-ADC. **Verify:** inspection + host test + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R41.** The bus channel shall acquire time-stamped samples at 20 ksample/s on a dedicated path, preserving unaveraged validated acquisitions for faults independently of telemetry averaging. **Source:** DEC:59; D11:117,120. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** accepted sample rate; derived processing separation.
- **R42.** A calibrated acquisition ≥1.210 V shall stop PWM and latch BUS_MEAS_OVERRANGE without producing a precise bus-voltage estimate; automatic recovery after falling below the threshold shall be prohibited. **Source:** DEC:59; D11:120–124. **Verify:** host test + bench. **Status:** accepted.
- **R43.** Bus validity shall reject missing rails, calibration/conversion failure, stale data and fail-safe-low readings; the proposed 150 µs stale deadline and ADC <100 mV invalid threshold require owner confirmation and dynamic qualification (O13). **Source:** D11:117–121. **Verify:** host test + bench. **Status:** D11 proposed targets.
- **R44.** Re-arm shall require explicit operator action after readings return to the qualified range, cleared hardware faults, healthy rails/calibration and validated line cycles; the proposed 1.100 V re-arm level and line-cycle validation criteria remain open and one zero-crossing sample shall never suffice (O13). **Source:** DEC:59; D11:122. **Verify:** host test + bench. **Status:** accepted manual action; detailed criteria proposed.
- **R45.** ADC/receiver acceptance shall verify the magnitude/timing targets across the declared environment, Wi-Fi activity, actual loaded bus waveform and dynamic settling; ESP32’s ±10 mV calibrated error at its stated 25°C/DC/Wi-Fi-off conditions shall not be asserted as an environmental bound, nor INL added twice to total error (O12). **Source:** D11:22,74–109; ESP-ADC. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R46.** The bus channel shall qualify open/short pair, partial power, ESD and fail-safe recovery and retain a separate invalid/over-range state; plausible zero from a wiring fault shall not count as complete diagnostic coverage (O12). **Source:** D11:70,113–124. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R47.** Bus/OVP consistency tests shall allow over-range with BUS_FAULT low around 260 V and either fault state near nominal 280 V, without extrapolating U4 codes or substituting the conditional 271.902–288.209 V U7 planning interval for a full protection bound. **Source:** D11:128–147. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R48.** U7 trip characterization shall probe OVP_OK_HOT directly during a controlled ramp while observing U4 and aggregate BUS_FAULT, because J4.10 cannot isolate U7 from OCP/CT. **Source:** D11:147. **Verify:** bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R49.** The CT interface shall retain the local 1.5 Ω burden, 100 nF filter and BAS116H clamps, and shall not export or add a burden across T1’s secondary through J4. **Source:** D10:164–170. **Verify:** inspection. **Status:** derived.
- **R50.** The CT_MON receiver shall accommodate the conditional 0.490–2.810 V ±71 A planning range plus separately qualified transient headroom without loading R47/CT transfer; ≥10 MΩ input resistance is a proposed engineering target, with acquisition-current/filter limits to be selected (O14). **Source:** D10:168–178,275–280. **Verify:** analysis + bench. **Status:** derived range; new explicit engineering target.
- **R51.** The controller shall allocate a CT_MON ADC/receiver and a CT_ZC capture input, disconnecting any existing GPIO1 current-source driver if that candidate channel is reused; no existing source output shall be wired against J4.14 (O14). **Source:** D10:54–55,68,71,186. **Verify:** inspection. **Status:** derived.
- **R52.** CT_ZC processing shall tolerate either idle level and chatter and ignore transitions until switching and sensor validity are established, with an approved phase/zero-cross qualification policy (O06/O14). **Source:** D10:180–188; POWER:28. **Verify:** host test + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R53.** The harness shall provide the separate thermal faults, RTD SPI/DRDY/HW_FAULT, interlock watchdog/reset/live/latch monitoring and any approved shared-reference branches; equal supply/net labels shall not justify joining push-pull outputs or HOT and SELV 15 V supplies (O01/O10). **Source:** D10:64–76. **Verify:** inspection + bench. **Status:** derived.
- **R54.** Release evidence shall identify the actual controller board, harness, firmware binary/SDK revision and approved settings, demonstrate all requirements or retain an explicit blocker, and preserve D-8’s unresolved S4 recovery and D-16’s native-18 copper-identity qualification gates. **Source:** D5:94–99; DEC:57,61; D11:153. **Verify:** inspection. **Status:** derived release traceability.
