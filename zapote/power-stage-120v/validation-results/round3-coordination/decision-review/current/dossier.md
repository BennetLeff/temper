# Proposal A: controller tank-current envelope

Source revision: `44417ae1489fd00e2d652fd3b2c1582b76d17630`, integration checkout. This is a read-only analysis of the 135 ideal frequency-control rows in `05-resonant-tank-envelope/round3/outputs/cases.csv`. `screen.json` retains exact counts. A row passes only when its **maximum modeled tank-current peak** (`i_pk_a`) is at or below the threshold; no waveform has been clipped and no new operating point has been solved.

The three 1710 W rows at 42 A are cast-iron/high L/R, at 108/120/140 V: 41.56793/41.27881/41.10694 A peak. At 40 A, all 15 of the 300 W rows pass at each line voltage, while 7/15 of the 855 W rows pass at each. At 42 A, 855 W retained counts are 9/15, 8/15, 8/15 at 108/120/140 V. These are discrete sampled pan/line/power combinations. Rejected requests need fresh lower-power frequency-control solutions, with phase, ZVS, hot capacitor and thermal checks.

The task-02 **static** CT minimum is 50.558351 A. If “20% margin” means trip ≥ 1.2×maximum actual operating current, the ceiling is 42.132 A and 42 A leaves 8.558 A between actual-current ceiling and static trip. If it means actual current ≤ 80% of trip, the ceiling is 40.447 A; this retains the same 66 rows as 40 A. Neither equation budgets CT ratio/transfer and temperature errors, controller measurement error, sampling/control delay, load-step overshoot, or current while the gates turn off. A 42 A controller *command* is not justified; any command must be below the selected **actual waveform ceiling** by a measured/bounded combined error and response allowance. A 37 A command under a 40.447 A true ceiling has only 3.447 A for that allowance, also not yet qualified.

At 40 A screen, R5's maximum modeled line RMS is 18.6979 A, giving 0.3496 W at nominal 1 mΩ. At 42 A, 21.6289 A gives 0.4678 W nominal. The maxima of C23 RMS are 3.4626 and 4.0054 A. The exact CDE 942C ratings at 30.74–57.65 kHz and hot installation remain unavailable, so these smaller currents are **not** capacitor acceptance. Individual maxima need not co-occur. This screen removes the unconstrained 2.04 W shunt stress point from the retained set but does not establish a thermally valid derating law.

The existing `firmware/config.yaml` 40 A constant is a **software fault latch**, not a 40 A power regulator. It was justified for an older IGBT design. `firmware/main/state_machine.c` compares `read_dc_bus_current()` to that threshold and faults; `firmware/components/control/pll_control.c` adjusts frequency from phase error without a tank-peak input. `firmware/components/safety/safety.c` uses a simulated current reader for non-ESP builds and an external declaration for ESP builds. `zapote/current-sense/INTERFACES.md` explicitly says direct MCU ADC loading and host shutdown require qualification. Define/measure a real tank-peak sensing/control path and reconcile its threshold with the new board before treating any command as implemented.

Primary manufacturer grounding: Infineon's [Smart Induction Cooktop Reference Design](https://www.infineon.com/assets/row/public/documents/60/44/infineon-ug-2024-05-smart-induction-cooktop-usermanual-en.pdf) describes a resonant-coil current sensor with a hardware overcurrent output able to disable the DC supply without software latency. TI's [cycle-by-cycle current-limit example](https://www.ti.com/document-viewer/lit/html/SSZTBO4/GUID-C1D449D9-BB78-4D42-BD53-9D2151523CC0) routes comparator output into a PWM timer fault input; its measured ~356 ns response is **only that TI example**, not a delay claim for Temper. These support separation of normal current regulation from independently verified fast hardware protection.

Decision: set a **provisional analysis ceiling** (42 A under trip/operation ratio language, or 40.447 A for a literal 20% of-trip separation); specify a lower command after error/transient budget; re-solve all power requests beyond it, then judge shunt threshold and parts against the sustained envelope. Keep hardware CT action independent of firmware. Do not claim the envelope or 1710 W performance qualified until the actual controller measurement, derating law, current overshoot, and hot parts are tested.
