## Later four-Sol-agent decision review

### A — Current-limited operation first

Assistant recommends 42 A only as a provisional maximum ACTUAL tank peak for analysis, not an unqualified firmware command. The command must sit below the ceiling by the sensing/control error and transient allowance. CT trip is 20.4% above 42 A; a literal operating current 20% below the 50.56 A trip means 40.45 A. Static CT analysis assumes ideal transformer transfer, so ratio, frequency, temperature and saturation effects still need qualification.

Existing-grid screening by maximum `i_pk_a`, without clipping or inventing new operating points:

| Peak ceiling | Retained / 135 | 300 W | 855 W | 1710 W |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 37 A | 57 | 42 | 15 | 0 |
| 40 A | 66 | 45 | 21 | 0 |
| 42 A | 73 | 45 | 25 | 3 |

The three full-power retained rows are cast-iron/high-coupling at all three line voltages. At 42 A, retained maximum tank/R5 line RMS is 21.6289 A, nominal R5 power 0.4678 W, and each large resonant capacitor 8.8118 A RMS. This does not establish a hot capacitor rating. Rejected power requests need fresh derated solves. Existing firmware's 40 A constant is a legacy DC-bus-current fault latch, not a tank-peak regulating loop.

Machine-local temporary evidence: `/tmp/ps-r3-decision.5sy79t/current/dossier.md` and `screen.json`.

### B — CT primary tank trip; do not approve resistor-only shoot-through protection

T1 is in the tank path and cannot see same-leg shoot-through. The shunt comparator gain is 0.5 mV/A: nominal 61 mV shunt drop becomes about 30.5 mV comparator differential. ±4 mV offset alone is ±8 A.

Existing corner calculation at +85°C board and assumed +50°C R5 rise: R34/R35=10.5k/10k gives38.44–85.55 A; 10.6k/10k gives49.73–97.98 A; 10.7k/10k gives60.91–110.28 A. These are exploratory calculations, not selected values. Retuning does not fix the nominal 0.5µs input RC or uncertain small-overdrive delay. An amplifier also requires fast-response/overload-recovery proof. Fault-current, device-energy and actual gate-off evidence are needed before choosing backup architecture/values. T1's88 A limit applies to tank current, not pure same-leg shoot-through that bypasses it.

References: task02 README and `thresholds.py`; TI TLV3201 https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv3201.pdf . `ORACLE-ANSWER.md` records a historical decision to lower shunt threshold, but its old bus-capacitance/468V/511V figures are explicitly obsolete and must not become current margins.

### C — Evaluate450ns; hold the hardware change

Reference-L ZVS threshold improves from20.93–21.99 A at348ns to10.78–11.09 A at450ns. 51kΩ at R9/R17 gives about451.6ns nominal. TI characterizes50kΩ at399/443/487ns min/typ/max, not a guaranteed450ns. Driver-output timing differs from effective MOSFET dead time. Saved outgoing turn-off heat barely changes, but extra diode dwell, recovery, hot losses and hard turn-on are missing. Peak tank current is not switching-instant current.

Machine-local temporary dossier: `/tmp/ps-r3-decision.5sy79t/deadtime/dossier.md`. Primary source: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ucc21550.pdf .
