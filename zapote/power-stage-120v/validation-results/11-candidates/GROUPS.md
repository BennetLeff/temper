## How to read the interface lists

`nets_interface` lists every net with pads in more than one group and the other groups it reaches. Shared rails (`v3v3`, `selv_gnd`, `n_ls`, `v15_ls`, `hv_ret`, `leg_ret`, `bus_p`, `hot5`, `ocp_kelvin_p`) appear in many groups; they are the couplings to respect first. Copper areas: union of tracks, vias, pads and zone fills per layer (mm2) from `native21s19c-all-copper.json.gz`; 'internal' = nets whose every pad is in the group, 'interface' = whole-net area of the shared nets (so planes such as selv_gnd/hv_ret dominate it and are not the group's own).
