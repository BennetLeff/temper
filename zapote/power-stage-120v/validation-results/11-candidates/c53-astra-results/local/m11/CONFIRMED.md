## What this changes
1. **Thickness gap.** The spec uses 2.032 mm AlN tiles. Valley has nothing at that thickness in stock now (1.016 mm only); Almath's plate is 2 mm and is "not always in stock". The AlN tile is therefore a custom or special-order item, with a lead time not found.
2. **Cost.** Almath's price is up about 14 % on the doc's figure. The doc's per-stack estimate (USD 190-320) is for a 7-stack lot; **the lot is now 10-20 boards**, so 10-20 stacks would put the AlN alone at roughly USD 1,900-6,400 (estimate, from the doc's per-stack range; no quote exists). The owner may want fewer stacks than boards.
3. **Spring.** The comb spring spec (1.2 mm 301 full-hard) is not a standard gauge at Xometry; it needs a spring-shop RFQ or a spec change.
4. **G-10, screws, washers:** small quantities are the problem, not price; a small-pack source (McMaster) is still to be read.
