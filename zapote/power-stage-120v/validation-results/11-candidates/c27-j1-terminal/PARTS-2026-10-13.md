**Recommendation: rank 1, Phoenix 1714971.** It is the only candidate with all of these published together:
- use-group B, C and D values;
- a CSA group-B value (30 A, which fixes the 10 A CSA gap in #4);
- the UL file number.

It covers the 13.5 A hold, the 15 A trip and the 16.5 A envelope at 45–55 % of its rating, and F1's 25 A at 83 %. That makes #11 a question of how much margin there is, not a failed rating.

Its cost is board area: 19.05 mm against the 15.35 mm corner window (below). If the corner cannot give about 4.5 mm, use rank 2: same family, 7.62 mm pitch, about 0.5 mm short of fitting. Rank 2's catch is that LCSC holds only 7, so JLC would assemble 5 boards but not 500 without consigned DigiKey stock.

Rank 3 is the JLC-stock, low-cost route. It cannot go into a cULus submission until Degson supplies the UL (XCFR2) file number and the lab accepts it. DORABO DB910-7.62-2P-GN-S (C395876; LCSC 2,460; $0.35 @5, $0.19 @500) is an equivalent fallback with the same gap: UL B 30 A/300 V, D 10 A/300 V, 26–10 AWG, Ø1.60 hole, 21.8 × 12.6 mm, and no file number on the drawing [5b].

## FASTON (6.3 mm / .250) or soldered pigtail?

**FASTON .250 is rated by wire size, not by the tab.** TE Catalog 82004 (rev. 3-11, PDF p.8; TE-hosted, fetched 2026-10-13 [10]) gives "Test Parameters … Based on UL-310 Temperature Rise and Current Requirements". For the 250 series, at a 30 °C maximum rise:

| Wire | Continuous | Intermittent |
|---|---|---|
| 14 AWG | 15 A | 30 A |
| 12 AWG | 20 A | 40 A |
| 10 AWG | 24 A | 48 A |

The TE note says this applies to UL-*listed* terminals; a Recognized part "deviates". The catalog gives TE's UL-310 file as E66717 [10].

- **On today's 14 AWG leads:** a .250 FASTON is a 15 A connection, the same ceiling as J1's group-B rating. It does not fix #4.
- **On 12 AWG leads:** it reaches 20 A, which covers the 16.5 A envelope but not F1's 25 A. Use positive-lock receptacles so the connection cannot back off.
- **The PCB tab:** TE 63824-1 (.250 × .032 brass, tin, solder THT), at [6][11]:
  - LCSC C575074, 4,600 in stock, $0.049 @20, $0.035 @600;
  - DigiKey 518,856 in stock, $0.063 @500.
  - Use two per pole, with the tabs' insertion force taken by the PCB joint.
- **Verdict:** FASTON is the better choice only if the harness becomes 12 AWG and tool-free mating is wanted on the line. It also removes screw-torque variation in assembly. On 14 AWG it gives no current margin over J1, so the 30 A UL group-B screw terminal is the better fix.
- **Soldered pigtail:** removes the contact interface but makes the board and the inlet harness one assembly, and it is not a listed component. Appliance standards typically require a soldered conductor to be held mechanically, independently of the solder. **I did not verify the clause; ask the lab.** Not recommended for a re-spin.

## Not verified

- **Phoenix's own pages:** phoenixcontact.com returned 403 to both curl and WebFetch. The 1714971 group B/C/D and CSA values come from a 2017 Phoenix catalog extract hosted by LCSC [1]. The 1868076 values come from a 2011 extract hosted by DigiKey and LCSC [3]. Neither is checked against Phoenix's 2025 sheets or UL Product iQ (login needed).
- **1868076:**
  - Its CSA rating and UL file number are not in the copy read.
  - Its text table says "Length 19.05 mm", but the dimensioned drawing gives a+7.62 = 15.24 mm. I used the drawing.
- **Degson and DORABO:** no UL file numbers, no CSA ratings, and their XCFR2 listings are not confirmed. Both drawings carry the cURus/UL mark (Degson [5]) or a UL/cUL table (DORABO [5b]) without an E-number.
- **Mouser:** not checked; mouser.com timed out on every fetch.
- **DigiKey:** price breaks beyond qty 10 for 1714971, and beyond qty 1 for 1868076, are not shown on the page.
- **Wire-size basis:** UL 1059's 30 A rating is established with conductors sized for 30 A. Temperature rise with 14 AWG leads at 13.5–16.5 A is not given by any datasheet; it needs the end-product test (FINDINGS #4/#11).
- **Layout:** I did not check that any candidate fits the J1 corner with barrier and edge clearances. That needs a layout pass, which was out of scope (no builds).
