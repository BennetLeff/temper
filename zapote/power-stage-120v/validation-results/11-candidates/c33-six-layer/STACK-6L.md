Source: JLC's stackup API, queried 2026-10-14 for 6 layers / 1.6 mm / 2 oz outer / 2 oz inner

**Chosen: JLC061622-1080A** (`stackup-6L-proposed.json`).
- Same finished thickness as today's 4-layer build (1.658 vs 1.653 mm), so connectors, fixings and the enclosure fit
  see no change.
- Symmetric with identical 0.30 mm cores (warp on a 290 x 190 mm board).
- In2 / In3 each 0.30 mm from their own plane (In1 / In4), 0.231 mm from each other: route them orthogonally.
- Thickest outer spacing of the valid stacks (0.2215 mm): less switch-node capacitance from F / B into the planes.
- Cost of that: F-In1 loop inductance higher than on 1080 / 1080B (0.1375 / 0.1415 mm). Task 01 re-runs the commutation
  loops on the chosen stack; if they fail, 1080B is the fallback (only if JLC confirms >= 1.6 mm).
- The thinnest dielectric is 0.2215 mm. Not relied on for insulation: every through-laminate path stays >= 8.0 mm in
  plan view (DECISIONS item 18), as on four layers.

Fab rules on 6 layers (JLC capabilities page, read 2026-10-14): 2 oz multilayer track / space 0.15 / 0.15 mm (same as
4 layers; our floor is 0.2 / 0.2); inner via-to-copper 0.2 mm, inner PTH-to-copper 0.3 mm; POFV "is the default for
6-layer and above"; filled holes <= 0.5 mm. Laminate: not named in the API; the impedance calculator assumes Nan Ya
NP-155F; Shengyi S1000-2M TG170 is orderable on 6 layers and would settle the CTI question (CTI-JLC.md) by naming it.
