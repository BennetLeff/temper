Product description

THFU 1 is a stainless steel retaining spring for securing power transistors to heatsinks. The spring provides a defined spring force and is designed for several common transistor packages.

Specifications:

  • Manufacturer: Fischer Elektronik
  • Model: THFU 1
  • Type: Retaining spring for transistor
  • Material: Stainless steel (RS)
  • Spring force: 60 ±5 N
  • For transistor packages: TO-218, TO-220, TO-247, TO-248 and TO-3P
  • Width: 10 mm
  • Material thickness: 0.8 mm
  • Height: 17.3 mm
