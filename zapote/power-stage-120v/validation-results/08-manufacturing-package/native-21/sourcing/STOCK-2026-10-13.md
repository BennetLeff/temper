## Off-board items

- **Coil litz (270 x 0.10 mm, class 200) and N87/N97 ferrite bars:** no MPN and no public stock or price found. A search turned up only Elektrisola's general litz catalogue (its listed thermal classes there are 155/180 C, so class 200 availability is unconfirmed) and a retailer's out-of-stock 120 x 0.1 mm spool. The 10-12 outbox RFQ (10 prototypes, 70/80 uH) is unsent; lead time is unknown until the winders quote. Treat as the longest and least-known lead on the whole build.
- **Knob SmCo (32 per knob, 2.0 x 2.5 x 1.8 mm):** not a catalogue size. Euromag 3312B871 (6 x 4 x 2 and 20 x 10 x 2.5 mm) is the nearest public listing: 200 in stock, 1 to 3 days, EUR 1.13 at 21+, 0.74 at 81+, 0.50 at 1,001+, above that "contact seller" (page read 2026-10-13). A custom grind is needed; no premium or lead is published. Prototype cost for five knobs (160 blocks) is about EUR 118 at the 81+ tier before grinding.
- **Inlet module (out-D22):** see the long-lead table; the TDK B82726S2203A020 choke is the same part as L1 and is single-source, 0 stock. Note PARTS.md was read at `01-switching-parasitics/round17/delegation/out-D22/`; the path in the request (`07-conducted-emi`) does not exist in this worktree.
