# Inquiry 1: laminate CTI for native-21, to JLCPCB

Status: draft, **not sent**. Prepared 2026-10-08 for the owner to send.
Gate: FAB-NOTE.md (native-21) says "CTI ≥ 175 V (material group IIIa), a hard order condition. Don't place the order until JLC confirms it in writing." D5-BASIS.md requires "laminate with verified CTI ≥175 (group IIIa or better)". IIIb is restricted to working voltages ≤ 50 V (IEC 60335-1:2020 Table 17 footnote, as cited in D5-BASIS).

## Bottom line

- **Public sources don't answer the question.** They can't tie a laminate brand to a JLC041622-7628 order, so the inquiry is still needed.
- **Every FR-4 laminate JLC lists for 4-layer boards that has a findable datasheet is rated PLC 3 (CTI 175–249 V, group IIIa).** That covers four grades: NP-155F, S1141, S1000-2M and KB-6164. Three listed grades (KB-6165, S1000H, NP-140F) have no verified CTI.
- **Correction to FAB-JLCPCB.md.** Its "Laminate" row says the "Stackup data names Nan Ya NP-155F". That is not JLC's statement.
  - JLC's raw stackup API response for JLC041622-7628 names no laminate brand or grade. It gives only the prepreg styles 7628/2116 and a "0.50mm 2/2OZ without copper" core.
  - The NP-155F label was added by the third-party repo (gsuberland/jlcpcb_autogenerated_stackups) as an assumption for impedance modelling.
- **The order-page default is unspecified material.** Per that repo's README, the JLC quote page defaults 4-layer boards to "FR4-Standard TG 135-140", and "JLC do not state which material is used for TG135-140 boards". If nothing else is selected, the laminate is unspecified.
- **PLC 3 is the bottom of group IIIa (175 ≤ CTI < 250).** It meets ≥ 175 with no margin. UL 746A PLC (often measured per ASTM D3638) and an IEC 60112 CTI are not automatically interchangeable for an IEC 60664/60335 material-group claim. The certifier should accept the form of evidence; this is a separate item for the lab, not for JLC.

> Hello JLCPCB team,
>
> We are about to order a 4-layer board with these settings:
> - FR-4, 1.6 mm, outer copper 2 oz, inner copper 2 oz, stackup **JLC041622-7628**;
> - 290 × 190 mm, ENIG, epoxy-filled and capped vias.
>
> The board carries 120 V mains. Our safety design requires laminate with a **Comparative Tracking Index of at least 175 V**, which is material group IIIa (IEC 60112), or equivalently **UL PLC 3 or better (PLC ≤ 3)**. Before ordering, we need written answers to the following:
>
> 1. **Laminate.** Which laminate brand and grade (core and prepreg) is used for stackup JLC041622-7628 when ordered as (a) "FR4-Standard TG 135-140" and (b) "FR-4 TG155"? For example: Nan Ya NP-155F, Kingboard KB-6165 or Shengyi S1000H. Is it fixed, or can it vary between production lots?

> 4. **Documentation.** Can the laminate brand, grade and CTI/PLC be stated on the Certificate of Conformance (or a material certificate) shipped with the order?

## After the reply

- **If JLC confirms a named laminate with CTI ≥ 175 / PLC ≤ 3 and a way to specify it:**
  - record the reply (date, agent, ticket ID) in FAB-NOTE.md;
  - put the exact wording in the order remark;
  - ask for it on the CoC.
- **If JLC cannot guarantee it:** D5-BASIS must treat the laminate as group IIIb, which is restricted to working voltages ≤ 50 V. The board then needs either:
  - a fabricator that specifies a high-CTI laminate (PLC 0–1 / CTI ≥ 400–600 grades exist, e.g. the Kingboard brochure's "CTI 600V" lines); or
  - redesigned creepage.
- **Separately, for the certifier:** confirm that a UL PLC 3 rating (or a laminate-maker "PLC 3" TDS entry) is accepted as evidence of IEC 60664-1 group IIIa for the IEC 60335-1 / CSA creepage tables.
