# Independent pre-fab review: power-stage-120v native-21 (stage 7)

Reviewer: independent senior PCB review (read-only). Date: 2026-10-08.
Board reviewed: `review/section.kicad_pcb`, SHA-256 `ebdfdb3c…6124`. This matches the hash in FAB-NOTE.md and final-manifest.json. Note that `native-21/section.kicad_pcb` in the repository has a different hash (`087300…5550`); see N6.
Tools used: KiCad 10 pcbnew (geometry, pads, zone fills, fields), shapely (distances), DRC JSON, BOM/CPL CSV cross-check, and TI datasheets (TPS7A47, ISO7710, UCC21550, AMC1311, SN6507; downloaded to `review/datasheets/`, read with pdftotext). Scripts are in `scratchpad/rv/`.
Coordinates are KiCad board mm (x right, y down).

## Executive summary

| Severity | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| BLOCKER | 2 |
| MAJOR | 14 |
| MINOR | 13 |
| NOTE | 6 |

BLOCKERs:
- **B1:** The UCC21550 output supplies have no local decoupling. The nearest high-side VDDA–VSSA capacitor is 48–53 mm from the pin, fed through 0.2–0.25 mm tracks. The low-side VDDB caps are 14.5–16 mm away.
- **B2:** The exposed pads of the TPS7A4700 (U26/U27/U28) and SN6507 (U35) have no thermal vias and about 12 mm² of copper. U26 is allocated about 1.36 W.

MAJORs, in short:
- isolator/ADC decoupling is not local;
- the gate loops are long and the high-side return taps the switch-node pour;
- every THT pad is solidly connected to 2 oz planes, and the REDCUBE terminals need a THR process;
- fixing holes have HOT/SELV copper under the hardware, and only the screw material is specified;
- the 8 mm barrier is held with a 0.047 mm margin;
- the coil studs are at 15 mm centres against a 30 mm target;
- the TCO connector J3 has a 1.26 mm L gap;
- F1's fuse clips are missing from the BOM and are rated 15 A;
- CPL centroids are footprint origins;
- the outline and connector placement contradict D-36/L15;
- the TO-247/BR1 tab face is 1.8–2.3 mm inside the board edge, and HOT↔PE at the sink is not evaluated;
- there are no safety or identity markings on silk, and no assembly drawing is exported;
- the package cannot be ordered (zero stock);
- the PE/Y-cap/R38 routing is long and thin, and R38 is not near J6.

## 1. DFM (JLCPCB, 4 layers, 2 oz)

**Checked, no finding:**
- PTH annular ring: minimum 0.29 mm (J4), C3/C4 0.30 mm, all others ≥ 0.5 mm (≥ 0.254 required).
- Via ring: 0.15 mm on 0.6/0.3 vias (JLC via rule is ≥ 0.1 mm diameter over hole).
- Drills: 0.3–2.5 mm plated, 1.02/3.2/4.3 mm NPTH (drill-report.txt matches the board: 852 PTH, 16 NPTH).
- Hole-to-hole: minimum 0.53 mm (vias at (60.6,106.0), (33.5,128.2)).
- Minimum track 0.2 mm; minimum different-net copper gap 0.20 mm on all layers.
- Copper to edge: minimum 0.60 mm (inner selv_gnd/hv_ret planes).
- Paste on exposed pads: U26–U28 have 4 × 1.27² windows (65 % of 3.15² EP); U35 65 %.
- Pad-to-mask expansion 0; no mask slivers below 0.2 mm were found between fine-pitch pads.

### M3 — MAJOR — All THT pads are solidly connected to 2 oz planes (no thermal relief)
- **Where:** All 42 copper zones have `pad_connection` = FULL (2).
- **Evidence:** Pads measured as solidly embedded (fraction of the pad perimeter inside the same-net fill):
  - **J4 Micro-Fit** (1.02 mm pins): pins 2, 4 and 15 (selv_gnd) in the In2 plane (1.00); pin 3 (v3v3) in the In1 plane (1.00).
  - **J7/J8/J9/J10** REDCUBE: solid on F, B and In1 or In2.
  - **J1:** ac_l_in on F (0.50); ac_n_in on B (0.63).
  - PS1.3 in In2; BR1, the TO-247 legs, C5/C6, C38–C41, D3 and L1 are solid on 1–3 layers.
  - With 4 × 2 oz and 1.6 mm, hand, wave or selective soldering will not reach IPC barrel fill on J4 and the terminals without heavy preheat.
  - ASSEMBLY.md says Würth 74650074 is **THR (reflow) only, wave not applicable**. The BOM marks these six terminals "THT", and JLC THT is hand or wave.
- **Fix:**
  - Use thermal reliefs on all signal/low-current THT pads (J4, J3, PS1.3/PS1.4, PS2.3/PS2.4, C3/C4) with ≥ 4 wide spokes.
  - For power pins keep solid connections, but specify selective soldering with board preheat and barrel-fill inspection.
  - Agree a THR-in-paste or hand process for the REDCUBEs with JLC before ordering.

### M5 — MAJOR — The 8.0 mm plan-view barrier has no manufacturing-tolerance allowance
- **Evidence:** Independent shapely recomputation over all layers gives a minimum HOT↔SELV distance of **8.047 mm**:
  - via leg_a-dis at (75.5,58.8) ↔ n_ha B.Cu zone;
  - via leg_b-dis at (28.5,58.8) ↔ n_hb.
  - 284 SELV↔HOT feature pairs lie between 8.0 and 8.2 mm.
  - 2 oz etch variation and layer-to-layer registration (about ±0.075–0.1 mm) consume this margin.
  - D5-BASIS says to "check finished board tolerances"; no such allowance is in the rules (`section.kicad_dru` uses min 8.0 mm).
- **Fix:** Re-run the barrier gate at 8.0 mm plus a tolerance allowance (≥ 0.2 mm, so 8.2 mm design value) and fix the violators. The two DIS vias are trivially movable. Alternatively, get JLC's written etch/registration tolerance and document the budget.

### m4 — MINOR — Copper slivers under 0.4 mm wide in pours
- **Where:**
  - hv_ret In1 around (48.6,43.9), about 3.8 mm²;
  - res_a B.Cu at (127.2,70.6), 3.6 mm²;
  - n_ls In1 at (102.4,78.7), 2.0 mm²;
  - hv_ret In1 at (254.9,110.8).
- **Evidence:** Opening each fill by 0.2 mm removes these regions. On 2 oz they are lift/flake risks. Zone min thickness is 0.25 mm.
- **Fix:** Raise zone min width to 0.4 mm or remove the islands/necks.

### m2 — MINOR — The via-in-pad count in the order note is wrong
- **Evidence:** Excluding test pads, **50 via drills sit fully inside SMD pads and 60 partially overlap SMD pads**. Examples: U35.11 (105.1,86.6), U26.10, U32.7/.13/.14, C52.2, R5.1. FAB-NOTE says 42, FLOORPLAN says 44, DECISIONS says 43.
- **Effect:** POFV on every 0.3 mm via covers them, so the process is fine. Partial overlaps would be a paste/wicking defect if the POFV option were dropped.
- **Fix:** Correct the note and state "all 730 × 0.3 mm vias POFV, no exceptions". The KiCad setup has `filling/capping no`, so the order note is the only carrier of this requirement.

### m14 — MINOR — Fine pitch on 2 oz outer copper
- **Evidence:** U35 HVSSOP pads are 0.30 mm wide at 0.5 mm pitch, a 0.20 mm gap. U26–U28 VQFN pads are 0.35 mm at 0.65 mm. JLC 2 oz outer etch at 0.2 mm space is at the limit (FAB-NOTE: "8 mil at 2 oz"). FAB-JLCPCB.md claims 0.15/0.15, which contradicts FAB-NOTE.
- **Fix:** Confirm with JLC; specify ENIG (see m7).

### M8 — MAJOR — The F1 fuse clips are not in the BOM and are under-rated
- **Evidence:**

### m3 — MINOR — Parts close to the board edge; the L outline has no straight conveyor edge

## 3. Electrical layout

**Checked:**
- TPS7A4700 pinout against TI SBVS204G Table 4-1 is correct: SENSE pin 3 to OUT; 6P4V2/6P4V1/1P6V/0P8V/0P4V to GND, giving 17.0 V; EN tied to IN.
- ISO7710 DW pinout is correct (GND1 1/7, VCC1 3, IN 4, OUT 13, VCC2 14, GND2 9/16).
- The R5 Kelvin pair is routed as a coupled pair: median separation 0.85 mm, maximum 4.2 mm near (108–112, 72.4), 129 mm long, mostly on In1. R5.2 (kelvin_p) is at the LEG_RET end and R5.3 (kelvin_n) at the HV_RET end. See N3.

### B1 — BLOCKER — The UCC21550 output supplies (VDDA/VDDB) have no local decoupling
- **Where:** U1 (80,51), U2 (33,51).
- **Evidence:**
  - Distance from U1.16 (VDDA, p_ha) to the nearest p_ha–n_ha capacitor is **52.7 mm** (C104, the U27 LDO output cap). No p_ha–sw_a capacitor exists: R71 (4.99 kΩ bleed) is the only part across it.
  - For U2.16 (p_hb) the nearest is **48.2 mm** (C108).
  - p_ha is routed in 0.2–0.25 mm tracks (212 mm of net copper on F/B/In2).
  - Low side: U1.11/U2.11 (VDDB, v15_ls) nearest caps are C9/C16 (10 µF) at **14.5 mm** and C8/C15 (100 nF) at 16 mm.
  - The F6 reservoirs (C110–C113, C50/C51, C90/C91, C126 …) sit between **source and VSS only** (n_ha/sw_a). `elec/src/f6_bias.ato` BiasReservoir has no p–n or p–src capacitor.
  - UCC21550 SLUSE89C pin table: "VDDA — Locally decouple to VSSA using a low ESR/ESL capacitor located as close to the device as possible" (same for VDDB).
  - Every turn-on edge (about 4 A peak through 3.9 Ω) is drawn through about 50 mm of 0.2 mm trace (tens of nH). This risks VDDA dips into UVLO, slow or ringing turn-on, and a large radiating loop.
- **Fix:**
  - Source ECO: add ≥ 1 µF + 100 nF (X7R, ≥ 25 V; ideally also a 10 µF) directly across pins 16–14 and 11–9 of each driver, within 2 mm.
  - Add ≥ 10 µF p→src at the driver.
  - Route p_ha/p_hb as short wide copper.
  - Re-run the F6 rail deck with the real VDD-side impedance.

### B2 — BLOCKER — The LDO and transformer-driver exposed pads have no thermal vias
- **Where:** U26 (112.2,78.8), U27 (49.6,87.0), U28 (4.0,82.3), U35 (104.2,87.2).
- **Evidence:**
  - Zero vias inside any EP.
  - F.Cu copper connected to each EP is only **12.5 mm²** (U26/U28), 12.9 mm² (U27), 8.0 mm² (U35), with 2–3 vias within 3 mm on stubs.
  - ECO.md says "The TPS7A4700 EP needs its specified copper/thermal vias; its approximately 1.36 W LS heat allocation cannot be evaluated from θJA alone".
  - TI SBVS204G gives RθJA 32.5 °C/W on a JEDEC 2s2p board with vias and says "Thermal vias are recommended". With a bare EP on about 12 mm², θJA will be several times that.
  - Unverified estimate: at 1.36 W and 50 °C inlet, Tj approaches the 170 °C thermal shutdown. That would collapse V15_LS, which drives both low-side drivers and HOT5.
- **Fix:**
  - Put a 3 × 3 (U26–U28) or 2 × 2 (U35) array of 0.3 mm vias in each EP; these are POFV-filled and capped anyway.
  - Tie them to n_ls/n_ha/n_hb pours on In1 and B.
  - Enlarge the F.Cu ground pour around each EP.
  - Re-run the task-03 thermal estimate for U26.

### M1 — MAJOR — Isolator and modulator supply decoupling is not local
- **Evidence** (pin to nearest capacitor across the correct pair):

  | Pin | Nearest capacitor and distance |
  | --- | --- |
  | **U4 (AMC1311) VDD1, pin 1 (hot5)** | **47.3 mm** (C34). All hot5 caps are in the protection block at y 52–73; U4 is at y 116. |
  | **U9 (ISO7710) VCC1, pin 3 (hot5)** | **47.7 mm** |
  | U9 VCC2 | 21.3 mm |
  | U4 VDD2 | 12.6 mm |
  | U32 VCC1 | 11.8 mm |
  | U33 VCC1 | 18.6 mm |
  | U34 VCC1 | 19.5 mm |
  | U33 VCC2 | 12.9 mm |
  | UCC21550 VCCI, pin 3 | 8.1 mm |

  The AMC1311 datasheet (§ Power Supply Recommendations, Fig 10-1) requires 100 nF ‖ 1 µF on each side "as close as possible". The ISO7710 needs 0.1 µF at each VCC. AMC1311 accuracy and both isolators' CMTI depend on this.
- **Fix:** Add 100 nF (+ 1 µF for AMC1311) within 2 mm of each supply pin. The hot5 net reaching U4/U9 is about 65 mm of 0.25–0.4 mm track.

### M2 — MAJOR — Long gate loops; the high-side return taps the switch-node power pour
- **Evidence:**
  - Driver to gate resistor: U1.15 (76.8,46.1) → R10 (62.6,14.1) is 35 mm straight.
  - out_h track lengths: leg A 44 mm, **leg B 63 mm**. out_l: 38 + 32 mm (leg A, F + In1), 43 mm (leg B). Widths 0.5–0.6 mm.
  - The outputs run on F.Cu over In1 HV_RET, not over their own return.
  - The high-side return (VSSA ← reservoir ← sw_a) enters the driver block through the sw_a power pour "sw_a Q2.3-T1.1" (F.Cu 72.5–148.6 × 0.6–101.5). That pour also carries the 22 A tank current, so the driver reference sees I·R and L·di/dt of the pour.
  - The TO-247-3 packages have no Kelvin source.
  - DECISIONS 2026-10-08 says the FEM passes 252/252. It is unverified whether that extraction includes the 35–60 mm driver-to-gate-network runs and the shared sw pour.
- **Fix:** At minimum, run a dedicated Kelvin source return from each FET source leg to the reservoir src node, stacked under or beside the out trace on the adjacent layer. Confirm the FEM gate-loop model covers this copper. Moving U1/U2 closer is preferable.

### M14 — MAJOR — PE/Y-cap routing is long and thin; R38 is not near J6
- **Evidence:**
  - The pe net is **260 mm of 0.2/0.4/0.6 mm track** (F 195 mm, B 65 mm), from C3/C4 (215,102.8/109.1) to J6 (150.6–165.8,134.5) to R38 (24.6,132.9), with a branch into the tongue to y 163.7.
  - D5-BASIS: "one removable 0 Ω functional connection **near** the separate J6 PE branch". R38 is about 125 mm from J6.
  - The Y-cap return to J6 is about 65 mm of thin trace, which adds tens of nH (degrading the CM filter the D-22 model assumes) and carries surge current through a 0.2 mm track.
- **Fix:**
  - Place R38 at J6.
  - Give the Y-caps a short, wide (≥ 2 mm) path to J6, or a dedicated PE stud near C3/C4.
  - Remove the PE branch into the tongue unless it is needed.
  - Re-check the D-22 CM model inductance.

### m1 — MINOR — A 39.8 mm dangling LEG_RET track on In1
- **Where:** (55.6,40.0)–(95.4,40.0), 0.4 mm. DRC `track_dangling` at (55.6,40.0).
- **Evidence:** It joins vias only at x 91.6/96.5. About 36 mm is an open stub inside the LS rail band under the drivers. FAB-NOTE calls all 6 dangling items "short stubs".
- **Fix:** Delete the stub or connect it at the west end deliberately.

### m11 — MINOR — Current density in R5's in-pad vias
- **Evidence:** final-manifest gives 7.27 A in the worst 0.3 mm via (coherent 34 A case). The barrel is about 0.017 mm² at 18 µm average plating.
- **Fix:** Confirm this is a transient case. Check RMS per via against IPC-2152 at the real duty, or use larger vias (0.5/0.85 mm) where the pad allows.

## 4. Insulation / safety

**Checked:**
- HOT↔PE minimum is ≥ 8.3 mm over all copper (C3 land gap 8.507 mm, as in D5-AUDIT).
- HOT↔SELV minimum is 8.047 mm (see M5).
- Barrier parts and land gaps match D5-AUDIT: U1/U2/U9/U32–U34 8.1 mm, U4 8.85 mm, U36 8.2 mm, T1 18.5 mm, PS1 42 mm.
- Silk is the only feature crossing the barrier besides the parts; it is non-conductive.

### M4 — MAJOR — Fixing holes: HOT/SELV copper lies under the screw/washer/standoff footprint, and only the screw is specified
- **Evidence** (centre to nearest copper, all layers):

  | Hole | HOT copper | SELV / PE copper |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | H2 (42,62) | n_ha In1, 3.25 mm | selv_gnd In2, 4.96 mm |
  | H3 (20,122) | n_hb In1, 3.0 mm | v3v3 In1, 5.2 mm |
  | H4 (84,120) | U32 HOT pad, 7.0 mm | selv_gnd In2, 3.7 mm |
  | H5 (134,54) | hv_ret In1, 3.5 mm | — |
  | **H6 (218,18)** | **l_filt track on B.Cu (outer, mains), 3.75 mm** | — |
  | H7 (200,128) | — | v3v3 In1, 3.0 mm; PE track 6.4 mm |
  | **H8 (164,126)** | — | **v15_selv B.Cu track 2.2 mm (0.6 mm from the hole wall)**; J6 PE pad 7.4 mm |

  - An M3 washer or standoff covers a radius of about 3.5 mm. The B-side standoff or enclosure boss therefore sits on mask over mains copper at H6 and over SELV copper at H8.
  - BRINGUP-PROBES specifies "M3 nylon only" for the screw, but not the standoff, washer or enclosure boss. A metal boss (PE) at H2/H3/H5/H6 is within 8 mm of HOT, and at H7/H8 it creates a second SELV–PE bond (D5-BASIS).
  - The "NYLON" markings exist only on F.Fab, which is not fabricated.
  - Hole positions are not yet matched to the enclosure (D5-AUDIT open item).
  - H1 is fine: 22.0 mm from HOT, 68 mm from SELV.
- **Fix:**
  - Specify nylon screw, nylon washer **and** nylon standoff, with an insulating enclosure boss, at H2–H8.
  - Clear all copper within ≥ 4 mm radius on every layer (outer layers at least) around each fixing; reroute the B.Cu l_filt track at H6 and the v15_selv track at H8.
  - Put "NYLON ONLY" on silk.
  - Close the boss-match item before fab.

### M7 — MAJOR — J3 (TCO loop) pins are 1.26 mm apart across a safety function
- **Where:** J3 JST B2P-VH at (215.5,6.0). Pins are l_filt and tco_l.
- **Evidence:**
  - The copper pad gap is **1.26 mm** (2.7 mm pads at 3.96 mm pitch).
  - The project's own 3.2 mm "between potential groups" rule is waived by "HOT pins within one component: 0.2 mm".
  - With the TCO open, the gap sees line voltage. Tracking or contamination across it re-energises PS2, defeating the thermal cut-off. PS2 is the only TCO-switched supply.
- **Fix:** Use a VH header with the middle position depopulated (B3P-VH with pin 2 removed gives 7.92 mm), or a 5.08/7.5 mm-pitch rated terminal, and keep ≥ 3.2 mm.

### M6 — MAJOR — Coil studs at 15 mm centres against a 30 mm target
- **Where:** J5 (132,70.5) RES_A and J2 (132,85.5) COIL_FEED.
- **Evidence:** ASSEMBLY.md: "Use at least 30 mm centers between the coil studs … ≈ 540 V peak nominal … not a maximum fault bound". The board has 15 mm (FLOORPLAN "5 mm tank rule", copper gap 5.8 mm). M4 ring lugs (10 mm wide) and screw heads leave about 5 mm metal-to-metal at 30–40 kHz. IEC 60664-4 high-frequency distances are still open.
- **Fix:** Move to ≥ 30 mm centres, or document an insulation analysis of the lug-and-screw envelope with barriers or shrink sleeve.

### M11 — MAJOR — Sink interface geometry: tab offset from the board edge, and HOT↔PE at the sink not evaluated
- **Evidence:**
  - TO-247 Fab body/tab face at y = **1.83 mm** (Q2/Q3/Q5/Q6); BR1 at y = **2.30 mm**.
  - RELAYOUT L13 says "tabs flush/proud". The L14 sink keep-out starts at the board edge (world y 236 = board y 0).
  - The heatsink is PE-bonded and the tabs and leads are HOT. The TO-247 leads (y 4.21) are about 4 mm horizontally from a sink face at y = 0.
  - HOT pours (n_filt and sw_a on F, l_filt and sw_b on B, hv_ret on In1, bus_p on In2) run to 0.6 mm from the edge under the sink.
  - D5-AUDIT lists only "heatsink insulators" as open. No HOT↔PE (8 mm basis) clearance from leads or board-edge copper to the sink body was measured.
- **Fix:** Model the real sink, insulator and clamp stack against this board. Either move the TO-247/BR1 row so the tabs reach the sink face, or add a sink standoff. Measure lead and pour to sink clearance and creepage against the D5 HOT↔PE floor (8 mm), or justify basic insulation there explicitly.

### m5 — MINOR — J1 L–N copper gap is 2.48 mm
- **Evidence:** Phoenix MKDS 3/2-5.08 with 2.6 mm pads at 5.08 mm pitch. It is exempt from the 3.2 mm rule by the same-component rule.
- **Fix:** Shrink the pads to about 2.2 mm (ring is still ≥ 0.45 mm) to reach ≥ 2.9 mm, or justify under IEC 60335 functional insulation at PD3.

## 5. Mechanical

### M10 — MAJOR — Outline and connector placement contradict the packaging decision
- **Evidence:**
  - Edge.Cuts is an L shape: 290 × 140 plus a 91 × 50 tongue (x 0–91, y 140–190).
  - DECISIONS D-36 says "outline of at most 290 × 140 mm"; FLOORPLAN.md still says 290 × 140. `board_in_enclosure.py` does model the L, so the fit gate passes.
  - **J4** (controller harness) is at (34.5–59.6, 177–187), at the far front edge of the tongue. FLOORPLAN/L15: "Every connector that leaves the board (… J4 pigtail) is on or near the top edge … enclosure connectors on the rear wall".
