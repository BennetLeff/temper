## Remaining question for JLCPCB support

JLCPCB's capability page lists "Grade A laminates from suppliers including Nan
Ya, KB, Shengyi", so the brand is not guaranteed per order. That leaves one
question:

> We're ordering a 4-layer, 1.6 mm, 2 oz/2 oz board on stackup
> JLC041622-7628. Our design requires laminate with a comparative tracking
> index of at least 175 V (IEC material group IIIa, PLC ≤ 3). Can you confirm
> the order will use Nan Ya NP-155F, or another laminate with CTI ≥ 175 V, and
> state the CTI on the Certificate of Conformance?

If they can't confirm it, D5-BASIS must treat the laminate as group IIIb. IEC
60335-1 then restricts it to working voltages ≤ 50 V, which would force a
specified high-CTI laminate or larger creepage.

- Layers 4; thickness 1.6 mm; outer copper 2 oz; inner copper 2 oz; stackup
