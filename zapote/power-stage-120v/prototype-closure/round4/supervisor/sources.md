# Primary source authority

Reviewed on 2026-10-04. URLs below are manufacturer documents, not distributor pin summaries. Component selection remains a candidate until footprint, voltage/current/thermal margins and fault cases are reviewed in the actual assembly. Copies of downloaded manufacturer PDFs are not republished in this source package.

The inlet fuse, Schurter switch, Schneider LC1D18BD and HS200/HS100 resistor selections are inherited from the round3 inlet packet. Their qualification is not strengthened by merely including their terminals in a compiled schematic. See `../../round3/inlet/` for the existing selection/source record and round4 coupled-model work for proposed resistor changes. Source explicitly retains the old resistor pair until a coherent revised value, proof limits, mounting and protection budget is accepted.

Passive MPN strings and standard KiCad footprints are provisional selections, not a procurement release. Connectors marked `REVIEW_ONLY` and harness identities marked `HOLD` intentionally remain visible in the BOM/ERC. Those require exact mating parts, terminal drawings and insulation/routing decisions before a board or harness can be ordered.
