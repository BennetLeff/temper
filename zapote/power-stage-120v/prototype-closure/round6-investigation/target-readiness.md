# Round 6 target firmware readiness investigation

**Artifact and decision.** This examines the round-five STM32G071RBT6 supervisor and the dedicated ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N8 controller in `../round5/firmware/`, together with the shared `firmware/components/power/` core and legacy application demand calls. It is an engineering-prototype closure plan, not a powered-test or manufacturing release. No assembled hardware exists. The linked target images, host tests and model pass their recorded digital scopes, while both targets remain intentionally inhibited (`../round5/firmware/verification-evidence.json`; `../round5/integration/verification-snapshot.json`). Round-five R5I corrections are implemented findings with physical qualification still open; they are not reopened here. The FPGA capture experiment is optional bench research and has no product dependency.

## Executable work packages

1. **Freeze record contracts and authority.** Define versioned, bounded records for each STM32 channel's zero/injection/independent-check readings and absolute uncertainty, both installed NTC paths and lag qualification, the approved STM32 supervisor configuration, and the ESP32 scoped waveform/phase-map configuration. Bind records to exact board export, sensor-divider revision (VPRE is **801:1**), MCU target, image/interface version, unit identity, record sequence and approval evidence. Require integrity, completeness, range and monotonic version checks before copying into live structures. Specify who produces and approves each record, its nonvolatile location and programming tool, and a reproducible manifest. Keep a missing or unknown field invalid. **Digital acceptance:** table-driven corrupt, truncated, mismatched-unit/board, old-divider, unsupported-version, partial-write and mixed-version fixtures all inhibit at boot; a fully populated synthetic *test* fixture reaches only the expected digital qualification stages. **External dependency:** reviewed pin/analog revision and approved factory record schema/authority; physical calibration values and scope evidence are not available yet.

2. **STM32 cold-boot loading and POST service.** Load and validate fixed factory records before `r5_sensors_configure`; call existing `r5_calibrate`/`r5_sensors_ntc` and `r5_supervisor_init` rather than creating another estimator. Introduce a boot-scoped nonce and a defined, bounded operator submission channel only while source is isolated and contacts are proven released, discharged and cold. Feed two separately identified four-wire branch measurements with uncertainty into `post_record_accept`; display/report success only after that function accepts. Bind the submission to the current boot and serial; do not persist an accepted POST across reset. Leave the existing consume at mechanical START/fault and 60 s expiration intact. A boot nonce must change across resets; choose a generation/anti-replay mechanism with the factory interface rather than using a compile-time constant. **Digital acceptance:** reset, watchdog, brownout, reboot and update clear accepted POST; wrong nonce, repeated or failed serial, delayed >60 s submission, whole-pair measurement, one missing branch, lost cold/release/discharge before START and two START attempts reject. Complete UART/operator framing must not accidentally become an `ENERGY_LINK_COMMAND` or block its deadlines. **External dependency:** an operator tool/procedure and the actual isolated resistor measurement method.

3. **ESP32 boot-only commissioning loader and live guards.** Validate the scope record and characterized monotone phase map against the exact unit/board, four measured output identities, pad timing/endpoints/nonoverlap, source frequency, deadtime, build identity and approved operating envelope before setting `commissioned` or calling `r5_pwm_init`. `r5_pwm_prepare_capture` is a boot-only connection under a *physically verified* REQUEST-low inhibit, not a method for clearing a later fault. Provide continuously sampled rail, interlock and bus-fault values from reviewed hardware sources; define polarity, freshness, fault latching and startup gating. Reuse `r5_power_context_t`, `sample`, `phase`, `r5_power_bind`, existing request-low/disconnect and `fullbridge_apply`; do not enable BENCH_CAPTURE or the optional FPGA path. **Digital acceptance:** absent/stale/corrupt/mismatched scope or map, nonmonotone/out-of-range phase, stuck/high/unknown guard, provider timeout, post-bind guard loss and boot inhibit failure never assert REQUEST or healthy command; fault cannot be cleared by rebind or re-init. Inject both guard loss during zero demand and a reset while rails decay. **External dependency:** approved final sensing wiring and physical four-gate scope record/phase characterization. An ID alone is insufficient.

4. **Join application demand to the one power owner.** Choose a new-carrier application interface that passes bounded demand and a separately qualified pan power budget to the existing `power_set_level` owner. Map state entry/exit, stop, no-pan, overtemperature, watchdog and loss of request to zero demand; retain the service's fixed frequency and ban on uncharacterized burst pan detection. In particular, the legacy `STATE_PAN_DET` 5% call cannot simply become a real energizing pulse before the bounded continuous probe/detection map exists. Avoid concurrent callers of the single-owner bridge. **Digital acceptance:** full application-to-service tests cover 0→demand→0, no-pan/STOP/fault, invalid >100%, stale line telemetry, pan-budget withdrawal, zero-demand resume after qualified RUN and startup failure before first RUN. Preserve the 446 ms first-RUN rule and the independently guarded zero-demand session behavior. **External dependency:** product policy for the new controller carrier and characterized pan/current limits.

5. **STM32 programming and execution contract.** Add a factory programming/readback receipt for image hash, record pages, protection/boot/watchdog/BOR option bytes and reset cause. Choose BOR thresholds from measured POD_3V3 and supervisor reset/inhibit timing; document whether field updates exist and their rollback policy. Isolate record writes from RUN, and require a complete verified image/record pair before any new boot can qualify. Establish static stack analysis plus fill-pattern high-water under all ISR nesting, and timing traces for TIM3 entry/write/settling, EXTI DRDY, SPI transfers, UART and 500 µs owner step under flash/ADC fault and maximum traffic. Use actual MCU clocks/flash wait states in the analysis. **Digital acceptance:** linker/map and image checks, option-byte manifest mismatch, interrupted record or image update, bad CRC, wrong reset cause and fail-safe boot regressions; timing tests instrument the same source and record worst observed plus margin. **Physical acceptance later:** scope/logic-analyzer timing on powered silicon and supply dip/reset ramps confirm the chosen bounds. The present linker assertion and host schedule sweep alone do not close either bound.

## Cross-cutting failure-path checks

The boot path must hold REQUEST and contactor outputs low before record reads, through partial initialization and on any validation error. A reset/power loss must erase volatile POST and runtime authorization; persistent factory/commissioning records may survive only if their integrity, version and identity still validate. A firmware update must reject old or partially migrated records until explicitly reapproved. Any stale line cycle, mirror scan, heartbeat, rail/interlock sample, ADC sample or supervisory acknowledgment must withdraw permission according to the existing wrapper and hardware gate, including during zero demand. Test these as transitions, not only cold-boot boolean fixtures. The physical gate remains the last independent inhibit; a passing software trace cannot establish its analog level or propagation delay.

The round-five `verify.sh` already supplies acquisition, mirror, register-readback, joined-wrapper and linked-image regression coverage; extend those fixtures at the new loaders/providers and at the application boundary. The independent manufacturer/SDK facts used here are the ST [STM32G071RB datasheet](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32g071rb.pdf) and [RM0444](https://www.st.com/resource/en/reference_manual/DM00371828-.pdf). Espressif's [ESP-IDF 5.2 timer guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/release-v5.2/esp32s3/api-reference/system/esp_timer.html) says the default timer task can be delayed by higher-priority tasks and SPI-flash work; therefore the existing 250 µs notification and >1 ms trip need measured loaded scheduling, especially during provisioning/update. A successful build or host-model replay is evidence of software compatibility, not physical deadline, BOR, calibration or pad behavior.
