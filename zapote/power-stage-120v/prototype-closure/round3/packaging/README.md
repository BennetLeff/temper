## External pod: datums and fabrication concept

Stand the pod vertically. X runs left-to-right, Y front-to-back, Z upward; the removable front is Y=0, back Y=220. The CAD represents a custom 1.5 mm folded-steel shell and tool-removable door, 460 × 460 × 3 mm backplate at Y200 and a separate 340 × 210 × 6 mm aluminium hot plate at Y194–200. Material, bend allowances, finish, fasteners and enclosure certification require fabricator/engineering review; these are proposed dimensions. Do not import an IP, fire-containment or touch-protection rating from the material alone.

Lower zone: K1/K2/KB sit at X50/120/190, Z100 with conservative 45 × 101 × 85 mm reservations. Their main screws face forward; keep the designated front tool corridor clear of wires. A 70 mm-wide reservation at X265 holds three LPSC0001Z fuse holders for F0 KLDR015.TXP, F2 KLDR002.TXP and FT KLDR001.TXP, following the circuit team's current allocation. It includes three 17.78 mm widths with separator allowance. Reserve Y20–200, X260–340, Z95–195 for holder opening/removal; exact hinge and rail-clip datums still need the selected manufacturer's drawing/sample. Place the manual switch on the front outside this corridor, nominal X330/Z230; its 34.1 × 26.1 mm cutout, boots and rear wiring must be added before fabrication. The pod STEP currently represents body allocations, not the switch or drilled sheet metal.

The HDR-60-24 is upright at X385, Z75. Its 52.5 × 90 × 54.5 mm body is source-backed. Preserve at least the manufacturer's 5 mm side, 40 mm above and 20 mm below ventilation spaces; do not lay the enclosure flat during a powered test. The 115 × 100 × 45 mm control/isolated-sensing board reservation is above/right, X345–460/Z270–370. It accommodates KT proof relay and sensing as circuit-board design allocations, not existing validated electronics. A separate grounded divider and segregated terminals must enforce the actual mains/SELV isolation system; the whitespace in this model is not an approved clearance.

Upper hot zone: RPRE1/RPRE2 each occupy B × max(C,D) × A = 145.7 × 45.5 × 72.5 mm, at X30/195, Z365. RTEST occupies 88 × 27.3 × 47.5 mm at X35/Z285. Those conservative envelopes include the larger C/D dimension from the Ohmite table; do not use 41.8 mm as the entire HS200 depth without reading the drawing. Orient terminations into the side wiring corridors, with sleeved strain-relieved wires; do not bend a wire at the lug or use a solder joint as mechanical retention.

A separate tool-removable guard at Y120 separates fingers and service tools from the hot-resistor zone. It is not modeled as a certified touch barrier. The plate is a mounting/heat-spreading proposal only: **neither 200 W continuous dissipation nor pulse survival is credited to this plate.** Measure mounting-face and cutoff-body temperatures during the bounded precharge/reclose/failure sequence. Fit one DF128S candidate cutoff to each HS200 branch, in its own mechanically captive, electrically insulating sleeve/clamp, thermally coupled to the corresponding body. The CAD's 30 × 12 × 12 mm blocks are complete assembly reservations, not the thermal-fuse body dimensions. Do not put a live cutoff body in contact with bare grounded metal or route its mains leads through the SELV coil circuit. Exact cutoff transfer lag, sleeve dielectric system, clamp pressure and damage from lead attachment remain qualification items. A hot-surface limit must come from the selected component/material and exposure analysis, not an assumed cutoff temperature.

Give the steel shell, door, backplate, hot plate and any separate metal guard individual protective-earth bonds. The proposed incoming stud is [25,190,35], M4; make it a dedicated bare metal bonding land with secured hardware and an inspectable lug stack. A hinge, DIN clip or painted mounting bolt is not the sole bond. Bonding conductor/lug/terminal size, torque and fault-current verification remain tied to the circuit's fuse/cord coordination.

The [local operator panel](operator-panel.md) is a required part of startup: START PRECHARGE, RESET FAULT and independent STOP use local AUX power while downstream PS1 is off. Its stated front-panel reservations are not yet modeled operator hardware.

## Wiring and the R4 handoff

Use fixed power cables for this first article. Bottom pod entries are X45/Y160 for incoming L/N/PE, X290/Y160 for outgoing L/N/PE, X440/Y160 for the separate control/feedback harness. The candidate M20 LAPP 53111020 gland accepts a manufacturer-stated 6–13 mm cable diameter and has 9 mm thread length. The specific cord candidate is Southwire 55808799 Royal SJOOW, 12 AWG three-conductor 65/30, 300 V/90°C, nominal outside diameter 10.92 mm. It fits both published gland ranges nominally. Final ambient ampacity, exact length, termination compatibility, gland locknut, jacket pull/twist retention and minimum bend radius still need confirmation. Hubbell HBL5266C is the candidate normal 120 V/15 A plug; its 125 V rating does not cover the 140 V laboratory corner. See [cord selection](cord-selection.md). In particular, a common 125 V appliance cord cannot silently become the 140 V laboratory supply lead.

Inside the pod, keep unfiltered incoming L/N, switched/precharged outgoing L/N and control wiring in separate retained corridors. Terminate the jacket in a clamp before separating conductors. The outer gland limits jacket movement; the inner clamp stops torque and cable weight reaching electrical terminals. Route incoming PE to its dedicated stud first and make its free length outlast L/N under a failed restraint. Ring/spade terminals, ferrules or direct stranded wire must follow each selected terminal's allowed conductor class. Do not insert arbitrary fine-stranded cable into a terminal qualified only for another conductor class.

R4 receives the power cable through its existing rear cord aperture centered X135/Z34. Replace the blank by a 70 × 30 × 1.5 mm adapter at X100/Y436.5/Z18, with a nominal Ø20.2 gland hole. Two Ø3.4 mounting-hole reservations at X106/164, Z34 match the existing mounting centres. The four historical adapter screw/nut solids are omitted from the proposal because their installed stack is not valid for this plate; select the actual bolt/washer/nut stack after verifying panel thickness and available thread. This preserves the original rear opening; the M20 grip's Ø25 wrench envelope and 37 mm overall-length allocation remain to be fitted with the actual locknut and cord. The initial taller adapter collided with the right duct; the revised 30 mm adapter clears the nominal duct. Leave a jacket clamp reservation centered [135,418,34]. The unfiltered two-wire channel runs across the rear at X−140..142/Y416..432/Z22..42 into the rear edge of D22. This is a routing allocation, not a swept harness or proof of bend-radius fit. Its support and the exact D22 terminal boots remain to be modeled. Incoming PE goes directly to the existing [10,415] stud, then independently to the sink bond. Do not make D22's Y-cap wiring the protective bond.

The new circuit's electrical bypass proof load connects across the pod's precharged output **before D22**, as confirmed by the circuit owner. It therefore adds no remote post-filter power loop. D22-output and DC-bus sensing belong locally in R4 with appropriately isolated telemetry. The power link still changes common-mode current paths and wiring parasitics; previous installed EMC margins do not transfer automatically. Record cable length, PE routing and pod location in the first-unit build identity.

Removing the R4 switch leaves a 50 × 40 × 1.5 mm external rear blank at X45/Y440.1/Z35, nominal 0.1 mm compressed gasket allowance and 1.6 mm added rear projection. Fastener and gasket details remain open. The old switch/holder envelopes are removed only in the proposal STEP; the round2 source is retained.

## Replacement PCB barrier and service cover

The proposal replaces the omitted historic PCB chamber with a removable glass-laminate hood; exact laminate grade, CTI, flame/temperature data, insulation role and edge treatment remain unselected. It preserves front appearance and fits the existing exterior in the modeled state. It is **not yet a complete qualified electrical enclosure**: the front profile mates to the sink/cradle/duct, and the cable and sensor openings need actual retained closures. The boolean-profile contacts reported in the checks identify every such seam. They must become manufacturable compression/overlap joints with a tolerance drawing; zero-volume overlap alone is not insulation.

The roof spans X−125..142.5/Y154..327 at Z82..83.5. C5 and C6 have source-model tops at Z80, leaving **2.0 mm nominal lower clearance**. The coil support's conservative bounding minimum Z84.953 leaves **1.453 mm nominal upper clearance** above the roof. Neither is an electrical spacing verdict. Together, the total available roof/stack margin is only 3.453 mm. Allocate cap seating/height, coil support sag, roof flatness, laminate thickness, assembly datums and hot movement before machining the cover. If that worst-case stack does not close, lower the capacitor assembly or change the coil support height/shape; do not thin an insulation barrier merely to obtain a nominal collision pass.

A 50 × 52 mm central well at X−25/Y235 descends to Z60, allowing the sensor hardware above it while clearing PS1/J6 below. The initial Z52 well intersected both and was rejected. A 10 × 10 mm sensor-cable opening at X−5/Y256 is a retained feedthrough reservation, not a bare access hole. Likewise two rear wall openings, 20 × 15 mm and 20 × 12 mm, reserve power/control exits. Keep the appropriate insulation domains separated all the way through the connector/boot, not just on the PCB.

Left, right and rear walls extend to Z10, mating to the existing floor. The right wall at X141..142 avoids the right duct; an earlier X142.5..144 wall cut it. The front is profiled around the existing grounded sink/cradle and duct. Dedicated cover tabs should use the carrier rails rather than clamp the PCB; fastener holes/tab geometry are intentionally not drilled until the laminate and tolerance stack are approved. Do not bridge the board's floating edge restraint with rigid cover screws. The roof, well and walls are separate solids for fabrication concept review; joint fasteners, edge seals and structural stiffness remain to be detailed.

For inspection, isolate and verify discharge, remove the top/coil assembly, disconnect the captured sensor feedthrough, then release the hood fasteners and lift the roof/well. The pod's fuse service no longer requires this operation. Do not probe a powered board through an unqualified open service cover: bring measurement access out through the planned rated fixture/feedthrough system. No live-service approval follows from this CAD.

The energy team's optional catch module has a collision-screened **80 × 60 × 60 mm rear reservation at [−14,334,12]**, after moving the fuse into the pod. It is excluded from the exported assembled STEP because it is a conditional allocation, not selected hardware. A full 80 × 60 × 60 mm catch box therefore does not simply fit beside them. The rear location requires roughly 150–220 mm routing to the bus region, before real bends and terminal access. ## Build sequence and next evidence

1. Make a full-size unpowered pod backplate mockup with exact bought-part samples. Check the three contactors, three fuse doors, tool approach, terminations, wire bend radii and PSU convection orientation. Do this before drilling final sheet metal.
2. Build the separate resistor plate and captive cutoff clamps; document part/lot, compound, torque and insulation. No hot test until the circuit protection and qualified station are defined.
3. Assemble protective-earth lands and bonds first. Fit DIN hardware and controls, then wire with point-to-point inspection against the circuit's named terminals. Independently verify each normally open contactor, bypass proof and sense lead; do not infer correctness from a matching enclosure picture.
4. On the cold R4 assembly, gauge cap tops, coil-support underside and proposed roof/well using actual parts. Close the worst-case stack and select the barrier material/joints/feedthroughs. Then construct and inspect the cover, attach cable clamps and route separated harnesses.
5. Update the complete assembly with exact terminal boots, wire sweeps, cover fasteners and selected catch hardware. Re-run geometry and insulation review; perform bond, strain-retention and guarded access checks using defined acceptance methods. Only then can the first-unit electrical/thermal traveler decide whether powered commissioning is permitted.

These artifacts support cold fit review and a manufacturable-detail discussion, not a completed first unit. No prototype, contact-force, pull-test, bond-test, pulse-survival, temperature or touch-probe result has been invented.
