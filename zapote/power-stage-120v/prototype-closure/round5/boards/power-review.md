# Power and sequencing review — no powered-release acceptance

The nine routed boards preserve the round4 power architecture: an external HDR-60-24 supplies AUX_24V; wired R-78HB5.0-0.5/W supplies POD_5V through J13; onboard LD1117S33TR supplies POD_3V3. Neither the native controller nor the FPGA module may draw from POD_3V3 without a new budget. CTRL_3V3/CTRL_GND remain a separately powered domain.

Six AMC3330s alone require **171 mA typical / 246 mA maximum** with no external HLDO load (6 × 28.5 / 41 mA). The source adds one STM32G071, ADS131M08, four TLV9061, twelve TLV3201, nine ISO7710 isolators, logic, timers, reference bias and passive loads. `component-census.json` records the actual placed devices. The inherited **350 mA allocation is not an accepted worst-case total**: 104 mA remains after only the isolated amplifier maxima; mode-dependent MCU/ADC/isolator current, gate charging, pullups and startup charging still need a common corner calculation. Do not replace maxima with typical values to close this budget.

At the illustrative 350 mA load, nominal LD1117 dissipation is at least (5 − 3.3) × 0.35 = **0.595 W**, before its ground current. ST specifies 110 °C/W SOT-223 junction-to-ambient in its thermal table, and a 125 °C operating-junction ceiling for the selected standard part. Using that table only as a screen gives a 65.5 °C rise and leaves less than 60 °C ambient before ground-current loss. It is **not an extracted thermal model of this PCB**. The candidate has no designed regulator heat-spreading region, so its thermal implementation is a digital design hold. At an illustrative 5.25 V input / 3.235 V output and 350 mA, dissipation rises to 0.705 W before ground-current loss. This corner is a sensitivity case, not a certified bound on the upstream module.

The wired RECOM module is rated 500 mA and recommends a 10 mA minimum load; its specifications are not guaranteed at zero load. Its 5 V accuracy is ±3% maximum at the stated full-load condition, with additional line/load/temperature behavior. The full rail corner must be compared to the LD1117's **4.75 V minimum characterized input**, including wiring drop; nominal 5 V is insufficient evidence. At 350 mA 3.3 V load, the 5 V output must additionally feed the regulator's ground current, preload and direct 5 V loads; the nominal current headroom is under 150 mA, before those additions and module derating.

**Implemented R5 ECO:** R_MINLOAD (central R1) changes from 499 Ω to **470 Ω, 0.1%, TNPW1206470RBEEA**, same 1206 footprint. At 4.75 V and +0.1% resistance it draws 10.096 mA; even +0.25% additional resistance drift leaves about 10.071 mA. The previous 499 Ω draws only 9.510 mA at that lower input boundary. At the illustrative 5.25 V upper case the new part dissipates about 58.7 mW. This closes the standalone preload tolerance gap over that stated boundary; it does not validate the complete 5 V rail, startup or thermal budget.

Sequencing/backfeed holds are explicit:

- An energized CT retains both burden resistors and its bidirectional TVS locally even when its signal cable disconnects. Removing a single burden resistor leaves the other, but doubles gain; this is a fault requiring shutdown, not continued operation.
- An external debugger must sense POD_3V3 and must not drive its SWD voltage-reference pin as a supply. Otherwise it can backfeed the unpowered MCU and regulator. No reverse-current blocker has been added to this candidate.
- The external ADC clock input must remain low/high-impedance when POD_3V3 is absent, or have a qualified isolation/series-clamp implementation. The present J7 is a clock interface, not an implemented oscillator.
- AMC3330 isolated high-side DC/DC comes from each card's low-side supply. No external high-side power is permitted. Its startup settling is not a permit-to-switch signal by itself; diagnostic and analog validity must both be checked after rails stabilize.
- CTRL power may be absent while AUX power remains, or vice versa. ISO7710 default-state variants are retained, but actual output-pin clamping, inactive-side load, native-permit behavior and both power orders need circuit-level fault tests. A PCB DRC cannot prove these conditions.
- The 24 V contactor switching return shares AUX_0V with the supervisor. There is no demonstrated low-impedance return-plane or extracted common-impedance immunity for this first connectivity layout. Review this before allocating fast digital/analog routing acceptance.
