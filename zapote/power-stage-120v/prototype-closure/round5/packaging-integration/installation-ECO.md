# Candidate installation ECO — support and sense harness

This document defines a candidate internal installation. The historical floor, hood, duct, boards and enclosure sources remain unchanged. The candidate STEP and its drill records must travel together; a historical floor STEP is not interchangeable with the candidate floor. Do not fabricate from a partial or failed geometry receipt.

## Support hardware

The pod uses a removable supervisor carrier, an adjoining CT carrier and a separate rear LINE/PRE carrier. Their detailed board-hole positions come from pcbnew, not a nominal drawing grid. `support-patterns.json` records screw axes and bearing planes.

- Supervisor frame: four Ø12 × 118 mm 6061-T6 posts, axes parallel to Y, between Y82 and Y200. X29/306, Z34/248. M4×10 screws enter from the front of the 2 mm carrier and back of the 3 mm backplate. Blind threads are 8.5 mm deep at each end. Dedicated PE continuity is required for metal frame members; an anodized joint is not a bond specification.
- CT frame: two corresponding posts at X469, Z129/166. Two 39 × 10 × 3 mm PEEK bridge straps at X301…340, Z125…135 and143…153 connect the left edge to the supervisor carrier. M3 holes at X306/329; do not put a long support post behind the left CT edge because it intersects KPB.
- LINE/PRE carrier: Ø10 × 8 mm spacers at X387/453, Z361/468; M3×18 through bolts and nuts behind the backplate. Short spacers are not assigned incompatible opposed blind screws.
- Board mounting: central M3×25; LINE/PRE M2.5×25; CT M2.5×20; catch M2.5×12. Candidate nonconductive screws and nuts are unfilled PEEK. Native NPTH diameters remain unchanged. Threads are represented as clearance cylinders, not helical contact surfaces. Torque, creep, vibration and material traceability need prototype qualification.
- Front tray: four Ø3.4 floor holes at (−86,26), (131,26), (−86,70), (131,80). The rear-left station moved from Y79 to Y70 for route access. The rear-right station moved Y79→80 to increase the return-guide/post gap from0.5 to1.5 mm. M3×8 floor screws pass through the 2 mm floor, a 0.5 mm shim and 1.5 mm tray into Ø6 PEEK posts. Separate top M3×6 PEEK screws retain the stepped cover. Posts have two blind 5.2 mm threads; the 12 mm short post retains a 1.6 mm internal web. Upper screws must not be substituted with a longer screw that meets the lower screw.
- BUS/TANK board screws: M2.5×10 pan heads sit in Ø4.7 × 1.8 mm underside recesses. Integral Ø7 bosses increase local tray thickness; the remaining standoffs are 2.5 mm. This avoids placing a projecting screw head in the 0.5 mm floor/tray gap. A custom round PEEK nut reservation is shown; its final wrench form and controlled torque remain part details.
- Catch: three M2.5 clearance holes through the candidate guard at Y391/Z46, Y351/Z46 andY351/Z75. Screws enter from X121 toward −X, clamping guard, 1.5 mm rail, 3 mm spacers and the board. Disconnect both board interfaces before removing the guard. The card cannot be left attached to a fixed harness while the guard is pulled away.

Accu's SIP-M2.5-10-PEEK drawing gives a Ø4.5 × 1.7 mm pan head; SIP-M3-6-PEEK gives Ø5.5 × 2 mm. Other lengths use the same explicitly dimensioned candidate head envelopes; their availability is not established by the CAD. The modeling of a material does not establish its electrical classification.

## OUT support: rejected and revised options

Rear posts collided with the actual duct wall. Posts beside the filter collided with the duct, an existing foot, or the PE routing allocation. A narrow blade option left only 0.25 mm between the fastener head and the filter/PE allocation. **That option is rejected as a manufacturing stack**, even though its simplified bodies could be made nominally separate.

The revised candidate raises OUT to native origin (−130, 410, 75), 7 mm above the original nine-board placement. Its insulating tray is Z69.5…71. Two machined 6061-T6 brackets pass below the transverse PE routing allocation and rise beyond it. Each has a 12 × 19 × 5 mm foot at Y312…331, Z10…15; a 12 × 3 mm upright at Y328…331; and a 12 × 10 × 3 mm top flange at Y328…338, Z64.5…67.5. The left bracket center is X−129.5, the right X−73. Four Ø3.4 floor holes are at (−133,316), (−126,316), (−76.5,316), (−69.5,316). M3×6 bottom screws engage 4 mm into the blind foot threads. Two M3×5 screws at (−128,335) and (−73,335) attach the frame through its 2 mm thickness into 3 mm flange threads.

The stiffened frame uses 2 mm rails at Z67.5…69.5, 4 mm downstands to Z63.5, and a crossmember at Y375…381. It carries OUT above D22 without loading the filter cover. Three M2.5×12 board screws pass through the frame, tray, 4 mm standoffs and PCB; their heads remain below the frame, with local web relief. The top flange is offset beneath the left rail to keep the floor foot clear of the existing native-board carrier rail. Do not replace it with a centered symmetric bracket.

The bracket feet require local lower-edge notches in candidate hood parts, with captured edge seals. The main housing exterior and historical hood remain unchanged. Install bracket feet before the hood, then seals, hood, frame, tray and board. The metal frame requires its own specified PE continuity connection; the CAD does not qualify a screw joint as a protective bond. OUT service requires discharge, harness disconnection and removal of its three board fasteners; the two frame screws release the frame if needed.

The rejected round-post trial intersected the transverse PE allocation; it is not the selected arrangement. The selected bracket foot stops at Z15 below the PE allocation's Z16 bottom, its upright starts at Y328 beyond PE's Y327 edge, and its rear face is Y331 ahead of D22's Y332 front. Those three nominal mechanical margins are 1.0 mm. The frame web is 1.5 mm above the filter cover. Allocate at most 0.60 mm total adverse variation: floor-hole position 0.15, filter/PE harness placement 0.15, adjacent envelope 0.15, bracket dimension 0.025 and thermal/assembly movement 0.125 mm. These are proposed drawing budgets, not measured capability. They leave at least 0.40 mm mechanical separation; verify loaded displacement and actual supplier/assembly capability. None is an electrical clearance determination.

## BUS/TANK sense routes

`native-pickoffs.json` records pcbnew's native coordinates and the native19 content hash. The four proposed sense-only branches are C5.1 BUS_P, C5.3 HV_RET, C21.1 RES_A andC21.2 SW_B. They use existing underside solder lands; no extra stud stack, new board hole or unreviewed copper tap is introduced. Actual underside solder joints and capacitor lead projections are not supplied by the source STEP and remain a physical termination check.

The 3D routes use Alpha39224022AWG silicone wire at its maximum stated OD0.114in=2.8956 mm. The published bend requirement is10×diameter, or28.956 mm. Each modeled jacket centerline bend is R32; even the inside of the maximum wire envelope is30.5522 mm. The custom guide-tube candidate is5.0 mm OD,3.3 mm ID. Its bend/kink behavior and insulation material are not established by the wire datasheet. Wire length and all bend tangency margins are in `harness-probe.json`.

The corrected rigid-native routes take BUS_P/HV_RET rightward from C5 to X174, then forward along the right duct at Z27/21 to Y48/73 and into BUS. RES_A runs from C21.1 to X−170, then forward at Z27 to Y79; SW_B runs to X−176, then forward at Z20.5 to Y34. Both enter TANK from the left. All bends remain R32. The SW_B jacket endpoint is 2 mm from the native pad in Y; all four terminal tails still require defined end restraints and insulation. Their complete tube solids are screened against each other and retained material. Current lengths are about236.0/248.5/311.8/411.3 mm in that net order; use the matching receipt for exact values.

The candidate hood and duct gain only the recorded guide penetrations. Custom grooved insulating grommets follow the actual sheet surfaces; the guide OD is smaller than the grommet bore. Their flanges retain them at the sheet. Material choice, insertion damage, pull-through force and hot aging need qualification. Four bolted guide blocks use the candidate floor-hole pattern in `harness-probe.json`. Each block has two M2×8 joining screws at explicit coordinates and a split plane 4 mm below its top. The BUS side block sits on the duct floor at Z13, with a 2.2 mm floor-to-duct spacer and M3×10 through bolt; the other blocks use M3×8 floor screws. A compliant grip insert and specified tightening force still require a pull test: a nominal sliding bore does **not** prove axial strain relief.

At each end, the wire jacket stops before the solder attachment; a short formed conductor tail reaches the actual pad. The manufacturer R32 jacket path must not be misrepresented as extending through an abrupt solder-tail bend. The conductor formation, insulation sleeve/boot, permitted strip length, wetting/strain-relief geometry and repair procedure remain a termination coupon requirement. Until those details are qualified, this is an existing nominal route with explicit termination gaps, not a build-released harness.

Install grommets and guide tubes before soldering; label both ends with net and source pad. The harness is disconnected at the board-side termination for service, with stored energy removed. Do not add an arbitrary service loop in a space checked only for the nominal route. The long BUS/TANK leads require separate transfer-function, common-mode pickup, fault and transient validation. A 10 kV DC wire rating is not automatic acceptance for the tank's high-frequency waveform.

## Evidence and limits

Successful receipts must match the current native capture and source hashes. The geometry checks establish nominal route/support existence and explicit absence of modeled solid interference. They do not establish powered behavior, safety insulation, touch accessibility, thermal acceptance, fatigue, clamp torque or EMC. No FPGA or optional bench fixture is included in the product assembly.

## Dedicated sink bond and current floor

The corrected cooling assembly includes a dedicated custom copper braid from the sink terminal at (−66,157.9,72) to the existing cradle terminal at (−128,160,12), with separate M4 termination/locking stacks. The cooling mounting screws are not the bond specification. The braid is represented by a conservative 4.4 mm swept envelope; its nominal 4 × 1.5 mm section and at least 2.5 mm² effective copper area are design requirements, not an approved supplier part or a fault-current rating. Its split strain guide adds floor holes at (−139,167) and (−139,173), both Ø3.4. The current new floor therefore has 14 cooling/PE holes plus 12 support/guide holes. Use the matching cooling interface for the full route, split wall bushing and terminal stack. Verify braid manufacturing/bend capability, weld and locking process, measured bond resistance, thermal cycling and fault-current withstand before powered acceptance.

The catch guide bores are closed in their respective halves: the Z75.5 split does not open either bore; mount access wells interrupt only the end of the BUS guide. Thread the wires before forming their terminals; guide removal requires terminal release. The Ø4.3 guide bores around the modeled Ø3.937 jacket provide routing support only, not axial clamping. Exact independently qualified strain relief remains required.
