faulting500us after heat demand became zero with the startup window expired.
The target regression now covers full two-proof startup, source age1s,
zero demand100ms, resumed RUN50ms and OFF. Firmware tests and the rebuilt

the stronger replacement and final receipts are recorded below.

Automatic approval review rejected native regeneration that would change
the budget escape from instantaneous SUP_RUN_OK_LOCAL to BYPASS_PROVEN.

BYPASS_PROVEN while the proof window, precharge threshold and isolation
are true, without MCU_RUN ever arming the runtime checks. RUN_NOT_ARMED
would then mask absent controller rail/heartbeat/native fault health.
That proposal is abandoned, not an approved safety correction.

stronger design was accepted.29 independent pin-driven state cases pass the
