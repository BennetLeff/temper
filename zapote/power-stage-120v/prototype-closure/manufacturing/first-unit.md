## CF01 cold impedance fixture — build description

Use one rigid, nonconductive **450 × 450 mm nominal bench base** as an experimental support, not a product part or certified insulation barrier. This proposed base size accommodates a200mm coil and position references; enlarge it if the selected pans or actual support cannot sit stably. Use an uncoated polymer/wood board with no embedded metal and measure/document its material and thickness. Keep metal fasteners and guide hardware out of the coil's field region; do not put foil, steel rulers or a metal workbench directly under the fixture. The unknown field reach is a measurement variable: compare reference readings with nearby metal removed and with the intended enclosure parts positioned, and retain both configurations.

Locate the **actual** coil/ferrite/support on three stable nonconductive datum pads outside sensitive windings, without flattening/damaging litz or ferrites. Support the real glass on independently adjustable nonconductive supports; it must carry the pan without loading the coil. Choose pad/clamp geometry after the physical sample arrives. Measure gap at several named points rather than trusting spacer thickness. R4 CAD's3mm coil-top-to-glass-underside gap and4mm glass are initial nominal references; never copy them into measured fields. Mechanical minimum/maximum remain unapproved.

Add a paper/polymer XY overlay centered on the measured coil center and removable nonmetal pan stops at centered, +30mm X and +30mm Y. They are experiment positions, not allowed cooking offsets. Photograph the actual pan base relative to those references and record positioning uncertainty. Fix lead dressing to nonmetal anchors without changing coil terminal geometry. Terminate four-terminal-pair or instrument-approved Kelvin leads at the **same coil terminal plane** used for compensation. No resonant capacitors, power PCB, mains cord or energized heater belongs on CF01.

At room temperature, record an unloaded measurement and labeled pans at20/30/40/50/60kHz, two approved small-signal amplitudes, three independent re-seats and additional dense points where behavior changes. Raw `R+jX`, excitation amplitude, uncertainty and compensation are required. Reuse [coil-runs.csv](../../../../docs/research/mit-product-design/readiness/power/coil-runs.csv) and [coil-points.csv](../../../../docs/research/mit-product-design/readiness/power/coil-points.csv) unchanged. Hot/high-power work is a separate reviewed fixture, not an extension of this wood/polymer bench setup.
