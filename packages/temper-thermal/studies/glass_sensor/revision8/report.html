The next build, in order

  1. Resolve exact manufacturing instructions

    Get the received-lot Resbond 908 process, sensor-compatible cure and bondable face, plus approved lead forming, trimming and joining details. Supplier questions are drafted and not sent. Define product loads, duty, leakage and fault-cut acceptance before qualification.

  2. Commission fixtures and test process witnesses

    Use dummy specimens to establish installed force/displacement uncertainty and fixture freedom. Section material witnesses at the proposed gaps and a manufacturer-directed reference thickness. Then test compatibility with the exact sensor construction.

  3. Compare the dry thermal cartridges

    Start with M222 / 0.100 mm as the numerical control, subject to the process gate. Compare matched M222 / 0.075 mm, then the complete IST308 coupon after its face and lead questions close. Measure contact-force and body-temperature sensitivity separately; retain whole-pan holdouts.

  4. Qualify the sealed mechanism and physical detector

    Test hot cyclic seal reaction, pressure reversal and leakage, then jam, pressure and insulating-debris counterexamples against independent contact ground truth. Test +0.20 mm cap capture and +0.10 mm island capture separately and together. Existing firmware logic cannot establish physical contact.

  5. Run integrated induction and endurance trials

    With defined lab and product limits, separate electronic pickup, cap self-heating and pan hotspots using paired field-off/on trials and reference-perturbation controls. Repeat contact, retention, leakage and calibration checks after the declared cleaning and thermal-cycle duty.
