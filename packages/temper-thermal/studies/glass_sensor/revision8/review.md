## Remaining open decisions, already documented

1. Exact received-lot bond process, sensor-compatible cure, bondable face, native lead routing and joining process. A supplier-directed witness and process stability evidence precede thermal assembly.
2. Actual force/displacement/reference instruments, installed uncertainty and completed fixture hardware. The proposed uncertainty values do not establish capability.
3. A continuous wet boundary covering both motion stages, cap/island interface, feedthroughs and pressure reference/drainage; actual hot cyclic force curves must leave uncertainty margin.
4. Physical observables that separate clean contact from each relevant jam/contamination state, plus product-derived maximum heat-after-loss or end-to-end inhibition requirement.
5. Carrier-to-housing retention load path, process/material allowables and product-derived pull/side/impact/permanent-set criteria; combined upward-capture geometry remains future work.
6. Final power/coil/AFE identities and approved lab exposure limits; cleaning chemistry, temperature/duty/life, leakage limits and post-stress acceptance remain undefined.

These are hardware/process/product decisions, not defects that another numerical pass can close. No new load, voltage, cycle count or physical latency acceptance threshold was introduced by this review.
