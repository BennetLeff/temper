# R8: resolve the pre-bench decisions

Continue the user's authorized simulation and test-preparation work. Preserve the R7 design, numerical results, current CAD pointers and historical manifests. No hardware operation, purchasing, external outreach or production enablement is authorized by this package.

Deliver a build-readiness packet covering:

1. Exact supplier evidence for RTD, bond and lead processing; identify conflicts between modeled dimensions and published process guidance.
2. Complete seal load paths and independent contact-observable requirements, with existing force allocations and their authority kept explicit.
3. Current retention geometry versus historical strength screens; valid load application and reaction fixtures.
4. Ordered induction/endurance experiments that separate interference, physical self-heating and local pan heating.
5. A staged first-build and measurement matrix, status/owner/evidence register, unsent supplier questions and an honest readiness decision.

The next product stage remains a controlled engineering prototype after its particular build blockers are resolved. Public ratings, mathematical screens and prepared tests cannot close physical qualification. Research uses primary manufacturer sources. Existing numerical engines and acquisition tools remain authoritative; do not create a parallel physics/reduction implementation.

R8 is an evidence/readiness review, not a new CAD or thermal design revision. New material or process evidence may justify a future separately modeled geometry; it must not silently change the R7 baseline.
