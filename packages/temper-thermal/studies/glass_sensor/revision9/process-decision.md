# R9 bond and native-lead process decision

**Decision: preserve R7 as a numerical study, exclude its unqualified 75/100 µm Resbond 908 layers and sub-millimetre lead forming from the next fabrication baseline, and prepare an open process coupon using Ceramabond 569 before another enclosed sensor assembly.** Keep 908 as a conditional material candidate; it is not disproved, but its exact thin-layer process remains undocumented. No reviewed alternative closes continuous 250°C service, thin-film processing, sensor compatibility and installed insulation simultaneously.

Its 908 column specifies room-temperature cure and water-insolubilization after 250–300°F exposure/post-cure, without a duration. A distributor's detailed recipe is not accepted as manufacturer/received-lot authority. |

Welding/crimping/brazing are listed; no 1 mm stub or forming procedure is approved by that list. The exact code does **not** specify the optional metallized backside. Do not promote the family option into a received-part feature. IST's general electrical datum is **5 mm from the wire end**, unlike M222's body-referenced datum. Subtracting this from a nominal 7 mm wire suggests 2 mm from the body, but the subtraction is an inference requiring exact-part confirmation. Until then, report the actual measurement-contact coordinates and perform assembly calibration; do not label the assembled value as catalog Class A by default. Four extension wires cannot remove resistance inside the two native leads upstream of the Kelvin split. [IST ATP §6](https://www.ist-ag.com/sites/default/files/downloads/ATP_E.pdf).

## R7 assumptions to remove from the build baseline

1. **75/100 µm Resbond 908 as a reproducible process:** retain the old geometry as a sensitivity study; replace fabrication status with `PROCESS_UNDEFINED`. Also apply this to the separate ceramic cover attachment films.
2. **One verified active-film distance:** retain opposite-face/full-envelope bounds until exact orientation is established. Package height is neither a homogeneous ceramic slab nor a measured bond-to-film distance.
3. **Trimmed/bent native leads as an approved routing solution:** defer both R7 stub variants. No new lead strength or bend-fatigue claim follows from geometric clearance. Evaluate a full-lead open article first, then route an enclosed successor using an approved process.
4. **Pure nickel lead thermal properties as exact:** mark as a proxy for Pt-clad Ni or Au-coated Ni. Cladding geometry and composite conductivity remain unknown.
5. **Catalog Class A after assembly:** remove as a claim until measurement-contact geometry and process-induced resistance shifts are accounted for. Keep untreated same-lot witnesses.
6. **A room-temperature-cured bond is an established hot wet insulator:** defer. Bond coverage, hot leakage, cure evolution, thermal expansion and sensor-glass chemistry require independent measurements.

These changes are dispositions for the next revision; no R7 files were edited by this workstream.

## Concrete next coupon: P9-569-OPEN, process candidate only

Purpose: determine whether a controlled thin insulating attachment can be formed without voids, progressive cure damage or a sensor-resistance change. Intended volume is a handful of engineering specimens, not a production pilot. No new cartridge performance is claimed.

**First article geometry:** use the existing D6 316L cap as the metal witness, supported externally without deforming its roof. Bond a supplier-identified bare alumina tile, nominal 2.3×2.1×0.25 mm, on a centered 2.7×2.5 mm pad. Select **0.100 mm nominal gap** with an external removable height-control fixture; use a second **0.150 mm** process witness to expose gap sensitivity. These are our experimental geometries, not guaranteed process tolerances. No beads, conductive shims or permanent spacers enter the heat path. Keep the assembly open for dispensing, imaging and destructive sectioning; omit covers, retaining cartridge, seal and extension harness from this process-only specimen.

Use the manufacturer's standard-569 published cure above as the **candidate witness process**, recording actual ramp/dwell and received instructions. Do not dilute by default, substitute VFG, or claim a fully matured wet-service bond from this cycle. Measure both cured gap and gap change during subsequent dry hot exposure; do not equate fixture separation with cured adhesive thickness. Section a witness and record voids/cracks/wetting rather than inventing a universal permissible void percentage. Initial screening can falsify the process but cannot prove repeatability.

**Second article, only after witness credibility:** substitute exact M222 32208550, preserve its complete delivered leads and do not form them near the body. Keep electrical connections outside the cap in an open fixture. Place voltage contacts at the documented body-referenced datum and current contacts distally, with each actual contact coordinate recorded. No near-body weld, trim or cover is needed for this process-isolation experiment. Choose the bonding face only after exact-part disposition or identified sacrificial cross-section; a convenient flat-looking face is insufficient. Repeat untouched-element resistance measurements after fixturing, cure and hot exposure.

This open geometry is manufacturable in the ordinary fixture-access sense, but **sensor-process permission remains conditional**. It deliberately does not fit inside the R7 cartridge. Full native wires, external contacts and missing covers change heat capacity and heat leakage: model them as a distinct article, never reuse R7 result labels. The later enclosed article needs a new routing/clearance/force and thermal export before comparison.

**Modeling allowed now:** geometry and unknown-property sensitivity for P9-569-OPEN; independent orientation branches; actual full native-lead lengths when available. **Not allowed as a factual prediction:** a named-adhesive performance score without cured material properties, a bond-face assertion from a catalog illustration, or translating the witness result to complete-cartridge behavior. All specimens are NOT_RUN.
