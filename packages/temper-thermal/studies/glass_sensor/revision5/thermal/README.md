# R5 thermal and contact screen

Simulation and test preparation only. All hardware measurements are **NOT_RUN**. This model compares the actual CAD-derived D8 and D6 faces, including their added retained ears, cap-moving ceramic join covers, native nickel leads and full developed copper/PFA routes. It does not establish that either face makes a particular contact pattern.

## Reproduction and evidence

Run `./run.sh` from any directory. It checks pinned full SHA256 identities before compiling with standalone `rustc`; no Cargo or shared extension cache is used. `inputs.sha256` pins the frozen original kernel, this source and the final CAD scalar export. The runner does not update pins. Historical R4 remains unchanged.

The scalar reader rejects missing required keys, nonfinite/negative values, malformed row lengths, missing heads, inconsistent 60mm total developed length, anchor longer than the hot route, cover outside the face and impossible wire diameters. Geometry length units are mm; conversion into the solver is SI. Thermal capacities use actual cylinder length, stripped length and material diameters, never the larger bend/sweep envelopes from CAD. The four wire routes use their mean developed hot length: this preserves total capacity but not the exact parallel-conduction distribution of unequal routes. Route-by-route refinement remains a limitation.

The entire small weld process envelope, including its overlap with separately counted native/Cu solids, is conservatively assigned3.95MJ/m³K at the first hot-wire cell; its real weld fill and alloy remain unqualified.
- Ceramic covers use actual CAD volume and assumed2.8MJ/m³K. Their cap attachment includes actual CAD film volume at2MJ/m³K and film resistance t/(2.1634A), in series with unknown interface conductance. Covers, individual leads and three posts/hooks are smeared axisymmetrically, so one-sided contact and independent join temperatures are not resolved.

## Contact is a hypothesis, not a CAD result

The comparison uses equal total spring force and the inherited pressure law `h=href*(F/(5000*A))^0.7`, followed by a circular-equivalent pan spreading resistance `1/(4*k_pan*sqrt(A/pi))`, with k_pan45W/mK. `href=1000/2000/4000W/m²K` is deliberately swept. It is not a surface-finish specification or a prediction. Contact areas are full face, centralØ4mm, or outer annulus r0.75R–R. Annular spreading is especially approximate: the circular equivalent is a screening surrogate. Force redistribution, asperities, elastic pan/cap distortion and gas gaps are not solved.

This distinction matters: reducing face area increases pressure but does not preserve total conductance. The middle D6 case can respond faster while retaining a similar steady underread. Contact only at the rim remains poor for both heads. A smaller face is therefore a comparative coupon candidate, not a selected production solution.

`requirements.csv` instead overrides totalG to find conditional conductance thresholds for t90-pan≤2s and thermal bias≤1 or2°C, under nominal and higher loss assumptions. Search stops at0.5W/K; no-solution is only within that interval. These are not universal scalar acceptance requirements because contact location is independently assumed.

## Mechanics and validity envelope

`mechanics.csv` uses the R2 series spring balance: main preload0.12N, main stiffness0.2N/mm, local stiffness1.6–2.4N/mm. It solves force and both deflections together. Main travel1.2mm and local stop0.25mm are screened. The upper stop is solved piecewise: for local_rate×travel≤preload only the local spring moves, and the main displacement remains zero. A rigid-pan support-hull test uses Ftip=m*g*(1-e/R). It identifies possible rocking; it does not solve pan elasticity, dynamic impact, seal drag or liquid forces. Pan masses0.05/0.15/0.5kg and eccentricity fractions0/0.8 are scenarios, not a declared cookware envelope.

`ear_clearance.csv` compares the lowest point of a parabolic/tilted pan over the sensing face with the D6 ears recessed0.15mm at the exported maximum ear radius. Continuous ring clearance is conservative in azimuth versus the actual six ears. Negative clearance means ear contact is possible and face-only thermal cases are invalid. The stress cases up to±0.2mm bowl at r4mm and10° tilt are not promised operating conditions. A positive result only means geometric clearance under the rigid-profile hypothesis, not assured thermal contact.

## Metrics and experiments

`t90_own_s` reaches90% of the sensor's eventual step; `t90_pan_s` reaches90% of the25→100°C pan step, glass/body held25°C. Bias is at pan200/glass80/body60°C. The long-ramp field is additional lag relative to instantaneous DC at5°C/s. `finite_ramp.csv` separately runs25→200°C over35s then holds35s, glass/body fixed25°C; it includes DC loss plus transient lag and is not controller overshoot.

`pickup_lead_sensitivity.csv` adds0/0.0001/0.0003/0.001W/K direct cap-to-body conductance for an optional diagnostic pickup. This is an instrumentation disturbance screen only: no pickup hardware, wire capacity or azimuthal location is included in the baseline CAD. Instrumented physical results must account for actual pickup conductance and mass before extrapolation.

Verification includes cylindrical resistance, equal-temperature boundaries, full heat balance of an implicit step, zero-convection wire resistance, full wire mass conservation, an independent CAD cylinder-volume check, an independently collapsed isothermal-cap limit, and joint radial/wire/time refinement. Numerical convergence is not validation of the assumed contact/material/boundary values. Physical data, independent holdouts and full-system error budgets remain required.

`uncertainty.csv` contains three illustrative parameter bundles, not a complete worst-case tolerance stack or a probability distribution. Cover-to-cap interface and join-to-cover interface use the same swept proxy value for tractability; a physical assembly needs independent characterization. The higher-loss inverse case changes wire cooling, direct loss and seal loss while retaining nominal anchor/cover assumptions, so it does not certify a full uncertainty envelope.
