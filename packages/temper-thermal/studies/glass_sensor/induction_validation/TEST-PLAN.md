## Phase A: establish electronics susceptibility without thermal ambiguity

1. Photograph and identify coil, ferrite, pan, glass aperture, cartridge, sensor board and wire harness revision. Log actual coil-current and frequency waveforms, pan offset and gap. Characterize field with a calibrated suitably small search coil/field probe at candidate location if feasible, including pan and ferrite effects. The probe perturbs and samples space; include orientation, area calibration and placement uncertainty. No-pan field does not bound loaded-pan field.
2. Mount a stable calibrated precision resistor outside the thermal path, connected through the complete production RTD harness near the cap location. The resistor/fixture itself must be field-characterized and thermally monitored. Test100Ω and hot-range resistance values, all used converter modes, and positive/negative loop orientation. Dummy resistance isolates ADC/SPI/harness effects from actual pan tracking.
3. Run at least three off→on→off brackets per point; later add randomized ABBA/reversed order blocks. Choose windows containing complete fresh settled conversions; confirm drift curvature below its allocated uncertainty. Record all fault and contact-detector events, including invalid frames; do not discard outliers caused by switching.
4. Repeat with actual cartridge and independent local optical references, matching pan temperature and heating slope. A reproducible jump faster than thermal response suggests electronics susceptibility; a slower response may be real cap self-heating, pan gradient or sensor dynamics. Separate with the dummy test, measured cap reference, local pan references and thermal time constants. The paired evaluator cannot diagnose the cause automatically.
5. Sweep actual minimum/maximum frequency, full/deep phase shift, low-duty bursts, startup/stop, supply corners and pan offset/size classes within an approved operating envelope. Repeat with worst allowed harness routing. Log any stale sample, false valid value, false contact or unexpected latch reset. Commission fault injection on de-energized/limited-energy fixtures before full-power experiments.

Proposed design actions for review: minimize effective RTD loop area, keep sense/force return geometry controlled and away from fast-node copper, route contact detection independently, consider matched differential RC and shield termination without creating a conducting loop near the coil. Do not add an unreviewed closed metal shield or change protection filters without checking self-heating, leakage and fault latency. These are candidates pending circuit/layout and instrument evidence.

## Phase B: cap and spatial temperature

Determine cap parasitic heating from measured cap/pan/body trajectories with coil-off calibrated conductances and coil-on independent cap temperature, keeping pressure and geometry fixed. Use several fields/frequencies and thermal conditions. A quadratic fit versus field is evidence only within tested conditions; extrapolating to unknown cookware is not qualification. A reduced field model at normal frequency requires 3-D coil/ferrite/pan geometry, complex temperature-dependent permeability/conductivity of all metals and validated field boundary conditions. Obtain manufactured cap magnetic response (including seam, forming and welding) first. Slotting the skirt may reduce circumferential currents but changes hygiene, ingress and mechanics; no slot is recommended for release here.

Map pan center plus radii15/30/45/60/75/87mm at8 azimuths for a nominal180mm support pan, with denser samples around the measured hot annulus. Scale map to each actual pan; capture full-area IR images when an optical path is valid. Record top vs bottom separately: polished/reflective cookware makes uncorrected IR misleading. Calibrate emissivity targets against contact/fiber references, quantify their effect on food/contact and heating, and account for occlusion. A pan-top map cannot certify bottom or aperture temperatures. For filled pans log load mass, material and stirring; for empty conditions use the approved abnormal-operation envelope.

Test representative steel/cast iron/clad pans across diameter, measured flatness and offsets, dry/loaded states, heating ramps and hold levels within the allowed envelope. Track `max(T_pan(x,y)) − T_sensor`, not just center error. Instrument glass rim at multiple azimuths, cap bond/lead exit and hottest seal location; combine with thermal expansion/fit observations. No single center RTD can establish whole-pan maximum without a proven cookware/field-dependent bound.

`results/synthetic_hotspot_fixture.csv` is a wire-format and analysis rehearsal: unchanged prior radial finite-volume kernel, imposed500W radius30–75mm annulus,40cells,dt0.1s,120s,pan/glass h200 W/m²K,3mm pan,4mm glass,9mm aperture radius. It repeats the axisymmetric values at8 angles; it **cannot** represent azimuthal hotspots, actual coil geometry, temperature through pan thickness, seal gradients, or the revised selected cap. The sensor node is the prior study's default cap network. A result here is not physical validation or an updated candidate forecast.

## Phase C: cleaning, leakage and endurance records

Define product service profile and approved limits before testing: use temperature range, cycles/dwell/ramp, spring stroke/count, cookware drop/slide impacts, allowed cleaning agents/concentration/pH/temperature/exposure, rinse/dry sequence, spill fluid/volume/head/duration, accepted corrosion and ingress, accessible-part electrical test network and supply/PE states. Do not borrow the fixture's example1000/500cycles or leakage values as product specifications.

Use traced cartridges across at least the process/material/tolerance corners selected by reliability engineering; one prototype cannot establish field reliability. Store lot, cure schedule/bond thickness, cap finish/permeability and seal compression. Measure baseline force/stroke/return/rocking/error/leakage, apply defined combined sequences (hot spill → cool/dry, cleaning residue → thermal cycling, force cycling → hot spill), and repeat interim/final measurements. Preserve failures, rework history and censored tests. Inspect lead strain, bond cracks, seal abrasion/compression set, corrosion/contamination and glass aperture damage. Use separate fresh controls to distinguish aging from measurement drift.

The endurance summary requires eight check IDs; values alone are insufficient without record IDs pointing to the actual raw traces/photos/procedures. `water_ingress=0` requires a stated detection limit included in uncertainty. `corrosion_after=0` needs documented magnification/locations/criteria. Electrical tests require approved test methods; passing resistance alone does not establish insulation integrity, dielectric withstand or touch-current compliance. All configured numerical examples are explicitly SYNTHETIC. Missing measurements or approved requirements cause rejection, not a pass.

## Fault and inhibition matrix

| Stimulus / condition | Observable mechanism | Required response / independent observation | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Differential magnetic pickup at RTD | Lead-loop emf; plausible in-range false temperature | Bound paired error with independent reference; inhibit invalid/stale measurement per control contract | Prepared only |
| Common-mode switching injection | Converter/protection rectification, SPI corruption, brownout | Capture raw fault/status/DRDY and supply; no false healthy persistence or automatic fault clearing | Prepared only |
| RTD open/short during switching | Electrical out-of-window diagnosis may be delayed by filter | Observe hardware latch, firmware diagnosis, PERMIT then actual current extinction; include stored tank energy | Legacy dual-path documented; new120V complete integration unverified |
| Hot cap loses pan contact | RTD remains plausible/hot | Independent contact indication must dominate enable; record contact event to current-extinction latency | Contact workstream supplies implementation/test preparation |
| Contact channel EMI/stuck state | False-present or false-loss | Fault injection and cross-check with force/stroke reference; no restart solely because noise clears | Prepared only |
| Coil/burst off with hot pan | No coil current while heat remains | Continue truthful temperature/contact monitoring; no stale sample interpreted as cooling | Prepared only |
| Conductive spill reaches probe | Leakage paths, seal friction, electrical/contact shorts | Electrical safety test and contact force/return after spill; no reliance on glass aperture as intact original barrier | No physical evidence |
| Pan offset/local hotspot | Center reads below off-axis maximum | Spatial peak bound and approved overtemperature protection | Synthetic radial sensitivity only |
| Spring/seal/bond aging | Sticking, altered force/lag, creeping conductive path | Combined stress + post mechanical/thermal/electrical evidence, trace to lot | NOT_RUN |

No fixture should bypass the dominant interlock for convenience. The new120V power concept exposes PERMIT on J4pin9 (low/floating/unpowered disables), and BUS_OCP_OK on pin10 per its README. This names the measurement interface; it does not prove a built interlock path. The legacy electrical RTD window circuit does not independently prove mechanical pan contact. Integration with the contact workstream must retain artifact identity and bound filtering, detection, latch, driver, tank-current decay and power-fault behavior separately.
