# Bond and lead assembly preparation — NOT_RUN

Drawing intent PR2-BL. Dimensions below are proposed process/inspection targets unless attributed in SOURCES. No manufacturing capability or life qualification is asserted.

1. **Incoming inspection.** Preserve part and lot identity; photograph the RTD and lead exits under magnification. M222 maximum envelope is 2.5 × 2.3 × 1.2 mm. IST maximum substrate is 1.8 × 1.4 × 0.35 mm, with fixing-drop total height up to 0.9 mm. Obtain clarification of the IST 154366 A/B order-table conflict and compatibility with Resbond before approving substitution. Measure the cap roof and bonding-face flatness. Do not distort the roof to close a bonding fixture.

2. **Bond coupons.** Prepare identified 316L discs, Ø8 × 0.15 mm, matching the PR2 face. Orient the bare ceramic back toward the cap, leaving the passivated meander and fixing drop on the free face; supplier confirmation of received-part orientation is required. Use a 2.7 × 2.5 mm M222 bond footprint or 2.0 × 1.6 mm IST footprint; thermal conduction uses the actual smaller chip face. Prepare three specimens at each proposed 0.075 / 0.10 / 0.15 mm bond thickness. Control thickness with calibrated external fixture stops. Sacrifice one specimen per process point for center/edge thickness, void and particle-bridge inspection. Do not load the fixing drop. Follow current batch mixing/cure instructions and record actual time and temperature; this specification does not invent a substitute cure.

3. **Dielectric plate coupons.** Request custom 2.8 × 2.8 × 0.25 mm AD96 alumina or a specified AlN grade, with rounded corners and inspected edges. Use two separately measured 0.075 mm bond films. Verify installed insulation after reviewed thermal/wet stresses at electrical-test levels derived from requirements. Catalog V/mil is not an assembled barrier rating; plate edges and contamination remain part of the system.

4. **Kelvin transitions.** Prepare four Omega TFCP-003-100, 40 AWG PFA copper wires, marked current+, sense+, current− and sense−. A proposed resistance/laser-weld coupon uses 0.5 mm stripped overlap at 1.0 ±0.2 mm from each native Ø0.2 mm Ni/Pt lead's body exit. Preserve factory strain relief. The two extension joins on a terminal remain electrically separate until that terminal. Two native stubs are still shared by sense and current: this is not true four-pad Kelvin access at the Pt film. Welding energy, tooling, pull criteria and joint cross sections require process qualification. Standard tin soft solder is not selected for 250°C service. No weld has been made.

5. **Calibration datum.** Moving M222's connection from the catalog 8 mm-from-body datum to 1 mm changes the resistance included in its nominal rating. IST's general datum is 5 mm from the wire end, equivalent to 5 mm from the body on a 10 mm lead. Calibrate the installed assembly at its actual joins; do not claim unchanged Class A interchangeability. Two 1 mm Ni stubs model 4.393 mΩ at 20°C, about 0.0113°C if uncompensated. At 250°C an explicitly assumed Ni resistivity factor of 2.38 gives about 0.0289°C. The corresponding 8 mm hot term is about 0.231°C. These are residual-conductor estimates, not the actual factory trim error. Obtain Ni/Pt lead R(T) or measure it; do not turn the assumed linear factor into firmware compensation.

6. **Join isolation.** Reserve two 3 × 1 × 1 mm pockets with smooth ceramic edges and a slotted cover over exposed joins. Keep joins clear of the cap and opposite terminal throughout the stroke; visible air space alone is not an insulation qualification. Continue PFA to the stripped weld zone. Support native leads while forming them once; do not bend the fixing drop or use stiff native Ni as the cyclic flexure. Exact forming limits need supplier review.

7. **Flexible route.** Four nominal OD 0.232 mm wires pass through the Ø2.8 mm central bore as identified pairs. Reserve 0.30 mm per wire until measured. Provide a 60 mm developed S-loop, initially with bend radius at least 2 mm, in approximately 8 × 12 mm of downstream body space. These are coupon proposals, not proven fatigue limits. Strain-relieve fixed and moving ends without cutting or crushing PFA. Each 1.2 mm stroke should change loop curvature without pulling the weld. Inspect full travel and measure added return force. The available mechanism force budget governs acceptance. Packing the wire against a cold ceramic or metal body creates an additional thermal shunt.

8. **Thermal guard experiment.** The baseline retains conservative copper-fin loss. A reflective shield or warm cavity is only an experiment: measure its temperature profile, anchor heat flow and added mass, then rerun the assembled model. Thermally joining wires to the hot cap transfers their load to the cap rather than eliminating it.

9. **Prepared validation.** Inspect bonds and welds; compare two-wire and four-wire resistance at calibrated reference points; reverse current on the bench to expose thermoelectric offsets; compare 0.3 and 1 mA only within reviewed self-heating limits. Measure lead resistance, insulation and force before/after flex and thermal cycles. Run the separate bench-validation protocol on complete cartridges and preserve routing photographs. All results remain NOT_RUN. Three coupons do not establish lot capability or life.

The 260°C PFA rating provides only 10°C nominal headroom above a 250°C target. It does not cover overshoot, cap self-heating or join chemistry. Future physical experiments must remain within the reviewed complete-assembly temperature and use independent limiting. This package applies no heat.
