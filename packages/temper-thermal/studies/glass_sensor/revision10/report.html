Retention changes the heat path

The bracket contact is open at rest and closes at the capture stop. Once engaged, heat can pass through the bracket foot directly and through the screw route. The sensitivity model treats their contact conductances as unknowns.

No new complete-cartridge response claim: mass changes, gap transfer and induction self-heating remain outside this calculation.

The seal alternative is conditional

A drained wet well above a stationary sealed electronics bulkhead could avoid a moving membrane. It changes the cleaning and ingress architecture, exposes guides and fasteners to contamination, and has no qualified parts or CAD. It is a comparison experiment to assess—not the selected cooker design. A blocked drain and flooded well must remain contained.
