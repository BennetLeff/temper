# P9 and full-lead M222 engineering traveler (blank)

All operations here are **planned / NOT_RUN**. Use one `article-template.json` copy per physical article and append observation rows to `observations-template.csv`; preserve raw files under the identifiers recorded there. Do not replace missing measurements with nominal CAD values. A reviewer supplies a signed requirement record before assigning pass/fail or enabling a later stage.

## 0. Freeze the run definition before dispensing

Record build ID, intended specimen count and article IDs, CAD/STEP byte hashes, drawing revision, cap material/finish/lot, alumina tile vendor/grade/lot and dimensions, exact adhesive product **standard Ceramabond 569** (no VFG substitution), received adhesive lot/expiry/storage and label/TDS revision. Capture the currently received mixing, surface preparation and cure instructions; stop for engineering review if they conflict with the Aremco 25F candidate schedule. Aremco calls for cleaning/preparation of smooth surfaces, thorough mixing of one-part products, even clamping, excess removal and a controlled ramp; **the exact chosen D6 surface treatment must be documented and reviewed** because abrading/oxidizing can change cap thickness/finish and the thermal interface. No default dilution or permanent shims. [Aremco 25F pp. 10–11](https://www.aremco.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/A0-Catalog-25F.pdf).

Record the external height fixture drawing/revision, flatness reference, separation measurement method, contact pressure/holding method, clean/dry conditions, oven profile, thermocouple placement, instrument calibration and imaging/sectioning resolution. For each characteristic, enter `requirement_id`, `source`, `acceptance_rule`, `instrument_capability` and reviewer. If no rule exists, mark `CHARACTERIZATION_ONLY` or `HOLD_PENDING_REQUIREMENT`; never infer a production pass from clean-looking photos.

## 1. Incoming inspection: P9 process witnesses

1. Assign cap, tile and adhesive lot to each **P9-569-OPEN-100** or **P9-569-OPEN-150** article. Read the R9 STEP part names and nominal gaps, then verify actual cap roof and tile dimensions with calibrated metrology. Record multi-point roof flatness/roughness as measured values, not a substitute catalog finish.
2. Image/label both tile faces and cap underside. Record any incoming chip, crack, contamination or cap deformation with photo IDs. Record cleaning and surface treatment actually performed, operator, elapsed time before dispense and any deviations.
3. Record the fixture open/closed height before adhesive, adhesive mixing method/time and measured dispense mass if a scale is capable. The R9 CAD rectangular bond volumes are ideal nominal geometry; **dispense mass cannot be calculated from uncured specific gravity as a guaranteed cured thickness**.

## 2. Dispense and cure: P9 process witnesses

1. Apply/hold the selected candidate per approved received instruction. Place the bare tile on the centered nominal 2.7 × 2.5 mm pad. Use only a removable external height-control fixture. Record target **0.100 or 0.150 mm**, actual pre-cure separated height, parallelism, pressure/holding setup, squeeze-out and optical coverage. Keep both faces accessible for inspection where practical.
2. Record full temperature-vs-time data at the **specimen**, including air-set start/end, ramp, 200 °F (~93 °C) 2 h candidate dwell and cooldown. Aremco's general guide recommends around 5 °F/min (~2.8 °C/min) ramp and warns that blistering can be caused by rapid water release. A profile entered in this traveler is a planned candidate, not evidence it occurred or that it completes all 250 °C chemistry. Do not claim the guide's 700 °F-conditioned moisture result for this profile. [Aremco 25F pp. 8–11](https://www.aremco.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/A0-Catalog-25F.pdf).
3. After cooldown, measure a **cured thickness map** at declared locations with uncertainty; compare measured geometry to fixture height. Image perimeter and any visible blisters, cracks or incomplete wetting. Keep one article or paired sacrificial witness for sectioning; map section location and actual void/crack morphology. Record whether the article is only an early process falsification sample or part of a predefined repeatability campaign.
4. If a separate *dry* hot exposure is authorized for the material witness, freeze temperature/time/cycles and instrumentation before starting. Re-measure thickness, surface and section after exposure. The bare alumina article has no RTD and cannot establish sensor leakage. A later wet/cleaning condition needs its own reviewed specimen/electrode boundary and requirement; do not infer it from the catalog moisture line.

**P9 gate:** wrong identity, untraceable actual temperature, invalid height metrology or unreviewed surface/adhesive substitution puts the result on `HOLD_DATA_INVALID`. Cracking/voiding/flow are observations requiring engineering disposition; there is no invented permissible percentage. Promotion to a *sacrificial live-element experiment* needs a face identification, a credible observed thickness and measurement method, and predefined electrical damage/leakage observations. One P9 witness cannot establish process capability or RTD chemistry. A failed 100 µm witness does not automatically promote 150 µm as the production geometry.

## 3. Incoming live M222: only after P9 and face decisions

1. Identify exact YAGEO **32208550**, lot, packaging revision and two individual native leads. Measure body and lead lengths without straightening a factory V-shape merely to fit CAD. Photograph both faces, lead exits and fixing drop. Keep at least one same-lot sensor untouched as a metrology/process control.
2. Record the manufacturer's or reviewed cross-section basis for the intended **bonding face** and which features (active glass, fixing drop, lead welds) adhesive may contact. If this remains unknown, stop before sensor bonding. No R7 1 mm trim, near-body bend, local weld, cover or moving cartridge is part of this article.
3. Before bonding, collect four-wire resistance at a controlled reference temperature. Measure both voltage and current contact coordinates from photographed body edges. First preference is repeatable voltage contacts at the supplier's **8 mm from body** nominal-resistance datum, with distal current contacts if feasible. If that setup is mechanically impractical, select and freeze other safe contacts and label the dataset `ASSEMBLY_DATUM` rather than a catalog Class A check. Record contact pressure/tool, excitation, current-reversal or zero-current EMF observation, dwell/stabilization and raw instrument output. Test the untouched control using the same datum/setup. [M222 v03 pp. 1–2](https://yageogroup.com/content/datasheet/asset/file/YAGEO_Nexensos_M222_Datasheet_EN).

## 4. Conditional open M222 bond and electrical checks

1. Use the **same documented cap, 569 lot, external gap fixture, surface preparation and cure profile** selected by P9. Mount the M222 only on a reviewed face; keep full native leads free and supported outside the D6 cap. Treat this article as a sacrificial chemical/process experiment until exact-RTD compatibility is demonstrated. Record lead path, anchor/support and force on each lead so fixture forces are not silently attributed to adhesive or sensor damage.
2. Repeat four-wire measurements using the **same per-lead voltage contact coordinates, temperature and excitation** after placement, after air set, after oven cooldown and after any separately approved dry hot exposure. Maintain an untreated same-lot sensor on the same metrology schedule. If contact geometry shifts, record both coordinates and do not subtract readings as pure process drift.
3. At each stage, record continuity, two-lead symmetry, resistance-to-cap and leakage under a **reviewed voltage/current limit and temperature**; never carry over the bare M222 data sheet's 20 °C/500 °C element insulation numbers as installed-assembly acceptance. Obtain hot leakage only with a rated fixture and electrical review. Source the criterion from the actual front end, protective isolation and appliance requirement.
4. Image the sensor face, lead fixation and bond perimeter before and after cure. Section a sacrificial matching article only after electrical characterization if the process is to advance. Record observed chemistry, glass cracks, wetting, gap and lead-fixation damage. A bare-alumina section cannot replace this evidence.

**M222 gate:** advance to a fully enclosed successor only after engineering has accepted exact face compatibility, full-lead electrical stability against the untouched control, bond geometry, hot isolation and a lead-support/join plan with requirements. The R9 P9 STEP does not contain an RTD. A later enclosed design requires fresh CAD and thermal/force modeling for any new full-length lead route, joining and cover.

## 5. Review and archive

For each article, tie every image, time trace, metrology file and resistance run to article ID, operation, instrument ID/calibration, operator, date and source hash. Preserve unfiltered raw values and note failed measurements. The reviewer records `CHARACTERIZATION_ONLY`, `HOLD_DATA_INVALID`, `PROCESS_CANDIDATE_FOR_NEXT_STAGE` or `REJECTED_WITH_CAUSE`, with evidence and requirement IDs. These are engineering dispositions; none means production qualification.

Do not issue a response-time or thermal-underread number from these process specimens. A complete thermal cartridge must be measured under the separate R7 calibration protocol, with its own geometry and material property record.
