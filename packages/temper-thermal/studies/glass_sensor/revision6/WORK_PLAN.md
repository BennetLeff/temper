# R6 implementation and verification scope

The user approved exploration of all four state-of-the-art directions, with Astra subagents and simulation/test preparation only. This stage answers what can usefully advance into a measured cartridge experiment.

1. Contact: reuse immutable R5 physics and CAD scalar export; compare M222, IST308 and clearly conditional 161 geometry with consistent capacities, bond and lead assumptions. Preserve baseline parity and report sensitivity/convergence.
2. Estimation: compare raw, steady correction, bandlimited lead and a model bank against a distinct synthetic plant, including holdout parameters and misleading contact/freshness inputs. Do not issue heating commands or claim hardware accuracy.
3. Seal: calculate pressure and stiffness feasibility envelopes, including allocation competition. Do not infer membrane qualification from temperature ratings or hardness.
4. Optics: implement spectral radiation balances with filter/glass self-emission, inversion, emissivity and background sensitivities. Invented spectra must remain visibly hypothetical; no detector or installed t90 claim.
5. Integration: preserve R5 and the primary checkout; reproducible standalone Rust runners, tests, negative checks, source provenance and a readable report. Review code and scientific claims before committing locally.

Completion is a reviewable in-box comparison and an explicit evidence queue, not physical qualification, manufacturing CAD for unmodeled alternatives, controller release, purchasing or publication.
