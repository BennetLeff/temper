# Rev C PCB layout and assembly constraints

Canonical board: [`cad/differential-probe.kicad_pcb`](../cad/differential-probe.kicad_pcb). This is a fully routed engineering prototype for the stage-53 probe. It is not a released or voltage-qualified instrument. Reasoning and Rev B comparisons are in the [Rev C record](revc.md). The Rev B layout notes are in git at `ca79d882f` and in [layout-revb-handoff.md](layout-revb-handoff.md).

## Board and layers

| Item | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Outline | 150 × 70 mm, 3 mm corner chamfers. A 1.6 mm inter-leg notch, open at the tip-end edge, runs on y = 35 mm to x = 36 mm. |
| Board | Four copper layers, nominal 1.6 mm. SMD on top. The two 9 V holders mount on the bottom. |
| F.Cu | HV ladders and nearly all signal routing |
| In1.Cu | GND plane, x ≥ 59 mm; no signal tracks |
| In2.Cu | +4.8 V pour; signal hops for BUF_P, RAW_OUT, OV_N, OUT_LIM_N and LIM_P |
| B.Cu | −4.8 V pour; BUF_N, LIM_N, SELECTED and BAT_P hops |
| Plane boundary | x = 59 mm. No copper fill, hidden-layer tracks or vias at x < 58 mm on any layer. |
| Signal tracks / vias | 0.25 mm (0.3 mm rail stubs); 0.6 mm via diameter, 0.3 mm drill |
| General clearance | 0.20 mm; explicit HV rules override it |
| Fine-pitch exception | U6 pad-to-pad only: 0.15 mm, matching the selected VSSOP land pattern |
| Slots | Ten non-plated 1.2 × 8 mm cutouts, one under each HV capacitor, plus the notch |
| Assembly | 116 circuit footprints (103 SMD, 13 THT) plus the copper jumper JP1, two HV landings, four mounting holes |
| Reference designators | F.Fab / B.Fab layers; silk carries outlines and board text only |

The fabricator stackup, laminate CTI, copper weight and finish are not yet selected, and there is no controlled-impedance claim. Keep the In1 ground reference directly below F.Cu. Changing the dielectric thickness or copper geometry requires a new compensation/CMRR assessment.

The slots are rectangular design envelopes; a 1.2 mm router leaves about 0.6 mm internal corner radii. Review the fabricator's routed-slot drawing. Solder mask is not credited as insulation, and no solder, coating or adhesive may bridge a cut-out.

## Divider symmetry

The legs are **mirror images about y = 35 mm**:

- R1–R5 mirror R21–R25.
- C1–C5 mirror C21–C25.
- The low-leg cells mirror each other: R11/R31, R12/R32, C41–C43/C51–C53, RV1/RV2, CT1/CT2 and D1/D2.
- The tip landings W_HV1/W_HV2 mirror each other.

Resistor rows face each other 16 mm apart, and capacitor rows sit 7 mm outboard. The leg pitch, between each leg's resistor/capacitor centroids, is 23 mm.

The native-geometry audit compares 19 footprint pairs and 39 pad pairs, and finds them exact mirror images. A SOT-23 clamp swaps pins 1 and 2 under the mirror, which is expected. The audit also finds identical mirrored track geometry for all seven paired divider/tap/trim nets.

All ten elevated ladder nodes are on F.Cu, with no vias.

Two signal pairs are matched in length and via count:

| Path | Length, each leg |
|---|---|
| Tap to buffer input | 14.478 mm |
| Buffer output to subtractor input resistor | 7.565 mm |

The N-side input jogs to absorb U1's 1.27 mm pin offset.

This matching covers the input sections and the first stage only. The comparator taps, U3's internal channel layout and the enclosure boundary still contribute to the measured CMRR.

## High-voltage spacing budget

The rules in [`differential-probe.kicad_dru`](../cad/differential-probe.kicad_dru) are generated by `Copper::rules()` in `tools/layout.rs`. They use the extended ±3 kV per-tip target and assume intended R/C voltage sharing in intact legs. They are engineering constraints, not an IEC 61010 determination.

**Ranks.** Each 2 MΩ section is one rank of 600 V; the tip is rank 5.

**Pairing rules.**

- Two nodes in the same leg are spaced by their rank difference.
- Opposite-leg nodes are spaced by the **larger** rank, under `|V+| ≤ 3 kV`, `|V−| ≤ 3 kV` and `|V+ − V−| ≤ 3 kV` together. `check_pcb.rs` independently maximizes every rank pair over the six vertices of that envelope.
- A ladder node versus any LV net is spaced by its own rank.

| Pair | Design voltage | Minimum air clearance | Minimum PCB creepage |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Adjacent ladder nodes (across one section) | 600 V | 2 mm | 4 mm |
| Tip to low-voltage/earth net | 3 kV | 10 mm | 20 mm |
| Opposite-leg nodes, within the combined envelope | up to 3 kV | up to 10 mm | up to 20 mm |

How each section meets the 4 mm creepage rule:

- **2512 resistors.** The pads are 4.70 mm apart in a straight line, which meets 4 mm without help.
- **1812 capacitors.** The pads are 2.70 mm apart, so a routed slot is required.
- **Facing tip pads.** They are 12.65 mm apart in air. The notch provides their 20 mm creepage.

`make pcb-oracle` checks that each rule is active. Opposing +3 kV and −3 kV tips are outside this envelope.

## Enclosure, shield and mounting

**Enclosure:** owner-designed, earthed and bonded to the BNC shell and scope earth. It must provide a fixed earthed shield (per-node strays stable to about 0.01 pF for CMRR), ≥ 20 mm from exposed HV conductors to metal, the tip-end insulating feedthrough and strain relief, the 22.0 mm standoff height below, and panel access for the two BNCs, toggles, reset button and trimmer tools; the full list is in [revc.md §9](revc.md#9-size-layers-enclosure). The Hammond 1455N2201 is the reference geometry used by the 3-D setback calculation there and by `tools/leads.rs`.

**Mounting.** Put the board on standoffs so its underside is 22.0 mm above the inner floor. The BNC and toggle faces sit at the output end plate. Keep every exposed HV conductor at least **20 mm from metal**, including W_HV lead loops (≤ 5.5 mm above the board) and trimmed lead tails (≤ 1 mm). Put an insulating sheet on the floor under the battery holders.

| Mount | Position, mm | Hardware |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | (131.0, 31.2) | GND, plated 3.2 mm; metal M3 standoff and screw (moved from the (145.5, 4.5) corner for the trigger BNC J4) |
| H2 | (145.5, 65.5) | GND, plated 3.2 mm; metal M3 standoff and screw |
| H3 | (5, 5) | Unplated 3.2 mm; **nylon** standoff and **nylon** screw |
| H4 | (5, 65) | Unplated 3.2 mm; **nylon** standoff and **nylon** screw |

H3 and H4 sit on the two prongs on either side of the notch. A metal screw through a nylon spacer defeats the purpose of H3/H4. The H3/H4 nylon screws also clamp the arms of the printed lead bridge to the board top, through its 3 mm thickness, so they are 3 mm longer than the board alone needs ([HV leads](hv-leads.md#feedthroughs-and-strain-relief)).

**Lid holes.** Drill holes in the lid over CT1 (62, 23) and CT2 (62, 47), the RV1/RV2 adjustment screws, the RV3/RV4 zero-trim screws (x = 68.6 mm, y ≈ 10–20 / 50–60 mm), the SW3 reset button (131.85, 46.25) and the D9 LED (115, 24). The holes take the printed snap-in trimmer guides (Ø7.2 mm holes; `trim-guide-ct`, `trim-guide-rv`), which set the depth for the printed handle with its Vessel CA-18 zirconia blade. Keep the hole geometry fixed after calibration.

## Leads and panel components

**HV landings.** `W_HV1` and `W_HV2` are 4 mm solder pads with 1.6 mm plated holes for the captive HV leads. They are not a rated connector. The leads are generic 20 AWG silicone wire accepted by incoming hipot (rated fallbacks Rowe R800-1020 and Alpha Wire 392040, UL 3239 10 kV DC), entered from the top, with the insulation ending ≤ 1 mm above the pad and the tail ≤ 1.0 mm below the board. Each lead is tied to the printed lead bridge 11 mm before its joint, and leaves through a nylon gland in the printed tip-end wall insert. The design, budget and assembly steps are in [HV leads](hv-leads.md); the landings did not change.

**Panel components.** The output BNC (J3), trigger BNC (J4), range switch (SW1) and power switch (SW2) are PCB-mounted. Their faces point at the output end plate. Along the panel from the y = 0 edge: J4 at y = 7.72, SW1 at 21.36, J3 at 35.0 and SW2 at 56.0 mm; SW3 is set back at (131.85, 46.25).

| Part | Panel cut-out | Axis height |
|---|---|---|
| BNC | D-hole Ø12.83 mm with an 11.7 mm flat; panel ≤ 5 mm | 8.6 mm above the board top |
| Toggles | 6.35 mm holes (1/4-40 bushing) | 6.35 mm above the board top |

**Reset button.** SW3 (C&K PTS645SL43-2 LFS, thru-hole) sits in the panel strip at (131.85, 46.25), between J3 and SW2, and is pressed through a lid hole; its actuator is 4.3 mm above the board top. It cannot sit over the LV band (x 71.3–126.6 mm), because its tails would hit the bottom-side holders; its bottom courtyard makes DRC enforce that. Four Ø1.0 mm holes on 6.5 × 4.5 mm. The two RESET pins are joined on F.Cu and fed by an In2 hop; the two +4V8 pins join the In2 pour. SW_P runs under it on B.Cu.

**Battery holders.** BT1 and BT2 (BH9VPC) mount on the underside. Their three 2.34 mm holes take 2-56 screws or eyelets. The screw heads land on top-side keep-outs, which are courtyard circles in the footprint.

BT1's contacts face the tap cell. BAT_P therefore runs on B.Cu along y = 27 mm to SW2. BT2's contacts face the panel.

**Before power-up,** check battery polarity against the footprint (pin 1 = +).

## Footprints and visual review

The HV capacitor footprint uses standard 1812 lands, with the body outline on F.Fab so it does not print across the slot.

The edge-part footprints are drawn from manufacturer dimensions. The BNC pin orientation and the E-Switch toggle axis height and bushing fit must be confirmed against samples (see [revc.md §7](revc.md#7-pcb-mount-panel-parts)).

The BNC, toggles and holders have no 3D model, so the render omits them.

## Verification and regeneration

Recorded with KiCad 10.0.4:

- **Schematic ERC:** zero violations.
- **Native PCB DRC** with `--all-track-errors` and the HV rules: zero violations, zero unconnected items, no exclusions. Warnings are included, and silk is included.
- **Schematic/PCB checks:** connectivity, value, footprint and DNP all pass.
- **Divider checks:** mirror symmetry and matched length pass.
- **Negative controls:**
  - A GND trace 6 mm from TIP_P fails the 10 mm rule.
  - Removing the C1 slot fails the 4 mm rule.
  - Closing the notch fails TIP_P–TIP_N 20 mm creepage.
  - Shortening the notch to x = 23 mm fails rank-4/rank-3 opposite-leg creepage.
- **Opposite-tip fixtures:** isolated pads pass at 20.5 mm and fail at 8 mm edge spacing.

No high-voltage, CMRR, bandwidth, thermal or recovery test has been run.

**Regeneration.**

- `make pcb` regenerates and checks everything.
- `make pcb-check` audits the saved board.
- `make pcb-oracle` builds known-bad fixtures in ignored `build/`.

Placement and every track are explicit in `tools/layout.rs`; no autorouter is used. The Python bridge only loads footprints, applies KiCad transforms, fills zones and exports geometry. Reconcile any manual KiCad edits into `tools/layout.rs` before running `make pcb-generate` again.
