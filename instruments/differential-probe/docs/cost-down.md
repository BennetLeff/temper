No stocked 2 W 2 MΩ chip resistor was found at JLC (2 W parts in stock are axial carbon or metal film, 500 V). Every stocked 3 kV 2512 is a 1 W, 1 %, 100 ppm/°C thick-film part, so the choice among them is availability. HV12 is stocked at JLC and needs no global sourcing.

**HV12 ratings** (Uni-Royal "HV" high-voltage thick-film chip resistor datasheet, as listed on Mouser): 1 W at 70 °C derating linearly to 0 at 155 °C; maximum working voltage 3000 V; maximum overload voltage 4000 V; dielectric withstanding voltage 500 V; ±100 ppm/°C; −55 to +155 °C. **Approval basis: none listed** (no UL recognition; the same as the incumbent Stackpole part, which also has no UL file). The voltage coefficient is not specified, as it was not for the incumbent. The protective function relies on the body count and creepage, which do not change.

### Margin costs (`validation/cost-down.txt` §1, §3, §4)

1. **Stress.** Every normal case stays inside the HV12 rating: the intact leg at 3 kV DC is 600 V and 0.18 W per body (18 % of P, 42 % of the 1414 V power-limited voltage); one shorted section at 3 kV is 28 %. The 2 + 3 split still gives two independent impedance bodies. **One case exceeds the rating:** at the ±3 kV *extension*, with the 3-body group bypassed and held at continuous DC, each of the two remaining bodies carries 1500 V and 1.125 W, which is 112.5 % of the 1 W rating (the HVCB ran at 56 %). The same fault at the ±1.5 kV guarantee is 28 %, and at a 3 kVpk sine it is 56 %. The 1500 V is under the 3000 V working and 4000 V overload ratings; the excess is thermal. The guaranteed envelope is unaffected; the extension loses its continuous-DC margin for that double condition (fault plus sustained 3 kV DC).
2. **DC CMRR drift with temperature.** After DC trim at one temperature, the worst-case leg-to-leg ratio drift from the datasheet TCR limits is 254 ppm/°C instead of 60.5 ppm/°C. A 10 °C change from the calibration temperature gives 51.9 dB DC CMRR instead of 64.4 dB; holding the 60 dB goal at DC needs the probe within about ±3.9 °C of its calibration temperature instead of ±16.5 °C. Above the 2.41 kHz divider crossover the capacitors set the ratio, and at 35 kHz / 60 kHz the same 10 °C gives 75.2 / 79.8 dB. These are TCR *limits*; same-reel parts usually track much better, but that is not specified, so the bench must measure it.
3. **Self-heating at 3 kV DC.** Estimated rise 7.6–15.3 K per body (θ from the two derating curves; an estimate, not a bound). If both legs heat equally, the ratio mismatch is up to 1528–3057 ppm (56.3–50.3 dB) instead of 382–764 ppm. At 1.5 kV DC it is 382–764 ppm (68.4–62.3 dB).
4. **AC ratio from 1 % sections.** With equal 33 pF the ±1 % section mismatch gives at most 6.59 ppm at 35 kHz and 3.86 ppm at 60 kHz (§5), negligible against the 5 % capacitors that per-leg compensation already absorbs.

The HVCB part stays a **drop-in upgrade**: same 2512 footprint, and the new trim covers a 0.1 % string (122.6–142.4 Ω needed, §2), so no other part changes. If the bench shows the DC CMRR drift is too large for the coil measurement, fit HVCB2512BDE2M00; refitting the old RNCF0603TKY4K99 + 50 Ω trim as well restores the previous TCR budget and trim resolution. No board change either way.

**This departs from a recorded decision.** The [Rev B build decision](revb-build-decision.md) kept the 0.1 % grade and cancelled a 0.1 %-versus-1 % experiment as not worth its cost for a one-off instrument. The cost-down replaces that experiment with calculated margins and three bench checks (temperature tracking, self-heating, trim settability), with the 0.1 % part as the fallback.

### At stage 53 (`validation/tradeoff-stage53.txt`, `tools/tradeoff_stage53.py`)

The margin costs above are stated at 3 kV DC. The probe's job is the stage-53 bank and coil at 35–60 kHz, so the trade is judged there, with worst-case datasheet TCRs and a 10 °C ambient change after trimming:

| Error at 35–60 kHz | HVCB 0.1 % | HV12 1 % | Shared by both: NP0/C0G capacitors |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Leg-to-leg ratio mismatch | 24–42 ppm | 102–174 ppm | 1,197–1,198 ppm |
| SW_B common-mode leak (0–170 V square), referred to input | 2.6–4.5 mV | 11–19 mV | 130 mV |
| Self-heating, design case 566 Vpk (3.2 mW per body) | ≤ 0.5 ppm | ≤ 1.9 ppm | — |
| Self-heating, OCP-trip fault 1.79 kVpk if held | ≤ 4.7 ppm | ≤ 18.7 ppm | — |

The HV12 adds 133 ppm (about 0.01 %, under 0.01°) at 35 kHz, about a tenth of what the capacitors' own ±30 ppm/°C allowance can already contribute. Its larger penalties are DC-only: 52 dB instead of 64 dB DC rejection, and a 215 mV instead of 51 mV DC offset from SW_B's 85 V average, neither of which enters the AC bank or coil reading. **The $238 saving is worth it for this probe's purpose.** Fit the HVCB drop-in if the probe is later wanted as a precision DC instrument.

The same calculation shows that the capacitor allowance alone permits 58.4 dB leg-to-leg rejection after a 10 °C change, below the 60 dB goal. That is independent of the cost-down (KEMET and Yageo share the 0 ± 30 ppm/°C limit); parts from one reel normally track far closer, and the bench temperature-tracking item measures it.

## New bench items

1. **DC CMRR versus temperature** with HV12 sections: trim at room temperature, then measure the DC ratio of each leg after a 10 °C ambient change and after a 30-minute warm-up. Accept if CMRR at DC stays ≥ 60 dB over the operating range; otherwise fit HVCB2512BDE2M00 (drop-in).
2. **Self-heating at 3 kV DC** on one leg: ratio shift over 10 minutes.
3. **500 Ω trimmer settability**: can the DC ratio be set and held within 100 ppm?
4. **E-Switch fit**: panel hole, bushing length and flange against the 1.5 mm output end plate; axis height against the BNC.
5. **SW1 break-before-make**: scope WIDE_SEL and FINE_SEL during a toggle.
6. **Yageo 1812 thickness** against the slot footprint on the first assembled board.
