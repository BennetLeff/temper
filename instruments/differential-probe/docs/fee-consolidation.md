# Rev C JLC fee consolidation (2026-10-20)

Scope: after the [cost-down](cost-down.md) and the thru-hole reset button, strategy E (Economic, owner solders THT, JLC global-sources what it does not stock) still placed **23 Extended MPNs**, each with a $3.07 feeder fee per order. This pass asks, part by part, whether a Basic part, or an MPN already on the board, can take that position without losing a stated tolerance, rating or margin. Nothing was ordered, carted or quoted from an account.

**Result:** two changes adopted, F3 (output filter) and L1 (warning ladder 1 k). They remove three Extended MPNs (23 → 20) and save **$9.74** for two boards. Both are value and MPN changes on existing 0805 footprints, so the layout is unchanged. Every other candidate is either rejected on a rating or deferred because its margin trade is larger than its saving.

## Series and parallel copies do not tighten a bound

The question was whether 30 k = 3 × 10 k (L4), 499 Ω = 1 k ‖ 1 k or 2 k = 1 k + 1 k (G3) buys back precision. Not on a datasheet basis. Each part is independently ±0.1 % and ±25 ppm/°C, and these parts carry no ratio-tracking specification. When every part can sit at the same limit, the combination sits there too, so the worst-case bound equals one part's. Only the statistical spread narrows, by about √n, and the budget does not use statistical spreads.

## Pre-existing budget gap found on the way

The warning budget's "≥ 98 % calibrated gain" condition (`validation/checks.txt`) covers the HV divider's TCR drift (≤ 0.76 % over −25 to 85 °C) but not the gain resistors' drift (0.060 % wide, 0.108 % fine with the RNCF parts). The margin is there (the condition needs 98.75 % and the fine path then needs about 98.86 %), but the check does not yet include it.
