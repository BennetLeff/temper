•   High current support: 400 mA (maximum)                             supports single supply (4.5 V to 44 V), dual supplies

•   1.8 V logic compatible inputs                                      VDD = 12 V, VSS = –5 V). The TMUX7234 supports

•   Rail-to-rail operation                                             (Sx) and drain (D) pins ranging from VSS to VDD.

                                                                       All logic control inputs support logic levels from

                                                                       1.8 V to VDD, ensuring both TTL and CMOS
