# Datasheet audit, Rev G100: relays and connectors (group `relays-connectors`)

Scope: K1–K4 (TQ2SA-L2-5V-Z), Q2–Q6 (AO3400A) with gate networks R74–R79 / R102–R103 / R108–R109, D5–D9 (1N4148WS),
VRLY (R101 1 Ω, C112 22 µF), J3 / J7 / J8 / J9 (SAMZO BNC-KYWE-295-W4-N), D4 (LTST-S270KFKT) with R12, R105 / R106 / R107.
Board: `cad/temper-lcr.kicad_pcb` / `cad/routing.json` / `build/pad-coordinates.json` on `study/lcr-rev-g100` (HEAD be071cbcf).
Read-only. Copper resistances below come from a Dijkstra pass over `cad/routing.json` (ρ_Cu at 60 °C, 35 µm outer / 17.5 µm inner,
segments split at T-junctions; script in the scratchpad `datasheet-audit-g100/rc/trunk.py`). It reproduces the regulator check's
110.5 mΩ trunk figure exactly, which validates it.

## 0. How the coil supply is built on G100 (traced)

USB_VBUS → U16 (TPS2553) pin 6 (54.5, 116.9) → **5V trunk**: 0.6 mm B.Cu down the west edge and along the south edge to x 92, then
1.0 mm B.Cu to x 99 (71.6 mΩ, shared with the switcher feed), then the 0.9 mm In2 riser at x 99 and 0.4 mm F.Cu up to R101.1
(99.7, 116.4). The sum is **110.5 mΩ from U16 to R101**. → R101 1 Ω → VRLY: C112 (103.3, 115.5) and D5–D9 cathodes, then 0.2 mm
copper (In2 x 100 and y 113.6) to every coil "+" pin (R101 → K1.1 279 mΩ, → K4.10 392 mΩ, → K4.1 420 mΩ). VRLY also feeds the
TLP172AM LED strings (R132 / R133, about 2 × 4.9 mA).

Coil "−" pins: RLY_RST (all four RESET coils, pin 6) → Q2 at (97.3, 121.6). K4.6 → Q2 is 93 mm and 268 mΩ of 0.2 mm copper.
Each SET− goes to its own AO3400A. D5 / D6 / D7 / D8 sit at the drivers, 50–90 mm from the coils; D9 and Q6 sit at K4.

## 2. Versus the Rev F audit (`validation/datasheet-audit-rev-f/relays-connectors.md`, README dispositions)

- **RC-01 (fixed by R101 2.2 → 1 Ω) is undone on G100.** Rev F computed 4.19 V at the coils from a 4.30 V rail *at R101*.
  - G100 adds 110.5 mΩ of trunk between U16 and R101 (−46 mV): U16 moved north and the trunk narrowed from Rev F's 2.0 mm B.Cu strip to 0.6 / 1.0 mm.
  - It adds the TLP172AM LED strings on VRLY (−10 mV).
  - The budget also omitted the VRLY / RLY_RST copper and the FET, both present on Rev F too (−30 mV).
  - The result is about 4.10 V, Rev F's pre-fix figure (RCG-01).
- **RC-01's Fig. 9 reading (+8 % at 40 °C)** reads more like +6 % on the plot. The Panasonic guidelines' 0.4 %/°C gives +8 %. I bracket both. Either way the zero-margin temperature is 44–48 °C.
- **Note *2 (4.5 V coil):** Rev F did not raise it; `rev-e-range-switching.md` §3.2 dismissed it with a BJT-drop argument that does not hold for this board (RCG-02). The 4.5 V-Z part is unstocked.
- **RC-04 said "K4 latched closed only puts 1 kohm across the jacks (harmless)".** On Rev G it also shorts HCUR to HPOT. More importantly, it exposes the 0.1 % R107 to a charged DUT while unpowered, with the protection unable to isolate it, because K4 and R107 are on the jack side (RCG-05).
- **RC-06 said a kick in the unused coil "is clamped by D5 / the Q body diode".** Only one polarity is. A SET release drives reset-direction current into the other relays through the shared RESET bus (RCG-06). Rev F missed this sneak path.
- **RC-08 (RLY_RST couples relays' coil capacitances):** now also reaches the jack side through K4. It stays second-order. The suggested DNP 1 nF on RLY_RST at Q2 was not added on F, G or G100.
- **RC-11:** the drawing gives legs Ø1.14 and centre pin Ø1.0. Rev F wrote "1.4 mm holes on 1.0 mm legs". The centre-pin tolerance stack is a new note (RCG-12).
- **RC-12 (no jack protection, MISSING):** resolved by Rev G's network. The relay contact check against it is RCG-04, all fine.
- **RC-02, 03, 05, 07, 09, 10:** unchanged on G100 and re-verified: pulses 20 ms against ≥ 10 ms; coil polarity and set / reset coil identity against the 2-coil schematic; the land pattern (pads 1 × 2.94, 9.56 c-c); gate drive; wetting; single reflow; CPL rotations K1–K3 270°, K4 90°, still for the JLC preview.
- **Not in Rev F's scope, new here:**
  - R105 / R106 / R107 (on Rev F they were in the AFE audit, on the board side);
  - the drive-budget gap (RCG-03);
  - stock (RCG-15).
