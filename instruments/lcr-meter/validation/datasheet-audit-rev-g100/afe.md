# Datasheet audit, group AFE, LCR meter Rev G100 (2026-11-09)

Scope: U5 OPA197 (source difference stage), U6 OPA2810 (A: source driver, B: HIGH/HPOT follower), U7 OPA2810 (A: TIA, B: LOW/LPOT follower) and every passive in their networks. The Rev G protection parts on the AFE input nodes are treated as loads: D25 to D27 H3V3LD5B (DUT_HI, HPOT, LPOT), D28 BAV99 (DUT_LO), C119 / C120 1 nF C0G (HPOT, LPOT), R122 to R125 (10 Ω CUR, 100 Ω POT). The protection network itself belongs to another auditor.

## Topology as built (G100 netlist)

- **Source.** DAC_P / DAC_M go through R30 / R31 (2.2 kΩ) into U5, a difference stage with R32 / R33 = 2 kΩ, so G = 0.909. Two 560 pF caps in series (C34 + C114, C35 + C115) sit across each 2 kΩ. U5 drives EXCITE.
- **Source driver.** U6A is a follower: pin 2 is tied to pin 1 (SRC_DRV). It drives R104 (47 Ω), then SOURCE_BUF, then K1 / K2 / K3 pole 1, then RG (R40 100 Ω, R41 5 kΩ, R42 100 kΩ) to DUT_HI. From DUT_HI the path runs through R122 (10 Ω), ISO_HCUR, U18 TLP172AM and JK_HCUR.
- **HIGH follower.** U6B: +IN is HPOT, pins 6 and 7 are tied together, and the output is HIGH_BUF. HPOT carries C119 (1 nF) and D26 to GND, and R123 (100 Ω) to ISO_HPOT.
- **TIA.** U7A: +IN is GND and −IN is TIA_SUM. TIA_SUM connects to R94 (22 Ω) toward DUT_LO, to C104 (100 pF) toward TIA_OUT, and to R43 / R44 / R45, which reach TIA_OUT through K1 / K2 / K3 pole 2. DUT_LO carries C78 (100 pF) and D28 (BAV99 pin 3; pins 1 and 2 to GND, so the pair is antiparallel), and R125 (10 Ω) to ISO_LCUR.
- **LOW follower.** U7B: +IN is LPOT and the output is LOW_BUF. LPOT carries C120 (1 nF), D27 and R124 (100 Ω).
- **Rails.** The rails come from G100 `validation/rev-g100/regulator-check.json`: +12 V is 11.36 / 11.86 / 12.38 V and −12 V is −11.55 / −11.87 / −12.19 V. The raw rails reach +14.74 V and −14.81 V.
- **Firmware guard** (`firmware-rev-e/src/jackguard.h`): `JG_CLOSE_MV 1000` and `JG_TRIP_MV 3000`. The same thresholds apply to both the HCUR and the LCUR jack.

## 2. Versus the Rev F audit (`validation/datasheet-audit-rev-f/afe.md` and README dispositions)

**What Rev G and G100 changed:**
- **AFE-01 to AFE-04** (no protection) were deferred to Rev G. The Rev G network addresses the charged-capacitor cases: powered class A to 200 V and ESD class A to 30 kV.
  - **AFE-01's 100 Ω row persists for DC sources.** Rev F gave 40 mA at 0.9 V on DUT_LO. In G, R43 overheats from 1.05 V and U7A passes 40 mA from about 1.4 V on the jack, and the guard allows both up to 3.0 V indefinitely: G-AFE-01.
  - **AFE-02 is fixed.** The MISSING series R at the follower inputs is now R123 / R124, 100 Ω CRCW-HP.
  - **AFE-03 (HCUR back-drive) is fixed powered.** D25 clamps DUT_HI at 6 V or less. Unpowered, U6A's cells are now the limiting part at the brief's 50 V design case: G-AFE-02.
- **AFE-09 (blank firmware: the TIA integrates and DUT_LO floats to ±7 V) is resolved in G.** The jacks are isolated until the firmware closes U18 to U21, and the BAV99 holds DUT_LO within about ±0.5 V.
- **AFE-12 changed.** Rev F said only C78 could exceed 12 V. In G, C78 sits behind the BAV99 (≤ 1.25 V at 150 mA), and the new C119 / C120 see at most the second-stage clamp (≤ 12 V at 8 A, 50 V parts).
- **AFE-13 and AFE-10 are unchanged in G100.** The relayout kept the 10 nF caps at 1.65–1.85 mm. G100's rail corners are 11.36–12.38 V and 11.55–12.19 V; Rev F used the TCR-included 11.29–12.46 V.

**What the Rev F report missed or got wrong:**
- **AFE-13** read the 2.2–6.8 µF capacitors as optional ("allowed to be shared"). §11.1 says they "must also be used"; the board has only 1 µF X7R, below 0.6 µF effective at 12 V (G-AFE-05).
- **Footnote (3) of §7.1** (the differential limit is the lower of 7 V and VS) was not considered. It bears on every unpowered row, in Rev F's threshold table and in the G screen (G-AFE-04).
- **The threshold table** applied the 40 mA "continuous" output limit to the TIA without asking whether the overdrive lasts. For DC sources it does, and the R43 power limit (0.1 W, reached at 31.6 mA) comes before the 40 mA one.
- **The "things checked" list** says the followers and TIA see "hot-plug slew steps up to 7 V differential by design". That is true only when powered (§8.3.2).

**Rev F debug items, status in G100:**
- Done: the residual-voltage sense (item 6, now JACK_HC / JACK_LC), and the series R and clamps on the jacks (items 4 and 5).
- Still absent: the EXCITE pad (item 1) and an AFE-area GND pad (item 3). The nearest GND test point, TP2 at (103.1, 126.5), is about 47 mm from U7.

## 3. Debug and bring-up features (AFE)

1. **EXCITE test pad** at U5 pin 1 or R33 pad 1. OPA197 drives 1 nF and the probe adds about 10 pF. Cost: one pad. Risk: none.
2. **AFE-area GND pad** next to R94 / C78 (C78's GND via is 0.9 mm from its pad) and one near U6. Without them, scope grounds on the AFE are about 45 mm long. Cost: two pads on existing GND copper.
3. **Document the free probe points** (no copper):
   - SRC_DRV = R104 pad 1, SOURCE_BUF = R104 pad 2;
   - HIGH_BUF = R60 pad 1, LOW_BUF = R61 pad 1;
   - TIA_OUT = R67 pad 1. TIA_OUT carries about 12–15 pF already, and a 10× probe adds about 10 pF, which stays under the 35 pF CL;
   - DUT_LO = R94 pad 1. C78's 100 pF is already there;
   - the DUT current = a differential probe across R94 (8.9 mA = 196 mV).

   Never probe TIA_SUM (U7 pin 2) except with an active probe below 1 pF.
4. **DC-fault bench test** for G-AFE-01, no hardware:
   - setup: the 100 Ω range, switches closed, and a current-limited bench supply (100 mA) on LCUR stepped through 0.5 / 1.0 / 2.0 / 2.9 V;
   - read the TIA_OUT DC at R67 pad 1, and U7's case temperature with a thermocouple;
   - expected: −1.5 / −3.0 / −4.5 / −5.2 V. This confirms the firmware LCUR threshold once it is added.
5. **Optional DNP SOT-23 footprint** for a BAT54S from SOURCE_BUF to ±12 V (G-AFE-02b). Unfitted, it costs about 0.3 pF of pad on a 47 Ω-driven node; fitted, about 10 pF. It needs a screen rerun before fitting.
6. **Lift-to-measure parts, no 0 Ω links needed.** D25 to D28 and C119 / C120 are discrete. The bring-up plan should list lifting D28 and C120 to measure the BAV99 shunt and the LPOT diversion (G-AFE-07 and G-AFE-10) against a fitted board.
7. **Unpowered 8 kV contact ESD on LCUR and HCUR** on a sacrificial board, followed by an op-amp offset and leakage check (G-AFE-03).
