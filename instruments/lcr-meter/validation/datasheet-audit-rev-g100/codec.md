# Datasheet audit G100, group `codec`: U14 TAC5112, U15 AP2112K, D11-D14, codec networks

Board audited: `cad/temper-lcr.kicad_pcb` sha256 `59ca312c4e51af04…` (branch study/lcr-rev-g100, HEAD be071cbcf), netlist dump
`validation/datasheet-audit-rev-g100/parts.txt` / `nets.txt`. Copper was read with KiCad 10 `pcbnew` (read-only).
`validation/rev-g100/copper-delta-vs-g.json` confirms the 403 analog items are identical to Rev G/F, so the codec
region is the Rev F copper; the G100 changes near it are the power entry (U16 at 19 mm, U3 at 24 mm) and the
re-routed I2S lines to TP15-TP18.

Severity: VIOLATION / RISK / MISSING / OK-NOTE as in the brief. Pass 1 (own) is complete before pass 2 (Rev F report).

## 2. Versus the Rev F audit (`validation/datasheet-audit-rev-f/codec.md` and README dispositions)

Missed or wrong in Rev F:
- **C-03 sink argument (wrong at power-up).** "The codec's ~10-18 mA AVDD draw sinks it in under 1.5 ms, so 3V3A should not pump up" applies only while the codec runs. Firmware starts the codec after `rails_wait()`, so through the ±12 V ramp U14 draws 9 µA (SLASF24A p15). AFE-07's "codec load absorbs most of it" and the charged-DUT model's 1.5 mA floor have the same gap. New finding: **G-C1**.
- **CM bound only argued for CH1.** C-01 bounds the common mode with "H ≤ 1.7 V". CH2 is bounded too, because RG = RF per range (G-C3). Rev F did not note that overload on a single leg saturates at ~71 % FS, below the host clip code.
- **C-08 tenting wording.** C-08 says "tented on the bottom only (per rev-e-plan)". The board file tents both masks, but the EP mask opening exposes the top, and the order specifies JLC "Plugged". The disposition (X-ray) stands.
- **U15 not covered by codec.md.** It is audited here (G-C10). It was not wrong anywhere; it just was not in the codec scope.

Changed since Rev F (G / G100):
- C-02 fixed: C62 is 2.2 µF 16 V X5R; firmware sets VREF_QCHG = 10 ms (`TAC_WAKE` 0x19, was 0x09).
- C-04 fixed in firmware: INCAP_QCHG 12.5 ms and a 300 ms ADC settle (`TAC_ADC_SETTLE_MS`).
- C-05 fixed in firmware: the CLK_CFG0 fallback chain is implemented (`tac5112_start_report`).
- C-07 fixed: TP15-TP18 (BCLK, FSYNC, DOUT, DIN) with GND pads TP19/TP21. In G100 they are at x = 56.2 mm with 1.8-2.9 mm stubs, rerouted around the new power entry. SDA/SCL still have no pad (R72.2/R73.2 can be probed).
- C-12 / D-2: series-R footprints were declined in favour of RP2040 drive/slew in firmware. Firmware item 5 is still open.
- Rev G jack protection (TLP172AM, TVS, series R) removes the charged-DUT route into the follower legs (bring-up-rev-g drops §10). It does not cover the TIA leg at power-up (G-C1), so §10 should come back in a power-up form.
- G100 layout: the codec analog copper is identical (copper delta 403/403). U16 and U3 now sit 19-24 mm from U14. The 5 V feed to FB1 runs on B.Cu at x = 54.5 mm, ≥ 8 mm from U14, and carries only U15's current.

Unchanged and agreed: C-01 (SBAA634A settles mode 1 at 1 kHz; the wideband 27-60 kHz check stays), C-03 negative side (G-C2), C-06, C-09 to C-11, C-13 to C-15.

## 3. Debug and bring-up features (codec subsystem)

| ID | Feature | Why | Cost / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| DG-1 | Firmware: wake U14 and power the ADC before `rails_wait()` | Gives 3V3A a ~6 mA sink through the ±12 V ramp (G-C1). It also exercises I2C and the clocks before the analog rails, so a codec fault is told apart from a rail fault | No hardware. The ADC sees railing inputs harmlessly, and the 300 ms settle already gates captures |
| DG-2 | Firmware `STATUS`: add AVDD_IOVDD_STS (P0_R3, BRWNOUT_SHDN_STS) and DEV_STS0/1 | Latches a codec brown-out shutdown that a capture would otherwise show only as noise | No hardware |
| DG-3 | Bring-up: scope C60 (3V3A) DC-coupled through the first power-up with the relays reset, and through one SELFTEST | Settles G-C1 on the bench (pass: ≤ 3.6 V) | 10 minutes; TP2/TP19 for ground |
| DG-4 | 0 Ω 0603 between U15/C68 and the codec side of 3V3A (C60, C61, D11-D14 cathodes), Rev F D-3 | Measures IAVDD and makes clamp injection visible as reverse current. Optional if the G-C1 diode is added | One part on the analog supply. It changes "analog" copper, so it needs re-extraction |
| DG-5 | Schottky 3V3A → 3V3 (BAT54S, one half) | The hardware fix for G-C1, not a debug feature; listed so the respin carries it | One SOT-23; ≤ 25 µA forward in normal running (≤ 0.1 V across). Noise coupling through ~1 kΩ dynamic resistance into 6 µF is negligible |
| DG-6 | Pad on GPIO1 (pin 9, default IRQ) | Scope-triggerable clock or ASI error without I2C | ~1 pF on a digital pin on U14's south edge. Low value: CLK_ERR_STS already gives this over I2C |

Declined, as in Rev F: pads on VREF, DREG and 3V3A (probe C62.1, C63.1, C61.1 directly); any pad on the IN/CLP/DIV nodes; series R on the I2S lines.
