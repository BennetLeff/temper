# Datasheet audit, Rev G100, group `switcher-ldos`

Scope: U13 TPS65131RGER (boost RAW_P / inverter RAW_N) with L1/L2, D2/D3, C80-C92, R80-R84, R111; U17 LM3880MF-1AA
with R119/R120, C116/C118; U11 TPS7A4901 (+12V) and U12 TPS7A3001 (-12V) with R95/R96, C93-C102, R85-R89, R112;
rail monitors R97-R100, C110/C111 into U1 pins 38/39. Board: `cad/temper-lcr.kicad_pcb` at be071cbcf (G100),
read with KiCad pcbnew (read-only). Desk audit 2026-11-09. Scripts: scratchpad `datasheet-audit-g100/switcher-ldos-scripts/`.

Netlist trace: every pin of U11, U12, U13, U17 checked against the pin tables (TPS65131 Table 5-1, TPS7A49/30 Pin
Functions, LM3880 pin table). All connections are correct: U13 1/24 INP = SW_P, 2/3 PGND, 19 AGND, 9 PSP, 11 PSN,
25 EP = GND, 4 VIN = CONV_VIN (R80 100R / C81 100n), 5/6 INN = 5V, 7 BSW and 12/20 NC open, 8 ENP / 10 ENN from U17
FLAG1 / FLAG2 with 100k pull-ups to 3V3, 13/14 OUTN = SW_N, 15 VNEG = RAW_N, 16 FBN, 17 VREF (C84 220n, R84 150k),
18 CN (C85 4.7n), 21 CP (C86 10n), 22 FBP, 23 VPOS = RAW_P. U11/U12: 1 OUT, 2 FB, 3 NC open, 4 GND, 5 EN = IN,
6 NR/SS (10n), 7 DNC open, 8 IN, EP GND. U17: VCC 3V3, EN = C116 only, FLAG3 open.

### Arithmetic

- VPOS = 1.213 (1 + 430/40.2) = 14.19 V; worst 1.237 (1 + 10.696 x 1.0202) = 14.735 V. INP = 14.735 + 0.55 = 15.29 V (abs 17).
- VNEG = VFBN - (R3/R4)(VREF - VFBN), R3 = 1.74 M, R4 = 150 k: worst -14.805 V; OUTN-INN = 5.5 + 14.805 + 0.55 = 20.86 V (abs 24).
- LM3880 EN delay 1.25 x 100 n / 7 u = 17.9 ms; ENP at 17.9 + 0.4 + 10(±15 %) = 26.8-29.8 ms; ENN 8.5-11.5 ms later; U12 tSS 0.9 x 10 = 9 ms; U11 tSS 1.4 x 10 = 14 ms.
- Op-amp IQ into -12V: 4 x 3.6 + 1.0 = 15.4 mA typ; 4 x 4.05 + 1.3 = 17.5 mA max (25 °C).
- Trace inductance over In1 (h 0.21 mm): about 0.28 nH/mm at 0.35 mm, 0.18 nH/mm at 0.6 mm.
- Inductor L range 4.7 x (0.7 ... 1.3) = 3.29 ... 6.11 uH.

## Pass 2: versus the Rev F audit (switcher.md, ldos.md, README disposition)

- **S1/S2 (surge, ENP/ENN abs max):** fixed by U17 + C117, confirmed (SL-16). The fix created SL-1: ENP leads ENN by 10 ms, and the boost pass-through powers +12 V before ENP. Rev F L6 ("-12 V leads by ~5 ms") was written before U17. It analysed only DAC injection into op-amp inputs, not the floating V- rail. It is now wrong in direction and missed the mechanism.
- **L10 is wrong:** it says "no paste on the 6 via-in-pad vias". On the board, 4 of 6 vias per LDO and all 9 of U13's sit inside paste windows (SL-9). S15 noted U13's 9 paste windows but not the vias under them.
- **L8 cited "GND pin must be tied directly to the PowerPAD" but did not check it:** pin 4 is not tied on either LDO (SL-7). Rev F routing has the same copper.
- **Missed in Rev F:** the under-package EN-IN tracks beside NR/SS and DNC (SL-8). They are present in Rev F's `routing.json` (x 131.153 / 144.153), so they are inherited.
- **S9 gave cap distances only.** It did not look at the ground structure: single vias, no PGND pour, PGND not bridged to EP, the 20 mm inverter loop, C82 9.5 mm from INN (SL-2/3/4). S4's "unknown layout inductance" is now estimated at about 7 and 9-10 nH.
- **S7 and S3 recommendations were not applied:** G100's `regulator-check.json` still uses ±20 % / 3.8 A for L1/L2, and still says VNEG is "0.195 V beyond the recommended range" (it is inside).
- **S5 (inverter in DCM, about 5 MHz ring), S6 (FBN routing) and S16/L5 (monitor pins at power-down):** unchanged; the internal block layout is a rigid move of Rev F's.
- **Missed in Rev F:** SL-11 (CN cap dielectric) and SL-12 (LM3880 EN brown-out; U17 is new since that audit).
- **G100 changes in this scope:**
  - The block moved 10 mm north, D3 to U7 now 54.6 mm (pickup +2-4 dB, plan §3).
  - RAW_P now runs 12 mm (0.3 mm F.Cu) north to R95, and RAW_N 0.3 mm to R96.
  - -12V now runs on B.Cu under the block (x 135.2, y 109-124; y 124.4 west to x 110.6), shielded by In1.
  - 3V3 to U17/R119/R120 runs on In2 under the block.
  - The ENP/ENN flags run 16 mm on In2.
  - U13 VIN min is 4.266 V (Rev F 4.294).
  - The MON cluster moved away from U1 (SL-13).
  - None of these breaks a limit.
- **Debug:** TP1-TP9 now cover 5V, 3V3, ±12V, RAW_P, RAW_N and VRLY (Rev F's TP item, partly done). There is still nothing on SW_P/SW_N, FBN or ENP/ENN, and no GND pad near the switcher.
