# Datasheet audit, group `switcher`: U13 TPS65131RGER dual-output converter (RAW_P / RAW_N) and everything on its nets

Board: Rev F, `instruments/lcr-meter` in worktree `lcr-in-box`. Read-only desk audit, 2026-10-29.
Primary datasheet: TPS65130/TPS65131 SLVS493E (March 2004, revised April 2022), local `sources/ti/TPS65131.txt`.

Netlist trace (nets.txt / parts.txt): U13 pins 1,24 INP = SW_P; 2,3 PGND, 19 AGND, 25 EP = GND; 4 VIN = CONV_VIN (5V via R80 100R, C81 100n to GND);
5,6 INN = 5V; 7 BSW open; 8 ENP and 10 ENN = 3V3; 9 PSP and 11 PSN = GND; 12,20 NC open; 13,14 OUTN = SW_N; 15 VNEG = RAW_N; 16 FBN = CONV_FBN;
17 VREF = CONV_REF (C84 220n, R84 150k); 18 CN = C85 4.7n; 21 CP = C86 10n; 22 FBP = CONV_FBP; 23 VPOS = RAW_P.
L1 5V - SW_P; D2 K=RAW_P, A=SW_P; L2 SW_N - GND; D3 K=SW_N, A=RAW_N (matches Fig 8-1: L2 OUTN-to-GND, D2 anode on VNEG).
RAW_P: C87 (15p FF), C89, C90 (10u/50V), D2.K, R81 (430k), R95 (1R to LDOIN_P). RAW_N: C88 (4.7p FF), C91, C92, D3.A, R83 (240k), R96 (1R). Nothing else is on RAW_x.
All 25 pins checked against Table 5-1: every connection is correct.

### Computation record (used in the table)

- VPOS: nominal 1.213 x (1 + 430/40.2) = 14.19 V; max 1.237 x (1 + 10.696 x 1.0202) = 14.735 V; min 1.189 x (1 + 10.696 x 0.9802) = 13.655 V.
- VNEG: R3 = 240k + 1.5M = 1.74M, R4 = 150k. VNEG = VFBN - (R3/R4)(VREF - VFBN). Nominal -14.07 V; max -14.805 V (VREF 1.225, VFBN -24 mV, ratio x 1.0202); min -13.347 V. Bias term 50 nA x 1.74M = 87 mV; TCR 100 ppm/K x 35 K x 2 = 0.7 % of the ratio.
- Duty: boost D = 1 - 4.29/(14.74 + 0.45) = 0.72, inverter D = 15.26/(15.26 + 4.29) = 0.78, below the 87.5 % maximum, above 12.5 %.
- Eq 3/4 at 2x load (80 mA), VI 4.29 V: boost 14.74/(4.29 x 0.64) x 0.0716 = 0.385 A; inverter (4.29 + 14.81)/(4.29 x 0.64) x 0.0798 = 0.555 A. Eq 3/4 capability at ILIM min 1.8 A: boost 0.335 A, inverter 0.259 A (>= 3x the 2x loads).
- Inverter CCM boundary: Iout_crit = (dI/2)(1 - D) with dI = VI D/(L f): 63 mA (VIN 4.29 V, 4.7 uH, 1.25 MHz) to 130 mA (5.5 V, 3.29 uH).
- Input current at estimated load, power 1.07 W, Eq efficiency 0.64: 1.07/0.64/4.29 = 0.39 A; regulator_check's full estimate 0.461 A including 3V3 and LEDs, 39 mA under the 500 mA USB budget. A realistic converter efficiency (75-80 %) lowers the converter part to about 0.31-0.33 A.

## Debug and bring-up features worth adding (frozen layout; cost and risk noted)

1. Test points on RAW_P, RAW_N, SW_P, SW_N, CONV_FBP, CONV_FBN, CONV_REF, 5V at U13 and CONV_VIN. There are no test points on the board at all (0 TP in parts.txt). Use small unmasked pads with a ground pad beside them for a spring probe. Why: S1, S4, S5, S6 are all settled by looking at these nodes. Cost: 0 BOM. Risk: stray C on SW nodes (about 0.2 pF) is harmless; on FBN/FBP any pad adds capacitance against the 4.7 / 15 pF feedforward, so place the probe pad on the 5V-side end of R81/R111 (RAW_x side) rather than on the pin.
2. 0 ohm link or solder jumper in series with ENP/ENN (or the S2 100k) with a pad to GND: isolates the converters to measure the 3V3/5V/RP2040 current before the raw rails run and lets one converter run alone to separate S1 contributions. No effect on analog nodes.
3. 5V input-current shunt: a 0R/10 mOhm 1206 pad pair between U16 OUT and the 5V trunk (or in the 5V to U13 INN/L1 branch) with a sense pair. Currently the converter input current can only be inferred from the 5V total. R95/R96 (1R) already give rail load current from the drop on LDOIN_x.
4. RC snubber pads (0603, DNP, 10R in series with 220 pF) across D3, and one across D2: answers S4/S5 without a respin when the scope shows ring. Placed across the diode the added C is only on the switch node.
5. Bring-up: bench supply >= 1.5 A (not 0.5 A) for the first power-on; trigger the scope on 5V falling below 4 V to catch UVLO hiccups; measure fsw once on SW_P; hot-plug test already in bring-up-rev-e step 4.
6. Optional: DNP footprint for a 10-100 k series resistor in the FFC paths (C87/C88) is what the datasheet itself suggests ("10 kOhm to 100 kOhm" to cut noise coupling); only useful if S6 shows ripple on FBN. Adds nothing to the node when 0R is fitted.
