# Datasheet audit: LDOs (group `ldos`), LCR meter Rev F

Scope: U11 TPS7A4901 (+12 V), U12 TPS7A3001 (-12 V), R95/R96, feedback dividers, NR/SS and feed-forward caps, EN,
caps, exposed pads; U3 and U15 AP2112K-3.3. Independent arithmetic: `scratchpad/datasheet-audit/ldos_calc.py`.
Bottom line: **no VIOLATION found.** The `regulator_check.json` FLAG on R95/R96 headroom does not hold against the
datasheet text (L1/L2). Eight RISK/INFO items below, none blocking.

Netlist trace (nets.txt): RAW_P -> R95 -> LDOIN_P {C94 10u, C97 100n, U11.5 EN, U11.8 IN}; U11.1 -> 12V {C95 10u,
C96 10n to FB, R85 82k, 7 more decoupling caps, op-amps U5/U6/U7, R97 monitor, R115 LED}; REGP_FB {R85 82k top, R86 9.1k
bottom, C96 10n to OUT}; REGP_NR {C93 10n}; U11 pin 3 NC open, pin 7 DNC open, EP -> GND (6 vias in pad). U12 mirrors
with RAW_N/R96/LDOIN_N/-12V, divider R87 100k over R89 5k + R88 1k + R112 5k (11 k, 0.1 %), C98 NR, C101 FF. U3: VIN
and EN both on 5V, NC pin open, VOUT 3V3. U15: VIN and EN both on 5VA (5V -> FB1 -> C103 22u/C67 1u), VOUT 3V3A.

## Dissipation and closed-box temperature (U11/U12)

- Worst corner (RAW max, VOUT min, 2x load, IGND 1.5 mA): U11 (14.736 - 0.072 - 11.360) x 0.0717 + 14.736 x 0.0015 = 0.259 W; U12 (14.805 - 0.080 - 11.549) x 0.0798 + 0.022 = 0.276 W. RthJA 63.4 C/W (TPS7A49/30 6.4, JEDEC): 16.4 K and 17.5 K (the repo's 15-16 K used 0.24/0.26 W). Nominal 1x load: 0.083 / 0.088 W, 5.3 / 5.6 K.
- Wall rise 8.0 K (anodised, on a table, 2.3 W, 25 C ambient; docs/rev-f-before-after.md section 8). Internal box-air rise is not modelled in the repo. With ambient 35 C, wall +8 K, air +10 K (assumed), local dTj 17.5 K: Tj ~ 70 C; with air +25 K, 85 C. Limit 125 C (TI recommends triggering TSD, 170 C, 45 C above the maximum ambient). OK.
- R95/R96: I^2R = 5 mW of 100 mW.
