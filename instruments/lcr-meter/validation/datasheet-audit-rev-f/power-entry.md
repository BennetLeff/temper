# Datasheet audit, group `power-entry` (USB-C power and data entry), Temper LCR meter Rev F

Scope: J1 (HRO TYPE-C-31-M-12), R1/R2 (CC), D1 (SRV05-4), R3/R4 (D+/D- series), U16 (TPS2553DBVR), R110, C113, C15,
FB1 (GZ2012D101TF), C16/C80/C82/C83/C112 on 5V, C67/C103 on 5VA, R5/R6 (VBUS sense), the 5 V trunk.
Nets traced from `validation/datasheet-audit-rev-f/nets.txt`: USB_VBUS = C15, C113, D1.5, J1.A4/A9/B4/B9, R5.1, U16.1, U16.3 (EN tied to IN);
5V = C16 C80 C82 C83 FB1.1 L1.1 R80 R101 R113 U3.1/.3 U13.5/.6 U16.6; 5VA = C67 C103 FB1.2 U15.1/.3;
USB_DP = D1.1/.6, J1.A6/B6, R4.1; USB_DM = D1.3/.4, J1.A7/B7, R3.1; CC1/CC2 = J1 + R1/R2; VBUS_ILIM = R110.1, U16.5; U16.4 (FAULT) unconnected; J1 SBU1/SBU2 unconnected.

Retrieval status: TPS2553 (SLVS841F, local), RP2040 datasheet and hardware guide (local), SRV05-4 (Zhuhai Hongjiacheng Rev 2.1, downloaded), HRO TYPE-C-31-M-12 drawing (downloaded) were read in full for the relevant sections.
NOT retrieved (any number from them is marked "estimate" or "listing"): the USB 2.0 and USB Type-C specifications (quoted through TPS2553 section 11.2 and the repo's own `regulator_check.py` notes), the Sunlord GZ2012D101TF datasheet (LCSC link returns an HTML page; 800 mA / 150 mOhm are from JLCPCB/LCSC listing attributes), Samsung DC-bias curves for CL32B106KBJNNNE / CL31A226KAHNNNE / CL21B105KBFNNNE.

## Direct answers to the key questions

- CC pull-downs: correct for a sink (5.1 k on both CC); SBU unused; shield to GND. OK.
- VBUS capacitance at plug-in: 1.1 uF ahead of U16, well under the 10 uF limit, and U16 sits at the receptacle (8 mm) so the placement satisfies the rule; the problem is what is behind U16 (PE-2, PE-4).
- ILIM versus start-up: 24 k gives 0.989-1.17 A, correct programming per the datasheet method and 2x the steady estimate; it is not shown to cover the simultaneous converter soft-start at 4.40 V (PE-5, bench-check).
- Ferrite: carries about 60 mA, not 0.46 A; no saturation concern (PE-15).
- ESD part: ratings fit the signals (VRWM 5 V, 1 pF), but it is not a hot-plug clamp for U16's 7 V limit (PE-1, PE-6).
- DC-bias: no capacitor on this net is below its requirement; the estimates need vendor curves (PE-16).

## Debug / bring-up features worth adding (power-entry)

Existing bare pads (design.json `board.testpoints`): 5V, GND, 3V3, +-12, VRLY, RAW+, RAW-, K1-K4 SET, RST. None on USB_VBUS, VBUS_ILIM, U16 FAULT, CC1/CC2, USB D+/D-.

1. USB_VBUS test pad at C113/U16.1 (bare 1.5 mm pad on a DC node, zero cost). Why: needed to scope hot-plug ringing (PE-1) at the part that matters, to read VBUS-minus-5V (62 mV at 0.46 A across U16's 135 mohm max + trunk; gives a current estimate), and to log VBUS at the board versus at the cable (host sag). Existing 5V pad is 50 mm away at the converter.
2. U16 FAULT pad plus 100 k pull-up to USB_VBUS (two parts, no analog impact): shows current-limit hiccups at 4.40 V (PE-5, PE-7) and reverse-voltage trips (PE-8). Optional second use: spare GPIO through a pull-up to 3V3.
3. EN link: make U16.3 reach IN through a 0 ohm 0603 (cut or lift), with a bare pad on the EN side. Lets bring-up hold U16 off to check the VBUS side alone, or drive EN from a bench source or later a GPIO (PE-2 fix) without a respin of the net. Cost: one resistor, about 5 mohm leakage on EN, no effect on ILIM.
4. DNP footprints at U16 IN: a SOD-123/SMA TVS to GND and a 0805 + 1206 damping branch (series 0.5-1 ohm resistor with 4.7 uF). Populate after the hot-plug scope (PE-1). Cost: board area at the receptacle; no effect when unpopulated.
5. DNP parallel footprint for R110 (0603) to trim ILIM (e.g. add 56 k to lift IOS) without lifting R110. ILIM is a sensitive node (datasheet: never drive ILIM, keep traces short); a DNP pad adds about 0.3 pF, which the loop tolerates, but do not add a probe pad on ILIM.
6. CC1 test pad (on the R1 trace, near-zero capacitance): measures the Rp the source advertises (Rp current x 5.1 k: 80 uA default = 0.41 V, 180 uA 1.5 A = 0.92 V, 330 uA 3 A = 1.68 V; values from memory of the Type-C spec) so the Type-C current class of any host or charger can be logged.
7. D+/D- probing: use R3/R4 pads (already two exposed pads per line, one on each side of the 27 ohm); do not add stubs, even 1 pF stubs on an unterminated FS line change the eye slightly.
8. Series "ammeter" link between U16.6 and the 5V trunk (0 ohm 0603 or a cuttable trace): break-and-insert for a DMM or sense amp. Low priority; adds up to 20-50 mohm (about 10-23 mV at 0.46 A) unless a solder-bridge footprint is used. The planned inline USB-C meter covers the same need.
9. Firmware only: print VBUS-divider state and U13/U3 reset reasons so a PE-5 hiccup or PE-8 reset is visible over CDC without a scope.
