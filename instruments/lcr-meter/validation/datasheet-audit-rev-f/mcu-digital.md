# Datasheet audit, Rev F: group `mcu-digital`

Scope: U1 RP2040, U2 GD25Q16ESIGR, Y1 ABM8-272-T3 + C12/C13/R10, core regulator + decoupling, supply pins, RUN/SW2,
BOOTSEL/SW1, TESTEN, SWD J4/J5, UART J6, ADC inputs (MON12/MON_N12/USB_SENSE), I2C/I2S, relay-driver GPIOs, LEDs D4, D15-D20.
Method: parts.txt/nets.txt, cad/design.json, cad/temper-lcr.kicad_pcb (pad positions and track lengths parsed with a script,
scratch file `scratchpad/datasheet-audit/mcu_pads.json`), firmware-rev-e `pins.h`/`main.c`/`relay.c`, rev-f-plan.md,
bring-up-rev-e.md, `validation/power-spurs/rail_monitor_window.py`. Nothing in the repository was modified.

Datasheet shorthand: RP2040 DS = `sources/new/RP2040-datasheet.txt` (build-date 2025-02-20). HDG = `sources/new/RP2040-hardware.txt`
(Hardware design with RP2040, Release 2 / A.2 15 Oct 2024). GD = `sources/new/GD25Q16E.txt` (v1.1, 2020-04-15).

## Items checked and fine, no finding

- RUN: pulled to 3V3 by R9, RC to GND C14, SW2 to GND. RUN has an internal pull-up (DS Table 619), FT. Fine.
- BOOTSEL: SW1 -> R8 1 k -> QSPI_CS exactly as HDG 2.2. With R7 10 k also fitted, a pressed switch gives 3.3 V x 1 k/(1 k + 10 k||50 k) = 0.35 V, below VIL max 0.8 V (DS Table 625). Fits.
- USB: R3/R4 27 R (DS Table 614 "external 27 ohm series resistors are required"); USB_VDD = 3V3.
- USB_SENSE (GPIO13): R5/R6 100 k/100 k, VBUS 5.5 V -> 2.75 V max; FT pin.
- SWD: SWCLK/SWDIO pads on J4 (DNP), GND/3V3 on J5 (DNP). SWD pads reset to pull-up; the RP2040 SWD has no reset line, RUN is on SW2.
- Unused GPIOs (0, 1, 12, 15-21, 24) unconnected: legal, internal pull-down at reset.
- Pin-by-pin: all 56 pads of U1 checked against nets.txt and firmware pins.h (RNG_SET_HI 6, LO 7, RESET 10, MED 11, REF 14, USB_VBUS 13, STATUS 25, FLT 22, HB 23, MON 26/27, UART 28/29, I2S 2-5, I2C 8/9), no mismatch. GD25Q16E pins 1-8 against the GD pin table (CS, SO/IO1, WP#/IO2, VSS, SI/IO0, SCLK, HOLD#/IO3, VCC) and against RP2040 QSPI_SD0-3: all right.
