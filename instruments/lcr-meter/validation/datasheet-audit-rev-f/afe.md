# Datasheet audit, group AFE (analog front end), LCR meter Rev F

Scope: U5 OPA197 (source difference stage), U6/U7 OPA2810 (source drive, HIGH/LOW followers, TIA), precision resistors,
C0G capacitors, R94 (R_iso), R104, R105/R106, K4 reference path, BAT54S clamps D11-D14, the four BNC jacks (J3 HCUR,
J7 HPOT, J8 LPOT, J9 LCUR) and the divider/attenuator networks feeding the TAC5112. Netlist traced from
`validation/datasheet-audit-rev-f/nets.txt`; topology from `docs/rev-e-front-end.md`, `docs/rev-e-range-switching.md`,
`docs/rev-d-decision.md`.

## Topology as built (from nets.txt)

- DAC_P/DAC_M -> R30/R31 2.2k -> U5 (OPA197) difference stage, R32/R33 2k, 2x560 pF C0G across each 2k -> EXCITE (+-1.7 V peak).
- EXCITE -> U6A (OPA2810, unity buffer, feedback at pin) -> SRC_DRV -> R104 47 R -> SOURCE_BUF -> K1/K2/K3 pole 1 ->
  R40 100 R / R41 5k / R42 100k -> DUT_HI (J3 HCUR, K4.8, R105).
- DUT_LO (J9 LCUR, C78 100 pF C0G to GND, K4 reference via R107) -> R94 22 R -> TIA_SUM = U7A -IN; U7A +IN = GND;
  feedback C104 100 pF plus K1/K2/K3 pole 2 and R43 100 R / R44 5k / R45 100k. All relays are latching DPDT; NC contacts unconnected,
  so "reset" = no feedback resistor connected.
- J7 HPOT centre pin -> U6B +IN directly (no resistor); R105 1k ties HPOT to DUT_HI. J8 LPOT centre pin -> U7B +IN directly; R106 1k ties to DUT_LO.
- HIGH_BUF/LOW_BUF/TIA_OUT -> 1k + 1k divider (K = 0.5, 500 R Thevenin) with 560 pF C0G at the divider node -> 1 uF X7R ->
  BAT54S clamp node (CxP_CLP/CxM_CLP, D11-D14 to GND and 3V3A) -> 470 R -> TAC5112 pin.
- Rails: +12 V / -12 V from TPS7A49/TPS7A30. `validation/power-spurs/regulator_check.json` gives 11.29..12.46 V (+) and 11.53..12.20 V (-) with tolerance and TCR.

## Findings

project rule is "isolated, discharged DUT only" (README, bring-up, silk "DISCHARGED / DUT ONLY"). I judge that argument below.

Judgement on the "discharged DUT only" argument: it holds for the production-style use (small parts, measured after a short),
but the thresholds are 5-15 V, a level that a charged 9 uF film capacitor, an electrolytic after a bias test, or a resonant tank
capacitor can plausibly carry, and "discharged" is not observable on the bench. For the energies involved
(0.5 x 9 uF x 20 V^2 = 1.8 mJ into 22 R in about 0.2 ms) neither Yageo nor Viking give pulse curves, so survival of R94/R43 is
unverified. The datasheet explicitly asks for series resistors where beyond-supply voltages are possible, so AFE-02 is a MISSING
item against 8.3.2 even though the board is inside the limits for the stated use.

### Things checked that were fine (not worth a finding)

- BAT54S pinout/polarity: pin 1 = anode1 (GND), pin 2 = cathode2 (3V3A), pin 3 = common (clamp node). Correct.
- Resistor ranges: RT0603 0.1 % / 25 ppm is offered for 1 R..1 MOhm (2.2k, 22 R, 100 R, 1k, 100k all in range); AR03 0.1 % 25 ppm is offered 1 R..1 MOhm (2k, 5k).
- Divider resistors at worst-case TIA_OUT saturation (11.06 V across R67 + R62): 5.5 mA, 30 mW in R67 (0.1 W derated to 0.082 W at 85 C): fine; 1 V on 100 R range: 10 mA, 10 mW.
- U5 DC accuracy: mismatch of 0.1 % + 0.1 % pairs against 1.65 V CM gives at most 1.65 x 0.909 x 0.002 = 3 mV at EXCITE, matching "a few mV" in the doc; irrelevant for AC-coupled capture, tiny DC across a DUT.
- OPA2810 TIA with C104 on the 100k range: the feedback is mostly capacitive above 16 kHz (pole 1/(2 pi x 100k x 100 pF) = 15.9 kHz); this is part of the Zf model and the 3-standard calibration.
- OPA2810 differential-input clamp: the unity-gain followers and TIA see hot-plug slew steps up to 7 V differential by design (datasheet 9.2.2, "no external resistors required").

## Debug / bring-up features worth adding (analog, all cost-aware)

1. **EXCITE test pad (U5 output).** Safe: OPA197 specifies capacitive load up to 1 nF; U6A +IN is high impedance; a 10 pF probe changes nothing. Gives a direct view of the difference stage before U6A and R104. Cost: one pad.
2. **Document the free probe points (no new copper):** SRC_DRV = R104 pad 1, SOURCE_BUF = R104 pad 2, TIA_OUT = R67 pad 1 (behind 1k, safe), HIGH_BUF/LOW_BUF = R60 pad 1 / R61 pad 1, TIA current = differential probe across R94 (22 R: 10 mA = 220 mV). Do not probe TIA_SUM (R94 pad 2, U7 pin 2) with anything but an active FET probe under 1 pF; the loop margin is 57 deg.
3. **Analog-area GND pad** (or a GND via next to EXCITE and R94): the only GND pads are in the power area, so a scope ground lead on the AFE is long and picks up relay coil switching and digital edges. Cost: one pad on existing GND copper.
4. **DNP series-R footprints at J7 and J8 inputs (0 R fitted)** so that a 100 R..1k input resistor can be tried later against AFE-02 without a respin of routing. Cost: two 0603 pads in the sense path, about 0.2-0.4 pF each at the follower +IN; risk is only a few mrad of phase, equal on both followers.
5. **DNP TVS/Schottky pad pairs on the jack side of J3, J7, J8 and J9 (not on TIA_SUM).** J9 already has C78 on that node, so a 1-3 pF device adds little. Do not populate until the leakage (nA at +-12 V) and capacitance are checked against the follower input (12 GOhm || 2.5 pF) and the 57 deg TIA margin.
6. **Residual DUT voltage sense (respin item).** 1 MOhm + 100 kOhm divider from DUT_HI to an RP2040 ADC pin with a small Schottky to 3V3, read by firmware before the source and the relays are enabled; refuse to start above a few volts. Cost: 3 parts and one ADC pin; side effect is an 1.1 MOhm DC load on HCUR (0.9 % on the 100k range), removed by open calibration since it is linear and resistor-stable, and is a better fix than anything on the TIA node. A switchable version (relay K4 pole 1 is unused) removes the load.
7. **C78 and C104 are DNP-able already** (both 0603 C0G, same footprint). Keep them out of the BOM-locked state in the bring-up plan so loop-stability experiments (51-58 deg vs 38 deg) are one soldering iron away.
8. **A "relay state unknown" bleed is NOT recommended** (a resistor from TIA_SUM to GND adds noise current and bias error on the 100k range); use firmware ordering instead (AFE-09).
9. **K4 reference check** (existing) is the right tool for AFE-05/06/08: run it at the start of each session and log board temperature.
