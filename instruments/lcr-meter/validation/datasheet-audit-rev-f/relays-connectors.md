## How VRLY is made (traced from nets.txt)

USB_VBUS -> U16 (TPS2553, R110 24 kohm, ~1.0-1.16 A limit) -> net `5V` (also feeds L1/U13 boost input, U3 3V3 LDO, FB1->5VA,
C16/C80/C82/C83) -> **R101 2.2 ohm** (0603, 1 %, 100 mW) -> net `VRLY` (C112 22 uF 25 V X5R 1206, TP5, D5-D9 cathodes, all eight
coil "+" pins: K1-K4 pins 1 and 10). Coil "-" pins: SET- (pin 5) on RLY_SET_{LO,MED,HI,REF} = Q4/Q3/Q5/Q6 drains; RST- (pin 6) of all
four relays on one bus RLY_RST = Q2 drain. Flyback: D6-D9 across each SET coil, D5 across the four paralleled RESET coils (anode on the
driver side, cathode VRLY). Gates: GPIO -> 100 ohm -> gate, gate -> 100 kohm -> GND (RNG_RESET=GPIO10/U1.13, SET_HI=GPIO6/U1.8,
SET_LO=GPIO7/U1.9, SET_MED=GPIO11/U1.14, SET_REF=GPIO14/U1.17). NC pins (2, 9) of K1-K4 and K4 pole 1 (pins 3, 4) are unconnected.
TP10-TP14 (design.json `board.testpoints` entries K1, K2, K3, K4, RST; bring-up-rev-e.md table) are bare 1.5 mm pads on the drains.

## Debug / bring-up features worth adding

| feature | why | cost / risk |
|---|---|---|
| Coil current is already measurable: differential probe TP1 (5V) to TP5 (VRLY) across R101: I = dV / 2.2 ohm (RESET about 0.25 V) | Shows actual coil current and rail dip, i.e. checks RC-01 directly with no new parts | none; use a differential probe or two probes subtracted |
| K4 pole 1 state readback: COM1 (pin 3) to GND, NO1 (pin 4) to a spare GPIO with the RP2040 pull-up (pin 2 NC stays open) | K4 is the only relay whose failed RESET matters functionally; this reads closed/open with no analog exposure and replaces the "capture and look for 1 kohm" check | one spare GPIO, no analog node: but NO1/COM1 sit 0.5 pF class from DUT_HI/REF_A (static DC level only, no switching), so the stability impact is nil; needs a layout change (only a few mm of routing) |
| DNP 1 nF 0603 pad from RLY_RST to VRLY (at Q2) | Hard-grounds the shared coil bus for RC-08, fit only if the measured coupling (bring-up step 8, loose relay) or stability needs it | zero on analog nodes; ~5 mm^2 |
| DNP 0 ohm / jumper in series with the VRLY feed or in parallel with R101 (second 0603 pad on R101) | Lets R101 be shorted to test RC-01 mitigation (2.2 -> 0 ohm) without a respin | one footprint; fit-time choice |

| Relay-state ledger in firmware (last commanded state) exposed in a status command | the boot RESET is the only recovery from an unknown state; showing "unknown after reset until first RANGE" avoids trusting a stale range | firmware only |
| A spare-contact test jig (loose TQ2SA on a breakout) for contact-to-coil and pole-to-pole capacitance, and Rc cycling at 1.7 / 8.5 / 17 uA | closes RC-07 and RC-08 against datasheet silence | bench time only |
| Combine SWD J4 and J5 into one 1x3/1x4 (SWCLK, GND, SWDIO, 3V3) | a Pico debug probe cable (3-pin) fits; today J4 and J5 are 23 mm apart | layout change; DNP anyway |
