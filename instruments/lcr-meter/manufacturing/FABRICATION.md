# Manufacturing instructions — revision F engineering prototype

Revision F is the Rev E circuit (TI TIDA-060029 based, TAC5112 codec front end, relay ranges, four-terminal BNC jacks; [rev-e-plan.md](../docs/rev-e-plan.md)) re-laid by hand on a **100 × 100 mm board** for a 110 × 110 × 36 mm enclosure ([rev-f-plan.md](../docs/rev-f-plan.md), [rev-f-before-after.md](../docs/rev-f-before-after.md)). Rev F's only netlist change is the indicator LEDs: D4, the STATUS light, is a side-looking orange LED on the front edge, and six red debug LEDs (D15–D20) show the rails, a heartbeat and a fault ([rev-f-plan.md](../docs/rev-f-plan.md), "Indicator LEDs"). Revisions A–E are superseded and must not be mixed with this package; Rev E's outputs are archived in `rev-e/`. No manufacturer upload, placement-preview approval or order has been performed.

## Fabrication

- Four layers: **F.Cu / In1.Cu GND / In2.Cu signals and power / B.Cu**. FR-4, **FR4-Standard TG 135–140** (JLC's 4-layer default; TG155 is not needed: the board dissipates under 3 W, runs near ambient, and one lead-free reflow is within standard FR-4's rating), stackup JLC04161H-7628 as in the parasitic extraction, 1.6 mm finished thickness, 1 oz outer copper and standard inner copper. ENIG is recommended for the 0.4 mm-pitch RP2040 and exposed-pad packages. Confirm the offered stackup before release.
- Finished outline 100.00 × 100.00 mm (board coordinates x 50–150, y 45–145). Four 3.2 mm NPTH mounting holes, 92 × 87 mm pattern. All components are on top. Empty bottom silk is intentional.
- Design clearance 0.15 mm; narrow local tracks 0.15 mm. **Every via has a 0.30 mm drill** (305 vias: 82 × 0.60 mm, 27 × 0.55 mm and 196 × 0.45 mm pads), JLC's standard "0.3mm/(0.4/0.45mm)" via option; the board rules enforce a 0.30 mm minimum hole and 0.075 mm minimum via annular ring. Do not select the 0.2 or 0.25 mm minimum-via option: it adds cost and is not needed. Native DRC is authoritative for final geometry.
- **Via covering "Plugged" (JLC's free 4-layer default); do not order POFV.** JLC's quote page (2026-10-29) greys out "Tented" for this 4-layer setup; the CAD still tents every via on both masks. Select Plugged, and in the vendor DFM confirm that the exposed-pad thermal vias are plugged from the bottom only with their top lands clean. The only vias in SMT lands are the 33 thermal vias in the exposed pads U1.57 (8), U11.9 (6), U12.9 (6), U13.25 (9) and U14.25 (4, the TAC5112), open on top under the paste and tented on the bottom. The TI package notes for TPS65131, TPS7A30, TPS7A49 and TAC5112 say vias under paste should be "filled, plugged or tented"; SLUA271C §3.4.1 warns that backside tenting can increase voiding, so expect some solder loss into these vias and X-ray the exposed-pad parts on the first build. No via drill or via ring touches any other land. `cad/audit_dfm.py` checks drills, rings and tenting, and `validation/via-in-pad-process.json` records the result with the board hash. POFV on 4-layer boards is a paid option. Obtain vendor DFM acceptance before assembly.
- Keep the deliberate plane voids around the op-amp signal pins and feedback networks. Ground remains a plane elsewhere. Do not merge or redraw these voids during CAM preparation.
- Use the separate plated and nonplated Excellon drill files. USB shell slots must remain routed slots, not substituted round holes. Review the slot maps against the supplied connector drawings.
- Drill/place origin is lower-left outline corner, board coordinate (50,145), exported (0,0). Positive exported placement Y points upward. Gerber, drill and CPL use the same origin.
- The Gerber zip is the fabrication input. Preview PNG/SVG/PDF files are for inspection only. Request removable vendor process rails/tooling/fiducials if required, returning depanelized 100 × 100 mm boards. The BNC barrels overhang the top edge: no rail on that edge after depanelization. This does not add another functional circuit board.

## Assembly

The JLC BOM and CPL cover **209 top-side SMT placements** (66 lines), including the four TQ2SA relays, the TAC5112 and the U16 TPS2553 USB power switch. The complete BOM contains **216 components, 213 fitted**. JLC hand fits the four SAMZO BNC-KYWE-295-W4-N right-angle BNCs (J3, J7, J8, J9; C41416668). J4–J6 (TSW-102-07-G-S, SWD and UART) are **do not fit** (DNP in the schematic, PCB and BOM): firmware loads over USB, and a header can be soldered by hand if SWD is ever needed. Each BNC's barrel faces the top edge; its square pad pattern does not key it, so follow the silk opening mark. DUT leads/fixture and the USB-C cable are external accessories, not fitted PCB components. There is no DC jack: the board is powered from USB-C VBUS.

D4 (LTST-S270KFKT, C160472, extended) is a side-looking LED: in the placement preview its lens must face the board's front (BNC) edge, with pad 1 (cathode) toward the left mounting hole; its CPL offset is 0 (EasyEDA record and Lite-On drawing agree). D18 (−12 V) is rotated 180° from D15–D17 on purpose: its anode is on GND.

J1 is a mixed-technology USB connector included in SMT assembly; its shield anchors must be soldered. It carries power as well as data.

Ranges are latching relays K1 (100 Ω), K2 (5 kΩ) and K3 (100 kΩ) under firmware control; the firmware selects 100 kΩ at boot because a latching relay keeps its last state through power-off. K4 switches the on-board 1 kΩ reference across the jacks for `REFCAPTURE`; every RESET opens it. The DUT connects to the four BNCs, left to right on the top edge J3 HCUR, J7 HPOT, J8 LPOT, J9 LCUR (silk labels); J4/J5 are debug and J6 UART.

`orientation-audit.csv` records native rotation, exact-library correction, final CPL angle and pad1 location. Corrections come from numbered pads of each exact LCSC-owned EasyEDA entry. See `validation/placement-library-audit.md` and `sources/placement-rev-c.json`; the Rev E parts' offsets come from `cad/tools/placement_offsets.py`. Check every keyed device against the final JLC placement preview and `previews/assembly-top.png`; native KiCad angle alone is not a vendor zero-angle guarantee. R/C values and references are on the assembly drawing, while critical labels remain on silk.

## Prototype release

Native ERC/DRC, schematic/PCB pin parity, BOM membership and placement coordinates must pass on the final source files before packaging. Review the actual exported copper, plane, solder-mask and drill plots. Before assembly approval, complete vendor DFM and the component-orientation preview, including exposed-pad via treatment. Physical stability, source-off noise, codec acquisition and calibrated accuracy remain prototype tests. `docs/bring-up.md` is the Rev D procedure (jumpers, ADS9224R); Rev E uses [`docs/bring-up-rev-e.md`](../docs/bring-up-rev-e.md). The Rev F verification receipt is in [`validation/README.md`](../validation/README.md); the circuit and bring-up are Rev E's.
