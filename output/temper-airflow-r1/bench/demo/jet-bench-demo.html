How the bench measures the jet box's cooling

Eight 3 mm air jets blow down on the power board's hottest strip. This rig rebuilds that channel over a heated aluminium plate. The plate stands in for the strip: if the heater puts in P watts and the plate sits ΔT above the jet air, the jets are removing heat at h = P / (A·ΔT).

Why it matters: the power stage will not raise the tank-current ceiling above 18 A until this number is measured. 28 A at a 61 °C bay needs W/m²K.
